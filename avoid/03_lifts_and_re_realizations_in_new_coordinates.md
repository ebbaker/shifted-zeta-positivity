# Lifting the flow, or re-realizing an already positive object, in new coordinates

15 October 2026. Claude Fable 5.1 (`claude-fable-5-1`), Claude Cowork.

## The pattern

The problem is embedded in a larger structure (a Fock space, a
supersymmetric pair, a gauge field, a quaternionic or octonionic lift, a
hyperkähler manifold, a graph Dirac operator, a fractal Laplacian) in the
hope that the structure carries a sign the original did not. The
embedding is exact; its restriction to the physical slice is the original
quantity; the sign is unchanged; and the structure's own theorems are true
of every member of a class that contains counterexamples.

## What it looks like here

Seventeen heat lifts in one day; four CCM realization rounds and a fractal
branch; quaternionic, octonionic, self-dual, hyperkähler, jet-surface and
theta-bundle coordinates for the Laguerre expressions; a realization of a
positive finite pencil as a string, a graph and an operator.

## Instead

Before lifting, write down the relation the lift is supposed to supply
that survives the Newman deformation and fails for the controls (Review 16
§8 item 5; [Review 19](../papers/investigations-2/active/22_theta_manifold_geometry/reviews/19_PATHS_FORWARD_AFTER_THE_SECOND_ROUND_CLAUDE_20261015.md) §2 states the geometric reason the surface-level modular structure has not entered the one-dimensional flow). If the relation cannot be stated, the lift is bookkeeping and gets one remark, not a manuscript. The durable outputs of these branches are their dictionaries and controls ([result-summaries 14](../result-summaries/14_theta_identities_and_arithmetic_bookkeeping.md)); record those and close.
