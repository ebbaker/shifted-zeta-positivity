# Realizing one factor, a boundary phase or a positive state space, and calling it the target

15 October 2026. Claude Fable 5.1 (`claude-fable-5-1`), Claude Cowork.

## The pattern

A physical or geometric construction reproduces one ingredient of the
complete form (the gamma kernel, a unit-modulus boundary multiplier, a
positive Hilbert space, a Ward identity) and the match is described as a
realization of the arithmetic target. The missing ingredient (the prime
comb, causality, an actual inequality) is exactly the RH-equivalent part.

## What it looks like here

A YM state space that reproduces a sentence true of \(\ell^2(\mathbb N)\)
with no group; the Robin Gaussian with the prime comb "outside"; an
all-pass boundary phase that follows from the functional equation; a
positive state space mistaken for a causal contraction; a symmetry of the
action mistaken for Osterwalder–Schrader reflection.

## Evidence

- [YM cross-model verification](../papers/susy-positivity/investigations/wilson-loewner/YM/reviews/REVIEW_CLAUDE_MANUSCRIPT_VERIFICATION_20260925.md) §3.1: "the same sentence holds for the Haar measure on one copy of SU(2) … or for ℓ²(ℕ) with no group at all."
- [Wilson–Loewner free kernel](../papers/susy-positivity/investigations/wilson-loewner/sections/03_free_kernel.tex): "The supplied factor \(\pi^\omega\), the prime comb, and the rational pole correction remain outside this Gaussian mechanism."
- [Brownian readout review](../papers/susy-positivity/investigations/wilson-loewner/WZW/reviews/review_codex_brownian_readout_20260923.md): "The exact unit-modulus boundary multiplier follows from the functional equation without RH. Causality … is the missing RH-equivalent assertion."; the [fractional-dimension trap list](../papers/susy-positivity/investigations/fractional-dimension/notes/NEW_SESSION_PROMPT.md): "CONTRACTION AT omega >= 1/2 IS NOT EVIDENCE."
- [Wilson-lines cross-model review](../papers/susy-positivity/investigations/wilson-lines/reviews/review_claude-fable-5-1_2026-09-17.md) §2.2: the step from boundary values to innerness was "false in the strip" and had to be repaired.
- [Lessons from WZW](../papers/susy-positivity/investigations/wilson-loewner/notes/LESSONS_FROM_WZW_FOR_YM_AND_N4SYM_20260923.md): a positive state space is not a causal contraction; the [Codex review](../papers/susy-positivity/investigations/wilson-lines/reviews/review_codex_2026-09-18.md) §3: "Positivity is an inequality; nonrenormalization needs an actual Ward identity or cohomological argument".
- The all-pass dichotomy itself ([result-summaries 05](../result-summaries/05_transfer_loewner_and_fractional_dimension.md)): the contraction margin "carries essentially no arithmetic information".

## What it cost

The YM manuscript was reframed as a negative-result record with errata;
the Brownian readout left causality open; the odd-\(H\) reflection was
disproved after a correction notice; the WZW branch was parked after its
four sibling results were found to cover it.

## Instead

Name the target first (the collar theorem's lesson: the transfer \(V\),
the form \(Q\) and the discrepancy \(D\) are different objects, [cross-program priorities](../papers/susy-positivity/investigations/wilson-loewner/notes/CROSS_PROGRAM_PRIORITIES_AFTER_WZW_AND_N4SYM_REVIEWS_20260924.md) §3); list which of gamma, pole and prime terms the construction carries, as the top-level [README](../README.md) requires ("The gamma, pole, and prime terms must be retained together"); and apply the YM four-question filter ([structural requirements](../papers/susy-positivity/investigations/wilson-loewner/YM/notes/STRUCTURAL_REQUIREMENTS_AND_NEXT_STEPS_20260925.md)) before writing a physical channel up.
