# Restating the target and counting it as progress

15 October 2026. Claude Fable 5.1 (`claude-fable-5-1`), Claude Cowork.

## The pattern

A proposal rewrites the quantity whose sign is wanted (a Weil form, a
Laguerre expression, a residual trace) in a new representation, proves the
rewriting exactly, checks it on polynomial or rational surrogates, and
records the result as a step toward the sign. The new representation
contains the unknown sign in a new boundary term, coefficient or
remainder, and the next note starts from there. Over a few days this
produces a hierarchy of "conditional criteria" that look like weaker
intermediate statements and are, when examined, each equivalent to the
conclusion.

## What it looks like here

- Stationary sign tests, chord laws, exponential averages, turning moduli
  and scalar trace targets for the heat flow, each with an exclusion
  theorem and a sharpness statement, none weaker than RH for the theta
  field.
- An arithmetic sign problem reduced to \(S_N\ge0\), to \(K\le B\), or to
  "the scalar signed arithmetic estimate", which is where the note began.
- Family-moment estimates reduced to the same family moment with a sector
  removed.

## What it cost

Two manuscripts of 56 and 96 pages, about a hundred notes and fifty
reviews in two days for Investigations 21–22; sixty-one notes in the mixed
and short-family lanes; the ground-state package's notes 11–20. In each
case the correct identities are kept and the sign is where it was.

## Instead

Classify every proposal at the outset (Reply 18 §7, [Review 19](../papers/investigations-2/active/22_theta_manifold_geometry/reviews/19_PATHS_FORWARD_AFTER_THE_SECOND_ROUND_CLAUDE_20261015.md) §5): if it implies RH and RH implies it, it is a reformulation, to be stated once and not supported by checks, since no check can be evidence for a statement that is true if and only if RH; if it does not imply RH, it is a milestone, judged by its number. The portfolio README's rule 7 says the same: "Continue only for a new applicable estimate, complete certified gain, faithful diagnostic, or precise obstruction" ([investigations-2 README](../papers/investigations-2/README.md)), and the [program meta-analysis](../papers/investigations/program-meta-analysis/reviews/PROGRAM_META_ANALYSIS_20260926.md) §4.1 asks of every representation "what theorem has become easier to prove, and why?" Equivalence by itself is not circularity (the same README, rule 1); the mistake is counting it as progress.
