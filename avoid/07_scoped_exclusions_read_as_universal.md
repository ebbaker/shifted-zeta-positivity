# Scoped exclusions upgraded into universal no-go statements

15 October 2026. Claude Fable 5.1 (`claude-fable-5-1`), Claude Cowork.

## The pattern

A correct negative result about one decomposition, one family, one
transport class or one tested mechanism is restated as an exclusion of
every realization, every geometry or every continuation. The mirror image
of [06](06_status_stronger_than_the_evidence.md): the overclaim is on the
negative side, and it closes doors the evidence did not close. Reviews of
reviews have had to re-qualify these repeatedly, including the reviews
written by the model writing this file.

## What it looks like here

"Unreachable", "all ... are closed", "the uniquely possible additional
input", "every anisotropic contour has the same estimating power", "only
all-prime relations can be load-bearing"; a collar lemma about the transfer read as a lemma
about the form; a map-induced delay theorem read as a theorem about all
Wilson observables.

## Evidence

- [Inverse-bulk exclusion map](../papers/weil-positivity/susy-positivity/investigations/previous/inverse-bulk-realization/notes/EXCLUSION_MAP_20260917.md) §6: "an exclusion about a decomposition was silently upgraded into an exclusion about a realization"; the v0.4 ghost-pair requirement and the "unreachable" remark were withdrawn in v0.5.
- [memo16](../early-chat-logs/initial-project-files/rh2/claude/memo16_scope_corrections.md): "a family-specific rigidity result being read as a universal structural principle".
- [WZW assessment](../papers/weil-positivity/susy-positivity/investigations/wilson-loewner/WZW/reviews/review_claude_wzw_program_assessment_20260924.md) §2.2: "The collar note conflates the two targets, and the lessons note inherits the conflation"; the [cross-program note](../papers/weil-positivity/susy-positivity/investigations/wilson-loewner/notes/CROSS_PROGRAM_PRIORITIES_AFTER_WZW_AND_N4SYM_REVIEWS_20260924.md) then separated \(Q\), \(D\) and \(V\) and narrowed the far-field theorem to "the specified translation-invariant map-induced transport".
- [Cross-model review of the Newman audit](../papers/investigations-2/newman_certificate_audit/reviews/CROSS_MODEL_REVIEW_OF_CLAUDE_AUDIT_CODEX_20261013.md): the benchmark "turns a reasonable decision about research priorities into stronger claims about impossibility, cost, and maximal parameter gains than its evidence establishes".
- [Portfolio closure review](../papers/investigations-2/brainstorm/reviews/PORTFOLIO_CLOSURE_REVIEW_CODEX_20261013.md): "only all-prime relations can be load-bearing" judged "too strong"; 14 and 16 downgraded from "close" to dormant.
- [Response 17](../papers/investigations-2/active/22_theta_manifold_geometry/reviews/17_RESPONSE_TO_JOINT_REVIEW_CLAUDE_20261015.md): "equivalence does not establish futility"; the controls "exclude arguments using the premises each control actually shares" and no more; [Reply 18](../papers/investigations-2/active/22_theta_manifold_geometry/reviews/18_REPLY_TO_RESPONSE_17_CLAUDE_20261015.md) §7 withdraws three theorem-level phrasings of Review 16.
- [Program meta-analysis](../papers/investigations/program-meta-analysis/reviews/PROGRAM_META_ANALYSIS_20260926.md) §5, "Corrections to overly broad conclusions".

## What it cost

Withdrawn remarks in two manuscript versions; a closure that had to be
re-qualified; a benchmark claim refuted by a 0.12% change in a barrier
time step; duplicate work in branches that read a scoped no-go as a
universal one and in branches that ignored a scoped no-go that did apply.

## Instead

State every negative result with the class it was proved for and the
hypothesis that distinguishes the next class ([`avoid/02`](02_shape_only_arguments_and_the_controls.md) gives the positive form). The repository's own phrasing is the template: "Closure of the program is a research judgment, not a theorem" ([YM README](../papers/weil-positivity/susy-positivity/investigations/wilson-loewner/YM/README.md)); "Inactive status does not assert mathematical impossibility" ([investigations-2 README](../papers/investigations-2/README.md)). A no-go that is meant as a decision rule should be filed as one (a kill criterion with its number), not as a theorem.
