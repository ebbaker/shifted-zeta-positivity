# Constructive stable-model obstructions

17 October 2026, America/New_York. Prepared for Edward Baker with substantial
LLM assistance. Recorded model: **gpt-6.1-sol (Codex), effort ultra**.

## Summary

Project 05's round one excludes independent Bernoulli cosine products by
an odd-dilation error floor; single quartic laws and a specified fixed
Bessel multiplier class by source tests; and independent scaled
pure-quartic arrays with a Gaussian component even as weak source limits.
A normalized GKS comparison requires any approximating interacting
quartic network to lose every persistent diffuse site at readout scale.

Round two reconciles the review: Newman–Wu weak closure upgrades the
persistent-site theorem to weak convergence. Fixed heat rollback plus
Lee–Yang and Rodgers–Tao excludes independent-spin preparation at every
nonpositive base time, including base times tending to minus infinity,
for approximation at each fixed nonnegative theta time. Newman's classical
Gaussian-convolution classification also excludes theta and the stable
Bessel source as limits of diverging heat tilts of symmetric LP seeds.

## Status

Round-one source claims: analytic proofs and bounded certificates. Its

## Caveats

The principal obstructions also exclude a known stable Bessel source:
they limit family expressiveness, not theta stability. Weak closure and
the all-Gaussian-downweight classification are classical, and the
fixed-base Bessel continuation obstruction is de Bruijn's. General
interacting infinitesimal arrays and fixed non-Gaussian Cardon multipliers
remain unresolved. No exclusion of all stable constructions is asserted.

## Reusable for

Checking construction scope before moment fitting; upgrading a proved
Laplace obstruction to weak convergence within the Lee–Yang class;
testing a positive removable heat margin; and using a stable Bessel
control to distinguish family limitations from theta-specific information.
