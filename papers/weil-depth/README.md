Everything at the top level and under `manuscript/` and `numerics/` belongs to the
current draft and is small (under 1 MB per file). The saved ball matrices (about 13 MB per
horizon) are deterministic output of `numerics/certify_arb.py`, regenerate in about a minute
each, and are therefore not versioned: `.gitignore` excludes `numerics-archives/` and any
`*.json.gz` under `numerics/output/`. Each certificate records both the hash of the archive it
was computed from and `matrices_content_sha256`, a hash of the matrices themselves; rebuilds
in the tested environments (Python 3.10–3.12 with python-flint 0.9.0 and FLINT 3.6.0)
reproduced it exactly. The content hash identifies the saved data — it is not a claim of
bit-for-bit portability across other arithmetic libraries — and the repository is verifiable
without the archives, since the sign tests are decided by ball arithmetic, not by a hash.

Nothing under `archive/` is current; it is kept so that each version and
the review that led to the next one can be read side by side (the git history has the
same information commit by commit).

## Result in one paragraph

For the central Weil form $Q_{0,L}$ on test functions of total support length $L$, the
paper certifies two-sided bounds on the bottom of the spectrum at seven horizons, using
no information about zeta zeros:

| Total horizon $L$ | generator delays | certified floor $m$ | certified upper bound | shift interval | decay |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `log 2` | none | `1.32e-3` | `1.33e-3` | `0 < ω ≤ 4e-2` | `exp(-6e-4 ω)` |
| `log 3` | 2 | `5.52e-8` | `5.54e-8` | `≤ 2e-4` | `exp(-2e-8 ω)` |
| `log 4` | 2, 3 | `7.48e-13` | `7.57e-13` | `≤ 5e-7` | `exp(-3e-13 ω)` |
| `log 5` | 2, 3, 4 | `8.95e-18` | `9.30e-18` | `≤ 1e-9` | `exp(-4e-18 ω)` |
| `log 6` | 2, 3, 4, 5 | `5.93e-23` | `7.33e-23` | `≤ 3e-12` | `exp(-2e-23 ω)` |
| `9/5` | 2, 3, 4, 5 | `3.38e-23` | `4.14e-23` | `≤ 2e-12` | `exp(-1e-23 ω)` |
| `log 7` | 2, 3, 4, 5 | `1.37e-28` | `6.81e-28` | `≤ 4e-15` | `exp(-6e-29 ω)` |

Each floor holds for every shorter horizon by compression, the bound
`||V_(ω,L)|| ≤ exp(-decay·ω)` holds on the stated shift interval, and at every horizon
the bottom of the spectrum lies in the even reflection sector. The enclosures fall
rapidly along the profile, at an increasing logarithmic rate; these are observations on
seven horizons, and no decay law at unbounded depth is proved. Lower bounds are exact
rationals passing a ball-arithmetic Schur test that includes the analytic infinite tail
and the profile remainder; upper bounds are ball Rayleigh quotients. A second
implementation reproduces all seven certificates. No all-depth positivity, RH result,
zero-free region, external human audit, or formal verification is claimed. Section 7.2 of
the manuscript gives resource projections for larger horizons; they are cost estimates for
the present implementation, not impossibility bounds.
