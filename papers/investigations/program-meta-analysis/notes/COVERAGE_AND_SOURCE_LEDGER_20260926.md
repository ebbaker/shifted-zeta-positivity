## External primary sources checked

These checks establish what the cited papers claim and how their hypotheses relate to the program. They are not independent proofs of those papers. No third-party PDF was added to the repository.

- [Suzuki, A canonical system of differential equations arising from the Riemann zeta-function, 1204.1827v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.1827v2): the explicit unconditional construction has the stated restriction omega > 1. It must not be conflated with different support/innerness ranges in the local manuscripts.
- [Suzuki, Aspects of the screw function, 2206.03682](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.03682): primary background record for the screw-function framework.
- [Suzuki, Weil's quadratic form via the screw function, 2606.09096v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.09096v2): localized operator domain, continuity and small-support simple-even statements. The latter does not establish the general large-support simple-even condition. The introduction also records the odd-test criterion.
- [Connes–Consani, Weil positivity and Trace formula, the archimedean place, 2006.13771](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.13771): Sonin-compression framework and the correction to the Weil distribution, studied at the archimedean place.
- [Connes–Consani, Quasi-inner functions and local factors, 2008.10974](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.10974): primary record for the local-factor/semilocal context.
- [Connes–Consani–Moscovici, Zeta zeros and prolate wave operators, 2310.18423v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.18423v2): semilocal prolate framework and stability of Sonin spaces. This does not itself prove the required full Weil positivity residual.
- [Connes–Consani–Moscovici, Zeta Spectral Triples, 2511.22755v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.22755v1): Sections 5.6, 7 and 8 distinguish the finite determinant relation, candidate-transform result, and missing actual ground-state comparison. The abstract record inspected listed v1 as the current version.
- [Zhu, Weil positivity in compact windows, 2608.24827v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24827v2): the current abstract distinguishes finite certificates, an empirical fitted law and a conditional large-window upper bound. The v1 announcement was also inspected. This review does not rely on its numerical certificate or fitted constant as an independently verified result; half-width versus total-width conventions differ from the repository's main notation.

## New deductions versus inherited results

The synthesis's positive-approximation implication, elementary unstable all-pass example, and explanation of sufficient weighted-profile convergence are direct mathematical observations made during review. They are not new constructions for zeta. The CCM quantitative rate, moment identity and method obstructions are inherited from the current local audit. The detector tail issue is a preliminary analytic concern requiring a dedicated follow-up audit before the manuscript's status is changed.
