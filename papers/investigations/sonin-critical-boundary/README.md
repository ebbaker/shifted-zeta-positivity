A standalone manuscript consolidating the analytic critical-boundary results from the Wilson–Loewner arithmetic-storage investigation.

The manuscript proves uniform smoothed-trace bounds, the critical-zero limit of a positive majorant and fixed Abel approximants, localization of the exact-projection defect, and an explicit rational example with complete projection loss. It retains the pole and off-line zero terms in the arithmetic comparison. Exact projection mass survival for the zeta family and identification with the complete arithmetic form remain open.
