The manuscript proves logarithmic unit-window bounds uniform over all sigma greater than one half, the critical-zero limit of a positive majorant and fixed Abel approximants, localization of the exact-projection defect, and an explicit rational example with complete projection loss. The revision adds a Toeplitz–Hankel interpretation and a general endpoint spectral-calculus corollary. It retains the pole and off-line zero terms in the arithmetic comparison. Exact projection mass survival for the zeta family and identification with the complete arithmetic form remain open.
