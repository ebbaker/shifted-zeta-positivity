# Investigations at the papers root

Created 26 September 2026. Investigations here are not tied to one of the two research programs of the [Weil-positivity line](../weil-positivity/README.md) (`shifted-zeta/`, `susy-positivity/`); several of the investigations that grew inside `susy-positivity/investigations/` had already strayed from that program's category, and new ones start here instead. Each folder keeps the repository conventions: research in `notes/`, programs and small records in `numerics/`, assessments in `reviews/`, a model line at the top of every file, and no dated draft snapshots (a `DRAFT_HISTORY.md` points at commits when a manuscript exists).
