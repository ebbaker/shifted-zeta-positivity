# The elementary Schur algebra has a one-dimensional trace space, and what that does and does not bound

**Author: Claude Opus 5 (Anthropic).** 16 September 2026. Checked by
[`check_qweyl_relations.py`](../numerics/check_qweyl_relations.py).

This note investigates whether the classification of positive twisted traces on
protected algebras can be used non-constructively, as
[the brainstorm note of 16 September](../../../../brainstorm/inverse-bulk-brainstorm/NONCONSTRUCTIVE_EXISTENCE_AND_THETA_SYMBOL_20260916.md)
proposed. The answer is yes in principle and no for the construction at hand,
for a reason worth recording; a conjecture in that note is **retracted** in §4.

## 1. The circularity, so it is not repeated

> **Corrected 16 September 2026.** This section as first written was too
> strong. "An existence proof would be a proof of RH" is the programme's
> goal, not an objection; and what is actually circular is only an argument
> whose *only* input is `||Pi|| <= 1`. Fixed point theorems take structure
> from the model as input and are **not** excluded by anything here. See
> [the fixed-point note](FIXED_POINTS_AND_THE_MARGIN_20260916.md) for what
> each family of them would need, which are blocked and by what, and for a
> measurement of the margin any method must forgo.

A realization is a linear `Phi` into a **positive** Hilbert space with
`||Phi(f)||^2 = Q_L[f]`. Its existence implies `Q_L >= 0` implies RH. So any
proof that a realization exists, constructive or not, is a proof of RH.
Corollary 7.9 already manufactures the dilation `1 - Pi = V*(1-P)V` the moment
`||Pi|| <= 1`, and `||Pi|| <= 1` *is* the Weil criterion. Abstract operator
theory applied to the target alone returns its input.

What remains available is: **rigidity** (what a realization must look like if it
exists — Corollary 2.2, Proposition 2.4, Proposition 3.4 are the three in hand);
**family exclusion** (an invariant of an achievable cone that the target
violates); and **finite-moment determination**:

> If a model's pairing is known *a priori* to lie in a cone `C` of positive forms
> that has been classified and embeds in `R^n`, then two elements of `C` agreeing
> on `n` independent linear functionals coincide. Verifying the Weil identity on
> `n` inputs verifies it on all of them.

That is the honest content of "existence without explicit computation": not
dodging the construction, but collapsing an infinite identity to `n` numbers.

## 2. What the classifications say

Klyuev, *Twisted traces and positive forms on generalized q-Weyl algebras*,
SIGMA **18** (2022), 009, studies `A(P)` generated by `U, V, Z, Z^{-1}` with

```
 Z U Z^{-1} = q^2 U,   Z V Z^{-1} = q^{-2} V,   U V = P(q^{-1} Z),   V U = P(q Z),
```

`P` a Laurent polynomial, and `n` the number of nonzero roots of `P` counted with
multiplicity (`n = k - l` for `P = a x^k + ... + b x^l`).

- **Proposition 2.1.** The space of `g_t`-twisted traces has dimension `n`.
- **Proposition 5.5.** For `t` not of the form `q^{-2k}`, the map
  `T -> (T(1), ..., T(Z^{n-1}))` is an isomorphism onto `C^n`.
- **Theorem 4.7.** The cone `C_+` of positive definite `rho_t`-equivariant traces
  is isomorphic to `C_l x R^r_{>0}`, with `l` the number of roots `alpha` of `P`
  with `q < |alpha| < q^{-1}` and `r` the number of distinct roots with
  `|alpha| = q`. Roots outside the closed annulus are shifted into it by powers
  `q^{2k}` and carry a remainder functional which must vanish (Corollary 4.6).

Etingof, Klyuev, Rains and Stryker, SIGMA **17** (2021), 029, do the analogous
thing for quantized Kleinian singularities of type `A_{n-1}` and obtain outright
**uniqueness** of the unitary short star-product for `n <= 4`.

I have read these statements, not their proofs.

## 3. The elementary representation, identified

Section 6.1 of the manuscript gives, on `H = l^2(Z) tensor L^2(S^1)`,

```
 (v psi)_m(zeta)   = q^{-m} zeta psi_m(zeta),
 (u_- psi)_m(zeta) = (1 + q^{-m-1} zeta) psi_{m+1}(q^{-1} zeta),
 (u_+ psi)_m(zeta) = psi_{m-1}(q zeta).
```

Composing the two magnetic actions,

```
 (u_+ u_- psi)_m(zeta) = (1 + q^{-m+1} zeta) psi_m(zeta) = ((1 + q v) psi)_m(zeta),
 (u_- u_+ psi)_m(zeta) = (1 + q^{-m-1} zeta) psi_m(zeta) = ((1 + q^{-1} v) psi)_m(zeta),
```

and `v u_- v^{-1} = q^2 u_-`, `v u_+ v^{-1} = q^{-2} u_+`. So with

```
 U = u_-,    V = u_+,    Z = v,
```

Klyuev's relations hold with **`P(y) = 1 + y`**: `U V = 1 + q^{-1} Z = P(q^{-1} Z)`
and `V U = 1 + q Z = P(q Z)`. All four identities are verified in exact rational
arithmetic on random finite Laurent states by `check_qweyl_relations.py`.

**Consequences.** `P(y) = 1 + y` has exactly one nonzero root, at `y = -1`, so
`n = 1`. By Proposition 2.1 the space of `g_t`-twisted traces is **one
dimensional**, and by Proposition 5.5 a trace is fixed by the single value
`T(1)`. Since `|-1| = 1` and `q < 1 < q^{-1}` for `0 < q < 1`, the root lies in
the annulus, so `l = 1` and `r = 0` in Theorem 4.7.

## 4. What this bounds, and a retraction

**The algebra carries no arithmetic parameter at all.** Its invariant positive
pairing is a single ray. Every prime parameter in Section 6 — `r_p = p^{-1/2}`
entering through the dressing `D_{q,a}` with `a = q r_p` — lives in the choice of
**state**, not in the algebra. This is an independent, algebraic confirmation of
Section 6.5: the reachable output states are all functions analytic past the unit
circle, so weight-matching in this sector carries little information. The trace
classification says the same thing from the other side, and more sharply: there
is no invariant data in the algebra for the arithmetic to be stored in.

**Retraction.** The brainstorm note of the same date conjectured a "budget
claim": that the roots of `P` are the prime parameters, so that `deg P` bounds
how many primes a fixed algebra can carry. That is **wrong**. The prime
parameters are not roots of `P`; the algebra of Section 6.1 has a single root, at
`-1`, independent of every prime. The correct statement is the one above: the
classification bounds the algebra's invariant pairing, which in this case is
one-dimensional and prime-free, and the construction evades that bound by putting
the arithmetic somewhere the classification does not see.

**Where the unjustified step is.** The Bloch lift (6.18) applies the source "to
each component of `B_d F` in an orthogonal copy of `H`" — infinitely many copies,
one per Bloch fibre. The trace classification bounds what **one copy** can do.
The lift is exactly the step that evades the bound, and it is exactly the step
the manuscript flags as lacking a field-theoretic interpretation ("No particular
four-dimensional interface has yet been shown to implement (6.18)"). So the
classification does not exclude the construction; it **localizes** the step that
needs justification, which is worth as much and is cheaper to state.

## 5. What would make finite-moment determination usable

The principle of §1 applies to a candidate whose pairing genuinely **is** the
algebra's twisted trace, in the sense of Section 3.3 (`H(a,b) = T(rho(a) b)`).
For such a candidate the Weil identity on `I_L` would follow from `n` numbers.
Two requirements follow, and they are concrete:

1. **Room.** The algebra needs `n = deg P` at least as large as the number of
   independent arithmetic data the pairing must carry. `n = 1` is not enough for
   anything.
2. **Position.** By Theorem 4.7 only roots in the annulus `q < |alpha| < q^{-1}`
   (together with those of modulus exactly `q`) contribute to the positive cone.
   *If* arithmetic data were to enter as roots at modulus `p^{-1/2}` — which is a
   modelling assumption, not a consequence of anything proved here — then
   `q < p^{-1/2}` would be needed for every active prime, that is `q < e^{-L/4}`,
   so one fixed `q` would serve only primes below `q^{-2}` and covering every `L`
   would force `q -> 0`. That would be a rigidity constraint on `q` of exactly the
   useful kind. It is conditional on the modelling assumption and is **not**
   established here.
