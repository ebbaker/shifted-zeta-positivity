\section{Collective feedback and its compact error}
\label{sec:feedback}
The delay source $A_0$ in \eqref{poles:delay-source} is closed on
$\mathcal D_{\log,L}$ and has $T_0=A_0^*A_0\ge mI$.
It permits a stable response to a prescribed input. This is the
first step toward replacing compulsory contact by a controlled
error.

\subsection{One response field}
For $\mu\ge0$, minimize
\[
\norm{A_0u}^2+\mu\norm u^2-2\re\ip f u
\quad\text{over }u\in\mathcal D_{\log,L}.
\]
This is stable for every fixed $f$ and has minimizer
$u=R_\mu f$, $R_\mu=(T_0+\mu)^{-1}$. Stability here does not
assert joint positivity in $(f,u)$.
Read out
$A_{\mathrm{fb}}=A_0(I-\kappa R_\mu)$, $\kappa>0$.
The correction $F_{\kappa,\mu}=-\kappa A_0R_\mu$ is bounded and
compact, so the source remains closed with the same domain and
maximal adjoint domain. Expanding all terms, including the
feedback self-pairing, yields
\begin{equation}\label{feedback:identity}
A_{\mathrm{fb}}^*A_{\mathrm{fb}}
 =T_0-2\kappa I+C_{\kappa,\mu},\qquad
C_{\kappa,\mu}=2\kappa\mu R_\mu+\kappa^2T_0R_\mu^2.
\end{equation}
This error is positive, compact and infinite rank.

Let $J=\bigoplus_{p\in\mathcal S_L}\mathcal D_pE_L$ and
$c_{\mathcal S}=\sum_{p\in\mathcal S_L}c_p$. Choose the gain by
\begin{equation}\label{feedback:gain}
2\kappa=e^D+c_{\mathcal S}-w_0.
\end{equation}
Then the assembled source
$\Gamma_{\kappa,\mu}=(A_{\mathrm{fb}},J)$ satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{feedback:complete}
\norm{\Gamma_{\kappa,\mu}f}^2
 =Q_L[f]+\ip f{C_{\kappa,\mu}f}.
\end{equation}
Its block charge is a closed-source supersymmetric construction,
and its form operator is $W_L+C_{\kappa,\mu}$ on $\Dom T_L$.
The gain and prime scales match arithmetic coefficients; the
positive response has not independently derived those parameters.
The quartic cap is an isometric tensor factor in this identity.

\subsection{The error cannot be removed by its regulator}
On a $T_0$ eigenvalue $\lambda$, the error equals
\[
c_{\kappa,\mu}(\lambda)
 =\frac{2\kappa\mu}{\lambda+\mu}
       +\frac{\kappa^2\lambda}{(\lambda+\mu)^2},\qquad
\partial_\mu c_{\kappa,\mu}(\lambda)
 =\frac{2\kappa\lambda(\lambda+\mu-\kappa)}
             {(\lambda+\mu)^3}.
\]
In particular,
\[
\inf_{\mu\ge0}c_{\kappa,\mu}(\lambda)=
\begin{cases}
\kappa^2/\lambda,&\lambda\ge\kappa,\\
2\kappa-\lambda,&0<\lambda<\kappa.
\end{cases}
\]
Every fixed mode retains positive error.
As $\mu\downarrow0$, the operator converges in norm to
$\kappa^2T_0^{-1}$; as $\mu\to\infty$, it converges strongly to
$2\kappa I$, but not in norm because it is compact for every
finite $\mu$. For fixed parameters,
$c_{\kappa,\mu}(\lambda)\sim(\kappa^2+2\kappa\mu)/\lambda$.
Compactness alone gives no smallness on the possibly negative
arithmetic modes.

\subsection{Schatten-class obstruction}
Choose a subinterval $J_0$ of length
$\ell<\min(L,\log2)$ and the orthonormal inputs
$f_n=\ell^{-1/2}1_{J_0}e^{2\pi inx/\ell}$.
They belong to $\mathcal D_{\log,L}$ by their sinc Fourier tails.
All prime overlaps vanish, the pole contribution is $O(n^{-2})$,
and $\norm{A_Lf_n}^2=O(\log n)$. Therefore
$Q_L[f_n]-\norm{A_Lf_n}^2=w_0+O(n^{-2})$.
If a correction $B$ made $\norm{(A_L+B)f}^2=Q_L[f]$ on the full
domain, then Cauchy--Schwarz in its cross term would force
\[
\norm{Bf_n}^2\ge c/\log n
\quad\text{for all sufficiently large }n.
\]
This is incompatible with $B$ in any finite Schatten class:
for $p\ge2$, convexity and the trace bound give
$\sum_n\norm{Bf_n}^p\le\sum_ns_n(B)^p$; for $p<2$ use inclusion
in the Hilbert--Schmidt class. The same reasoning applies to the
assembled reference $(A_0,J)$, whose target discrepancy is exactly
the nonzero constant $-2\kappa I$.
It excludes finite Schatten-class source corrections, not every
compact correction.

The spectral comparison sharpens this distinction. Since $P_L$
has rank two, finite-rank interlacing and
\eqref{source:eigenvalues} give
\begin{equation}\label{feedback:eigenvalues}
\lambda_n(T_0)=\log\frac{\pi n}{2L}-\psi(1/4)+e^D+O_L(n^{-1}).
\end{equation}
Consequently the decreasing singular values satisfy
\begin{equation}\label{feedback:singular}
\lim_{n\to\infty}\sqrt{\log n}\,s_n(F_{\kappa,\mu})=\kappa,
\qquad
\lim_{n\to\infty}\log n\,s_n(C_{\kappa,\mu})
                                  =\kappa^2+2\kappa\mu.
\end{equation}
Neither lies in any finite Schatten class. These exact leading
scales replace the earlier one-sided logarithmic comparison.
