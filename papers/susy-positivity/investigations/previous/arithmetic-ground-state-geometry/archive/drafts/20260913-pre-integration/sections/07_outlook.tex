\section{The narrowed physical-pairing problem}
\label{sec:outlook}
The results separate three requirements that a successful theory must satisfy together. It must have an ordinary positive Hilbert structure. It must retain the required arithmetic dependence in a specified observable. It must identify that observable, with its contact and poles, as the full norm of a linear preparation map. The cylinder and prime theories satisfy the first requirement and parts of the second. None satisfies the third.

The interacting family in Section~\ref{sec:quartic} is a useful exact control for the next calculation. Its mass parameter has no nonzero chiral class because it only dilates the fields. Changing the dimensionless interaction shape is a distinct deformation. For
\[
G_c(A,B)=\tfrac12(A^2+B^2)+\tfrac c4(A^4+B^4+A^2B^2),
\qquad \mathcal W_c=G_c/2,
\]
the superpotential variation is multiplication by
\[
[\partial_c\mathcal W_c]
=\left[\tfrac18(A^4+B^4+A^2B^2)\right]
\]
in the Jacobi ring. The Higgs operator for the differential \eqref{qua:dolbeault-convention} is half this variation. Its critical values are not all equal. Deriving its matrix geometry, the physical source frame and the boundary conditions is therefore a concrete next task, rather than a change of variables already covered by the dilation argument.

The reference period and physical norm define the comparison
\begin{equation}\label{out:ratio}
\mathcal R(c,\bar c)=\frac{H_c}{|C_c|^2}
\end{equation}
where the period is nonzero. A common source rescaling cancels from this ratio. It can distinguish a physical normalization relation from a removable frame factor, but its physical interpretation requires care: the real-cycle holomorphic integral is not already a normalizable boundary state. A gluing law would need to construct the relevant boundary state and establish how its positive Gram pairing relates to \eqref{out:ratio}. Neither a topological intersection form nor a signed index can be substituted for that Hermitian pairing.

If a shape $c=c(M)$ is introduced along the prime mass, then the period and metric become $C(c(M))/M$ and $H(c(M),\overline{c(M)})/|M|^2$. The compensating normalization needed to retain an Euler factor must be fixed by the physical boundary problem, rather than chosen after inspecting the desired arithmetic answer. A protected relation controlling the period and norm together would be substantive progress even before a complete Weil realization is available.

The periodic gamma sector presents a second concrete task. Its physical vacuum exists, but its arithmetic source-frame norm has not been calculated. The multivalued insertion $-Y/2$ requires deck, flavor or equivalent global parameter data. Identifying the relevant physical gamma pairing could determine whether this sector supplies more than the existing holomorphic period.

Finally, the source space cannot be omitted. A nine-dimensional vacuum fiber cannot represent all smooth compactly supported inputs. Boundary fields, periodic sectors, excitations or another specified infinite-dimensional construction must supply the full preparation map and the prime returns. The scalar sign test directs attention to internal interference or nonlocal observables, while the compulsory diagonal identity gives an immediate check of any proposed pole mechanism. The next theory should derive its complete bilinear response before large numerical overlap calculations are undertaken.

\subsection{Reproducibility and limitations}
The accompanying small programs verify nine common-core supercharge identities and two rational-algebra identities in the quartic period elimination. They use exact integer or rational arithmetic and Python's standard library. Their records do not verify self-adjoint domains, the analytic cycle hypotheses, the Hodge theorem or the full Weil form; those arguments are stated separately in the text. No floating-point positivity sweep or large numerical dataset enters the results.

The source package includes a manuscript build recipe, a deliverable manifest and a replay validator. Large future regenerable outputs are assigned to the external \texttt{szp-archive} layout, with source, parameters and small hash records retained in the repository. The conclusions of this draft are the explicit constructions and scoped obstructions above. The full positive arithmetic pairing remains the research objective.
