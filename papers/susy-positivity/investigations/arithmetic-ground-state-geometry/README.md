# Arithmetic ground-state geometry

This investigation seeks an independently positive supersymmetric theory whose ordinary boundary or operator pairing equals the complete finite-interval Weil form. It develops the arithmetic ground-state and extended-supersymmetry direction selected by the [theory landscape study](../../brainstorm/theory-landscape-20260913/REPORT.md).

Start with [note 20: arithmetic sign and boundary response](notes/20_ARITHMETIC_SIGN_AND_BOUNDARY_RESPONSE.md), the [continuation brief](CONTINUATION.md), and the updated [research report](REPORT.md). The strongest current result is a closed positive source equal to the full Weil form plus an explicit **finite-rank** positive error. It is exact on a proved high-mode subspace; an explicit finite matrix remains to be shown positive. The [integrated manuscript](manuscript.pdf) now includes every result through note 20, with the conditional statements and remaining sign problem explicit. See the [coverage map](MANUSCRIPT_COVERAGE.md) and [dated draft snapshots](archive/drafts/README.md).

The integrated draft consolidates the successive research passes. Notes 06–09 reduce the interacting sector to three states, derive the radial metric problem and identify the scalar-normalization and source-domain obstructions. Notes 10–13 now construct physically reflected boundary caps with absolute residue normalization, evaluate a homogeneous interacting source norm, derive the escaping-vacuum endpoint, and prove uniqueness of the radial metric under the inherited exact equation. They also construct the closed unbounded gamma source and retain the complete arithmetic remainder on its domain. Notes 14–17 continue with exact Robin and coherent pole gluing, a conservative graph for prime returns, and a stable collective feedback source whose norm is the complete Weil form plus an explicit positive compact defect. They derive contact and source-regularity restrictions and keep that defect visible. Notes 18–19 review those arguments, derive a two-sided Neumann/Dirichlet comparison, and use a joint response to reduce the error to finite rank while retaining every low/high coupling. Compatibility is proved inside a fixed support envelope; an all-support source law remains open.

Note 20 proves an exact bounded but noncompact gamma boundary correction, with essential norm pi/2 and controlled finite-input tails. It derives a full mixed-response residual bound and proves that replacing primes by their smooth density gives a negative form at length two. Four approaches and their precise missing arithmetic lemmas are evaluated. No all-support sign is claimed; a theorem for each length is distinguished from an all-envelope physical source construction.
