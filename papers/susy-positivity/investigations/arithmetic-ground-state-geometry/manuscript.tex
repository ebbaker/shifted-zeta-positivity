\begin{center}
{\LARGE\bfseries Arithmetic ground-state geometry\par}
\vspace{6pt}
{\Large Physical caps, arithmetic sources and analytical sign tests\par}
\vspace{10pt}
{Working draft, September 2026\par}
\end{center}
\begin{abstract}
We investigate positive supersymmetric theories and auxiliary responses as sources of the finite-interval Weil form. Interacting prime models retain Euler dependence, and a shape deformation produces a three-state sector inside a nine-vacuum theory. Physical caps fix the residue normalization; both metric endpoints give uniqueness conditional on the stated physical chiral equation. A separate gamma preparation is closed on the full logarithmic domain. Pole and prime-return constructions retain their compulsory contacts. A joint response on an independently positive high sector gives the complete target plus an explicit positive finite-rank error, leaving an unsigned finite Schur matrix. We derive its gamma boundary coupling: a bounded noncompact correction of essential norm $\pi/2$, with convergent finite-input tails and a rigorous mixed-response residual bound. An exact prime-discrepancy identity shows that replacing primes by their smooth density gives a negative form already at support length two. These results constrain analytical sign mechanisms; they do not prove the remaining ordering. Positivity at every support length would suffice without a compatible all-envelope physical source, whose construction is a separate open problem. Full Weil positivity and the Riemann hypothesis remain open.
\end{abstract}
