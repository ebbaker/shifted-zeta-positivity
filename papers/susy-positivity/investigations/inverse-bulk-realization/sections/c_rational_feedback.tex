\section{A finite rational-feedback obstruction}\label{app:rational}
A common attempt to fit a negative contact is to filter a positive
reference source by finitely many rational functions of its Gram
operator. The following elementary argument excludes a specific class
without estimating its residual.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:rational}
Let $A_0$ be a closed source into a positive Hilbert space and let
$T=A_0^*A_0>0$ have infinitely many distinct eigenvalues. On finite
linear combinations of eigenvectors, form the direct-sum source
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rational-source}
 A_{\rm rat}f=(A_0r_1(T)f,\ldots,A_0r_m(T)f,
                 s_1(T)f,\ldots,s_k(T)f),
\end{equation}
where all $r_j,s_\ell$ are rational functions with complex coefficients,
finite at every eigenvalue. For any $\alpha>0$, this source cannot
satisfy
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rational-target}
 \norm{A_{\rm rat}f}^2=\ip f{(T-\alpha\id)f}
\end{equation}
on all eigenvectors, even if $T-\alpha\id$ is positive on its spectrum.
Positive constant metrics within either group of channels can be
absorbed into the rational functions and obey the same conclusion.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For an eigenvalue $\lambda$, the proposed identity gives
$\lambda\sum_j|r_j(\lambda)|^2+sum_\ell|s_\ell(\lambda)|^2
=\lambda-\alpha$. Write
$r^\#(z)=\overline{r(\bar z)}$ and define the rational function
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rational-identity}
 R(z)=z\sum_jr_j(z)r_j^\#(z)
              +\sum_\ell s_\ell(z)s_\ell^\#(z).
\end{equation}
Clearing denominators, infinitely many distinct roots force
$R(z)=z-\alpha$ as a rational identity. Choose a real $x$ with
$0<x<\alpha$ outside the finite pole set. Its sum-of-squares
representation gives $R(x)\geq0$, while $x-\alpha<0$, a contradiction.
\end{proof}
No finite accumulation point of eigenvalues is needed. A nonzero
polynomial has only finitely many roots. Finite cascades of scalar
rational filters of the same reference remain in this class.
For example,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rational-example}
 \lambda\left(1-\frac\kappa{\lambda+\mu}\right)^2
 =\lambda-2\kappa+\frac{2\kappa\mu}{\lambda+\mu}
             +\frac{\kappa^2\lambda}{(\lambda+\mu)^2},
\end{equation}
whose last terms are nonnegative for $\lambda,\kappa>0$, $\mu\geq0$.
Adding finitely many channels of \eqref{eq:rational-source} cannot
remove the exact constant in this reference-only class.

The proposition does not exclude general finite-field models,
nonrational responses from continuum dynamics, couplings to operators
other than functions of $T$, infinite channel families, cross couplings
outside \eqref{eq:rational-source}, or agreement on only finitely many
inputs. It is a scoped selection result with no assertion of
literature priority.
