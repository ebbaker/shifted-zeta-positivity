# Reviews of the working manuscript

Dated external reviews of the manuscript versions preserved in
[`drafts/`](../drafts/README.md). A review records an assessment as it was made;
it is not revised afterwards. Revisions it prompts belong in the manuscript and
in the [research notes](../notes/README.md).

| Review | Subject | Reviewer | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| [review_claude_opus_5_20260915.md](review_claude_opus_5_20260915.md) | Working manuscript 0.1 | Claude Opus 5 (Anthropic) | No mathematical errors found. Missing explicit formula and literature placement; the scale criticism produced Theorem 7.5 in version 0.2, and the narrowed direction is recorded in the notes. |

Reviews carried out by a language model are labelled with the model. They
verify stated derivations and run the accompanying programmes; they do not
constitute refereeing, and their numerical claims carry the same scope
limitations as the repository's own checks.
