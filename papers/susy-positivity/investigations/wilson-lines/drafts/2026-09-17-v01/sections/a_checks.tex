\section{Checks}\label{app:checks}

One program,
\begin{center}
\texttt{numerics/check\_causal\_commutation.py},\qquad
\texttt{numerics/records/causal-commutation-checks.json},
\end{center}
the second being its preserved record. It uses the standard library only and prints JSON to standard output. Exact rational
arithmetic is labelled separately from floating-point work. Counts refer to
finite test cases, not to independent theorems, and none of the checks is a
positivity certificate.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.32\textwidth}p{0.60\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
Group & What is verified\\
\midrule
Multiplicativity of truncation &
Lemma~\ref{lem:multiplicative} as a finite matrix identity,
$P_LAP_LBP_L=P_LABP_L$, in exact rational arithmetic, for randomly generated
causal kernels at several truncation sizes.\\
Commutation, and its cause &
$[A,B]=0$ for truncated causal convolutions, in exact rational arithmetic
(Theorem~\ref{thm:abelian}, Remark~\ref{rem:toeplitz}); and the control that
truncated \emph{non}-causal convolutions have commutators bounded away from
zero, so that causality is what the lemma uses.\\
The causal pole kernel &
That $4\cosh(\omega x)\cosh(x/2)\mathbf1_{x>0}$, symmetrized by
\eqref{eq:symmetrize}, equals
$2\cosh(\omega(x-y))\cosh(\frac{x-y}2)$, and at $\omega=0$ equals $P_L$ of
\eqref{eq:poles} in the form $2|C(f)|^2-2|S(f)|^2$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:kernel}, Remark~\ref{rem:imaginary}).\\
Vanishing on the imaginary axis &
That $\re[2(p-\frac12)^{-1}+2(p+\frac12)^{-1}]=0$ at $p=i\tau$, exactly, over a
grid of $\tau$ (Remark~\ref{rem:imaginary}).\\
The hyperbolic identity &
Proposition~\ref{prop:hyperbolic} as a finite matrix identity: the
$\cosh(\omega(x-y))$-weighted form equals
$Q_{0,L}[\cosh(\omega X)f]-Q_{0,L}[\sinh(\omega X)f]$ on a discretized kernel,
at several $\omega$ and $L$.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Run from the investigation directory:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 numerics/check_causal_commutation.py
python3 validation/drafts.py check --replay
\end{verbatim}

The checks verify finite algebra underlying the identities of
Sections~\ref{sec:generator}--\ref{sec:hyperbolic}. They do not verify the
convergence arguments in the proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:kernel}, which are
analytic, nor Proposition~\ref{prop:trace}, which is a statement about domains.
