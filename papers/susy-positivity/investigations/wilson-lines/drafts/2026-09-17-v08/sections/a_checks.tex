library only and print JSON to standard output. Exact rational arithmetic is labelled separately
from floating-point work. Counts refer to finite test cases, not to independent
theorems, and none of the checks is a positivity certificate.

Section~\ref{sec:delay}: the causal symbol \eqref{eq:defectsymbol} against
quadrature and against its boundary-channel decomposition into primary and
descendants with positive coefficients; Proposition~\ref{prop:defectdelay} in
closed form, its total, and its identical vanishing at half-integer $\Delta$; the
Stieltjes bound of Proposition~\ref{prop:nowinding} for arbitrary positive
spectral measures; that $e^{2i\theta}$ is unimodular while
$\int_0^T2\theta'=2\theta(T)$ is unbounded; the two objects that do wind, the
half-line inverted oscillator at exactly $\log\pi$ from the target and the
Liouville amplitude at $4Q\log P$; the graded invariants of
Proposition~\ref{prop:dictfree} --- the target values in exact rational
arithmetic, the degeneracy $B_{2m}(\frac12)=2^{2m}B_{2m}(\frac14)$ to $m=8$
exactly, the single-factor formula and its independence of the width, the
classification of Theorem~\ref{thm:onefactor} together with a grid search finding
no solution beyond the three, every row of the survey table of
\S\ref{ssec:survey}, the closed-form counterexample of
Remark~\ref{rem:retraction} with $I_4=\frac5{384}=\frac{25}{28}$ of the target,
the two-factor family and the bisection sweep locating exactly the two $I_6$

\paragraph{Scope.} The resonance group uses the first twelve zero ordinates,
truncated to exact rationals, as \emph{published input data}; the Blaschke
identities hold for any rational $\gamma$, so the group verifies the mechanism of
Section~\ref{sec:allpass} and not the arithmetic. Nothing here computes with
$\xi$ or $\zeta$. The checks do not verify the convergence arguments in the proof
of Theorem~\ref{thm:kernel}, which are analytic, nor
Proposition~\ref{prop:trace}, which is a statement about domains, nor
Propositions~\ref{prop:monotone}--\ref{prop:dichotomy}, which are statements
about a limit. The numbers quoted in Section~\ref{sec:region} from
\cite{CriticalPath} are reproduced, not re-derived, and carry the status recorded
where they were first stated. Nor do they verify anything in
Section~\ref{sec:sampling} beyond its closed forms and its budget: every table
there comes from exploratory computations requiring \texttt{mpmath}, which the
repository's conventions exclude, kept outside the registered programmes in
\texttt{numerics/exploratory/} and labelled there. Those computations agree with
an independently written quadrature assembler of this program to a relative
$6\times10^{-25}$, which is a consistency check and not a certificate.
