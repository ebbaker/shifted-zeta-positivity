\begin{abstract}
A proposal for the bulk-realization program asks whether the shifted Weil family
is the deformation flow of a Wilson line: the deformation of a line's expectation
obeys a differential equation fixed by the variation of its endpoints together
with the non-Abelian Stokes theorem, and the shifted family already obeys a
differential equation of that shape. Pursuing it turns the transfer into a
recognizable object, and that object reorganizes the rest. We first write the
causal generator out in full, which the program's background had defined only by
naming its symbol, and show that its three factors deliver the three terms of the
target exactly: the shifted form is the central form with every off-diagonal
kernel and every prime atom multiplied by a single hyperbolic factor. We then
show that the transfer symbol is \emph{unimodular on the critical line}, exactly
and unconditionally, by the functional equation alone. The transfer is therefore
an all-pass filter, and four consequences follow. Its phase derivative is the
archimedean symbol, so the Weil symbol is a group delay and the density identity
of the companion investigation is explained rather than computed. Its phase jumps
are resonances at the zeros, each contributing a Lorentzian of width $\omega$ and
fixed mass, so the Weil form is a total resonant response. Under the Riemann
hypothesis it is inner, the transfer is unitary, and the contraction defect is
exactly the mass transported past the leading endpoint of the interval. And a
filter that changes only phase is a phase, which is why the shift direction is
abelian: truncation is multiplicative on causal kernels, the transfers and their
generators lie in one commutative algebra, and the non-Abelian Stokes mechanism
has nothing to act on along the shift. The contraction region in the
shift--length plane is then settled completely: it is monotone in the length with
supremum one or infinity and nothing between, so contraction at every length is
\emph{equivalent} to the absence of zeros at distance more than $\omega$ from the
critical line. The shifted family is a graded criterion, one zero-free strip per
shift; under the Riemann hypothesis the region is the whole quadrant and has no
boundary; and the plane is a disproof instrument and nothing else.
We then identify the margin itself. Under the Riemann hypothesis it is the
constant in the \emph{lower} sampling inequality for the Riemann zeros on the
Paley--Wiener space of type $L/2$ --- there is no upper one, since the zeros are
not relatively separated --- and it is strictly positive, because the divergence
of the group delay makes the form domain embed compactly and a counting argument
then forbids a null vector: the unboundedness of the Wigner--Smith delay and the
positivity of the margin are one fact. The zero density meets the Nyquist density
at $2\pi e^L$ with an exact sample deficit $2e^L-\frac74$, so the sampling
crossover is the explicit-formula balance. But the deficit is not what the
collapse measures. Replacing the zeros by the Gram points, which have the same
counting function and no arithmetic in them, leaves a margin that collapses at the
same superexponential rate, to within a ratio of logarithms falling towards one:
\textbf{the collapse is a density effect and the margin carries almost no
arithmetic information}, so reproducing it is weak evidence for a candidate
source, and a candidate is correspondingly not required to produce an arithmetic
rate. What remains is the endpoint direction, where the variation is rank one at
the leading end,
the connection is non-abelian but flat, and the generator's own length-variation
is its boundary value. We close with the operator conditions this leaves, one of
which excludes the displacement operator and another of which is satisfied
structurally by an open Wilson line but not by any character --- and with the
observation that the Weil form's exact symmetry group, translations and
reflection in the arithmetic coordinate, is precisely the residual conformal
group of a ray with one end pinned, so that conformal symmetry is aligned with
the target rather than in tension with it, and positivity is invariant along a
conformal orbit. Nothing here
constructs a source, proves Weil positivity, or assumes the Riemann hypothesis
except where a statement is explicitly labelled conditional.
\end{abstract}
