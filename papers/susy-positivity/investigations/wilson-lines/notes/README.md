| [THE_DELAY_TEST_20260917.md](THE_DELAY_TEST_20260917.md) | Current; results and a proposed replacement of Condition 10.1 | The group-delay test, run. In closed form the delay of a ray-frame defect two-point function of dimension $\Delta$ is $\pi\operatorname{Re}\cot(\pi(\Delta+i\tau))$ --- exponentially small, and **identically zero** for every protected operator of the $\frac12$-BPS Wilson-line defect CFT, whose dimensions are $1,\frac32,2,L$. The failure is structural: reflection positivity with $D\geq0$ makes the ray kernel completely monotone, so its causal symbol is a Stieltjes transform, which has **no zeros on the axis and hence no winding**, and accumulates at most $\pi$ of phase against the target's $2\theta(T)\to\infty$. What is really demanded is an **inner function** --- a ratio, not a correlator --- on a sector where the dilatation generator is **not bounded below**; discreteness is irrelevant. The clause is satisfiable and by something free: the half-line inverted oscillator has phase shift $\arg\Gamma(\frac14+\frac{i\tau}2)$, whose derivative differs from $2\theta'$ by exactly $\log\pi$ --- the conductor. Bulk Liouville winds at $4Q\log P$. **So the $\log\tau$ is archimedean and cheap; the discriminating data is the constant and the fluctuation.** Also: \cite{OWL} contains no two-point function, so the test as the manuscript words it names an object that does not exist. Written by Claude Opus 5. |
