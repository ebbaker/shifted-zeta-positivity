```
Continue the Wilson-lines investigation in the shifted-zeta-positivity repo:
papers/susy-positivity/investigations/wilson-lines. Working manuscript 0.3 is
"Deformation flows for the shifted Weil family: the transfer as an all-pass
filter". Take your time on the mathematics; I would rather have one result

Read first, in this order, and don't re-derive the framing from them:
  notes/CONTINUATION_20260917.md          - state, the seven facts, what not to redo
  notes/SAMPLING_AND_THE_DEFICIT_20260917.md - the current front
  manuscript.pdf sections 4 and 7        - the all-pass identification and the region

THE TASK. Settle, or sharply bound, the lower frame bound of the Riemann zeros
as a sampling set for the Paley-Wiener space PW_{L/2}. Under RH that bound *is*
lambda_min(Q_{0,L}), so "how does the margin collapse" is a question in sampling
theory with the generating function - xi itself - in hand. Four steps, in order:

(a) Literature. Suzuki arXiv:2301.00421 (the Hilbert space of the Weil
    distribution as a de Branges space; a totally ordered chain; inequalities
    converted to equalities) is now machinery, not background: the de Branges
    question and the frame-bound question coincide. Also the non-uniform
    sampling literature for PW spaces - Beurling, Landau, Seip,
    Ortega-Cerda-Seip, Lyubarskii-Seip. The specific question: for a zero set of
    superlinear density generated by a Hermite-Biehler function, what does the
    theory give for frame bounds on PW_a at types a below the critical one?
    If it gives an expression through the structure function, that is the
    theorem to chase.

(b) A basis adapted to the deficit. The obstruction lives below
    tau_c = 2 pi e^L, with maximal sample deficit D(L) = 2 e^L - 7/4 exactly.
    Recompute upper bounds on lambda_min with trial functions built from that
    band - prolates at bandwidth tau_c, not at gamma_1, and not polynomials.
    Calibrate against Zhu's certificates (2.3e-17 at L=0.8, 3.2e-283 at L=2,
    normalised against ||f||_2^2). Until this is done no decay law is testable.

(c) A control. Repeat (b) with an artificial zero set of the same density
    (1/2pi) log(tau/2pi) but no arithmetic. If the decay tracks D(L), the
    collapse is a density effect and not an arithmetic one, which would be a
    large structural statement in either direction. This is cheap; do it even if
    (b) is only partly done.

(d) If (a)-(c) yield something, ask what it says about the two-sided question:
    strict positivity is already proved by a counting argument (sampling note,
    section 3), so the content is entirely in the rate.

FALLBACK, if the above stalls: the group-delay test of manuscript Condition 9.1
on a protected open Wilson line two-point function in the defect setting of
arXiv:1102.4948 - extract the phase against the dilatation eigenvalue and
compare its derivative with 2 theta'(tau). No arithmetic, no positivity, and it
is the first test in this program a gauge theory can fail.

TRAPS, each of which has already cost time:
  - a_omega has poles at Re p = +omega, so its continuation to the imaginary
    axis is NOT the causal symbol; the residues crossed there are the entire
    form. K_omega may be contour-shifted to the axis; a_omega may not. Same for
    the Cayley symbol a_cum.
  - The apparent poles of a_omega at +-1/2 +- omega cancel between the three
    factors of xi. Do not attribute them to a_omega.
  - Dilation in x, i.e. r -> r^lambda, is not a conformal map. Dilation in r is,
    and the Weil form is exactly invariant under it. Do not repeat the argument
    that conformal invariance forbids the target; it is corrected in 0.3.
  - Every recorded margin in this program is a trial-space Rayleigh quotient,
    hence a basis-dependent upper bound. Two independent computations differ by
    ~250 orders of magnitude at L=2. Do not fit a law to them; say so if you are
    tempted.
  - Rayleigh quotients are one-sided. State, every time, which direction a value
    certifies.
  - Manuscript Proposition 8.2 (the rank-one endpoint variation) is a partial
    derivative at fixed omega. Along a path the motion is endpoint plus bulk.

sketch, a labelled numerical computation, a heuristic, or a reading, and label

inheriting it. Prefer a sharp negative result to a vague positive one. If the

Write results to notes first. Only change the manuscript when I ask. End the
```
