| [THE_SHIFT_IS_HALF_A_DIMENSION_20260918.md](THE_SHIFT_IS_HALF_A_DIMENSION_20260918.md) | Current; items 2 and 3 closed | **The archimedean factor is a horocycle integral in dimension $2\omega$:** $K^\Gamma_\omega(p)=\int_{\mathbb R^{2\omega}}\lvert t+i\rvert^{-(p+b)}dt$ exactly, the delay is $\log\lvert t+i\rvert$ --- the arithmetic coordinate along a horocycle --- and the kernel's prefactor $2\pi^\omega/\Gamma(\omega)$ is the area of the unit sphere $S^{2\omega-1}$ (Prop. 1.1). At $\omega=\frac12$, $d=1$ is the horocycle of the modular surface, the only integer the family's range contains: **the shift is half a dimension and every geometry quantizes it.** No radial chain produces the Beta law, for three independent reasons (Props. 2.1--2.3): the additivity fraction is independent of the total, so a dimension-$b$ chain cannot see the split; the antipodal exponent of any radial chord law is $-\frac12$ for every driving, because the Loewner drift vanishes there and only the Itô curvature survives, so the chord law is always $\mathrm{Beta}(\cdot,\frac12)$ while the target has both parameters below $\frac12$; and the delay's zero-delay singularity is a power law, $\mathbb E[U^q]\sim q^{-\omega}$, which no interior first-passage time has. Contraction at $\omega=\frac12$ is **unconditional** (Prop. 3.1), so the family runs from a free endpoint to RH and the whole content is the deformation inward, which the quantized offset blocks. With the comb and the correction already excluded, **the Loewner proposal closes.** Side run: the second-order coefficient of the first-order law is of order unity at every horizon ($\beta=1.64,3.33,1.79$) against an a-priori scale $\kappa_L^2$ of $10^3,10^{12},10^{22}$ --- the law's validity window does not collapse with $L$.
