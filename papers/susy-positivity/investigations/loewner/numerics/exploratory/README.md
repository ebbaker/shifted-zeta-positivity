# Exploratory computations

Programmes here are **not registered check programmes**: they use `mpmath`,
which the repository's conventions exclude from registered checks, and nothing
replays them automatically. They exist so that every number quoted in the
notes is reproducible.

| Programme | Record | Supports |
|---|---|---|
| [transfer_kernel.py](transfer_kernel.py) | [transfer_kernel_omega0.1.json](transfer_kernel_omega0.1.json) | The living copy of the programme written for the inherited Wilson-lines note, Sections 4--5: the causal kernel $k_\omega$ of the transfer assembled from the Beta kernel $k^\Gamma_\omega$, the positive comb $\widetilde c_n$ and the rank-two factor $R_\omega$; checks A--G (Laplace transforms, Dirichlet coefficients, $R\widetilde K=K$, complete monotonicity, the first-order law, the kernel table, and the truncated kernel transform against $K_\omega(p)$, which converges geometrically in the cutoff). `python3 transfer_kernel.py 0.1 2.5`, about two minutes. |

| [contraction_margin_gj.py](contraction_margin_gj.py) | `cmgj_om{omega}_{L}_N{N}[_nested{N'}].json` | The [note of 18 September](../../notes/REGISTERED_ASSEMBLY_AND_THE_HORIZONS_20260918.md): the same object as `contraction_margin.py`, by **Gauss--Jacobi quadrature for the exact weight $\tau^{\omega-1}$** (Golub--Welsch on the closed-form monic Jacobi recurrence, $\alpha=0$, $\beta=\omega-1$), one rule per atom over that atom's whole support, with the two exponential smoothings as inner Gauss--Jacobi integrals. Records: $L=\log3$ at $\omega\in\{0.002,0.01,0.02,0.05,0.1,0.3,0.4,0.49\}$, $N=24$, plus $N=16,32$ at $\omega=0.1$ and nestings $N'=40,64,96,144,192$ at $\omega=0.1$ and $N'=64$ at $\omega=0.05$; $L=\log5,\log7$ at $\omega\in\{0.002,0.01\}$ (50--70 dps), with $N'=64$ at $\log5$ and $\log7$ over $\omega\in\{0.01,0.02,0.05,0.1\}$ (the second-order fit `second_order_fit_N24_nested64.json`); $L=\log{11},\log{13}$ at $\omega=0.01$ nested in $N'=64$ (80--90 dps). **The $\omega=0.002$ and $\omega=0.01$ margin rows of the `cm_*.json` records are superseded by these** (note of 18 September, Section 3.1); their mass and $e_1$ rows agree to every printed digit. |
