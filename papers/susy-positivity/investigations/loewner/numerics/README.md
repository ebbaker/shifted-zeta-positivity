# Checks and records

No registered check programme yet. When the elementary assembly of the transfer
(item 1 of the opening note's plan) is ported, it goes here under the
programme's conventions: standard library only, JSON to standard output, a
preserved record under [`records/`](records/), and --- once a manuscript exists
--- registration in the `CHECKS` dictionary of its `validation/drafts.py`.

[`exploratory/`](exploratory/README.md) holds the unregistered programmes
(all `mpmath`) that produce the numbers quoted in the notes.

Counts, when they appear, refer to finite test cases, not independent theorems,
and no check here is a positivity certificate.
