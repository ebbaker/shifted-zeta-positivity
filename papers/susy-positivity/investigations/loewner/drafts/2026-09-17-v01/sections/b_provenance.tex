\section{Provenance, and the use of language models}\label{app:provenance}

This manuscript was drafted for Edward Baker by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic,
model \texttt{claude-fable-5-1}), at his direction and in dialogue with him,
on 17 September 2026, as the first consolidated report of the Loewner
investigation of the SUSY-positivity program. The investigation was opened by
Edward Baker from a research note of the Wilson-lines investigation
\cite{LoewnerNote}; the questions it pursues --- what exactly is abelian in
the shift flow, whether flatness excludes curved realizations, and whether a
Loewner-type evolution could construct the deformation paths independently
--- are his, and the answers here were developed in response to them. The
mathematical content consolidates three research notes of the program
\cite{LoewnerNote,OpeningNote,ContractionNote}, all written by the same model
with the model named at their head, and the numerical programmes and records
cited in Appendix~\ref{app:numerics}, also written by it. The proofs are
short and are meant to be checked line by line by a human reader; the
numerical statements are reproducible from the programmes and records in the
repository; the readings are labelled as such. Nothing in this document should
be cited as established beyond what its own proofs establish.
