\section{Research agenda and present status}\label{sec:outlook}

The fixed-window calculation now distinguishes a concrete failed
readout from the broader realization hypothesis. Its regular
first derivative cannot reproduce the arithmetic singularity, and
its persistent identity channel cannot reproduce the compact
positive-shift transfer. The auxiliary product supplies the full
prime-free factor under a prescribed normalization. These are
useful constraints on the next physical construction, not a
solution of the inverse problem.

\subsection{The next discriminating tasks}

The free localization control has now made that direction predictive:
it supplies a gamma amplitude but fails direct spatial descent.
The canonical-Hodge alternative retains only the lowest elementary
spatial mode. Further localization work should specify how an
enlarged sector, an interface, or a definite composite observable
retains the missing modes and their endpoint weights. A matching
counting character is insufficient. The mode-counting identity
\eqref{eq:main-mode-test} and the fixed finite normalization remain
comparison targets after the physical dictionary has been derived.
The physical shift must remain distinct from a $Q$-exact deformation
coefficient.

Alongside this test, define a source-to-response map capable of the
singular identity limit. A localized expectation or partition ratio
is not already the required operator. Its first variation must
include the gamma difference, fixed local term and both poles;
beyond the first window, it must also generate the arithmetic
delays. The bounded-readout obstruction should be checked before
investing in a full interacting bulge calculation. For a candidate
that survives, endpoint, defect and junction contributions remain
part of the physical sum \cite[Section~S8.4]{Supplement}.

The direct mathematical next step is an energy-weighted all-input
estimate for the adjoining-window coupling, starting from
\eqref{eq:main-ema-anchor}. Preserve the weak directions, both input
complements and the endpoint history. In parallel, a physical
construction would still have to derive the independent cumulative
balance \eqref{eq:norm-hypothesis} or estimate the full cross-interval
map in \eqref{eq:gluing-main}. A signed determinant
does not supply that positive norm. Finally establish a joint
depth--shift step, including complement estimates and a condition
preventing finite-depth accumulation. The critical-path
investigation remains the home for that continuation. No
all-depth bound follows from the fixed-window anchor or the
localization controls.
