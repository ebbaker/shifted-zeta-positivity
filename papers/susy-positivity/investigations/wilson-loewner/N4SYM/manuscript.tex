\begin{abstract}
We study the response of the half-BPS Maldacena--Wilson line of $SU(N)$ \Nfour{} super-Yang--Mills
theory to shape and internal deformations, and the expectation values of a family of contours grown by
the Loewner equation, with the aim of deciding whether any of these observables can serve as a causal
channel with memory. Four results are established. (i) About the straight line, the quadratic Euclidean
shape response is fixed by the Bremsstrahlung function $B$ up to one perimeter constant, and the linear
retarded response is local: $\chi_R(\omega)=m_R\omega^2+2\pi\ii B\omega^3$ for every conformal line
defect with a dimension-two displacement. The total work reproduces the Correa--Henn--Maldacena--Sever
radiated energy, but the renormalized finite-time account carries an indefinite Schott term.
(ii) The Loewner trace closed by a return chord with reversed scalar charge (the flipped return) is a
regular observable in every regime. Its first two smooth-field evolution equations are exact; in the
conformal theory the Loewner time collapses to $s=a\sqrt t$; the continuum short-time law is governed by
the near-BPS corner of the generalized cusp, whose coefficient is one-loop exact order by order with
effective coupling $\lambda|s|$, and which at strong coupling crosses over to $\sqrt{\lambda|s|}$ with an
explicit classical coefficient. (iii) A finite-mass source, the string endpoint at
$z_m=\sqrt\lambda/2\pi m$, is the first channel with memory. Its linearized dynamics are exactly
$F=m\ddot Y$, $X=Y+z_m\dot Y$, $m\ddot X=F+z_m\dot F$; its energy account is positive at every time; and
the boundary field of the moving source is exactly local in the retarded time, with a closed-form quartic
transfer function, because the string-depth integrand is a total derivative. (iv) The quartic
straight-line response is the first nonlocal one; its memory exponent is set by the non-protected
dimension $\Delta_6(\lambda)$, but the straight line has no scale. None of these channels has a power
front with a variable exponent or a hierarchy of fixed delays, so no arithmetic comparison is warranted;
the physical results stand on their own.
\end{abstract}
