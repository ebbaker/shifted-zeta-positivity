\section{Assessment and outlook}
\label{sec:outlook}

\subsection{What memory exists in the half-BPS line}

Three kinds of memory were met. The Compton-scale exponential of a massive source (Section~\ref{sec:finite-mass})
is rational: one pole, one fixed light-travel delay. The anomalous-dimension power laws of non-protected operators
(Section~\ref{sec:quartic}) are scale-free and enter first at cubic order in the sources. A finite temperature would
add a quasinormal tower, again rational poles. None has a power front with a variable exponent accompanied by a
logarithmic tangent, and none has a hierarchy of fixed delays. The growing-trace observables of
Sections~\ref{sec:loewner}--\ref{sec:corner} are Euclidean expectation values on a one-parameter shape family with
no input/output structure at all.

\subsection{The arithmetic reading}

Against the ordered tests of Section~\ref{sec:scope}: the linear straight-line channel fails at the front (it is a
polynomial); the finite-mass channel fails at the front (it is rational); the quartic channel is not linear and has no
scale, so it cannot pass the delay test even in principle; the Loewner family is not a channel. The one structural
rhyme with the arithmetic side is that passivity holds cumulatively but not instantaneously in the point limit
(Proposition~\ref{prop:schott}), and Section~\ref{sec:finite-mass} shows that even this is an artefact of the
infinite-mass truncation. We therefore run no arithmetic comparison and regard the displacement and Loewner
channels of the half-BPS line as closed for that purpose, at the orders and in the backgrounds examined. The
closure does not extend to backgrounds with a derived scale (a rotating internal direction, a cusp, a temperature),
where a linear channel with memory could be constructed; any such construction must derive its scale rather than
choose it, and would still face the absence of any mechanism for logarithms of primes in a conformal defect.

\subsection{The physics that stands}

Independently of the arithmetic goal, the following are results: the locality theorem for the linear response of
any conformal line defect with a dimension-two displacement; the exact evolution equations and regular short-time laws
of the flipped-return Loewner loop, with the Loewner time acting as the effective coupling of a near-BPS corner; the
one-loop exactness of the near-BPS corner coefficient order by order, its $\sqrt{\lambda u}$ strong-coupling window
with an explicit classical function that reproduces the exactly known BPS-line slope, and the $(\pi-\theta)^{3/2}$
antiparallel law; the exact linearized dynamics and positive energy account of the finite-mass source, exhibiting the
Schott term as a truncation; and the exact light-cone localization of the dilaton-coupled boundary field with its
closed-form quartic transfer function.

\subsection{Open items}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Confirm the Callan--Güijosa comparison by eye, and test whether the dilaton localization persists for the
nonlinear Mikhailov string.
\item Compute the Lorentzian cubic kernel of the tilt channel from the validated $\Delta=1$ four-point function, or
from the Lorentzian tree diagram on AdS$_2$, to make Proposition~\ref{prop:memory} quantitative.
\item Extract the three-loop corner $\Phi(g,0)$ from the three-loop generalized cusp \cite{CHMSthree} and the one-loop
string correction that smooths the kink of $F$ at $\theta=\pi$.
\item Obtain an independent specialist review of the whole; every audit so far was by the same assistant.
\end{enumerate}
