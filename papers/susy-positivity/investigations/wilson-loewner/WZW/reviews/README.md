It is a same-assistant audit with 80 separate floating controls; a full local modular assembly is not claimed.

It is a same-assistant audit with 65 separate controls, not an independent specialist review.

The [Brownian bridge audit](review_codex_brownian_readout_20260923.md) checks the two-exit model, Mellin/Laplace distinction, causal norm balance, arithmetic exclusion, and the exact logarithmic phase extension with its unresolved causality. The [bounded readout audit](review_codex_bounded_readout_20260923.md) covers the full-module collar, fixed reflected input/output exclusion, selected primary spectrum and first prime delay. It is a same-assistant audit, with 60 separate controls and no independent specialist review.
