\begin{abstract}
The arithmetic orbit coefficients in the shifted completed-zeta quotient
admit an exact thermal description. In the Bost--Connes system, a fixed
primitive projection $P_n$ has equilibrium expectation
$\varphi_\beta(P_n)=\prod_{\ell\mid n}(1-\ell^{-\beta})$.
With inverse temperature $\beta=2\omega$, the required coefficient is
$c_n(\omega)=n^{(\beta-1)/2}\varphi_\beta(P_n)$.
This law reproduces primes, repetitions, composites and the prime part of
the shift generator, but does not by itself construct a physical response.
We derive local positive models and complete two coupling tests.
A primitive-controlled delay network has an ordinary energy balance
and an identity limit, but fails the arithmetic repetition law.
Separately, the unchanged archimedean completion times any finite
Euler product has, for $0<\beta<1$, an uncancelled pole incompatible
with passivity.
Native energy-changing probes give a positive Kubo admittance coupled
to the full modular half-shift channel. Any such parallel load with
finite total response spectral mass preserves the original front
exponent and cannot generate the required shift or logarithmic tangent.
These scoped exclusions leave the coefficient identity intact and
motivate direct cumulative-storage and canonical-system investigations.
A causal completed physical realization remains open.
\end{abstract}
