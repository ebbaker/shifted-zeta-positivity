\begin{abstract}
We assemble two complementary calculations in the Wilson--Loewner program.
For a normalized four-point boundary observable in the diagonal SU(2) level-2
Wess--Zumino--Witten theory, the deterministic Loewner variation reduces to
$\dot M=-\nu(2Q^2+\dot uQ)M$, with $\nu=1/4$ and Hermitian $Q$ for real
boundary spectators. We give the boundary-selected algebraic conformal block,
the complete generator, composition and fusion, and a positive integrated
balance for a constant driver. A moving driver can increase the same tensor
norm. The Chern--Simons channel pairing is a different, transported metric,
and the raw spatially smeared four-point kernel has a compact initial limit,
so it does not supply the desired identity-initialized arithmetic transfer.
Independently, the completed xi logarithmic derivative, translated into a
known zero-free half-plane, defines normalized generalized Loewner flows and
recovers the full arithmetic source, including prime-power delays. One flow
is linearized by $\log\xi$. Removing the source translation is equivalent
to the Riemann hypothesis (RH) at the level of whole-half-plane positivity; the arithmetic
shift generator itself is not a positive-real Loewner driver, even under RH.
We derive the available weighted operator bound and identify the missing
cumulative boundary or canonical response with its unweighted norm.
\end{abstract}
