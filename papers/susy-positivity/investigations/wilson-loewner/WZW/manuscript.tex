\begin{abstract}
The arithmetic orbit coefficients in the shifted completed-zeta quotient
admit an exact thermal description. In the Bost--Connes system, a fixed
primitive projection $P_n$ has equilibrium expectation
$\varphi_\beta(P_n)=\prod_{\ell\mid n}(1-\ell^{-\beta})$.
With inverse temperature $\beta=2\omega$, the required coefficient is
$c_n(\omega)=n^{(\beta-1)/2}\varphi_\beta(P_n)$.
This law reproduces primes, repetitions, composites and the prime part of
the shift generator. We derive the identity, an explicit positive
finite-place model, and the local ordinary Hilbert-space norms.
The centering factor and logarithmic delay interpretation still require
a physical input/output construction. Two immediate assemblies fail:
the bare Euler delay train is noncontractive, and the natural thermal
half-density vectors do not converge in the Haar-based infinite tensor
product. These failures do not exclude the established equilibrium states
or a different coupled realization. We formulate a two-prime coupling
test and close with a brief account of earlier unsuccessful readouts.
\end{abstract}
