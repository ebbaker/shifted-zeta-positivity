Latest: [arithmetic orbit weights and the Bost–Connes thermal test](ARITHMETIC_ORBIT_WEIGHTS_AND_BOST_CONNES_TEST_20260923.md). Previous: [continuous exponent and fractional cusp-field test](CONTINUOUS_EXPONENT_AND_FRACTIONAL_CUSP_TEST_20260923.md). Its [80 floating controls](../numerics/records/fractional-cusp-20260923.json) and [audit](../reviews/review_codex_fractional_cusp_20260923.md) are separate from earlier suites.
