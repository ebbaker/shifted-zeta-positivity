\section{The two calculations and the question they leave open}
\label{sec:scope}

A physical realization of the shifted-zeta equation needs more than a
geometric evolution with a positive pairing. It must specify a family of
boundary operators, identify its initial operator and arithmetic generator,
and relate its physical norm to the norm in which arithmetic contraction is
required. This manuscript separates the results now available for the first
two ingredients from that remaining operator question.

The physical calculation uses a fixed unitary boundary WZW theory. The
boundary labels select a conformal block before any sign is tested, and the
Ward and Knizhnik--Zamolodchikov (KZ) equations determine its response to a
prescribed Loewner driver. This gives a completely explicit finite
calculation, including the endpoint normalization. It does not construct
the full sewing operator on the boundary affine modules.

The arithmetic calculation starts with the completed xi function and its
logarithmic derivative. Positive-real function theory produces a genuine
generalized Loewner evolution after a safe spectral translation. This
construction contains the desired arithmetic coefficients exactly, but
they enter through xi as input. An independently defined physical
boundary response with this transfer and its norm remains to be found.

The standard boundary-CFT, KZ and Loewner results used below are credited to
their sources. The explicit calculations are a synthesis of the project's
research notes, without a claim of literature novelty. Both results and
counterexamples are scoped to the observables and identifications actually
specified. In particular, failure of the raw four-point kernel does not
exclude a construction retaining descendants or a different boundary
readout.

This text is a separate companion to the parent Wilson--Loewner manuscript.
It does not add sections to that manuscript. Section~\ref{sec:outlook}
states the present priority after both calculations: a cumulative response
specified by the arithmetic source, with the WZW pilot retained as a
physical benchmark.
