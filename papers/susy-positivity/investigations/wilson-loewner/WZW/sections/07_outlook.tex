\section{Remaining avenues}
\label{sec:outlook}

The completed tests narrow the thermal realization program while
leaving the main arithmetic positivity question open. The following
priorities update the earlier research assessment \cite{AvenuesNote}
in light of \cite{InterfaceNote,BoundaryNote}.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Cumulative storage for the complete transfer.}
The next concrete calculation is an ordinary $L^2$ energy balance on
$\log2<L<\log3$. In this window the formal kernel contains the full
archimedean term and its first translated copy, so the first arithmetic
interference terms can be treated exactly. The goal is a positive
storage formula from independently specified states and dynamics.
Defining storage to be the desired norm deficit would not prove its
positivity. No such formula is established here.

\item \textbf{Explicit canonical systems from arithmetic data.}
A complementary route seeks a positive Hamiltonian directly from the
prime and gamma data without assuming the target's contractivity.
Suzuki's explicit unconditional construction has the restriction
$\omega>1$ \cite{Suzuki}; extension into the required small-shift
range is an unresolved step, not an available consequence of that
result.

\item \textbf{Global deformations of modular scattering.}
The exact-one-form half-shift channel remains a useful fixed reference.
An independently motivated deformation of its asymptotic propagation
or bulk structure could lie outside Proposition~\ref{prop:front_rigidity}.
Its first tests should be the varying front exponent and logarithmic
tangent, before arithmetic echoes are fitted. This is exploratory;
no qualifying deformation has been constructed.

\item \textbf{Causality and support tests.}
Contour, residue and support analyses can isolate what prevents an
unweighted causal realization and keep finite-window calculations
faithful to the completed target. Such diagnostics support the storage
and canonical-system work; they do not by themselves establish the
required positive norm.

\item \textbf{Independent review of the retained results.}
The thermal coefficient identity, finite-place completion obstruction
and finite-spectral-mass theorem merit specialist review with their
hypotheses kept explicit. Further loop fitting or adding regular
boundary modes within the excluded class is not a priority.
\end{enumerate}
