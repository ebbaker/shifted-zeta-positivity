\subsection{What the numerical records establish}

Two separate programs accompany the underlying 22 September research notes.
Their unchanged records contain 132 passing cases: 11 exact rational
controls and 121 floating diagnostics. These counts do not belong to the
parent manuscript's original 299-case suite, and this editorial synthesis
does not claim a new numerical experiment.

The WZW program has 72 cases, seven rational and 65 floating. It checks the
independent 16-dimensional Pauli construction, algebraic blocks against
the Frobenius recurrence, KZ derivatives, fusion and both pairings, the
square reduction, and full two-column deterministic evolution. Five
drivers are integrated at two resolutions, with composition and norm
controls. Negative controls detect missing tip drift, omitted Jacobians,
scalar component closure, and an unrestricted contraction claim.

The $u=4t$ state initially grows, although its final norm in the table is
below its initial value. For $u=0$, the integrated loss is approximately
$0.260973048240009$. The largest recorded floating equality residual is
below $6.7\times10^{-14}$; it is diagnostic agreement, not an interval
error bound or a proof of a continuum field theory.

The arithmetic-source program has 60 cases, four rational and 56 floating.
It uses mpmath 1.3.0 at 40 decimal digits for the main source identities,
and double-precision time integration with 25-digit function evaluations
for the flow. Checks cover the xi logarithmic derivative, the complete
prime/gamma source, the shift and Cayley equations, normalized source
transport, finite model measures and the two obstruction controls.
No xi-zero table is used; the finite polynomial models explicitly specify
their own zeros.

For prime powers through $N=1000$, the tail comparison uses
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{n>N}\Lambda(n)n^{-\sigma}
 \le N^{1-\sigma}\left(
 \frac{\log N}{\sigma-1}+\frac1{(\sigma-1)^2}\right),
\end{equation}
where $\sigma>1$ and $(\log x)x^{-\sigma}$ is decreasing for $x\ge N$.
The inequality follows from $\Lambda(n)\le\log n$ and integral
comparison. At $p=5.5+0.7\ii$, $\omega=0.2$, the floating residual
is approximately $8.81\times10^{-16}$ against the analytical tail bound
$6.24\times10^{-15}$. Evaluating a proven bound in floating arithmetic
does not make the residual comparison an interval certificate.

For \eqref{eq:koenigs_flow}, at $\eta=1/2$ and $\tau=0.3$, the initial
point $1+0.7\ii$ reaches approximately
$1.2393712363+0.6045293214\ii$. The directly evaluated xi ratio is
$1.0209360162$, agreeing with \eqref{eq:linearization}. Across the two
sample trajectories the 64-versus-128-step difference is at most
$1.36\times10^{-13}$ and the xi linearization residual at most
$7.31\times10^{-16}$. The composition check uses matching steps and
primarily tests consistency; the separate xi identity independently
tests the vector field.

Analytical arguments, rather than sign samples, establish the finite
tensor balance, compact initial limit and safe positive-real source.
The numerical work establishes neither the physical sewing operator nor
unweighted arithmetic contraction or RH.

\subsection{Source history and reproducibility}

The main internal inputs are the 22 September notes
\emph{SU(2) level 2: a completed deterministic boundary Loewner pilot}
and \emph{The arithmetic source and an actual generalized Loewner
evolution}, together with their same-assistant audits. The former followed
the Chern--Simons/WZW proposal; the latter refined the priority toward
arithmetic-source matching. The earlier commuting shift-flow and cumulative
Cayley observations are credited in those notes, and are not new claims
of this synthesis. The parent manuscript supplies the established causal
operator conventions used in \eqref{eq:accumulated_defect}.

The local README links the preserved notes, both programs, their JSON
records and replay instructions. The manuscript builds entirely from TeX
files in this directory; it does not load parent manuscript sections.
The build record identifies the delivered PDF, source files and unchanged
research inputs. Such hashes establish file identity, not mathematical
correctness. Future working milestones are indexed by commits or tags
when available, without new dated manuscript snapshots.
