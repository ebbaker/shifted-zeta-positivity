# Exact truncated spline integration and independent checks

29 September 2026. Prepared for Edward Baker with LLM assistance. Model:
GPT-6 (Codex); serving variant and configured effort not exposed. This is
internal verification, not specialist refereeing.

The helper [arithmetic_truncated_spline.py](../numerics/arithmetic_truncated_spline.py) evaluates exponential integrals
of an even cubic B-spline correlation. It introduces no interpolation,
quadrature, or series truncation error: all finite expressions are evaluated
in Arb. Source replacement and operator errors remain the caller's separate
obligations.

Let

\[
\kappa_h(t)=\frac h{s^2}\sum_{m\in\mathbb Z}a_m\beta_3(t/h-m),
\qquad a_{-m}=a_m,\qquad a=Nh,\quad N\ge4.
\]

The coefficient sequence has finite support and may contain signed exact
integers or Arb balls. Define \(q=\lambda h\), \(z=e^q\),

\[
K(q)=\left(\frac{\sinh(q/2)}{q/2}\right)^4,\quad
H(q)=\int_0^2\beta_3(v)e^{qv}dv,\quad
T(q)=\int_1^2\beta_3(v)e^{qv}dv.
\]

At \(q=0\), the values are \(K=1,H=1/2,T=1/24\).
The required correction at the lower endpoint is

\[
L(q)=-a_0H(-q)+a_1\{e^{-q}T(q)-e^qT(-q)\}.
\]

This subtracts the portions below zero from the full splines centered at 0
and 1, and adds the entering portion centered at -1. Thus, with
\(A(z)=\sum_{m\ge0}a_mz^m\),

\[
\int_0^\infty\kappa_h(t)e^{\lambda t}dt
=\frac{h^2}{s^2}\{K(q)A(z)+L(q)\}.
\tag{1}
\]

For the interval ending at \(a=Nh\), replace the polynomial by
\(A_N(z)=\sum_{m=0}^Na_mz^m\), then subtract the outgoing portions
centered at \(N-1,N\) and add the entering portion centered at \(N+1\):

\[
\begin{aligned}
\int_0^a\kappa_h(t)e^{\lambda t}dt
=\frac{h^2}{s^2}\{&K(q)A_N(z)+L(q)\\
&-a_{N-1}e^{(N-1)q}T(q)-a_Ne^{Nq}H(q)
+a_{N+1}e^{(N+1)q}T(-q)\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]

No other spline crosses either endpoint. Missing sequence coefficients are
zero. The upper correction is necessary; merely truncating the polynomial
used by the full-halfline formula is incorrect.

The implementation evaluates \(H,T\) using the exact cubics

\[
\beta_3(v)=2/3-v^2+v^3/2\quad(0\le v\le1),\qquad
\beta_3(v)=(2-v)^3/6\quad(1\le v\le2),
\]

and the finite antiderivative

\[
\int p(v)e^{qv}dv=e^{qv}
\sum_{j=0}^3(-1)^j\frac{p^{(j)}(v)}{q^{j+1}}.
\]

The zero-exponent case uses exact polynomial integration. A nonzero Arb
exponent interval containing zero is rejected; all proposed half-integer
exponents with positive grid spacing are valid. Small \(q\) introduces
arithmetic cancellation, which Arb encloses; precision must be chosen by
the final propagated interval width.

API: `TruncatedCorrelation(coefficients,h,N,scale).evaluate(lam)` returns
`(full_half,before_a)`. It builds the full and prefix polynomials once.
Already transported coefficients, such as
\(a_m^H=(3/2)a_m-(a_{m-N}+a_{m+N})/\sqrt2\), may be passed directly.

## Independent integration controls

The [independent integration record](../numerics/records/arithmetic_truncated_spline_check.json) records comparison against direct
Acb integration on every unit spline cell, using the cubics themselves
rather than their antiderivatives. The signed coefficient sequence is
`[3,-2,5,7,-11,13,-1,9,2]`, \(N=5,h=\log2/5,s=3\). Both outputs in
(1)--(2) have overlapping independent enclosures at exponents
\(0,\pm1/2,\pm239.5,\pm10^{-5}\), with 512-bit arithmetic.
These are implementation consistency checks. The identities above and
interval arithmetic establish the finite-spline integration statement.

## Audit of the source-adapted Krylov bound

The [program assessment](../../../../../investigations/program-meta-analysis/reviews/ARITHMETIC_STORAGE_PROGRAM_ASSESSMENT_20260929.md) proposes a finite orthogonal projection \(E\) on the
ambient input space, \(\varepsilon=\|W(I-E)\|_{\rm HS}^2\), and the Sonin
trial space generated by \(A^jW(\operatorname{Ran}E)\), \(0\le j\le k\).
This bound is correct. For a degree-\(k\) polynomial \(p_k\) satisfying
\(\sup_{[m,M]}|1-\lambda p_k(\lambda)|\le\rho_k\), choose the admissible
trial solution \(Y=p_k(A)WE\). The two error terms

\[
A^{-1}W-Y=A^{-1}W(I-E)+(A^{-1}-p_k(A))WE
\]

have orthogonal input columns, including in their Hilbert--Schmidt energy
norm. Thus the Galerkin variational minimum gives

\[
B-B_{\rm Gal}\le m^{-1}\{
\varepsilon+\rho_k^2(h_0-\varepsilon)\}.
\]

The exact Chebyshev residual constant is
\(\rho_k=2r^{k+1}/(1+r^{2k+2})\), with
\(r=(\sqrt M-\sqrt m)/(\sqrt M+\sqrt m)\), so at the first prime
\(r=1/\sqrt2\) and the looser bound \(2r^{k+1}\) is valid.

For implementation, if only an upper bound on \(\varepsilon\) is available,
use \(\rho_k\le1\) (the exact constant, or clip the loose bound at 1) to
preserve monotonicity in that upper bound. The factor-2 bound alone exceeds
1 at \(k=0\). Approximate vectors and polynomial coefficients require their
own enclosure errors. This audit establishes the abstract approximation
bound, not an actual captured-trace certificate or arithmetic comparison.
