The saved records report all listed controls passing
in their recorded environments. They were not rerun
merely to consolidate this manuscript. Several use
Haar and the prescribed density
$\rho_{\rm test}=(1+0.35\cos\theta+0.2\cos2\theta)/0.9$;
these are not interacting four-dimensional samples.
Only the finite-slab experiment in
Appendix~\ref{app:hierarchy} samples that Wilson action.

For the latest edge check the cutoffs were
$10^3,10^4,10^5,10^6$, with relative profile errors
approximately 0.148035, 0.050738, 0.016088, 0.006306.
The final two-edge energy ratio was approximately
0.999265. Refinement from 240 to 360 quadrature points
changed the normalized energy by $8.204\,10^{-10}$.
An initial 160/240 comparison missed the $10^{-7}$
refinement threshold at $1.0323\,10^{-7}$; quadrature
was refined rather than the threshold loosened.
Finite products with 28 and 40 factors check
conventions, not the zero-free strip proof.
No zeta zero list was used.

The \href{numerics/README.md}{numerics index} provides
the unchanged source programs and reproduction commands.
Intermediate arrays remain in memory or external
scratch space. The repository contains no new large
datasets or third-party papers. Source hashes, build
tools, page count, and PDF checks are recorded in
\filepath{BUILD_RECORD.json}; instructions are in
\filepath{BUILD.md}. Build intermediates are excluded
from the maintained manuscript files.
