\section{Status, corrections, and the remaining gap}\label{sec:status}
The strongest constructive fixed-state result is the
complete signed mixed identity \eqref{eq:complete-signed},
with the actual weighted adjoints, a controlled raw
prime distribution, and the full archimedean contact and
logarithmic growth. Its proof uses smooth positive
finite-slab weights, elementary compact-group analysis,
Fourier/Mellin identities, and the stated iterated limit.
It does not use RH. Strong recentering independently
produces a genuine source with ordinary \Ltwo{} norm, but
that norm is not Q.

The strongest current-specific obstruction is
Theorem~\ref{thm:current-spectrum} together with
Corollary~\ref{cor:nondecay}: the full weighted
electric--Wilson current has only absolutely continuous
spectrum and cannot carry even the single detecting
response between finite vectors. The prime-boundary
lower bound and the positive zero-domain theorem explain
two distinct failures of naive completion. Neither a
fixed closable \Ltwo{} completion nor the tested coercive
positive readout removes them.

The audits require the following corrections or
qualifications to informal earlier interpretations.
\begin{itemize}
\item Use the weighted adjoint
\eqref{eq:weighted-adjoint}; the Haar divisibility rule
is not the interacting adjoint.
\item Keep all winding phases. The phase $\pi$ carries
the opposite current; other nonidentity phases have
the growth in \eqref{eq:wound-current-growth}.
\item The infinite Euler insertion is distributional
on finite characters, not a Hilbert vector. Its
raw-packet tail theorem does not justify a joint limit
after an unbounded change of insertion or packet.
\item The archimedean insertion is a specified
compensated radial compression. Its classical
conductor calculation is exact, but a physical
principle selecting the compensation and angle
normalization has not been established.
\item Weak packet convergence does not retain norms.
Scale-dependent native recentering repairs the
identity-phase \Ltwo{} source and loses the extra winding
norm in the precise amount \eqref{eq:rec-defect}.
\item A divergent positive local coefficient cannot
be deleted while citing positivity of the original
form. The completed signed form requires a separate
positive realization or positivity proof.
\item Full OS axioms, a mass gap, and optional local
phase-space compactness have the roles stated in
Section~\ref{sec:occurrence}; none supplies the
unproved arithmetic finite-feasibility or correlation
identity. A native generator is an optional ansatz,
not a prerequisite imposed by Weil positivity.
\end{itemize}

The exact remaining mathematical gap is to prove
\emph{physical occurrence}: either independently
specified admissible YM sources with the full pairing
\eqref{eq:goal}, finite arithmetic feasibility with
the compact preparation bounds of
Theorem~\ref{thm:compact-occurrence}, or the one actual
stationary correlation in
Theorem~\ref{thm:one-occurrence}.
The last theorem reduces the amount of mixed data
that needs to be established, but its hypothesis is
substantial and unverified. It is not legitimate to
define the physical spectral measure by the zero
ordinates and then invoke that theorem.

The investigation is paused at the author's request.
This draft consolidates the completed calculations,
conditional theorems, scoped exclusions, and diagnostics;
it does not extend them into a proof of RH or an
implication from four-dimensional YM existence and
mass gap. All new deductions remain model-assisted
work awaiting independent mathematical review.
