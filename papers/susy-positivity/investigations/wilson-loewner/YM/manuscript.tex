\begin{abstract}
We investigate whether independently defined sources in a positive
four-dimensional Yang--Mills representation can have the complete Weil
form as their reflected mixed pairing. A fixed finite SU(2) Wilson slab
provides an explicit positive boundary representation. Character packets
in its actual interacting state have exact phase branching and limiting
winding pairings with the prime-power weights of the explicit formula.
A specified compensated radial electric derivative and logarithmic
angle insertion recover the full archimedean multiplier, including its
contact normalization and logarithmic growth. Together these yield the
complete \emph{signed} Weil mixed limit, but not a positive source norm.
The normalized prime and compensated archimedean limits are universal
over smooth positive marginals; other pairings and source formulas
retain state dependence. No specifically four-dimensional selection
of the signed identity has been established.
We prove weak escape, phase incompatibility, bounded-occurrence and
closability obstructions, and divergence of positive prime completions.
A native current recovers the identity packets strongly with an \Ltwo{}
pairing; its full spectrum is nevertheless absolutely continuous and
cannot carry the required arithmetic correlation. Sharp prime cutoffs
have explicit moving boundary responses of norm at least order
$\sqrt A$. An explicit compact pole-neutral probe detects every
nontrivial zeta zero: its translated response is bounded, or tempered,
exactly when RH holds. One actual stationary physical realization of
that correlation would yield the entire source law by a proved spectral
filtering construction. We also give pairing-determination and compact
occurrence criteria, and distinguish their unverified arithmetic
hypotheses from OS axioms, a mass gap, and phase-space compactness.
The physical occurrence and positive completion remain open.
For distributional source candidates we additionally prove that every
nonzero translation step of an exact source must have full-circle
essential spectrum. This excludes an uncompensated radial electric
wave in the actual scalar and adjoint channels. Under explicit
continuum vacuum assumptions, ordinary spacetime translations also
cannot carry the detecting correlation.
For the full electric operator, regular-point sources with fixed
Sobolev powers have incompatible power-law growth. A separate rank
argument excludes finite-order distributional observables passed
through the strict half-slab integral without central-link dependence;
it requires no native arithmetic generator.
\end{abstract}
