# A bounded occurrence obstruction for the limiting character winding module

## 1. The additional question settled here

The preceding notes prove that character packets escape weakly and that a fixed closable insertion with bounded outputs cannot restore a nonzero strong limit. They leave open scale-dependent recovery operations.

Here is a stronger, but differently scoped, result: **there is no nonzero bounded intertwiner from the limiting character winding module back into the original one-plaquette class space that preserves all integer windings.** Only forward winding intertwining is assumed. Intertwining adjoints, electric currents, the archimedean insertion, or an arithmetic translation generator is not assumed.

The result already holds on the closed module generated by the identity-phase packets and their windings; independent access to every rational-phase packet is unnecessary. In particular, uniformly bounded scale-dependent recovery maps cannot have nonzero strong limits on that module while asymptotically preserving all windings.

This gives a precise limitation on using the profinite comparison as an occurrence argument. It is a calculation of the already derived limiting representation, not adoption of a profinite physical model.

## 2. Physical and limiting representations

Use the [character note](CHARACTER_CHANNEL_LIMITS_AND_PRIME_MIXED_PAIRINGS_20260925.md)'s notation. The actual class space is

\[
\mathcal H_{\rm cl}=L^2(SU(2),\rho\,dg)^{\rm Ad},\qquad
0<c\leq\rho\leq C,
\qquad V_a\chi_n=\chi_{an}.
\tag{1}
\]

The coefficient map

\[
\mathsf C:\ell^2(\mathbb N_{\geq1})\longrightarrow\mathcal H_{\rm cl},
\qquad \mathsf C e_n=\chi_n,
\tag{2}
\]

is a bounded isomorphism, with bounds furnished by the actual weighted norm. It need not be unitary. If \(S_a e_n=e_{an}\), then exactly

\[
\mathsf C^{-1}V_a\mathsf C=S_a.
\tag{3}
\]

The limiting rational-phase Hilbert space, with its normalized packet metric, is

\[
\mathcal K_\rho=\bigoplus_{\alpha\in2\pi\mathbb Q/(2\pi\mathbb Z)}L^2(\mathbb R),
\]

and its winding is

\[
\mathcal V_a^\rho(e_\alpha f)
=a^{-1/2}\sum_{a\beta=\alpha}
\sqrt{\rho(\beta)/\rho(\alpha)}\,
e_\beta U_{\log a}f.
\tag{4}
\]

These are the actual-state branching weights. Define the bounded invertible diagonal operator \(D_\rho e_\alpha f=\sqrt{\rho(\alpha)}e_\alpha f\). Equation (4) gives

\[
\mathcal V_a^\rho=D_\rho\mathcal V_a^1D_\rho^{-1}.
\tag{5}
\]

Using these similarities for a proof does not replace the physical state or its adjoints by Haar ones. Bounded existence of an intertwiner is invariant under the explicitly displayed similarities.

For computation, Fourier transform the rational phase index. As in the character note, this identifies \(\mathcal K_1\) with

\[
L^2(\mathbb R\times\widehat{\mathbb Z},dx\,d\mu),\qquad
(\mathcal V_a^1F)(x,z)=\sqrt a\,\mathbf1_{a\widehat{\mathbb Z}}(z)
F(x-\log a,z/a).
\tag{6}
\]

Here \(\mu\) is probability Haar measure. Integer multiplication is injective on \(\widehat{\mathbb Z}\), its image has measure \(1/a\), and (6) is an isometry onto the entire subspace supported on that image. Therefore its range projection is

\[
\mathcal V_p^1(\mathcal V_p^1)^*=M_{\mathbf1_{p\widehat{\mathbb Z}}}
\tag{7}
\]

for each prime \(p\). The shift in \(x\) does not restrict the range.

## 3. An elementary measure-zero fact

For every finite set of primes \(S\),

\[
\mu\{z:p\nmid z\text{ for every prime }p\notin S\}=0.
\tag{8}
\]

For a finite set of primes outside \(S\), the Chinese remainder theorem gives the measure as the product of \(1-1/p\). These products tend to zero. An elementary proof needs no prime number theorem: the finite Euler product over \(p\leq Y\), expanded as a positive geometric series, contains every reciprocal \(1/n\) for \(n\leq Y\), and so its inverse product grows at least as fast as the divergent harmonic sum. Removing finitely many prime factors does not change divergence. Continuity of measure under decreasing intersections proves (8).

Equivalently, a Haar-almost-every profinite integer is divisible by primes outside each fixed finite set. An ordinary positive integer has only finitely many prime divisors. The proof below turns this difference into an operator obstruction.

## 4. No bounded forward intertwiner

**Theorem.** If a bounded linear map \(T:\mathcal K_\rho\to\mathcal H_{\rm cl}\) satisfies

\[
T\mathcal V_a^\rho=V_aT\qquad(a\geq1),
\tag{9}
\]

then \(T=0\). It suffices to assume (9) for every prime.

**Proof.** Set \(A=\mathsf C^{-1}TD_\rho\). Equations (3) and (5) imply

\[
A\mathcal V_p^1=S_pA.
\]

For each ordinary integer \(n\geq1\), put \(\eta_n=A^*e_n\). Taking the adjoint of this equality gives

\[
(\mathcal V_p^1)^*\eta_n=A^*S_p^*e_n=0
\quad\text{whenever }p\nmid n.
\tag{10}
\]

By (7), a vector killed by \((\mathcal V_p^1)^*\) vanishes almost everywhere on \(p\widehat{\mathbb Z}\). Thus \(\eta_n\) is supported on the set of profinite integers divisible by no prime outside the finite set of divisors of \(n\). That set has measure zero by (8). Hence \(\eta_n=0\) for every \(n\), and density of the \(e_n\)'s implies \(A=0\) and \(T=0\). Taking adjoints in this proof adds no adjoint-intertwining hypothesis. \(\square\)

**The identity-phase winding orbit is already enough.** Let

\[
\mathcal K_{\rm cyc}^\rho
=\overline{\operatorname{span}}\{\mathcal V_a^\rho(e_0f):a\geq1,
f\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R)\}.
\tag{11}
\]

In the representation (6), \(D_\rho^{-1}\mathcal K_{\rm cyc}^\rho\) is
\(L^2(\mathbb R)\otimes L^2(\widehat{\mathbb Z},\mathcal B_{\rm div},\mu)\), where \(\mathcal B_{\rm div}\) is generated by the sets \(a\widehat{\mathbb Z}\). To check this, the generators are \(\sqrt a\,\mathbf1_{a\widehat{\mathbb Z}}U_{\log a}f\). Their indicator functions contain the constant, multiply by taking least common multiples, and their finite linear combinations are dense in the corresponding measurable-function space. Translated compact smooth profiles are dense in the real-variable factor.

This subspace reduces (6): multiplication or division by a fixed integer transforms divisibility events into divisibility events. More explicitly the adjoint is \((\mathcal V_a^1)^*F(x,z)=a^{-1/2}F(x+\log a,az)\). Its range projection on the subspace is still (7). Thus (10) and (8) prove the theorem with \(\mathcal K_\rho\) replaced by \(\mathcal K_{\rm cyc}^\rho\). One cannot evade the obstruction simply by omitting separately prepared rational phases.

Restricting the initial profiles to the global pole-neutral space does not change this closure. That space is dense in \(L^2(\mathbb R)\): an L2 function orthogonal to \((-\partial_x^2+1/4)C_c^\infty\) solves \((-\partial_x^2+1/4)u=0\) distributionally, hence is a linear combination of \(e^{x/2}\) and \(e^{-x/2}\); no nonzero such combination is in L2 on the whole line. This density uses the global test space, not one fixed support interval.

## 5. Consequence for scale-dependent recovery

For a finite formal winding combination \(w=\sum_j\mathcal V_{a_j}^\rho(e_0f_j)\), use its actual representative

\[
u_R(w)=\sum_jV_{a_j}S_R f_j.
\]

The audited mixed limits give \(\|u_R(w)\|_\nu\to\|w\|_{\mathcal K_\rho}\), including combinations representing zero in the limit.

Suppose operators \(T_R:\mathcal H_{\rm cl}\to\mathcal H_{\rm cl}\) obey a common bound \(\sup_R\|T_R\|<\infty\), their outputs converge strongly for every such \(w\), and for every fixed \(a,w\),

\[
\|T_RV_au_R(w)-V_aT_Ru_R(w)\|_\nu\longrightarrow0.
\tag{12}
\]

The strong limits define a linear map \(Jw\). The common bound and the norm limit show that it is independent of the formal representative and extends boundedly to \(\mathcal K_{\rm cyc}^\rho\). Boundedness of \(V_a\) and (12) imply \(J\mathcal V_a^\rho=V_aJ\). The theorem forces \(J=0\).

Thus even scale-dependent unitaries cannot recover this entire module strongly with its original winding operations. They can still recover selected individual vectors if (12) or simultaneous strong convergence on the module is dropped. This is consistent with the basis-exchange example in the electric-parity note.

## 6. Scope and the research decision

This is not an impossibility theorem for the requested YM-to-Weil source map. In particular:

- The target is a continuous map from smooth pole-neutral tests, and its logarithmic growth already prevents ordinary input-L2 boundedness. The theorem does not exclude arbitrary unbounded source laws on that test space.
- The source objective does not require all native \(V_a\)'s to act as exact intertwiners on its image. The prime mixed coefficient identity by itself is weaker than (9).
- The target here is the specified one-plaquette class space. Other physical sectors, or different operations outside that space, require their own definitions and are not covered.
- Finite collections of winding primes do not yield (8). Finite numerical checks cannot certify or contradict this obstruction.
- No positivity assumption about the Weil form is used; nor is a choice of zeta zeros or a conjecture about YM dynamics.

The practical consequence is to stop treating bounded recovery of the complete character winding module as the next existence lemma. It is false under the explicit conditions above. A future source proposal must identify which of those conditions it changes and independently prove the resulting positive physical pairing. Merely improving the phase response or identifying the limiting Hilbert spaces abstractly does not address this issue.

No numerical experiment is needed for this proof. It uses the audited branching and actual norm equivalence, elementary Hilbert-space adjoints, and the finite Euler-product argument in §3. These deductions still merit independent mathematical review.
