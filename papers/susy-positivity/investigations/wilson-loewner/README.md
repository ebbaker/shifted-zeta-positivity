# Wilson-Loewner: contour evolution of endpoint Wilson observables

Opened 19 September 2026. Working manuscript **0.2** (27 pages),
*Wilson lines on Loewner-generated contours: defect supersymmetry,
reflection, and endpoint positivity*: [PDF](manuscript.pdf),
[LaTeX source](manuscript.tex), [build guide](BUILD.md), and
[dated drafts](drafts/README.md). Written by OpenAI GPT-6 (Codex),
for Edward Baker. Initial research remains in [notes](notes/README.md).

Version 0.2 expands Section 2.2 to display the add-and-subtract identity
defining the local remainder, its contribution to the archimedean integral,
and the complete Weil form in difference-energy notation.

The [shift-flow and critical-path research note](notes/SHIFT_FLOW_CUMULATIVE_STORAGE_AND_CRITICAL_PATH_20260919.md)
records a proposed next direction: prescribe the exact arithmetic shift
evolution, seek a reflected realization of cumulative contraction, and
connect it to the existing depth--shift gluing criteria. It is a handoff
to the new critical-path investigation, not a new result of this manuscript.

The manuscript is self contained: it reproduces the RH target, arithmetic
factorization, relevant free-field background and the three calculations,
and reviews the primary Wilson-line, defect, Loewner and reflection literature.
No shared background file is needed to compile it.

**Mechanism:** use Loewner evolution to generate or deform a contour, then
evaluate a gauge-field Wilson observable with specified endpoint matter.
The curve, field theory, endpoint states and resulting transfer operator are
separate parts of the construction.

**Current result:** the inherited common-origin semicircles are inverse images
of constant-driver chordal Loewner slits. Under the inherited fixed-scalar,
fixed-bulk-charge condition in one normal plane, these contours are rigid:
inversion must make the curve vertical. A smooth Wilson variation formula
identifies the field-strength, scalar and endpoint insertions generated by a
regular Loewner pullback. The tangent-coupled scalar prescription now admits
common chiral defect-compatible charges for arbitrary smooth contours,
including one charge across all defect directions. Its ordinary same-charge
scalar endpoints form an off-diagonal R-charged pair with zero vacuum
expectation. The third calculation gives an explicit ordinary-time reflected
network and a smooth reference junction. Its positive free kernel is the
opposite-ray kernel; interacting positivity remains conditional. Direct bulk
exchange has a finite nonzero quadratic response to a polynomial contour
bulge. The endpoint and defect contributions to that response are next.

**Status:** compiled first manuscript with classical variation, chiral
defect-projector, endpoint-polarization and reflection derivations,
with 268 finite diagnostics and a standalone dated snapshot.
The explicit bulk response is only one perturbative sector. The endpoint
result is restricted to the displayed constant-map Poincare ansatz; it does
not close the combined investigation. No closed arithmetic evolution,
interacting positive kernel, or RH proof has been obtained. There is no
independent review of this package yet.
