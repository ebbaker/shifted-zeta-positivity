\begin{abstract}
This companion develops the technical calculations behind the physical
Wilson--Loewner program and its longer-term arithmetic realization question.
For a genuine Loewner prefix completed by a return chord in fixed
four-dimensional Yang--Mills theory, we prove the moving-tip cancellation,
derive the first two insertion equations, and establish a short-time
expectation expansion with explicit smooth-field error bounds. We retain
the curvature ordering, transverse-current residual and field-flow response
needed for a Schwinger--Dyson closure attempt. Quantum averaging requires
stated regularity and moment assumptions; no continuum construction or
closed scalar loop equation is asserted. Earlier sections preserve the
causal arithmetic factorization, free defect kernel, smooth contour
variation, endpoint selection, reflected pairing and Gaussian response
controls. They also give the fixed-window readout obstructions, auxiliary
boson--fermion product, localized hemisphere amplitude, radial non-descent
and canonical-Hodge comparison. Arithmetic anchor and append results are
reported as separate internal work. The original 299 diagnostics and
additional localization controls retain their own records; new trace-growth
checks test geometry and ordered matrix calculus, not quantum expectation
values. None of these results supplies the physical arithmetic transfer or
its positive cumulative norm.
\end{abstract}
