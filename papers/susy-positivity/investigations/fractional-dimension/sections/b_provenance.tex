\section{Provenance, and the use of language models}\label{app:provenance}

This manuscript was drafted for Edward Baker by a language model of the Claude
family (Anthropic), at his direction and in dialogue with him: working draft 0.1,
on 18 September 2026, by Claude Opus 5 (model \texttt{claude-opus-5}). The
investigation it reports was opened by Edward Baker on 18 September 2026 from the
closed Loewner investigation \cite{Markov}, and the question it pursues ---
Problem~\ref{prob:main}, whether the dimension the transfer's factors carry is a
dimension or a parameter --- is his.

The mathematical content consolidates five research notes of this investigation
\cite{OpeningNote,WhichDimension,RankNote,WallNote,TradeNote}, each written by a
model named at its head, together with the six numerical programmes and preserved
records described in Appendix~\ref{app:numerics}, also written by them. Nothing
is inherited without being restated: the propositions taken from the parent
manuscript are listed in Section~\ref{sec:inherited} and are used as stated, and
every other proposition here is proved in place.

The proofs are short and are meant to be checked line by line by a human reader.
The registered computations are replayed by \texttt{validation/drafts.py} and
require byte-identical output; the readings, the calibration and the two
conjectures are labelled as such in Section~\ref{sec:status}. Nothing in this
document should be cited as established beyond what its own proofs establish.
