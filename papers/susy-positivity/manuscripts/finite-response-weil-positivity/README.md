The note leads with the bounded, noncompact boundary correction, its essential norm pi/2, finite-input mass tails and an image expansion with an exponential remainder bound. Standard Schur and residual identities yield lower and upper response enclosures. The matrix conjecture is stated after the response is defined: S_{n,N} is positive semidefinite for every positive integer length n and every admissible cutoff N. The classical Weil criterion makes this equivalent to RH; one admissible cutoff at each length suffices. The note does not prove the conjecture. The present smallest admissible cutoff grows doubly exponentially with length. The numerical length-one example illustrates small spectral margins in upper approximations and supplies no positivity certificate or demonstrated computational advantage.
