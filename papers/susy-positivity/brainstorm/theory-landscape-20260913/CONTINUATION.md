# Continuing the arithmetic ground-state investigation

Read [REPORT.md](REPORT.md), then [EXTENDED_SUSY_TEST.md](EXTENDED_SUSY_TEST.md) before attempting to add more supersymmetry. The preferred next task is a bounded theoretical construction test, not an open-ended numerical bound search.

## Research objective and priorities

Find an independently positive theory and a specified ordinary state pairing equal to the complete finite-interval Weil form for all required complex inputs, with compatible growing supports. Positivity should follow from the physical Hilbert space or bulk energy. Numerical checks are secondary to deriving this identity.

The favored architecture combines semilocal arithmetic operations with a dynamical Hermitian ground-state metric and a relative or polarized boundary completion. No fully specified theory currently realizes that combination. The arithmetic benchmark is the Morse–prime SUSY product; the nonlinear geometric benchmark is twisted-mass \(\mathbb{CP}^1\)/its Landau–Ginzburg description. Chiral-parameter \(tt^*\) and vector-parameter Bogomolny equations must be distinguished.

## Exact information already available

For finite prime set \(S\), an explicit positive supersymmetric tensor product has a ground state \(\Psi_\sigma\), self-adjoint observable \(X\), and
\[
\|\Psi_\sigma\|^2=\Lambda_S(\sigma),\qquad
\langle\Psi_\sigma,e^{i\tau X}\Psi_\sigma\rangle=\Lambda_S(\sigma+i\tau),
\quad
\Lambda_S(z)=\pi^{-z/2}\Gamma(z/2)\prod_{p\in S}(1-p^{-z})^{-1}.
\]
The normal logarithmic derivative of \(|\Lambda_S(\sigma+i\tau)|^2\) at \(\sigma=1/2\) is the full pole-free multiplier. Normalizing at \(\tau=0\) gives an exactly positive gamma-plus-prime jump energy. Restoring the target subtracts the fixed negative contact and adds the signed rank-two pole form. Deriving this complete accounting inside a positive pairing is the missing step.

For the chosen complex extension \(H_{\sigma,\eta}=e^{i\eta X/2}H_\sigma e^{-i\eta X/2}\), the physical holomorphic vacuum metric is \(\Lambda_S(\Re z)\), with nonzero variance curvature. It is not the squared transition amplitude \(|\Lambda_S(z)|^2\), which is a flat scalar metric with a frame ambiguity. This specified holomorphic ground line cannot be an ordinary rank-one chiral \(tt^*\) bundle; other partner-parameter prescriptions remain possible. The unchanged Hilbert space and Hamiltonian also have a multiplicity-two positive-energy interval incompatible with four standard real supercharges without central charges.

## Proposed next task

Construct, or establish a scoped incompatibility for, a four-supercharge extension of the Morse factor and one prime. Specify new fields, supercharges, domains, physical adjoints, parameter multiplets, boundary preparation and arithmetic observable before choosing a putative metric. Determine the actual vacuum bundle and Berry curvature. Keep the arithmetic Fourier coordinate distinct from a physical partner coupling unless their identification is derived.

A useful result must explain a new piece of the full pairing: physically fixed source normalization/contact, pole contributions through a positive constraint or coherent interference, or an identity converting the arithmetic derivative into an ordinary norm. Reproducing the already known amplitude, positive normalized energy, or a formal spectator doubling does not meet this standard.

Then test two-prime primitive selection and compatible support enlargement. At fixed finite \(S\), compare the full Weil form only where all active primes are included. Keeping \(S\) fixed and demanding the full pole form be positive at arbitrarily large support is impossible: odd separated bumps make the signed pole term dominate negatively. A one-prime amplitude with all repetitions is a different, legitimate test.

If the direct extended-SUSY attempt cannot fix the derivative and framing problem, investigate the semilocal positive moment/Toda construction or a polarized relative arithmetic complex. An independently passive realization of the shifted transfer remains an alternative if passivity is derived from the bulk rather than assumed for the zeta input.

## Constraints to preserve

- The full target includes gamma normalization, both poles, and all active prime-power returns. Reproduce formulas instead of citing background equation numbers.
- Interactions are allowed but do not automatically evade positive spectral-moment restrictions. State exactly which hypothesis changes.
- A fixed finite vacuum fiber cannot represent all input directions. Lifted periodic sectors, field observables, or a genuinely infinite operator tower need explicit source maps.
- A positive Hamiltonian, index, determinant, scattering phase or amplitude is not already the required norm.
- Do not assume RH, fit zero data, take the unknown positive square root of the target, or declare an unproved arithmetic polarization positive.
- Preserve the manuscript and shared background while exploring. Record research in this package or a clearly separated follow-on note.

The reports contain primary-source citations and the analytic derivations. No new numerical positivity sweep or formal manuscript revision is needed to continue this test.
