## Primary estimating problem

Use the symmetric normalizer \(\mathcal A\), distinct from the positive arithmetic carrier, and the prescribed analytic correction:

\[
Q=H/\mathcal A,\qquad
f_N=(G_N-\widehat C_N^{\rm an})/\mathcal A,\qquad
r_N=Q-f_N,\qquad J_v=(v,-v_x/\omega),\quad\omega=\theta_x<0.
\]

At a real common zero of H and Hx, \(J_{f_N}=-J_{r_N}\). Thus the complete endpoint enclosure forces the retained jet into a small disk. The primary question is what arithmetic information constrains that simultaneous cancellation. Endpoint error and ordinary prefix-integral error have different contracts and must remain distinct.

A new argument must retain the actual shared height, its logarithmic phases, heat weights, normalizer and cutoff, and identify which of these screens it escapes.

### FFA-02 Estimate the genuine signed arithmetic interaction

Derive one quantitative bound using the actual height dependence. Retain cross-block correlations or complete packet cancellations until an estimate has been proved. Compare with the existing bound for the same expression, norm and domain. Specify constants or asymptotic savings and the errors needed to transfer the result to the complete field.

**Useful outcomes:** a new restriction on simultaneous small values; an applicable signed correlation estimate; or separation on a meaningful stated domain. A measure estimate for exceptional heights remains an intermediate theorem because isolated collisions can lie in a measure-zero set. If an approach fails, state the exact norm, source or phase class, quantifiers and saturation mechanism. A scoped obstruction can close the attempt and guide a revised contract, but does not make a dependent physical application ready.

The fourth-jet condition is uninformative at multiplicity at least four; record that remaining obligation or prove the required higher-order extension with its own payments.
