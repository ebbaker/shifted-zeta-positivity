## What the exchange settled, and what it did not

The cancellation scale is a serious constraint. Fixed finite measurement
designs and endpoint perturbations deserve an explicit conditioning
analysis. However, the radius \(\|h\|/C\) in Claude's reply is a sufficient
nonvanishing guarantee, not automatically the minimum distance to a
feasible collision. A small sufficient radius does not exclude
directional or positivity-aware certificates. Project 07 will audit the
fixed-space/fixed-measurement scope, carrier bounds and finite numerical
claims. The suggested threshold near \(x=140\) is an extrapolation,
not a certified coverage limit. This does not close infinite-dimensional,
height-dependent or constructively admissible source classes.

The Jensen correction removes a mistaken literature barrier; it does not
provide a stable approximation to theta. Project 05 must supply a
construction with an independent preservation mechanism. Source
identification, exact arithmetic identities and generic positive
realizations from [summaries 14][s14] and [16][s16] remain useful inputs,
but they do not supply the missing signed estimate.

For Sonin, the exact place-addition laws are established source material.
The proposed identification of accumulated mixed responses with a
translate-span Weil form remains a claim to check. Project 04 begins by
retaining the inverse metric and determining the extra terms, if any;
it does not assume either a useful sign or a universal obstruction.

Heat zero-count information already exists. Project 06 starts from
Polymath's theorem and extracts useful explicit constants only for a
declared application. A total count does not show that zeros are real
or simple; an upper local count alone does not give a lower population,
an exact interior count or control of near-zero velocity denominators.
There is no separate attraction-law campaign in the opening portfolio.

The family direction is promising enough for a contract investigation,
but neither a substitution \(\log x\mapsto\log(qx)\) nor global strip
contraction proves the required finite-height implication. The conductor
range, two height directions for complex characters, approximation
errors and barrier replacement all need proof before an almost-all
exceptional-set exponent is reported.

## Decisions at each phase

**Review cadence update, 17 October 2026:** at the user's explicit instruction,
formal reviews follow an investigation round that may contain many notes.
Routine derivation and numerical checking continue within the round; an
individual note is not a review gate. The phase evidence below applies to
coherent round outputs. Update prepared for Edward Baker with substantial
LLM assistance, **gpt-6.1-sol (Codex)**, reasoning effort **ultra**.

| Gate | Evidence required | Decision if it is missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contract | Object, normalizations, domain, imported theorem hypotheses, target classification, eligible controls, one output and its error budget | Repair the contract before computation |
| First bounded result | One derivation, discriminating diagnostic or explicit failed budget that can resolve the stated question | Stop that phase after one cross-model reconciliation; record the unresolved input |
| Expansion | Separate review of the result, coverage, numerical uncertainty and scope; a credible quantitative gain or new structural estimate | Keep the result as a bounded note; no automatic larger run |
| Manuscript | Reviewed applicable estimate, complete certified gain, or substantial precise obstruction with its canonical sources | Keep proposal and notes; do not generate a manuscript merely to consolidate activity |
| Closeout | Current README, concise history, exact failed/successful class, and named reopening input | Finish the record before opening a successor |

Before every numerical run, write its domain, sample cases, accuracy,
maximum resource budget, baseline and stopping answer. Choose numeric
resource limits when the implementation is known, before executing it;
this plan does not invent performance measurements. A floating scout
may guide a proof budget but is not a certificate. Replay counts, printed
digits and file hashes do not supply a missing mathematical implication.

After one derivation and review cycle, a project should either pass its
gate, produce a precise obstruction, or identify one missing input and
decide between dormancy and one explicitly scoped repair cycle. After
two cycles without an improved bound, useful
estimate, faithful diagnostic or scoped obstruction, recommend dormancy
and update this overview. Further work needs an explanation of what
materially changed. These are research management criteria, not
impossibility theorems.

Specific stopping decisions are recorded in each proposal:

- **01:** suspend the chosen parameter box or density mechanism if its
  conductor losses, barrier assumptions or finite-height implication
  cannot be paid. Lack of overlap with existing verification data closes
  that benchmark, not all per-character work.
- **02:** do not produce full rows until the sharp inequality, uniform
  transfer, tail and coverage contract have a positive total margin.
  A gain is a complete certificate or a measured reusable cost reduction.
- **03:** reject a particular majorant when an unavoidable nonnegative
  error term already consumes the sign margin. Do not tune the inherited
  astronomical onset as though it were an attainable certificate.
- **04:** stop a generic algebraic reformulation or an estimate that simply
  assumes support-adapted Weil positivity.
- **05:** stop a family that assumes theta stability, repeatedly refits the
  heat flow, or offers only finite moment agreement.
- **06:** stop that application when the count uncertainty removes its
  proposed gain. A separately useful explicit theorem may survive.
- **07:** complete the declared cases and one reconciliation; an
  inconclusive result is not a reason to extend the grid or increase
  dimension.

## Controls and evidence discipline

The governing screen is [summary 12][s12] and [avoid 02][a02]. For every
claimed general mechanism, list the premises shared by each applicable
control and the exact premise or quantitative inequality it fails.
Weighted-square controls do not have full reflection; reflected controls
do not have the integer-square source; positive-primitive controls do not
have the full theta arithmetic; Euler-factor controls change the
arithmetic family. Generic smooth-density and matrix controls test
generic positivity arguments, not automatically the full Sonin or
primitive-character contract.

A finite certificate may correctly pass a collision-free region of a
control. It must fail to certify the contradicted claim on a region
containing that control's collision or contrary witness. Passing a
source-identification screen still leaves the quantitative sign to prove.

The key safeguards are [avoid 01][a01] on equivalent reformulations,
[07][a07] on scoped exclusions, [08][a08] on normalization, [10][a10] on
premature computation, [12][a12] on lost cancellation, [16][a16] on
literature imports and [20][a20] on volume without gain. The other
reviewed patterns continue to apply, particularly separate ownership,
honest review independence and synchronized status records.

## Directions retained without new project homes

Canonical-system continuation stays dormant until someone states an
independent quantitative estimate that the canonical representation helps
prove. Constructing another generator or assuming a contraction property
equivalent to the target does not open it.

Modular/transverse continuation stays dormant until a concrete admissible
deformation supplies a new estimate and explains how it avoids the
specific smoothness, growth or scalar-multiplier obstruction it encounters.
Those earlier exclusions do not cover every possible deformation.

Cancellation/localization belongs to Project 03 when attached to a
specific remainder or overlap estimate. A shrinking-window identity
alone does not earn another home. Controls exposition or a standalone
fixed-exponent result can be consolidation work in the canonical project,
without creating another exploratory lane.
