## Shared mathematical target and classification

Begin with the [result summaries][summaries] and [patterns to avoid][avoid],
especially [arithmetic bookkeeping][s14], [local collision laws][s13],
[control screening][s12] and [geometric realizations][s16]. The corrected
scope in [Reply 18][reply18] takes precedence over blanket claims that the
arithmetic or geometric direction is futile.

For the fixed zeta heat field retain

\[
h_j=\partial_x^jH,\qquad E=h_0^2+h_1^2,\qquad
\mathcal L_j=h_{j+1}^2-h_jh_{j+2},\qquad S_j=\{h_{j+1}=0\}.
\]

At an exact finite-order event of multiplicity \(m=2r+j\), \(r\ge1\),
\(j\in\{0,1\}\), the established stationary predecessor law is

\[
\mathcal L_j/E=-r/(T-t)+O(1).
\]

A suitable independently proved arithmetic lower bound with locally finite
loss could exclude the covered event. The all-event zeta criterion on
positive heat time is already RH-equivalent, with the exact time intervals,
endpoint qualifications and distinctions between finite loss and strict sign
in [summary 13][s13] and [Reply 18][reply18]. This is the common destination,
stated here once; it is not a new result of initializing projects.

Classify subsequent outputs according to [avoid 01][a01]:

| Output | How it will be assessed |
| --- | --- |
| Another all-event criterion or collision detector | A reformulation; favorable finite checks do not support its universal assertion |
| A new arithmetic restriction or transfer theorem | Structural progress when its hypotheses and conclusion go beyond the existing identities and the desired sign is not assumed |
| A restricted quantitative estimate | Report its domain, constants, baseline and remaining extension; immediate improvement of a Newman bound is not required |
| A negative result | Name the exact source class, norm, ansatz and quantifiers; apply [avoid 07][a07] |
| A finite computation | Diagnostic or certified bounded evidence with its scope; never a substitute for an infinite tail or universal implication |

Identifying the normalized theta source is not the same as bounding the sign
of its transform. Likewise, a small absolute tail need not be small relative
to vanishing physical energy. Every proposal names the step intended to
bridge one of these gaps. [Avoid 12][a12] governs complete paired expressions:
retain lower intervals, complex shifts, source insertions and cancellations
until a valid joint estimate has been made.

## How 11 becomes a concrete opening project

Its [expanded proposal][p11] begins with an exact double event, \(T>0\),
and the normalization \(s=T-t\), \(x=x_*+\sqrt{s}\,y\). The known local
profile is fixed; the research question is the exterior information that
profile cannot determine.

The first relation to examine is the complete \(d=2\) dilation packet,
retaining the lower interval, the prefactor and both half-line observations.
Its imaginary displacement tends to \(T\log2\), so it stays outside the
shrinking collision scale. The proposal explicitly distinguishes the
physical entire function from a single complex half-line transform and
requires a remainder estimate at the normalized scale. Full modular
compatibility is the second route if the first merely relocates the unknown.

The first research round should expose one named exterior quantity and ask
whether complete coefficient and modular information constrains it
independently. It can succeed through a new necessary condition for a
hypothetical event, without immediately excluding all events. A finite
measurement obstruction is useful only if proved for the stated class and
norm. The proposal leaves higher multiplicity, uniform nondegeneracy and
the zero-time endpoint as explicit later obligations.

## Controls and arithmetic premises

The screen below routes to the existing evidence; it is not a new numerical
validation. There is no proposed universal inequality yet to certify.

| Control | What can be shared | What the proposed arithmetic argument must add |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weighted three- and four-square sources | Positive square weights, support-moment facts, matched finite endpoint data | Full reflection and the complete normalized coefficient/excision relations |
| Reflected super-Gaussian and smooth shape sources | Reflection and the particular positivity, decay and endpoint properties proved for each source | The normalized integer-square arithmetic expansion |
| Positive-primitive collision control | Strong primitive positivity and the stated all-algebraic-order asymptotics | Complete theta arithmetic rather than primitive shape alone |
| Euler-factor modifications | Reflection and unmodified Euler factors; some finite prime algebra | The changed level, lattice and coefficients; an untested prime must not be silently excluded |
| Generic heat germs and shifted-field examples | Local collision geometry and the particular generic analytic hypotheses proved for each example | A new source-specific global restriction; arbitrary shifted amplitudes are not bounded by a vanishing physical jet |

Use [summary 12][s12] and [Reply 18][reply18] for the precise premise lists,
as required by [avoid 02][a02]. Different controls have different properties;
do not combine them into a hypothetical control satisfying every premise.
Project 12 needs a further beta-specific screen for any finite product or
coefficient identities it uses. Existing zeta controls test its generic
steps but do not automatically realize those beta premises.

## Research cadence and concrete deliverables

The next proposed action is one focused opening round in 11. Its first
deliverable should state the conditional event, complete global relation,
local/exterior split, remainder norm and control comparison. The same round
may contain several derivations and estimate attempts. Formal assessment
follows the coherent round; an individual note is not an approval gate.

For any activated project, the round should finish with an exact result or
a clearly identified unresolved estimate, a premise/control table, and a
decision about the next mechanism. A new structural restriction, transfer
or scoped obstruction can justify continuation even without a better
numerical constant. More identities or a larger computation alone are not
sufficient reasons to expand a campaign.

Separate reviews record the versions checked and distinguish self-checking,
same-model assistance, cross-model LLM review and specialist human review.
This portfolio setup has no mathematical review status. Collaborator briefs
should reduce each direction to its exact source, proposed estimate, known
obstruction and nearest control. No outreach or new chats have been initiated.

Incremental work belongs in each project's notes, computations in numerics,
and assessments in reviews. Follow the [large-file policy][large]; cite
third-party sources instead of copying PDFs. Future manuscripts name Edward
Baker or state that they were drafted for him, acknowledge LLM assistance,
and use concise draft histories with source paths or Git milestones, not new
snapshot folders.

Future project-specific chats register in their own project, while existing
registrations retain their unique identities under the [history workflow][chatguide].
