Riemann zeta function, or at least a fixed character, and accepts a much lower

## What the reviews actually exclude

[Joint Review 16][review16] made broad judgments about the futility of the
direction and the insufficiency of anything short of the complete Euler product.
[Reply 18][reply18] subsequently states, "equivalence does not establish
futility," corrects the control-premise lists, and replaces the universal
claims with scoped screening statements. These corrections are also recorded
in [avoid 07][avoid07]. They must accompany any renewed assessment of 21–22.

Three mathematical objections remain. A correct collision criterion is not a
proof of its arithmetic premise. The known controls disprove exclusions based
only on the properties each control shares. Existing geometric realizations
have not supplied the needed global physical sign estimate. These objections
do not establish that an arithmetic use of the collision framework is futile.
An exact geometric representation can retain the sign while still lacking a
bound on it; the scope is stated in [summary 16][summary16].

The revised priority is therefore the missing arithmetic-to-collision argument
within 21–22. It is not a general endorsement of every former lift or proposed
estimate, nor a claim that the probability of success has increased.

## The missing mathematical step

The fixed-function reduction and local collision laws are already available in
[summary 13][summary13]. A hypothetical positive threshold for zeta produces a
finite real multiple zero, using uniform positive-time confinement. Near an
event, the relevant stationary branch has the signed divergence
\(\mathcal L_j/E=-r/(T-t)+O(1)\), with the branch and multiplicity specified
in the sources. An arithmetic lower bound on the appropriate
ratio that remains finite in a neighborhood of a positive-time event would
contradict that event. The all-event statement is RH-equivalent;
this is an existing target, not a new intermediate result.

The research question is which independently usable relation of the complete
theta source can force such control after Newman heat deformation. Arithmetic
may be expressed through exact coefficients, level-one modular structure,
coupled theta identities, or transformed dilation relations. It need not appear
as an explicit Euler product for the positive-time function. The ordinary
Euler product cannot simply be assumed to survive the deformation unchanged.

[Summary 14][summary14] and [Reply 18, section 4][reply18] give exact surviving
relations, including complex shifts and heat-dependent weights. Their validity
does not determine the physical sign. Transferring those relations to a signed
collision observable, while retaining cancellations, boundary terms and tails,
is the substantive open step.

The control requirement is precise: identify an input or estimate that the
relevant colliding controls fail. It is not a theorem that every proof must
explicitly manipulate primes. In the current controls, weighted-square sources
lack full reflection, reflected smooth sources lack the normalized integer
square expansion, and Euler-factor modifications change the level or arithmetic
coefficient data. The conjunction and its quantitative consequences matter;
source uniqueness alone is not a sign inequality. See [summary 12][summary12]
and the corrected premise table in [Reply 18, section 3][reply18].

## Priorities for the next brainstorm

1. Keep an existing collision test fixed and investigate a new
   arithmetic estimate feeding it. Do not spend the main effort inventing
   further equivalent detectors.
2. Permit speculative geometric and analytic ideas when they offer a concrete
   way to retain or use the arithmetic constraints under the physical heat
   transform. Octonionic, modular and scalar formulations are judged by the
   implication they might make provable, without an a priori preference for
   an ambient dimension.
3. Judge early progress by a new arithmetic restriction, a transfer theorem,
   or a sharply scoped obstruction that changes the available mechanisms.
   Immediate improvement of a numerical constant is not required. Checks may
   falsify a proposed mechanism or its premises; favorable checks are not
   evidence for an RH-equivalent universal assertion.
4. Treat routine optimization of 01+03 as supporting work unless a connection
   to the fixed-function objective is supplied. Preserve its evidence and
   review requirements; this note neither cancels another chat's work nor
   changes manuscript or investigation status.
