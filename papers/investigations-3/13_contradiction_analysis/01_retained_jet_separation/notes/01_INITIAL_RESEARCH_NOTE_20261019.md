## Hypothetical event and exact contradiction

Use the symmetric nonvanishing normalizer and prescribed analytic
correction from [Project 11][uniform]:

\[
Q=H/\mathcal A=f_N+r_N,\qquad
f_N=(G_N-\widehat C_N^{\rm an})/\mathcal A,\qquad
J_v=(v,-v_x/\omega),\quad \omega=\theta_x\ne0.
\]

If the complete endpoint theorem gives
\(\|J_{r_N}\|\le\varepsilon(t,x)\), a real common zero of \(H,H_x\)
forces \(J_{f_N}=-J_{r_N}\), hence \(\|J_{f_N}\|\le\varepsilon\).
The direct contradiction would be an independent arithmetic bound
\(\|J_{f_N}\|\ge L>\varepsilon\) at every candidate point in a stated
class. This excludes arbitrary real collisions in that class; a threshold
application may restrict the candidates further.

The disk lies in normalized value–slope coordinates. Entry is necessary for
a collision but not sufficient. The exact requirement is cancellation of
both the value and derivative, including the actual residual direction.
The endpoint theorem must not be replaced by the separate prefix-integral
estimate. The positive arithmetic carrier is not this normalizer.

## Questions for the first deeper discussion

1. Which complete low, intermediate, or terminal block interaction most
   obstructs a lower bound on a specified subdomain? Keep its sign until
   an estimate has been proved.
2. If both first-jet coordinates are within their endpoint tolerances,
   what additional restriction follows for a block correlation,
   logarithmic moment, or higher jet? A consequence must constrain an
   additional observable rather than merely restate the two equations.
3. Can a joint enclosure for \((J_{f_N},J_{r_N})\) avoid the set
   \(\{(u,v):u+v=0\}\) even where the separate norm test fails?
   Specify the extra source information that would justify this enclosure.

The third question houses the correlated residual alternative. It is not
a sixth independent contradiction target: it refines the same exact jet
equality. A coordinate rotation or differently drawn ball does not improve
the available information.

## Control screen

Complete multiplicative phase twists can make the relaxed model jet zero.
Their missing premise is realization at one genuine height with the
corresponding weights, normalizer, and cutoff. Thus a bound for all unit
multiplicative phases is already excluded in that source's stated regime.
Finite-prime orbit controls preserve the selected finite arithmetic data
but fail full normalized reflection or unchanged complete coefficients.
Polynomial heat germs lack the theta source and approximation contract.

At a genuine event of any control with its correct approximation errors,
the exact identity requires the retained jet to lie in its allowed disk.
Any purported generic strict lower bound must fail there. This is an
analytic screen, not a claim that a new numerical evaluator was run.

## Useful first results and remaining scope

A useful first result is one signed interaction estimate with a quantified
gain over its absolute baseline, a restriction on simultaneous small
values, or separation on a meaningful declared domain. A bounded diagnostic
may select the interaction by examining cancellation-prone configurations;
favorable samples or an almost-everywhere bound do not remove isolated
events. A failure should identify its precise phase class, norm, quantifiers,
and saturation mechanism.

The all-event exclusion is RH-equivalent under the [finite-collision
reduction][predecessor]. A restricted margin is judged by its domain and
size. No positive constant uniform over an unbounded family is presumed.
This route can supply candidate restrictions to the threshold-jet route
or a concrete observation to the source-reflection route. It imports
Project 11 estimates rather than reassigning their ownership. Full
time/height coverage and any Newman barrier application remain separate.
