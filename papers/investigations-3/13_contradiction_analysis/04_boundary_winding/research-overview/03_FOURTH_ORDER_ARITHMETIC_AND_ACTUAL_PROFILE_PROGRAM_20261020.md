## Target, measurements and scope

Assume a hypothetical threshold \(T=\Lambda>0\) of the unchanged flow
\(H_t=-H_{xx}\), in the normalization \(H(0,z)=\xi((1+iz)/2)/8\).
At an ordinary double event \(a>0\), write
\[
h_j=\partial_x^jH(T,a),\qquad h_0=h_1=0,\qquad h_2\ne0.
\]
All zeros at \(T\) are real. The inherited reduction allows other finite
multiplicities; it does not guarantee a double event. No theta event has
been observed, and no uniform lower bound on \(|h_2|\) is assumed.

For \(b=\max(a,40)\), [BW-08][jet-bound] gives
\[
S_2=\frac{h_3^2}{9h_2^2}-\frac{h_4}{6h_2}>L_2(a),\qquad
L_2(a)=\frac1{2a^2}+\frac1{4b}\log\frac{b+1}{4\pi}
       -\frac4{a^2+b^2}
>\frac1{2a^2}+\frac1{40b}.
\]
Fix a declared event domain \(D\) and a proved constant
\(0<L\le\inf_D L_2(a)\). For a bounded height interval
\(0<a_-\le a\le a_+\), the final displayed floor supplies the admissible
choice \(L=1/(2a_+^2)+1/(40\max(a_+,40))\); a sharper choice must be paid.
The necessary inequality is
\[
Q_L=2h_3^2-3h_2h_4-18Lh_2^2
   =18h_2^2(S_2-L)>0. \tag{1}
\]

Use the polynomial expression as the primary consumer, avoiding division
by an unboundedly small curvature. For two rigorous upper bounds on the
same target, domain, \(L\) and normalization, record
\[
G_Q=U_{\rm base}-U_{\rm new},\qquad
d_Q=\max(U_{\rm new},0),\qquad \delta_Q=-U_{\rm new}.
\]
A proved \(G_Q>0\) is an improvement over the named baseline; it is not
an exclusion if \(U_{\rm new}>0\). A fully paid \(U_{\rm new}\le0\)
contradicts (1); \(\delta_Q>0\) is a strict numerical sign margin.
The [existing product allowance][product-budget] may be used for complete
physical jet enclosures. A normalized or correlated consumer requires its
own correct error transfer.

Check the event constraints before optimizing (1). If the acquired first
jet already excludes an event, report that exclusion and whether the
region was previously covered. An empty feasible set must not be reported
as a new fourth-order sign improvement. Conversely, a feasible interval
relaxation is not evidence that a theta collision exists.

The global exclusion of every positive threshold event would settle RH.
This continuation targets a restricted estimate or a precise failure of
one declared method. It does not schedule a global numerical cover.

## Primary arithmetic tasks

### BW2-01.A — Freeze one event and information contract

**Output:** a contract note in the threshold-sign home, with a source
request for Project 11.

Choose one bounded event domain \(D=[T_-,T_+]\times[a_-,a_+]\), or one
explicitly quantified analytic subdomain, with \(T_->0\), \(a_->0\).
Identify known coverage before choosing a physical test. The explicit
UAR sector is a candidate source domain only after its higher-derivative
hypotheses are justified; no new sector or numerical onset is assumed.

Name the retained full arithmetic formula, complex neighborhood, cutoff
convention and event-conditioned observation. Require the normalizer
to be real and nonzero on the real event domain and holomorphic and
nonvanishing on the neighborhood used for its analytic derivatives.
State which coefficient or affine information is retained beyond the
closed homogeneous multiplier ball, and how it enters a bound.
Freeze \(L\), target normalization, strongest applicable baseline and
the exact meaning of \(G_Q\).

Before any computation, specify precision, cell geometry, cutoff/mode
limits, required errors, maximum runtime/memory and stopping answer.
Select these from the proposed implementation; no measured costs are
invented in this plan. A diagnostic on already covered territory is
permitted when labeled as validation.

**Acceptance:** every hypothesis and error has a named producer, the
new information differs quantitatively from the rejected classes, and
one bounded calculation or analytic estimate can decide the stated
question. A contract alone is a planning output.

**Stop:** if the only new premise is positivity, an exact mass condition
that simply restores the unknown physical multiplier, or a restatement
of the target. Record the missing input before any sweep.

### BW2-02.A1 — Establish the complete fourth-order interface

**Depends on:** BW2-01.A. **Output:** Project 11 derivation and, if
numerical, source-pinned implementation and small validation record.

Produce complete enclosures or analytic bounds for components zero
through four of the actual source. For \(F=H/A=\widehat F+r\), bound one
shared analytic residual on the justified disk. Extend the real Schur
coefficient description through order four, or prove another enclosing
joint description. Cauchy bounds \(j!\eta/R^j\) are an available
fallback only when the full error is at most \(\eta\) on that disk.
Use [Polymath, Theorem 1.3 and Section 6][polymath], together with the
corrected Project 11 interfaces, as approximation inputs; a value bound
does not by itself justify the required derivatives.

Pay all omitted modes, source and contour tails, gamma/heat corrections,
quadrature, outward rounding, normalizer derivatives and cell propagation.
Freeze the cutoff during holomorphic differentiation. If the target
crosses a cutoff, prove overlap and transition compatibility; if it
does not, state that restriction. Distinguish local frozen derivatives
from derivatives of a varying-center family.

For time uncertainty, account for \(H_t=-H_{xx}\): direct first-order
time control of \(h_4\) requires \(h_6\). A higher Taylor scheme must
state its actual maximum order. Normalized quantities do not inherit
the unmodified heat equation.

At an exact event, with \(\alpha=(\log A)_x\) and
\(f_j=\partial_x^jF\), the required physical return is
\[
\frac{Q_L}{A^2}
=2f_3^2-3f_2f_4-18(L+\alpha_x)f_2^2. \tag{2}
\]
The [survey][survey] gives the elementary expansion. Verify it in the
adopted normalization and pay uncertainty in \(\alpha_x\).
The cancellations \(f_0=f_1=0\) apply to the exact field, not the
approximant's center values.
Because \(A^2>0\) on the event domain, (2) has the same sign as \(Q_L\).
Label bounds and margins for (2) as normalized quantities; converting
their magnitudes to physical \(Q_L\) requires the corresponding \(A^2\)
enclosure. A matched comparison must use one normalization throughout.

**Acceptance:** a written complete order-four contract on \(D\), with
all assumptions checked and numerical enclosures validated where used.
For numerical work, include exact derivative/normalizer fixtures and
a source-return comparison on the declared validation cases. Overlap
of two computations is a diagnostic, not a substitute for either
method's outward error proof.

**Stop:** if only the omitted error improves, higher derivatives lack
an admissible neighborhood, or an unpaid cutoff/normalizer term remains.
A complete interface is infrastructure; it is not yet an arithmetic
sign or physical coverage gain.

**Stop:** if projection discards the arithmetic center and recovers the

## Optional actual-profile tasks

### BW2-03.P1 — Prove the observation model and test its required accuracy

**Output:** a BW-08 feasibility note. It can start independently of A
but must declare its own event domain, observations, target gap and budget.

Use actual theta values of \(D_T(b)=\partial_b\log H(T,ib)\), not
generic lower floors. For \(s=a^2\), \(B_i=b_i^2>0\), remove the double
pair and use the finite weighted measure proposed in the [survey][survey]:
\[
R_i=\frac{D_T(b_i)}{b_i}-\frac4{s+B_i},\qquad
\mu=\sum_{\rho>0,\ \rho\ne a}\frac{2m_\rho}{\rho^2+B_0}\delta_{\rho^2}.
\]
The claimed observations are
\(\int d\mu=R_0\) and
\(\int (y+B_0)/(y+B_i)\,d\mu=R_i\).
Prove this interface from the inherited paired product, including
finiteness, multiplicities and dependence on the event parameters.
No probability normalization or finite mass for the unweighted
counting measure is assumed.

For a bounded neighborhood \(I\) of \(s\), define
\[
w_I(y)=\tfrac12(y+B_0)\mathbf1_I(y),\qquad
k_s(y)=\frac{(s+y)(y+B_0)}{(s-y)^2}.
\]
The first integral counts other positive zeros in \(I\), with integer
multiplicity; the second contributes to \(S_2\). Seek rational
combinations of the observation functions that majorize these targets
on the appropriate full support.

Use [Bertsimas–Popescu][bp] for the weak-duality method, not as a ready
theorem for this rational infinite-zero problem. Establish the required
majorization directly; strong duality and a finite-support optimizer
are not prerequisites. Use [Batenkov–Goldman–Yomdin][bgy] to motivate
a conditioning analysis, without importing its finite Fourier-data
rates into this observation model.

Select a finite sample schedule and justify the required errors
\(\varepsilon_i\). For fixed certified dual coefficients \(c_i\),
pay \(\sum_i|c_i|\varepsilon_i\), together with every parameter and
coefficient uncertainty. Forecast values are design inputs only;
their use cannot certify a physical gap.

**Acceptance:** a proved observation/dual interface plus one feasible
accuracy contract for a bounded actual-source experiment, or a concrete
failed conditioning/error budget. State why the proposed data could
distinguish the constructed profile controls.

**Stop:** if only generic floors or constructed profile vectors are
used, if conditioning requires an unprovided gap, or if the complete
error budget cannot reach the intended count threshold. Do not expand
the sample schedule merely because a relaxation has failed.

### BW2-03.P2 — Acquire the actual data and certify a residual-zero gap

**Depends on:** a useful P1 contract. **Output:** BW-08 observation
enclosures, a rational majorant and its certificate.

Acquire the complete profile intervals on the stated event domain.
Bound the denominator in the logarithmic derivative and pay source
tails, quadrature, rounding and parameter variation. Any numerical
search for dual coefficients is a design step; certify their final
pointwise inequalities on the entire half-line. Clearing positive
denominators must preserve the inequality, including endpoints and
the behavior at infinity.

For \(g\ge w_I\), obtain a fully paid
\[
N_I=\int w_I\,d\mu\le U_N<1,\qquad \delta_N=1-U_N>0.
\]
Integer multiplicity then gives \(N_I=0\). A small positive bound on
\(\mu(I)\) alone does not do this. If the whole-line majorant uses
complete observations, the infinite tail is already included; if a
truncation is used, add a separate proved tail allowance.

**Acceptance:** actual theta intervals, a validated global majorant,
a positive \(\delta_N\), and an explicit root-free neighborhood for the
other positive zeros. Handle all parameter values in the declared box.
This is a gap estimate, not yet the opposing \(S_2\) bound.

**Stop:** if only a floating objective is below one, or the own-profile
test falsely excludes a known neighboring zero of a control satisfying
the asserted premises. A losing count bound can be recorded without
activating P3.

### BW2-03.P3 — Convert the gap and profile data into an \(S_2\) bound

**Depends on:** a paid gap from P2, or another explicitly proved gap
with its own source and domain. **Output:** a BW-08 signed comparison.

On the gap complement, certify a majorant for \(k_s\), giving
\[
S_2=\frac1{2a^2}+\int k_s\,d\mu\le U_S.
\]
The pole at \(s\) must be excluded by the paid gap. A numerical cutoff
requires a kernel-specific distant-tail estimate. [Project 06][zero-counts]
can support one above its cutoff; all remaining finite ranges need
their own treatment. [Stopple's localization lemma][stopple] is an
optional comparison tool only after its contour-growth and localization
premises have been established for the actual deflated \(H_T\).
No RH assumption from the surrounding Lehmer-pair paper is imported.

**Acceptance:** a complete \(U_S\) and its matched-baseline improvement
or deficit. The stronger \(U_S\le L\) contradicts the inherited strict
lower bound. Report \(\delta_S=L-U_S\); do not convert it into a uniform
positive \(\delta_Q\) without a separately paid curvature lower bound.

**Stop:** if the gap is assumed, the singular neighborhood is omitted,
or an upper count is mistaken for a spacing theorem. A gap without the
subsequent signed estimate does not exclude the threshold event.

## Controls, review and physical follow-up

The [closed round][closed-review] already supplies the analytical screen.
New consumers require their own applicable checks; planning does not
claim those checks have run.

| Control class | Required treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Exact theta Gram relaxation | Retain the positive optimum as the baseline obstruction; a precision increase is not a repair |
| Positive continuous/atomic threshold sources | Preserve their genuine events and own data. They lack unchanged full theta arithmetic; a claimed arithmetic distinction must be quantitative |
| Fixed finite-profile families | Use their own fixed vectors and known nearby roots; they test false gap/upper-\(S_2\) conclusions, not equality with the actual theta vector |
| Euler-factor, weighted-square and finite-prime controls | Check the documented source and threshold premises separately. Full coefficients/reflection can fail; do not assign theta constants or invent all-real slices |
| Reflected smooth and positive-primitive controls | Retain their shape/reflection properties; those properties alone do not identify the theta square expansion |
| Polynomial heat events and phase relaxations | Test algebra and event acceptance. Free phases do not represent coupling to one actual height; polynomial sources do not inherit theta error budgets |
