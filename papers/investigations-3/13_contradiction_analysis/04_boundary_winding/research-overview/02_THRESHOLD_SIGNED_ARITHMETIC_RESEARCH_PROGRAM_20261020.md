## Starting point and priorities

Assume a hypothetical positive threshold \(T=\Lambda>0\) of the
unchanged flow \(H_t=-H_{xx}\), with an exact multiple zero at \(a>0\).
The inherited finite-event reduction supplies a finite multiplicity,
but does not guarantee multiplicity two. All zeros at \(T\) are real;
all zeros at later times are real and simple. The initial estimating
task restricts to

\[
H(T,a)=H_x(T,a)=0,\qquad h_2\ne0,\qquad
h_j=\partial_x^jH(T,a).
\]

Here \(T,a,h_2\) are unknown event parameters, not acquired observations.
An event-dependent positive radius is admissible; no uniform curvature
floor or uniform radius is assumed.

## The primary signed comparison

For \(b=\max\{a,40\}\), BW-08 gives

\[
S_2=\frac{h_3^2}{9h_2^2}-\frac{h_4}{6h_2}>L_2(a),\qquad
L_2(a)=\frac1{2a^2}
 +\frac1{4b}\log\frac{b+1}{4\pi}
 -\frac4{a^2+b^2}
 >\frac1{2a^2}+\frac1{40b}.
\]

Choose a proved fixed lower coefficient \(0<L\le L_2(a)\) on the
declared event class. The existing necessary restriction is

\[
Q_L=2h_3^2-3h_2h_4-18Lh_2^2>0.
\]

The preferred contrary estimate is \(Q_L\le0\), proved using applicable
arithmetic information independently of this necessary lower bound.
For complete enclosures \(|h_j-\widehat h_j|\le e_j\), \(j=2,3,4\),
use the inherited product allowance \(E_L\) in
[BW-08 Note 03, Section 4][jet-original]:

\[
\widehat Q_L+E_L\le0.
\]

A numerical result should report the positive margin
\(\delta_Q=-(\widehat Q_L+E_L)>0\); an exact nonpositive symbolic bound
also contradicts the strict necessary inequality. A verified improvement
in an upper bound that does not reach this sign can still be a restricted
estimate, with its remaining deficit stated.

For BW-01, the alternative consumer is the critical curve
\(H_x(t,c(t))=0\), \(c(T)=a\), and the pair

\[
P(s)=\frac{H(T+s,c(T+s))+H(T-s,c(T-s))}{h_2}.
\]

The inherited theorem gives \(P(s)\le-C s^2/2<0\) on its paid,
event-dependent interval, with \(C=6L\) sufficient. A contrary
arithmetic bound \(P(s)\ge0\) on that same interval would also exclude
the event. A direct \(Q_L\) contradiction needs no winding computation.
A claim of zero physical winding instead must cover the complete
boundary and preserve orientation; signing this pair alone does not
establish that degree.

Excluding every possible positive threshold event would settle RH by the
inherited reduction. That global reformulation is stated here once.
The next-round milestones concern a quantified restriction, a declared
event class, or a precise obstruction to a selected method.

## Work packages and acceptance conditions

### BW2 01 Select the arithmetic observation

Freeze one contract for the primary comparison. State the event domain,
normalization, source versions, actual physical time and height, required
derivatives, and all error norms. Name a quantitative use of the unchanged
theta coefficients or a complete arithmetic identity that is absent from
the nearest surviving control. Merely labeling an integral as theta does
not identify such a use.

Begin with the exact physical source in the [BW-01 contract][source]
and the oscillatory quadratic \(Q_L\), retaining signed mixed terms and
the constraints \(H=H_x=0\).
Test whether a specific interaction between complete source terms can be
bounded more sharply after those constraints are imposed. The failed
positive-measure covariance argument from BW-01 is not available again:
the real-axis cosine weighting is signed.

The corrected retained arithmetic sum from [Project 11][arithmetic] is
an alternative representation. Its existing first-jet or residual
estimates do not supply a complete fourth-jet contract. Before using it,
justify derivatives through order four, the normalizer conversion,
analytic correction, actual-height phase coupling, and cutoff crossings.
The explicit UAR sector may be selected only within those justified
contracts; it is not an automatic higher-jet domain.

**Deliverable:** one estimate request naming the signed term, current
bound, intended quantitative gain, exact source-specific input and
nearest controls. A contract is planning. If no distinguishing
quantitative input can be identified, record that finding and select a
different mechanism rather than launch a numerical sweep.

### BW2 02 Bound one signed arithmetic interaction

Execute the accepted BW2-01 contract. Keep cross terms and event
constraints together before taking absolute bounds. Use complete theta
coefficients and one actual height, including their heat weights and
reflection terms. Prove either a bound on \(Q_L\) or a bound on the
selected signed component with a quantified assembly into \(Q_L\).

Compare with the strongest bound using the same source information.
If conditioning on \(H=H_x=0\) is used numerically, retain the complete
residual tolerances and parameter uncertainty; a nearly vanishing sample
is not an exact event. Any imposed lower bound on \(|h_2|\) defines a
restricted class unless separately proved for the whole application.

**Useful outcomes:** a new arithmetic upper bound with an explicit
improvement and remaining deficit; a complete restricted contradiction;
or a proof that the selected estimate cannot attain its required sign
in its stated information class. An identity for \(Q_L\), more precision,
or another lower-bound table is insufficient. A scoped failure closes
that attempt but cannot satisfy a downstream need for a useful bound.

### BW2 03 Test a quantitative use of the imaginary-axis profile

This alternative may begin with its own BW2-01-quality contract without
waiting for BW2-02 to succeed. Select a concrete observation of
\(D_T(b)=\partial_b\log H(T,ib)\): for example, a signed combination at
several heights, an integral over a specified height range, or a
quantitative relation to the real-axis event jets. State the exact
inequality to be estimated and how it feeds the primary comparison or
another named physical consumer.

The atomic control fails the theta floor at sufficiently large \(b\),
but Euler-factor controls pass the existing lower floor at every
applicable \(b\). Therefore a larger collection of those lower floors
does not by itself distinguish theta. Together with one normalization,
exact analytic knowledge of the profile determines the function; that
uniqueness statement alone is also insufficient. A continuation needs a
quantitative comparison with its conditioning loss, parameter dependence
and complete error payment.

**Deliverable:** a new profile-to-jet restriction with explicit constants
and a named consumer, or a scoped obstruction for the selected profile
observation. Record which controls share the observation and which
specific premise they fail. Do not infer the sign of a control's derived
inequality solely from its failure of a literal theta identity.

### BW2 04 Transfer an estimate to a physical conclusion

Activate after BW2-02 or BW2-03 supplies a usable estimate, or after
BW2-06 supplies a genuine later-witness implication. Pay all retained
source, correction, tail, differentiation, interpolation, cutoff,
normalization and parameter errors needed by the chosen consumer.
Distinguish the raw-jet sign, critical-value pair and full-boundary
degree as different outputs.

For the raw double-event test, physical derivatives through order four
enter \(Q_L\); event uncertainty can require moment bounds through order
six. The finite critical-value transfer also uses derivatives through
order six. Shrinking a domain does not remove fixed absolute acquisition
errors, which grow after division by a small event jet or radius.

**Deliverable:** a restricted theta event exclusion, another precisely
stated physical restriction, or a complete ledger identifying the
remaining transfer loss. Record whether the domain or restriction adds
to existing coverage. A boundary certificate that reproduces an already
known box needs a changed application or a predeclared, matched total-cost
gain to justify a new campaign.

### BW2 05 Extend the actual signed estimate to higher multiplicity

Activate when a specific signed estimate or proved exclusion is
available. Choose between direct use of the original physical field
and differentiation according to the estimate's hypotheses.

For the direct option, use the existing all-multiplicity invariant and
BW-06 geometry; audit the new signed information rather than re-prove
the index law. For the derivative option, test the actual estimate on
the positive cosine or sine source weighted by \(u^{m-2}\). BW-07
preserves the threshold and necessary bounds, but does not preserve
every possible arithmetic hypothesis.

**Deliverable:** closure of the specified estimate on an explicit
multiplicity class, or a precise failed transfer premise. The existing
physical remainder requires derivatives through \(m+2\); the
differentiated critical-value transfer requires derivatives through
\(m+4\). Neither requirement alone covers the new estimate's own
derivative needs. A contradiction for an admissible derivative field
can exclude the original assumed event without identifying their
winding numbers; a claim about the original degree requires its own
physical argument.

### BW2 06 Reopen a later-witness route only with a nonlocal relation

Keep BW-02–05 available, but activate one only after selecting a
same-function arithmetic implication that reaches outside the paid
forward neighborhood. Specify \(t_1=T+\Delta\), \(\Delta>0\), or a
later domain whose closure lies strictly above \(T\), with the witness
location outside the relevant local exclusion. The separation may depend
on the event.

**Deliverable:** a conditional relation producing a nonreal zero, later
real multiple zero, negative physical degree, or negative real Laguerre
expression for the unchanged \(H\), with complete errors and a proved
positive time separation. These are alternative witnesses. A changed
function's larger threshold, a wider search box, or a failed sufficient
positivity test supplies none of them.

### BW2 07 Review the result and choose the next estimating step

Review each completed mathematical attempt, including scoped failures.
Check the source versions, new quantitative input, control premises,
complete error transfer, exact conclusion and remaining quantifiers.
Label self-review, same-model or cross-model LLM review, independent
implementation and specialist review accurately.

Close the round with a linked estimate, physical conclusion, precise
obstruction or unresolved estimating step. Update the active register
and affected route README when evidence changes. Stop expansion whose
only gain is another representation, control count, coefficient table
or smaller error that is not limiting the selected consumer.

## Scope and record ownership

A restricted double-event result leaves other multiplicities untreated.
An all-multiplicity necessary condition does not establish their
exclusion. Global completion must address arbitrarily small positive
times, unbounded possible event heights, simultaneous events and
admissible boundaries. Event-dependent positive radii are sufficient for
an analytic local implication; a finite numerical cover requires its
own coverage argument.

The sibling threshold-sign investigation owns direct higher-jet sign
derivations; BW-01 owns their boundary or critical-value application.
BW-08 owns the imaginary-axis comparison, and Project 11 retains its
arithmetic approximation contracts. Keep each derivation in one
canonical location and link it from the other consumers.

Save derivations in the responsible investigation's notes, computations
and small records in numerics, and assessments in reviews. Update only
[the active register][progress] for new round status; preserve the
first-round evidence. Use concise DRAFT_HISTORY entries for milestones
and no manuscript snapshots. This chat retains its unique
[boundary-winding registration][history-home]; follow the
[history workflow][history] and [large-file policy][large].
