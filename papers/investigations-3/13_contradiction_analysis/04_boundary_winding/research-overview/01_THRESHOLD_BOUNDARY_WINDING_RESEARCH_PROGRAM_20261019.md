## Hypothetical witness and scope

Write \(H_t=-H_{xx}\), where the subscript t here denotes differentiation,
and \(J=(H,H_x)\). Failure of RH gives \(T=\Lambda>0\) and a finite positive
real multiple zero a of the threshold slice. The first investigation adds
\(H_{xx}(T,a)\ne0\); this restriction is not guaranteed by threshold
existence. All zeros at T are real, and those at times greater than T are
real and simple. T and a are unknown parameters, not supplied numerical
events. The [predecessor][predecessor] gives the finite-event reduction,
using uniform positive-time confinement from [Polymath][polymath].

There is one threshold for the fixed function. A nonreal zero at t1>T
would imply Lambda>t1>T=Lambda. A real multiple zero at t1>T would imply
Lambda>=t1>T by local splitting. Any strictly positive gap suffices, even
if event-dependent. A larger upper bound or a changed function's larger
threshold does not give a contradiction.

The global exclusion of all possible positive threshold witnesses is
RH-strength. A result restricted to double events, a height/time range,
or a quantified subclass is a partial milestone. Follow [target
classification][a01]; do not count a reformulation or finite control replay
as evidence for an RH-equivalent conclusion.

## Two winding contradictions

For an isolating region D straddling an ordinary threshold event, the
event hypothesis gives W(D)=-1. The proposed arithmetic conclusion is
W(D)=0, or W(D)>-1, with a nonzero boundary jet. For a region D_+ whose
closure lies strictly after T, the threshold gives W(D_+)=0. The proposed
arithmetic conclusion is W(D_+)<0. Both are missing estimates.

Boundary nonvanishing alone does not give zero winding. The physical
spacetime degree of H+iH_x is also different from the holomorphic
argument-principle count of H on a complex contour at a fixed time.
The later-real-event and negative-Laguerre routes are alternative witness
interfaces; their outputs must refer to the same H and the same time scale.

## Shared tasks and acceptance rules

**BW-C01 — Freeze one event-relative contract.** Specify the parameter
class for T, a and any nonzero jet; the unchanged physical source; source
versions; normalizer and orientation; domain geometry; frozen cutoffs and
crossings; required derivative orders; and complete error norms. Identify
which threshold fact and which genuine arithmetic observation could
produce a new estimate. An invented event location or an assumption that
already supplies the desired winding does not pass.

**BW-C02 — Audit the controls and the conditional implication.** Before
substantial estimation, state which premise each relevant control shares
and fails. Use a control's own source and errors. For a numerical consumer,
test known event and event-free domains, boundary zeros, uncertain signs,
orientation changes and incomplete coverage. A merely local event control
is not automatically an all-real-threshold control. Existing analytical
screens remain screens; no numerical event is invented for an existence
construction.

**BW-C03 — Pay the transfer to a physical witness.** For every candidate,
include all retained-mode, correction, tail, derivative, interpolation,
cutoff, normalization and parameter errors actually needed. Preserve
correlations and signed combinations before independent norm payments.
Strict inequalities must survive the complete payment. The local
boundary method covers every boundary segment; a later-witness method
also proves its strictly positive time separation. Numerical sampling
alone cannot establish winding or existence.

**BW-C04 — Review and compare the claimed gain.** Review the exact theorem
or certificate, source version and remaining hypotheses. Internal LLM
checks are not specialist review. If the claim is computational advantage,
freeze a meaningful cost metric before running it and give the direct
baseline the same reusable data, propagation and optimizations. Include
source acquisition in total cost. A new analytic restriction can be useful
without a speed claim. Do not repeat the closed GEO-T1 box comparison
without changed information, domain or acquisition costs.

**BW-C05 — Audit multiplicity and global coverage.** Track untreated
multiplicities, arbitrarily small positive T, unbounded possible heights,
simultaneous events, admissible boundary placement and all approximation
domains. A conditional theorem valid for each hypothetical event need not
have a uniform numerical radius or time gap; it must nevertheless apply
to every event in its claimed class. A finite collection of certificates
does not supply the missing universal quantifiers. No simplicity claim
about the time-zero zeta zeros is required.

Each attempt should end with a linked, checkable estimate, a complete
restricted physical conclusion, or a precise obstruction to the selected
method. An unsuccessful method may be closed in its stated scope under
[the scoped-conclusion rule][a07]; it does not satisfy a dependency requiring a useful estimate.
Stop expansions whose only output is another identity, finer plots, a
larger assertion count, or a smaller irrelevant error.

## Local obstructions and multiplicity discipline

At a double event, the immediate future roots are real and simple, and
\(\mathcal L(T+s,a)=sH_{xx}(T,a)^2+O(s^2)>0\). This rules out obtaining
the required later negative scalar witness from that same-point leading
Taylor term. It does not analyze every nonlocal arithmetic implication.

Differentiation can turn a higher-order event into a double event while
changing the arithmetic source. Deflation of one slice and generic
perturbation also require their own admissibility arguments. A recursive
proof must preserve the double-event theorem's actual hypotheses and
decrease a well-founded measure. Multiplicities two and three both have
index -1, so the winding magnitude alone is not such a multiplicity
measure. A direct estimate avoiding nondegeneracy may instead cover all
finite m through the already-known index law.
