## Scalar witness and contradiction

For \(t>T=\Lambda\), the order-one all-real canonical product of H implies
\[
\mathcal L(t,x)=H_x^2-HH_{xx}\ge0\qquad(x\in\mathbb R).
\]
Seek a conditional arithmetic bound forcing \(\mathcal L(t_1,x_1)<0\) for some \(t_1>T\). This would imply a nonreal zero and give the contradiction in [BW-02](../../02_later_nonreal_zeros/README.md). Negativity is sufficient; it is not claimed necessary for every nonreal-zero configuration. This future-time expression is distinct from the deflated threshold inequality in [BW-08](../../08_deflated_jet_hierarchy/README.md).

## Complete scalar error

At the chosen point suppose \(|\partial_x^jH-f_j|\le e_j\), j=0,1,2, with complete errors. Then the elementary product enclosure is
\[
|\mathcal L-(f_1^2-f_0f_2)|\le
2|f_1|e_1+e_1^2+|f_0|e_2+|f_2|e_0+e_0e_2=:E_L.
\]
The target is \(f_1^2-f_0f_2+E_L<0\). Correlated enclosures may improve this payment, but the formula alone is not an arithmetic gain. If Q=H/A is used, retain
\(\mathcal L/A^2=Q_x^2-QQ_{xx}-(\partial_x^2\log A)Q^2\), including the normalizer's uncertainty.

\[
