## Region and contradiction

Assume the double threshold event at T. Choose a bounded Jordan region \(D_+\) with \(\overline{D_+}\subset\{t>T\}\). The threshold property and local splitting imply that every later real zero is simple, so the physical jet is nonzero throughout the closed region and \(W(D_+)=0\).

Seek an independent conditional arithmetic estimate forcing \(W(D_+)\le-1\). The index theorem then forces a later event, giving [BW-03](../../03_later_real_collisions/README.md). A negative-winding target here is distinct from the zero-winding target for an enclosure that straddles T in [BW-01](../../01_local_double_winding/README.md).

## Boundary estimate and error transfer

A polygon or reference boundary curve of negative degree can be transferred to J if the full boundary homotopy avoids the origin. The standard strict condition is \(\|J-\widetilde J\|<\|\widetilde J\|\) everywhere on the boundary. An alternative is a rigorous upper bound less than zero on the total phase integral; integer winding then forces at most -1.

Signed contributions from time faces and spatial walls must be retained together where they cancel. Sampling or principal-value angles without a consistent continuous lift can miscount a full turn. A normalized jet requires a proved orientation and degree transfer. All corners, interpolation errors, source tails, cutoff crossings and parameter dependence are part of the contract.
