## Event and contradiction

Assume \(T=\Lambda>0\), \(a>0\), \(H(T,a)=H_x(T,a)=0\), and \(h_2=H_{xx}(T,a)\ne0\). All zeros of \(H_T\) are real. A sufficiently small isolating Jordan region D contains this event and has nonzero physical jet \(J=(H,H_x)\) on its boundary. With orientation \(dt\wedge dx\), the inherited index gives \(W(D)=-1\). The missing arithmetic conclusion is \(W(D)=0\), or the strict lower bound \(W(D)>-1\).

Time faces must straddle T. A boundary through the event has undefined winding. The analytic existence of an isolating neighborhood is an inherited fact; its usable geometry and boundary errors still need to be obtained from the selected data.

## Three methods to assess

1. **Boundary homotopy.** Find a boundary approximation \(\widetilde J\) with known zero winding and complete error \(\|J-\widetilde J\|<\|\widetilde J\|\) on every segment. Strict half-plane enclosures can support an exact polygon certificate without resolving the interior.
2. **Paired phase estimates.** Bound the complete signed integral of \((H\,dH_x-H_x\,dH)/(H^2+H_x^2)\) from below by more than \(-2\pi\). Retain cancellations between faces and arithmetic blocks before taking absolute bounds. Direct integration requires its own derivative and denominator bounds; a homotopy need not use that integral numerically.
3. **Root-count comparison.** With simple roots on the time faces and H nonzero on spatial walls, \(W=(N_- -N_+)/2\). Equal counts would exclude an event, but cannot be assumed. Moving walls or root crossings require the full [wall-flux formulation][walls].

Each method uses a different observation of the same physical boundary. Boundary nonvanishing by itself does not determine zero winding. Normalized or rotating coordinates require an explicit degree/orientation transfer.
