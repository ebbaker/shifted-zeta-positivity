\(F=2\pi i(q\,dp\wedge dq-dr\wedge ds)\). Its pullback along
\(\gamma(u)=(ie^{4u},0)\) vanishes, as does the natural external product
on two such paths. Transverse differentiation before restriction can
retain lower-jet commutators; tangential pullback creates no curvature term.

assuming an automatic compact completion.
