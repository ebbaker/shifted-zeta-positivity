## Global obligations that must remain visible

- **Height dependence:** no fixed positive source-distance tolerance or
  low-window phase constant controls every height. Local time widths and
  collars may depend on height, but every dependency needs a coverage
  argument before ECE-04 closes.
- **Negative caps:** the goal is local signed information, not a common
  negative-time slice with all theta zeros real. The latter is ruled out
  by the threshold property and \(\Lambda\ge0\); the overview links the
  primary source and explains the distinction.
- **Higher multiplicities:** the other roots of a birth cluster cannot
  be discarded into a bounded exterior coefficient. [BW10 Note 19,
  RM17][bw19] records the singular terms for a selected pair at an
  \(m>2\) endpoint.
- **Endpoint identification:** charge one in a time window straddling
  zero may come from a negative-time event. Any exclusion based on
  consuming the single allowed event needs its location strictly below
  zero.
- **Finite versus infinite time:** a gap can be positive for every
  finite positive time and tend to zero as time tends to infinity.
  [BW10 Note 20][bw20] does not supply a second finite collision.
- **Weighted assembly:** the endpoint charges are positive integers,
  but their contributions to \(K-Z\) have height-dependent weights.
  Do not replace local coverage by an arbitrary fixed positive bound
  on that weighted sum.
