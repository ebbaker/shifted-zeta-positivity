## Event and contradiction

Start with the ordinary double threshold assumptions of [BW-01](../../01_local_double_winding/README.md). Seek \(t_1>T\) and \(z_1\notin\mathbb R\) such that \(H(t_1,z_1)=0\) for the unchanged theta flow. The threshold definition would then require \(\Lambda>t_1>T=\Lambda\). Any strictly positive time gap suffices; it may depend on the hypothetical event.

The new work is the implication from the assumed arithmetic cancellation to this later witness. An improved upper bound on Lambda, a zero of a truncated approximant, or a changed source's larger threshold does not meet it. No actual numerical value for T or event location has been supplied by the hypothesis.

## Possible existence interface

One option is an argument-principle certificate on a closed complex contour C at t1. Its interior must lie wholly off the real axis, H must be nonzero on C, and the certified zero count must be positive. Since H is entire, no pole subtraction is required. A Rouché comparison can transfer a known positive zero count when the complete boundary error is strictly smaller than the reference function's modulus.

This contour is in the complex z-plane at one time. It is different from physical spacetime winding of H+iH_x. Real-axis endpoint errors do not automatically control a complex contour; the source contract must justify the needed continuation and full contour error. Locating a small complex value is not an existence proof.

## First bounded task and acceptance

Select an arithmetic relation that genuinely connects the assumed threshold cancellation to later-time values. State its dependence on T, a, h2, and any other jets. Determine whether it supplies a signed complex observation or only an absolute upper bound. A first useful result is an additional conditional constraint on that observation, with a paid transfer to H; it need not yet force a zero.

If a numerical diagnostic is justified, predeclare the parameter class, t1-T>0, contour separation from the real axis, approximant, full errors, and falsifying outcome. A reference control with a certified nonreal zero before its own threshold can test the contour interface. It is not evidence for the proposed later-zero implication.

## Controls and scope

The quartic threshold model has no nonreal zeros after its threshold, so a mechanism based only on heat flow, evenness and all-real threshold data fails this screen. Positive-kernel threshold controls give a stronger generic-source screen; they lack canonical theta arithmetic. For weighted-square or finite-prime controls, do not invent an all-real threshold not established by their source audit.

The local pair born at the assumed double event is real for sufficiently small forward time by [Polymath's splitting theorem][polymath]. A later witness cannot be inferred from that expansion alone. Preserve this obstruction's local scope: it does not evaluate an as-yet-unproved global arithmetic implication.
