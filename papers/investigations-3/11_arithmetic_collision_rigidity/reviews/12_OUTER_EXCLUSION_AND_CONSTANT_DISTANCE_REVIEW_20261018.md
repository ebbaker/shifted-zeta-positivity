  their common derivative error allowances are respectively 1.1e-9 G0
  and 7e-11 G0 for x>=1000, 0<=t<=1/2, |Im z|<=1, orders 0–2.

  and its stated error payments: more than 10^433 retained modes at
  exp(2000), growing source cutoff, and failure of the fixed-degree
  G0-relative Taylor payment against its fixed 1e-8 allowance. The last
  conclusion is not a failure theorem for a growing physical-envelope
  margin or another quadrature/assembly method.
* The remote polynomial satisfies the exact heat equation, reflection,
  contracted strip, imaginary-axis positivity, complete finite wall and
  E exclusion, and the inherited all-real time 1/5. Its exact threshold
  bracket lies near 0.2 and above 0.01. For L=2000 all its roots stay
  inside modulus 3000 through t=1/2, so it also satisfies the new exterior
  conclusion while colliding in the open bridge. Its exact D1 signed
  inequality fails. It is a control for the named weaker premises and
  does not share the normalized theta-source contract. The variable-L
  threshold limit does not silently impose a fixed uniform outer theorem
