\begin{abstract}
We develop finite Euler operations and complete arithmetic packets for
the backwards heat evolution of the Riemann $\xi$ function.
For $0\le t\le1/2$, every nonzero bounded nonnegative packet of source
modes beginning at $m\ge2$ has positive real part in $|\Im z|\le\pi m^2$.
Its logarithmic derivatives satisfy
$\beta^2+|\beta_x|\le1/(4m^4)$; the carrier containing the second mode
therefore contributes at most $E/64$ to the normalized Laguerre identity,
where $E=H^2+H_x^2$. The packet selected by primes $2,3$ approximates
the positive higher-mode contribution with relative error below
$7.3\times10^{-25}$, but full modular reflection cancels all its
algebraic tails with the first source mode.
We preserve that cancellation by shifting the complete reflected source
before truncation. Nonstationary phase gives effective omitted-mode
derivative bounds; a contour at distance $A_c/x$ from the source-strip
boundary has error $O(x^{-1/4})$ relative to a positive gamma envelope.
Complete interval computations certify signed rectangles and a complex
wall at $\Re z=1000$. With published initial zero verification and a
finite heat-propagation lemma, these imply $E>0$ on
$[1/100,1/5]\times[-1001,1001]$.
Using the published effective Riemann--Siegel approximation, we also prove
$E>0$ for $\varepsilon\le t\le1/2$ and $|x|\ge e^{20/\varepsilon}$.
Finite-prime and remote polynomial controls delimit the premises of these
arguments. We also summarize the symmetry program's complete arithmetic
residual estimate, uniform signed averages, and scoped geometric comparisons,
with links to their detailed research notes.
The bridge $1001<|x|<e^{2000}$ remains open on the stated slab.
No global collision exclusion or new Newman bound is obtained.
\end{abstract}
