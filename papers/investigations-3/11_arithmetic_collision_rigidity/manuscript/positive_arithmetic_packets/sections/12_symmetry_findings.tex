\subsection{Complete arithmetic control of the rotating residual}

Use the symmetric normalizer $\mathcal A_t$ and phase $\theta$ of the
Riemann--Siegel approximation, rather than the positive carrier
$A_{\mathcal P}$. Write $Q=H/\mathcal A_t$ and $\omega=\theta_x$.
On the pilot
\[
 B=[1/100,1/5]\times[999,1001],
\]
S03 takes the coherent corrected approximation
$f=(G_8-\widehat C_8)/\mathcal A_t$ and its complete residual $r=Q-f$.
The rotating first-jet coordinate and its increment are
\[
 Z_r=e^{-i\theta}(r-i r_x/\omega),\qquad
 I_r(a,x)=i\{Z_r(x)-Z_r(a)\}.
\]
Differentiating gives the exact filtered-residual identity
\[
 I_r(a,x)=\int_a^x e^{-i\theta}\omega
 \left(r+\frac{r_{xx}}{\omega^2}
             -\frac{\omega_x r_x}{\omega^3}\right)\dd y.
\]
The research gain is a complete bound for this increment, beyond the
identity itself.

The prescribed finite gamma and heat corrections in
\symdoc{03_normalized_jet_rotation/notes/06_ARITHMETIC_GAMMA_AND_EDGE_CORRECTIONS_20261019.md}
{S03 Note 06} identify omitted Stirling and Gaussian-curvature terms.
\symdoc{03_normalized_jet_rotation/notes/07_COMPLETE_ARITHMETIC_EDGE_AND_RESIDUAL_INCREMENT_20261019.md}
{S03 Note 07} adds the complete Riemann--Siegel edge, gamma prefactor,
Gaussian tails and analytic-continuation payment. It recombines the signed
finite and edge corrections before estimating the increment. With fixed
cutoff $N=8$, the resulting bound is
\[
 |I_r(999,x)|<0.000854\qquad ((t,x)\in B).
\]
This bound is independent of physical $H$ values, source quadrature and
source Taylor coefficients. It reduces the earlier independent
analytic-only allowance $0.812260$ by more than a factor of $950$.
The source-informed allowance $0.000266448$ remains smaller; these are
bounds with different input information, not measurements of the actual
error or computational speed.

The complete-consumer comparison is separate.
\symdoc{03_normalized_jet_rotation/notes/05_CALIBRATED_FIRST_JET_AND_MATCHED_DIRECT_COMPARISON_20261019.md}
{S03 Note 05} obtains a calibrated first-jet radius greater than
$1.3859351576$, while the direct method with the same source information
and matched propagation gives a radius greater than $1.3898732635$.
Thus the independent arithmetic estimate is a useful local estimating
result; a stronger or cheaper complete physical test is still needed.

\subsection{Uniform scaling, signed averages and local transfer}

S04 retains the full arithmetic phases and heat weights in the sector
\[
 0<t\le1/20,\qquad 1\le\kappa\le2,\qquad
 L=\kappa/t,\qquad x=4\pi e^L.
\]
The \symdoc{04_time_height_scaling/notes/01_PHASE_RETAINING_FULL_CUTOFF_SCALING_20261018.md}
{full-cutoff scaling theorem} pays a uniform model and first-jet error
through the growing cutoff. In
\symdoc{04_time_height_scaling/notes/02_GAUSSIAN_SIGNED_CORE_AND_COMPLETE_AVERAGED_VALUE_20261019.md}
{S04 Note 02}, set $Y=8\sqrt L$ and
$\mu(y)=e^{-y^2/64}/(8\sqrt\pi)$. The complete phase-matched average
\[
 \mathcal B(t,x)=\int_{-Y}^{Y}\mu(y)e^{-i\theta(t,x+y)}
                  Q(t,x+y)\dd y
\]
satisfies $|\mathcal B-1|<0.11$ throughout the sector. The signed
intermediate-core contribution is below $1.5\,10^{-6}$ after its stated
window and model payments. Consequently the weighted $(Q/2)^2$ energy
exceeds $0.198$. This is a uniform averaged theorem; an isolated common
zero of $Q,Q_x$ is compatible with it.

\symdoc{04_time_height_scaling/notes/03_UNIFORM_LOCAL_RESIDUAL_TRANSFER_AND_CUTOFF_PAYMENT_OBSTRUCTION_20261019.md}
{S04 Note 03} establishes complete local residual charts of real radius
$1/(2L)$, with increment below $46.679e^{-b_*/t}$, where
$b_*=\kappa(\kappa+4)/16$. The chart residual is
$Q-F_{t,N}$, with its fixed cutoff and analytic disk, and differs from
the corrected residual used in S03. Across natural cutoff changes, the
specified independent coefficientwise disk rule incurs an assigned budget
greater than $.99e^{1/(4t)}$. This is an obstruction to that payment rule,
not a lower bound for actual signed jumps.
The corrected combination in Theorem~\ref{thm:coherent-edge} now gives
summable transitions, and \eqref{eq:uar-physical-box} supplies one paid
anchor box. Uniform physical separation across the sector remains open.
The sector begins near $x=6.097\,10^9$ and does not cover the full bridge
of Section~\ref{sec:global}.

\subsection{The next estimating problem}

The \symdoc{research-overview/03_UNIFORM_ARITHMETIC_RESIDUAL_RESEARCH_PROGRAM_20261019.md}
{uniform-residual program} and its
\symdoc{research-progress/02_UNIFORM_ARITHMETIC_RESIDUAL_PROGRESS_20261019.md}
{progress register} record UAR-01--06 as complete at their stated gates.
The coherent edge and complete residual estimates are analytic results;
the physical result is confined to one small box, and the matched
transport comparison loses to direct certification.
The next estimating problem is control of the retained arithmetic first
jet on a useful domain, or a cheaper complete acquisition.
A geometric continuation must supply a useful new observation estimate
or acquisition advantage. The explicit and qualitative extensions in
Section~\ref{sec:uniform-edge} do not themselves establish new physical
coverage or an improved Newman bound.
