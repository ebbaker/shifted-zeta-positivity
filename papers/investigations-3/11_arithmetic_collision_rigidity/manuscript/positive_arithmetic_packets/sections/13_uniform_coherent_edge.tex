\section{Uniform coherent edge estimates and analytic extensions}
\label{sec:uniform-edge}

The arithmetic cutoff changes as height increases. Its entering finite
mode is large compared with the desired approximation error, but the
corresponding change of the Riemann--Siegel edge cancels it. The central
analytic advance of UAR-03 is to preserve this cancellation in a single
holomorphic model, with a uniform error through every cutoff change.
UAR-04 then bounds the complete signed residual. This section states
these results and two extensions; the linked notes contain the longer
contour derivations and reproducible numerical records.

\subsection{A common edge model through cutoff changes}

Use the complete theta source and the symmetric normalizer
$\mathcal A_t$, phase $\theta$ and $\omega=\theta_x$ of
Section~\ref{sec:symmetry-findings}. The precise analytic definitions,
branches and removable correction profiles are fixed in the
\symdoc{notes/07_UAR01_CORRECTED_RESIDUAL_AND_TRANSITION_CONTRACT_20261019.md}
{UAR-01 contract}. In particular,
\[
 f_N=\frac{G_N-\widehat C_N^{\rm an}}{\mathcal A_t},
 \qquad r_N=\frac{H}{\mathcal A_t}-f_N,\qquad
 Z_v=e^{-i\theta}(v-i v_x/\omega).
\]
The prescribed finite gamma/heat correction is retained in the model
for $r_N$, rather than silently added to $f_N$.

Initially fix
\begin{equation}\label{eq:uar-sector}
 0<t\le1/20,\qquad
 J_t=[4\pi e^{1/t},4\pi e^{2/t}],\qquad
 L_c=\log(c/(4\pi)),\quad c\in J_t.
\end{equation}
At each center use the disk $D_c=\{|z-c|\le1/L_c\}$ and real
half-window $W_c=[c-1/(2L_c),c+1/(2L_c)]$.
Freeze either extremal value of
$N(x)=\lfloor\sqrt{x/(4\pi)+t/16}\rfloor$ on the disk's real
projection. Differentiation keeps $t,N$ fixed.

The exact edge uses
$M(s)=s(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)/16$ and the descending
Riemann--Siegel contour; see \cite[\S4]{Polymath}.
For $q=N+1/2$, put $w_q(r)=q+(1+i)r$ and define its finite contour
\[
 J_N^S(s)=(1+i)\int_S^{-S}
 \frac{w_q(r)^{-s}e^{i\pi w_q(r)^2}}
 {e^{i\pi w_q(r)}-e^{-i\pi w_q(r)}}\dd r .
\]
The principal logarithm along this contour is continuous. With
$V_t=S_t=2\sqrt{2/t}$ and
$h(v,z)=1/2+\sqrt t\,v+i(z/2+\pi t/8)$, retain
\begin{equation}\label{eq:compact-edge-model}
 B_N^\Box(t,z)=\frac{e^{t\pi^2/64}}{\sqrt\pi}
 \int_{-V_t}^{V_t}e^{-v^2-i\pi\sqrt t\,v/4}
 M(h(v,z))J_N^{S_t}(h(v,z))\dd v .
\end{equation}
For $B^\sharp(z)=\overline{B(\overline z)}$, set
\[
 \widehat e_{{\rm edge},N}
 =\frac{B_N^\Box+(B_N^\Box)^\sharp+\widehat C_N^{\rm an}}
 {\mathcal A_t}.
\]
This retains the true gamma factor, heat phase and reflected channel.
The limits are the same at every center for fixed $t$; models with the
same integer therefore agree exactly on overlaps. The finite integral
is an exact analytic definition. Numerical evaluation of it would
require a separate quadrature and rounding budget.

\begin{theorem}[Coherent edge error and summable transitions]
\label{thm:coherent-edge}
In \eqref{eq:uar-sector}, put
\[
 \mathcal Z_c=e^{-L_c/4-tL_c^2/16},\qquad
 \epsilon_c=1000\mathcal Z_c e^{-3.99L_c}.
\]
If $e_{{\rm edge},N}$ is the complete exact normalized edge discrepancy,
then for either frozen cutoff,
\[
 \sup_{D_c}|e_{{\rm edge},N}-\widehat e_{{\rm edge},N}|
 <\epsilon_c .
\]
Writing the difference as $d_N$, throughout $W_c$ one has
\[
 |(d_N)_x|<2L_c\epsilon_c,\qquad |Z_{d_N}|<9\epsilon_c.
\]
The sector ceilings are $\epsilon_c<4.3\,10^{-35}$ and
$9\epsilon_c<3.9\,10^{-34}$.

At a crossing $\xi_n=4\pi(n^2-t/16)$, let $h_n=f_n-f_{n-1}$.
On its common disk and at its center respectively,
\[
 |h_n|<8\mathcal Z_{\xi_n}/\xi_n,\qquad
 |Z_{h_n}(\xi_n)|<3\mathcal Z_{\xi_n}/n^2.
\]
Consequently every physical prefix satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:uar-transition-prefix}
 \sum_{4\pi e^{1/t}<\xi_n\le x}|Z_{h_n}(\xi_n)|
 <3.01e^{-13/(16t)}<2.639\,10^{-7}.
\end{equation}
The same result includes the specified left and right endpoint descriptions.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof outline]
The full argument is
\symdoc{03_normalized_jet_rotation/notes/09_UNIFORM_COHERENT_EDGE_AND_CUTOFF_MATCHING_20261019.md}
{S03 Note 09, UAR-03}. A global contour majorant and a gamma
reflection estimate control the infinite heat tails. The complement
of the retained rectangle is split into those tails with the entire
contour, and the contour tails with the central heat interval.
Both reflected channels are divided by the actual complex normalizer.
Cauchy's estimate is then applied to the combined holomorphic error.

The exact residue identity
$\mathcal E_n-\mathcal E_{n-1}=-m_n^{\rm heat}$ cancels the entering
exact heat mode before taking a norm. At the model level its complete
error is at most $2\epsilon_c$. For the original leading approximation,
the analytic correction jump is a single explicit wave. Matching that
wave, including the printed-to-analytic conversion, gives the stated
$O(\mathcal Z_c/c)$ bound. Coupled real Schwarz--Pick estimates pay
its first jet. Finally $n>e^{1/(2t)}$ and
$\sum_{n>u}n^{-2}\le1/(u-1)$ prove
\eqref{eq:uar-transition-prefix}.
\end{proof}

The model-error estimate and the corrected-transition estimate have
different roles. The exceptionally small former does not bound the
value of the retained model. The latter is summable without assuming
cancellation between different crossings. This overcomes the raw-mode
payment discussed in Section~\ref{sec:symmetry-findings} within its
specified class.

\subsection{The complete residual and its starting height}

Define $\mathcal I_t(x)$ as the ordinary piecewise integral of the
filtered residual from $a_t=4\pi e^{1/t}$ to $x$, using the natural
frozen integer on each subinterval. Since $r_n-r_{n-1}=-h_n$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:uar-telescoping}
 \mathcal I_t(x)=i\left\{
 Z_{r_{N(x)}}(x)-Z_{r_{N(a_t)}}(a_t)
 +\sum_{a_t<\xi_n\le x}Z_{h_n}(\xi_n)\right\}.
\end{equation}
The positive switch sum is part of this ordinary integral.

\begin{theorem}[Complete uniform arithmetic residual]
\label{thm:uar-complete}
On \eqref{eq:uar-sector},
\[
 \sup_{x\in J_t}|\mathcal I_t(x)|
 <3.246e^{-13/(16t)}<2.846\,10^{-7}.
\]
\end{theorem}

The proof is
\symdoc{03_normalized_jet_rotation/notes/10_UNIFORM_SIGNED_RESIDUAL_INCREMENT_20261019.md}
{S03 Note 10, UAR-04}. Its signed saddle estimate cancels the leading
correction and retains the signed Gaussian first moment. The two
finite-mode rotating weights are nonnegative and sum to two.
In the scale
\[
 \mathcal S_x=e^{-3L_x/4-tL_x^2/16},
 \qquad L_x=\log(x/(4\pi)),
\]
the retained edge and finite correction cost less than
$0.97\mathcal S_x$ and $0.558\mathcal S_x$.
The finite remainder from
\symdoc{03_normalized_jet_rotation/notes/08_UNIFORM_FINITE_GAMMA_HEAT_REMAINDER_20261019.md}
{UAR-02}, the edge remainder and every transition are included.
The estimate uses no physical $H$ calibration or retained contour
quadrature. Outward scalar checks support its explicit constants;
the written argument supplies its uniformity.

\begin{corollary}[Residual bound between arbitrary endpoints]
\label{cor:uar-rebased}
For $u,v\in J_t$, let $a=\min(u,v)$ and let $I_t(u,v)$ be the
ordinary piecewise residual integral between them. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:uar-rebased}
 |I_t(u,v)|<3.246\mathcal S_a
 =3.246\exp\left\{-\frac{\kappa_a^2+12\kappa_a}{16t}\right\},
 \qquad \kappa_a=t\log(a/(4\pi)).
\end{equation}
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
For $a\le b$, set $U_x=\sqrt{x/(4\pi)+t/16}$.
The decreasing-envelope argument of UAR-04, now begun at $a$, gives
\[
 \sum_{a<\xi_n\le b}|Z_{h_n}(\xi_n)|
 \le\frac3{U_a}\mathcal S_a+
 \frac{12}{\kappa_a+6}(\mathcal S_a-\mathcal S_b).
\]
The retained residual endpoints cost less than
$1.53(\mathcal S_a+\mathcal S_b)$. Since
$12/(\kappa_a+6)\le12/7$ and $3/U_a<.000137$,
their total is below $3.245\mathcal S_a$.
Both remaining endpoint errors fit below
$(4.2\,10^{-9}+1.8\,10^{-26})\mathcal S_a$.
Reversing the endpoints changes the integral's sign.
The complete derivation, including crossing ownership, is
\symdoc{03_normalized_jet_rotation/notes/13_REBASED_RESIDUAL_AND_COMPACT_SECTOR_EDGE_EXTENSIONS_20261019.md}
{S03 Note 13, Proposition 1}.
\end{proof}

Thus a generic $2\sup|\mathcal I_t|$ rebasing payment can be replaced
by an estimate at the actual starting height. At $t=.05$, a subinterval
beginning at $4\pi e^{30}$ has the bound $3.3\,10^{-11}$.
This improves residual control within the existing sector; a physical
anchor and complete signed approximant variation are still needed.

\subsection{Extension to other compact scaling bands}

The common contour construction extends beyond the particular
$1\le tL\le2$ band. The next statement is qualitative in its new onset
and constants.

\begin{proposition}[Compact-band edge extension]
\label{prop:compact-band-edge}
Fix $0<\kappa_-\le\kappa_+<\infty$, $p>0$ and $0<\eta<p$.
There are $t_0,C>0$, depending on these fixed parameters, such that
for $0<t\le t_0$ and $\kappa_-\le tL_c\le\kappa_+$, the model
\eqref{eq:compact-edge-model} with
\[
 V_t=S_t=\sqrt{p\kappa_+/t}
\]
satisfies, for either extremal frozen cutoff,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:compact-band-error}
 \sup_{D_c}|e_{{\rm edge},N}
                 -\widehat e_{{\rm edge},N}^{\,p}|
 \le C\mathcal Z_c e^{-(p-\eta)L_c}.
\end{equation}
Same-cutoff models agree on overlaps, and the adjacent exact-mode
compensation costs at most twice this allowance.
The original corrected transitions obey
\[
 |Z_{h_n}(\xi_n)|\le C_h\,\mathcal Z_{\xi_n}/n^2
\]
for a band-dependent constant. Their every-prefix sum is therefore
\[
 O_{\kappa_-,\kappa_+}\left(
 e^{-(\kappa_-^2+12\kappa_-)/(16t)}\right).
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Proof outline]
See
\symdoc{03_normalized_jet_rotation/notes/13_REBASED_RESIDUAL_AND_COMPACT_SECTOR_EDGE_EXTENSIONS_20261019.md}
{S03 Note 13, Proposition 2}. Uniformly on a fixed positive band,
$L_c\to\infty$, the saddle distance is at most $1/2+o(1)$,
and the gamma-normalizer ratio is $O(\mathcal Z_c)$.
The heat integrand has a majorant
$C_0e^{-(1-o(1))v^2+o(1)|v|}$.
Since $V_t^2\ge pL_c$, its tails have the order in
\eqref{eq:compact-band-error}.
On the retained heat interval the real gamma argument is bounded
by a constant depending on $p,\kappa_+$; the contour tails have
the smaller order $O(e^{-3pL_c})$.
The exact single-wave matching formula retains
$O(\mathcal Z_c/c)$ corrected jumps. The inverse-square sum then
gives the stated prefix order.
\end{proof}

For any fixed derivative order $j$, Cauchy's estimate also gives on
$W_c$ the component-error bound
\[
 |\partial_x^j(e_{{\rm edge},N}
                   -\widehat e_{{\rm edge},N}^{\,p})|
 \le j!(2L_c)^j C\mathcal Z_c e^{-(p-\eta)L_c}.
\]
This extends an edge-error budget; it is not yet a complete higher-jet
physical certificate. Increasing $p$ shrinks omitted tails by enlarging
the retained model, whose value and acquisition cost remain separate.

Proposition~\ref{prop:compact-band-edge} permits $\kappa_-<1$ or
$\kappa_+>2$ after a possible reduction of $t_0$.
The explicit constants $1000$, $3.246$ and the onset $1/20$ have
not been transferred to such a new band. Extending the complete
residual theorem also requires the finite correction and retained
saddle estimates there. A fixed compact band does not cover all heights
at a fixed time; nor does it supply a fixed-width complex strip.

\subsection{The bounded physical certificate and the remaining task}

The analytic estimates have one fully paid physical application.
\symdoc{03_normalized_jet_rotation/notes/11_PAID_ANCHOR_AND_BOUNDED_PHYSICAL_SEPARATION_20261019.md}
{UAR-05} certifies the closed box
\begin{equation}\label{eq:uar-physical-box}
 D=[1/20-10^{-14},1/20]\times[u-.01,u+.01],
 \qquad u=4\pi e^{20}+1.
\end{equation}
The approximant anchor has rotating norm greater than $1.5441778256$;
its complete signed variation costs less than $1.0051$.
After residual and physical-normalizer payments,
\[
 |J_Q|>.539,\qquad E/\mathcal A_t^2>.27
 \quad\hbox{throughout }D.
\]
The acquisition uses all $22026$ modes at the anchor and on the
closed cell. Its interval records and control tests are linked in the note.

The matched comparison in
\symdoc{03_normalized_jet_rotation/notes/12_MATCHED_COMPLETE_CONSUMER_AND_COST_COMPARISON_20261019.md}
{UAR-06} gives direct rotating margin $>.53908399$ and physical
energy ratio $>.27778656$, exceeding the refined local transported
bounds $>.53908372$ and $>.27778628$.
All methods acquire the same $44052$ finite-mode evaluations per
production precision; direct pays fewer errors. That transport
configuration is stopped. Corollary~\ref{cor:uar-rebased} does not
reverse this comparison: this box has no interior transitions and
the existing local two-endpoint payment is stronger.

The direct residual allowance is below $1.341\,10^{-7}$, whereas
the paid approximant variation is about $1.0051$. Improving the
already tiny omitted remainders therefore has little effect on this
particular margin. The next useful estimate concerns separation of
the retained oscillatory first jet, or a cheaper complete acquisition.
The box \eqref{eq:uar-physical-box} is a small additional island of
real noncollision; uniform separation across $J_t$, the bridge and the
required complex exterior remain open.

The compact-band extension concerns the same completed theta source.
Changing an arithmetic level or coefficient family requires the
corresponding contour, functional equation, normalizer and residue
matching to be proved anew. Source-specific collision controls do not
inherit these theta estimates merely because generic rotation
identities apply to them.
