## 1. Frozen domain and what is acquired

Keep the original physical normalization
\(H(0,z)=\xi((1+iz)/2)/8\). For the new direct interface choose
\(A=1\), hence \(F=H\), \(\alpha=\alpha_x=0\). This explicit choice
avoids importing high-height normalizer or approximation assumptions.

The supported rectangle remains
\[
D_0=[1/100,1/5]\times[25,45],\qquad |\Im z|\le1.
\]
Freeze four retained modes, source cutoff \(U=1\), contour shift
\(d=\pi/16\), spatial degree 50 and time degree 12. The matched real-source
method uses shift zero. Both are complete approximations to the same H;
their retained integrals and their tail decompositions differ.

The predeclared actual-source validation box is
\[
D_*=[99999/10^6,100001/10^6]\times
[29999999/10^6,30000001/10^6].
\]
It is centered at \((t,a)=(1/10,30)\). For every point in this closed
box, the residual disk has radius \(R=1/2\). All these disks lie in the
single rectangle with real interval
\([29499999/10^6,30500001/10^6]\) and imaginary interval \([-1/2,1/2]\).
There is no cutoff transition or changing contour parameter on any of
these sets.

The retained cases are three real endpoint/center points; two real cells,
one spanning the full time interval; one complex cell; six complex
endpoint points; and D*. Their exact rational coordinates are in the
record. There are 13 matched cases at each precision, each returning
seven components. This is a validation suite for the justified interface,
not a newly claimed cover by positive margins of the entire D0.

The total added allowance is \(10^{-10}\) per component, with mode,
source, and quadrature allocations \(10^{-12}\) each and Taylor allocation
\(10^{-11}\). The frozen setup cap is 90 seconds per evaluator,
a checked elapsed limit of 300 seconds for the retained run, and a 1 GiB
memory budget. The setup deadline interrupts execution; the whole-run
limit is checked after each precision, not enforced by a second alarm. Per-integral
limits remain 200,000 evaluations and depth 30. The run used under
4 seconds and 34 MB peak resident memory. The operating system rejected
setting a 1 GiB address-space hard limit; the record states that failure
and checks measured peak memory. A hard memory limit is **not** claimed
enforced. Polynomial dimensions and sample schedule stay fixed.

## 2. Derivatives through six and all complete payments

The original `max_component` parameter already enters every required
order, factorial, tail sum and propagated quadrature radius. Set it to
six. Midpoint derivatives now run through
\[
50+2\cdot12+6=80,
\]
and the common derivative remainder bound through
\[
50+2(12+1)+6=82.
\]
Thus every construction encloses 81 midpoint integrals; the shifted
method also encloses two positive majorant integrals. No nominal
order-two error constant is reused without its order-82 admissibility
checks.

For the shifted method, the omitted-mode theorem in the pinned source
already holds for every integer \(j\ge0\):
\[
E^{\rm mode}_j=e^{-dx}S_{m,d}
 \sum_{k=0}^j {j\choose k}d^{j-k}k!/B^{k+1},
\quad m=5,\quad B=4\pi m^2\cos(4d)-9-Y.
\]
Its positivity, time and geometric-ratio hypotheses are checked with
outward values. For the retained source beyond U, use the same positive
majorant as Note 07,
\[
q_n(u;Y)=(2\lambda_n^2+3\lambda_n)
 e^{t_{\max}u^2+(9+Y)u-\cos(4d)\lambda_ne^{4u}},
\quad \lambda_n=\pi n^2.
\]
The tangent denominator is
\[
4\cos(4d)\lambda_ne^{4U}-9-Y-2t_{\max}U-j/(U+d).
\]
It is positive for every returned order zero through six, and the
logarithmic majorant is concave. The moment bound's analogous
checks are performed at \(r=3/4\) for powers zero and 82. Because
\(r+d<1\), the common bound controls every lower derivative too.
All these checks succeed at both precisions. In particular the order-82
real and complex-strip shifted common moments are below 0.004266 and
0.004774 respectively. The corresponding real-source moments are below
0.076338 and 0.207508.

The real-source calculation independently bounds omitted modes using
\(u^j\le1\) on \([0,1]\) and the exact positive mode mass, and bounds
all source modes beyond 1 by the decaying tangent with derivative term
\(j/U=j\). Its source remainder includes the omitted modes there;
there is no missing infinite interval. Source positivity is used only
where it holds in this comparator.

For a physical component j, the common Taylor payment is
\[
M_{82,Y}\left\{R_z^{51}/51!+
 e^{R_z}\Delta_t^{13}/13!\right\}.
\]
Here \(R_z\) is distance from the fixed center 35, not the local residual
disk radius. The complete finite integrals satisfy the heat equation,
so their kth time derivatives are \((-1)^k\) times spatial derivative
\(j+2k\). Quadrature radius \(\rho_{j+\ell+2k}\) is charged with absolute
weight \(R_z^\ell\Delta_t^k/(\ell!k!)\). Midpoint integration and subsequent
polynomial evaluation both use outward arithmetic. The polynomial image
radius contains actual cell variation as well as evaluation rounding;
it is never relabeled as approximation error.

The contour formula is complete before truncation. There is no imported
Riemann–Siegel gamma or heat correction to drop in this direct source
calculation. The contour identity and all tails are the pinned sources,
not an inference from overlapping numerical answers.

In particular, the returned physical component six pays
\(\partial_t h_4=-h_6\). On D*,
\[
|h_6|<7.735814474079\times10^{-8}.
\]
This is a valid first-order time Lipschitz bound for h4 at fixed a within
D*. The actual source evaluation uses the higher Taylor scheme through
order 82 rather than silently using this first-order bound as its entire
time budget. A separately integrated finite mixed derivative
\(\partial_t\partial_x^4\) agrees with the negative sixth midpoint
integral at both precisions for both methods; the complete heat identity
and direct tails justify the physical relation.

## 3. One coherent approximant, not separate component polynomials

Let \(m_n\) denote the exact midpoint dyadic selected from the returned
nth finite-integral ball. Define the single polynomial
\[
P(t,z)=\sum_{\ell=0}^{50}\sum_{k=0}^{12}
 \frac{(-1)^k m_{\ell+2k}}{\ell!k!}
 (z-35)^\ell(t-21/200)^k.                         \tag{1}
\]
Its exact rational coefficients are formed from dyadic midpoint moments
and integer factorials. The record includes the exact mantissa and binary
exponent of all 81 midpoint moments for every method and precision, with
an exact round-trip check. Decimal ball strings are conservative displays;
the mantissa/exponent fields identify the exact polynomial without
repeating its acquisition. The coherent approximant jet is
\(p_j=\partial_z^jP\). Its spatial sum runs through **50−j**.

The old evaluator separately approximates each physical component using
a degree-50 polynomial \(P_j\) with coefficients \(m_{j+\ell+2k}\).
Consequently \(P_j\ne\partial_z^jP\) for \(j>0\). The wrapper explicitly
computes both and encloses their difference from the finite omitted
high-degree terms. On D* its fourth-order difference is below
\(2.712\times10^{-51}\). Its small size does not justify dropping this
logical distinction. Nor does the truncated P itself solve the heat
equation; only the complete retained integral and H do.

The j=0 mode, source, quadrature and Taylor payments on the enclosing
disk rectangle provide a uniform **complex modulus** bound
\[
|H(t,a+Rw)-P(t,a+Rw)|\le\eta,\qquad |w|\le1,
\quad(t,a)\in D_*.                              \tag{2}
\]
The Acb return enlarges each coordinate by this bound, but the proof of
the payment is a modulus bound. It is not the much larger radius of the
polynomial image across a cell. Conservative common bounds at both
precisions are:

| Coherent source approximant | Shared eta | Fourth-derivative Cauchy allowance \(384\eta\) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Real-source P | <9.473580706e−30 | <3.637854992e−27 |
| Shifted-source P | <1.374777712e−25 | <5.279146415e−23 |

The real-source payment is limited by the Taylor term
\(<9.463204929\times10^{-30}\); the shifted payment is limited by
omitted modes \(<1.374775526\times10^{-25}\). The record exposes all
four summands separately. A smaller residual does not change the event
feasibility result below.

For real t,a, the residual is real-symmetric. Therefore
\(g(w)=(H-P)(t,a+Rw)/\eta\) is a real Schur function. For five real
parameters \(s_0,\ldots,s_4\in[-1,1]\), set
\[
g_4(w)=s_4,\qquad
 g_k(w)=\frac{s_k+w g_{k+1}(w)}{1+s_kwg_{k+1}(w)}
 \quad(k=3,2,1,0).                              \tag{3}
\]
Every permitted real residual jet through order four is obtained by
taking the coefficients through degree four of g0. Indeed each Schur
step extracts the real value at zero and divides the disk automorphism
by w; five steps determine precisely these five coefficients. Replacing
the last Schur tail by its constant at zero cannot change those
coefficients. When a parameter has absolute value one the corresponding
Schur function is constant and later parameters become irrelevant.
The rational recursion remains well-defined as a formal power series
because every inverse denominator has constant term one. The exact
residual derivatives are \(r_j=\eta j![w^j]g_0/R^j\).

The wrapper checks 243 boundary-parameter combinations and one nontrivial
rational combination, including forward extraction and the known first
three coefficient formulas. The [threshold-sign consumer][consumer]
gives the explicit conditioned coefficients and the one-variable upper
bound. Pure Cauchy allowances are retained as a valid enclosing fallback;
no independent-coordinate assumption is imposed on the Schur consumer.

## 4. Normalizer return and the event gate

For any positive nonvanishing real normalizer A, put
\(\alpha=A_x/A\), \(f_j=\partial_x^j(H/A)\). At an exact double event,
\(f_0=f_1=0\), and
\[
h_2/A=f_2,\quad h_3/A=f_3+3\alpha f_2,
\quad h_4/A=f_4+4\alpha f_3+6(\alpha^2+\alpha_x)f_2.
\]
Substitution, retaining the cross terms before cancellation, proves
\[
Q_L/A^2=2f_3^2-3f_2f_4-18(L+\alpha_x)f_2^2.       \tag{4}
\]
The wrapper verifies exact rational jet return through order six for
three exponential quadratic normalizers, with both signs of alpha and
alpha_x, and verifies (4). These are algebra fixtures, not acquisition
of the UAR normalizer on a new domain. For this actual source contract
A=1 makes the normalization physical and (4) requires no conversion
factor. For a different A the hypotheses and its derivative intervals
must be paid separately.

One cannot impose \(p_0=p_1=0\) on the approximant. Exact event feasibility
requires \(r_0=-p_0\), \(r_1=-p_1\). The first Schur step gives the
necessary condition
\[
p_0^2+\eta R|p_1|-\eta^2\le0.                   \tag{5}
\]
On every point of D*, both methods give
\[
|H|>9.07289607819716\times10^{-5},
\]
and (5)'s left side is greater than
\(8.2317443245764\times10^{-9}\). Thus the complete event class is empty
on this validation box before a fourth-order sign optimization is run.
There is no meaningful claimed finite Q gain from optimizing that empty
set. The source-return overlaps at both precisions are diagnostics; each
method independently pays its complete proof obligations above.

## 5. Controls, limits and disposition

The exact heat polynomial \(x^2-2(t-T)\) has its genuine double event
preserved by the first-jet acceptance interface. Its own jets, including
h4 and h6, are used; no theta residual allowance is assigned to it.
Its \(Q_L=-72L\) is an algebra check, not a counterexample satisfying the
positive-source and paired-positive-zero hypotheses of the inherited
threshold lower bound. Schur boundary cases and nonconstant normalizers
supply additional exact derivative controls.

The Euler-factor, finite weighted-square and finite-prime controls do
not satisfy the unchanged complete theta source used in the direct
integrals. Full normalized reflection fails in the finite coefficient
constructions; the Euler-factor flow changes the source coefficients and
level. Smooth reflected and positive-primitive controls lack the exact
unchanged integer-square expansion. Positive atomic and continuous
threshold controls and fixed-profile families retain their own genuine
events and own profiles; this note makes no opposing sign claim for any
of them. Their analytical screens remain in [result-summary 12][controls].
No control is rejected merely because it lacks a name or because an
unrelated theta box has a nonzero value.

**A1 is complete for this declared bounded direct interface.** It does
not supply a fourth-order derivative theorem on the UAR high-sector
domain, whose finite sector-edge derivative error and coherent
normalizer neighborhood still require proof. Nor does it close the
bridge \(1001<|x|<\exp(2000)\). A useful next arithmetic consumer must
operate on a nonempty feasible event class in its own justified domain;
the [conditioned Schur construction][consumer] is infrastructure for
that missing acquisition, not evidence that it has been obtained.
