## Common source, physical normalization and complete errors

The fixed parameters match the contract: B=[1/100,1/5]x[999,1001],
center (21/200,1000), source modes 1 through 11, U=2, the unchanged
shifted contour d, degrees 24/12, midpoint derivative orders 0 through
50 and a common moment through order 52. No source parameter is retuned.
All three physical derivatives are divided by the same fixed positive
Gc=1000^(7/4)exp(-125*pi), which is never differentiated. In particular
the field whose degree is computed is the physical ordered pair (H,Hx)
up to positive scalar multiplication, with no phase orientation change.

The imported producer first establishes the complete reflected contour
identity and only then truncates. Its finite-integral Taylor remainder
and propagated midpoint quadrature radii remain distinct from the actual
order-0/1/2 whole-half-line omitted-mode and retained-source tails. The
comparison does not differentiate a tail inequality, give a finite source
the complete source's reflection, discard an endpoint, or add the complete
tail twice.

The additional Horner evaluation is exactly the same midpoint polynomial.
For each time degree k it evaluates the spatial polynomial with coefficients
H^(j+l+2k)/(l!k!), and then uses -dt as the outer Horner variable. This
supplies the same (-1)^k factor as the original heat Taylor polynomial.
Each ordered interval image adds the same complete error before their
intersection is taken. Both images therefore contain the true jet, and
so does their nonempty outward interval intersection. Their overlap and
finite-intersection guards are checked on every call.

## Whole boundary, polygon homotopy and exact degree

The boundary traversal has positive orientation dt wedge dx: lower wall,
final time face, upper wall, initial time face. Every accepted segment is
closed and its complete first-jet image has a rigorously strict coordinate
sign with a positive exact rational lower margin. A source point is not
substituted for a whole-segment enclosure. Depth and call exhaustion leave
the result inconclusive.

The exact boundary validator requires the start and end corner, contiguous
endpoints, each properly oriented face, all four faces and their positive
order. The rational target vertex associated with each shared source
endpoint satisfies both incident sign half-planes: constrained coordinates
are their signs and unconstrained coordinates are zero. Conflicting signs
on the same coordinate are rejected. This construction needs no endpoint
source evaluation and uses no guessed physical endpoint value.

Each polygon edge and the corresponding complete physical boundary image
lie in the same convex open coordinate half-plane. Their straight homotopy
therefore avoids zero, including their endpoint incidences. The rational
polygon has the physical boundary's degree. The ray-crossing winding
routine uses the proper half-open endpoint convention, allows repeated
nonzero vertices, and rejects an origin vertex or edge through the origin.
It computes an integer degree, including nonzero degrees; only degree zero
can pass the exclusion interface.

The resulting collision exclusion imports the existing same-sign
all-multiplicity collision-index theorem under its hypotheses. Equal root
counts at the initial and final time faces are not substituted for degree;
both spatial walls and their root flux are retained. Winding alone gives
no numerical interior norm radius.

## Direct baseline, midpoint propagation and closed coverage

The direct method is a new first-jet baseline rather than the earlier
stronger signed Laguerre test. Its Cartesian norm calculation gives an
outward lower bound for the distance of the complete component rectangle
from the origin. When that
bound is zero, the optional midpoint enclosure holds the same whole time
interval fixed. On its whole spatial cell the derivative vector is
(Hx,Hxx). Its interval upper norm times the exact spatial half-width is
a valid Lipschitz payment. Subtracting this from the complete midpoint
lower norm is consequently a uniform spatial-cell lower bound. Both calls
and their complete errors are charged.

The initial pass uses full-time spatial strips. Only unresolved strips
receive the predeclared four equal closed time slabs; the absolute spatial
depth limit remains nine. Already accepted strips are retained. The exact
recursive construction produces the full closed partition, and the final
coverage verification checks every elementary time interval against a
gap-free union of accepted spatial intervals. An unresolved cell or an
exhausted execution budget cannot acquire a passing coverage flag.

## Cost accounting and cache fairness

Both consumers share one fresh source acquisition within each precision
run. Each receives a separate shallow evaluator copy with an initially
empty coefficient cache and fresh counters. Immutable midpoint dyadics
and error bounds are common; cached consumer coefficients are not shared.
Both receive the same two-Horner intersection. The fixed-time cache
benefit arises from their chosen cell geometry and is not created by
prewarming one consumer's cache. Consumer order is reversed at 256 bits.

The primary reported cost is common fresh source setup plus the individual
consumer, including its evaluator/cache creation and every midpoint or
boundary range call. There is no extra source call for synthesized polygon
vertices. Consumer-only timing and range counts are secondary diagnostics.
A boundary coordinate margin and a direct uniform interior norm radius
are distinct quantities and are not compared as if they were the same.

The mathematical-content projection removes execution timing/callback
fields and its own stored hash before hashing. Exact source data, complete
errors, partitions, margins, polygon, degree and producer/source pins stay
inside that comparison. Matching hashes identify replay content; they do
not prove the source theorem or the homotopy argument.

new helper acceptance interfaces. The polynomial controls use their own
exact physical jets evaluated with outward Arb arithmetic on every
requested closed boundary segment; they are not fixed event balls copied
around a fictitious boundary and receive no theta error constants.
