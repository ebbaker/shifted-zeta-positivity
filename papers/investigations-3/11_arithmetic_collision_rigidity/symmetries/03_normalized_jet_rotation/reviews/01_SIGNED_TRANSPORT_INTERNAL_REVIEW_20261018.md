## Exact identities and hypotheses

Let theta be real C2 with real omega=theta_x nonzero throughout a real interval; use the same theta and normalizer for exact Q and approximant f. Put W_f=f-i f_x/omega and Z_f=e^{-i theta}W_f. Direct differentiation gives

`D_theta Z_f=-i e^{-i theta}(D_theta^2+1)f`, hence

`I_f(x)=i[Z_f(x)-Z_f(x0)]`.

The orientation of theta may be decreasing; the signed integral retains its oriented limits. No division by W_f or actual unknown jet angle is used. The endpoint reduction is an elementary antiderivative identity and should not be presented as a new arithmetic estimate. Evaluating it requires no quadrature, but each endpoint evaluation still needs outward rounding and the correct normalization.

For r=Q-f holomorphic on a neighborhood of each radius-d disk, with |r|<=eta there and real symmetry, r and r_x are real at the real centers. Cauchy gives

`|W_r| <= eta sqrt(1+(d |omega|)^{-2})`.

Real symmetry is essential to this Euclidean combination. For arbitrary complex residuals the corresponding separate Cauchy estimates give only a sum of component magnitudes. A smaller valid joint bound follows from the disk automorphism/Schwarz inequality: after scaling the disk and eta, |g'(0)|<=1-|g(0)|², whence maximizing the resulting convex quadratic in |g(0)|² gives

`|W_r| <= eta max(1,(d |omega|)^{-1})`.

Either choice can be replaced by its uniform version using omega_*>0. This review does not require optimality. The exact residual integral satisfies

`|I_r(x)|=|Z_r(x)-Z_r(x0)| <= e(x)+e(x0)`.

This is an endpoint triangle envelope. Oscillator residuals can have I_r=0 while both endpoint jet radii are nonzero, so the envelope does not capture all residual correlations.

If m0 is any valid actual anchor lower bound, then

`m0 <= |W_Q(x0)| <= |W_f(x0)|+e(x0)`.

Thus the scalar transport lower bound

`m0-|Z_f(x)-Z_f(x0)|-e(x0)-e(x)`

is at most `|W_f(x)|-e(x)` by reverse triangle. The same holds if the nominal integral modulus is replaced by a certified upper bound. This is domination by the ideal direct nominal first-jet margin; a particular broad interval implementation of the direct test can still return a poorer number, so computational formulations should be compared carefully. Correlated endpoint residual sets, a sharper directly estimated residual integral, and other vector transport schemes are outside this obstruction.
