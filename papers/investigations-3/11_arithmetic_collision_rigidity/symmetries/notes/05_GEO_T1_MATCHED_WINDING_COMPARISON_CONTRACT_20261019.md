orientation conversion. Traverse all four faces in positive order:
(tlo,xlo)->(thi,xlo)->(thi,xhi)->(tlo,xhi)->(tlo,xlo).
Subdivide whole closed segments until a strict sign of one complete jet
coordinate is certified, or the declared budget fails.

Construct each rational polygon vertex directly from its two incident
certified halfplanes: required coordinate signs become +1 or -1,
unconstrained coordinates zero. Reject conflicting incident signs,
gaps, overlaps or omitted faces. These vertices need no additional source
point evaluations, as allowed by the existing convex-homotopy theorem.
Check that both endpoints of every polygon edge satisfy its whole-segment
halfplane. The polygon may have repeated nonzero vertices.

Compute exact rational winding for any integer degree. A nonzero boundary
plus **degree zero**, together with the imported same-sign all-multiplicity
collision-index theorem, certifies the closed box. Nonzero degree is not a
pass; boundary uncertainty is inconclusive. Preserve physical margins,
