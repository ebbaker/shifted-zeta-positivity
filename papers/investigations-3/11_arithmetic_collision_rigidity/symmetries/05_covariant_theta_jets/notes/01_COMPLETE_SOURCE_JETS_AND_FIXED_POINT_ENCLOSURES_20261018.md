# S05 opening result: complete source jets and fixed-point interval evaluation

18 October 2026, America/New_York. Prepared for Edward Baker with substantial
LLM assistance. Actual model **gpt-6.1-sol (Codex)**, reasoning effort
**ultra**, verified from this turn's metadata. Analytic derivation plus
outward Arb/Acb computations; internal same-model review, including separate
agent checks. No independent specialist review.

The declared local comparison passes: modular fixed-point Taylor evaluation
encloses the complete source and its first two source derivatives more
tightly than the existing expanded evaluator on each of
`[0,1/64]`, `[0,1/256]`, and `[0,1/1024]`. The benefit persists against a
stronger, combined Horner baseline. It is a reduction in interval
overestimation near the source reflection point. No physical spectral jet
error, runtime improvement, new exclusion region, or global bound is obtained.

The output is `Phi`, `Phi_u`, `Phi_uu`, with derivatives in the **source

## Complete mode algebra and tail

Write `v_n=pi*n^2*exp(4u)`. The complete source has

\[
 \Phi^{(r)}(u)=e^u\sum_{n\ge1}P_r(v_n)e^{-v_n},\quad
 P_0(v)=2v^2-3v,\quad
 P_{r+1}=4vP_r'+(1-4v)P_r.
\]

For the existing normalized covariant jets, let

\[
 Q_m(v)=(2m)!\sum_{j=0}^m
 \frac{(-4v)^j}{(2j)!2^{m-j}(m-j)!}.
\]

Then

\[
 T_{2m}=e^u\left((2m-1)!!+
                   2\sum_{n\ge1}Q_m(v_n)e^{-v_n}\right).
\]

If `16 Phi^(r)=sum_m c_(r,m) T_(2m)`, coefficient combination gives
`sum_m c_(r,m) Q_m=8 P_r`. The factor 8 includes both signed lattice
indices. Its constant coefficient is zero. The checker verifies this
finite algebra through source derivative eight, using the known ladder
to generate the covariant coefficients and the direct recurrence
separately. The analytic identities follow from the established generating
formula, not from finitely many checks. The growing zero lattice mode is
cancelled **before evaluation or integration**. No separately divergent
positive-time transforms of the `T_(2m)` are introduced.

Here is an explicit complete tail used for every retained derivative.
For `u=a+ib`, `a_- <= a <= a_+`, `a_- >= 0`, `|b|<=b_*<pi/8`, set

\[
 A_-=\pi e^{4a_-},\quad A_+=\pi e^{4a_+},\quad
 c=\cos(4b_*)>0,\quad m=N+1,
\]
\[
 \rho_j=(1+1/m)^{2j}e^{-cA_-(2m+1)}.
\]

For every nonzero coefficient `p_(r,j)` require `rho_j<1`. Then

\[
 \boxed{|\operatorname{tail}_r|\le
 e^{a_+}\sum_j |p_{r,j}|
 \frac{(A_+m^2)^je^{-cA_-m^2}}{1-\rho_j}.}
\]

Indeed each summand is bounded by
`e^(a_+) |p_(r,j)| A_+^j n^(2j) exp(-c A_- n^2)`.
Its successive ratio is at most `rho_j`, since the power ratio decreases
and the exponential decrement increases with `n`. Summing the geometric
majorant proves the bound. It also gives normal convergence on the compact
source rectangles and justifies the fixed-order source differentiation.
The implemented shifted comparisons are on a line; the tail proof applies
to the whole stated rectangle or strip. At negative real source arguments,
the component can use the established complete-source reflection; it does
not reflect individual modes.

## Fixed-point Taylor enclosure

Full modular reflection makes `Phi` even, so all its odd derivatives at
zero vanish exactly. This is an analytic input from Note 07. A small
numerical residual at zero is only a consistency check and is never used
to infer an exact zero.

Let `M8` bound the complete eighth source derivative on `[0,h]`. Taylor's
theorem applied separately to each `Phi^(r)`, `r=0,1,2`, yields

\[
 \Phi^{(r)}(u)=
 \sum_{\substack{0\le j\le3\\2j\ge r}}
 \frac{\Phi^{(2j)}(0)u^{2j-r}}{(2j-r)!}+R_r(u),\qquad
 |R_r(u)|\le\frac{M_8h^{8-r}}{(8-r)!}.
\]

The absent coefficient of source order seven is zero by parity; this is
why the same eighth-derivative bound works for all three outputs. Endpoint
even derivatives through six include their complete mode tails. The
eighth-derivative enclosure includes its own tail before taking the
absolute upper bound. Subsequent arithmetic is outward rounded.

Evaluate the polynomial in `s=u^2` with its exact range `s in [0,h^2]`;
factor a single `u` from the odd output. This avoids introducing a
spurious negative square range. For the value and second derivative, the
first nonconstant term is quadratic. That accounts for the large reduction
against direct interval formulas whose dependencies produce linear width.
There is no finite differential closure of the physical field.

## Bounded quantitative result

The following conservative factors hold in **both** precision records.
Each entry is a strict lower bound for direct radius divided by Taylor
radius; the complete source tails and Taylor remainder are included.

| Source cell | Phi | Phi_u | Phi_uu |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `[0,1/64]` | 59.10 | 8.18 | 169.74 |
| `[0,1/256]` | 226.15 | 7.45 | 614.17 |
| `[0,1/1024]` | 894.47 | 7.29 | 2394.90 |

At `h=1/1024`, the stronger fused Horner comparison still improves by
factors greater than 694.40, 1.63, and 639.05, respectively. The complete
eighth-derivative absolute bound is below `1.413e9`. The three Taylor
remainder allowances are below `2.899e-20`, `2.375e-16`, `1.703e-12`.
The direct mode-tail allowances for orders zero through two are below
`4.020e-106`, `4.144e-103`, `4.288e-100`.

On the real 64-cell cover, fused Horner evaluation is tighter than the
expanded baseline for all three orders on every cell. On the shifted
line it improves orders zero and one on every cell, but improves order
two on only **30 of 64** cells. It widens that enclosure on the remaining
34, by up to about 8.71%. It is therefore not a uniform replacement for
the expanded second-derivative formula on complex cells.

The fixed-point comparison passes its declared local gate. The basis is
an organizing device; the principal gain comes from complete-source
Taylor evaluation and known parity, which ordinary derivative polynomials
can also exploit. Cold Taylor setup requires endpoint derivatives through
six and a complete cell bound on derivative eight. This additional work
has not been benchmarked against an accuracy-matched consumer. Whole-run
times in the records are diagnostics, not a speedup measurement.

For perspective, simply paying the value Taylor remainder by absolute
integration over `[0,1/64]`, with `0<=t<=1/5`, gives an allowance
`h*exp(h^2/5)*delta_0` of about `4.00e-11`. This is an explicit crude
payment, not a small relative physical error at height 1000. No spectral
cancellation or full-integral accuracy is certified by the source width
ratios. All other source intervals and tails would also need payment.

## Controls, review, and decision

The local ladder is shared by weighted square sources. A finite first-mode
countercheck has nonzero first derivative at zero; imposing theta's exact
odd endpoint zeros on it is rejected. Reflected smooth collision controls
can share parity and can benefit from the same Taylor optimization. This
is expected: the optimization is not a collision discriminator.

For an eventual theta-specific exclusion, the exact missing premises remain
those in [the control summary][controls] and [Review 14][review14]: finite-prime
orbits lack full normalized reflection; three/four-square sources lack
full reflection and the unchanged coefficient sequence; reflected and
smooth/primitive controls lack the exact theta square expansion; Euler
controls lack unchanged level-one theta data; generic polynomial/heat
controls lack the exact theta source. Auxiliary smooth-density/two-channel
positivity lacks the complete signed physical comparison. Each scope is
separate. Parity alone rejects none of the reflected families.

Fresh baseline replays reject the five existing event boxes and the
separate Euler negative-Laguerre witness. The latter is not a double-event
box. The existing ladder and complete signed-bridge checkers pass. These
are regression checks of their existing interfaces; they do not validate
the new tail proof or supply a new exclusion test. No event height is
invented for the analytic finite-prime or smooth/primitive controls.

The [implementation and small records][numerics] retain source hashes,
domains, complete tails, remainders, and both precision runs. The
[assessment][review] gives the internal review disposition. A separate
same-model agent checked the formulas and containment at 17 points per
fixed-point cell using 384 bits and ten modes; this remains a diagnostic
and same-model review, not an independent proof.

**Decision:** retain a bounded source-enclosure component for S01/S06
boundary or local derivative work. Do not expand the scan. Before claiming
a useful spectral handoff, choose a named consumer and compare its paid
error or total cost with its current method. In particular, the shifted
spectral quadrature does not directly consume the real fixed-point
Taylor improvement. The global bridge and complex exterior obligations in
the program guide remain open.
