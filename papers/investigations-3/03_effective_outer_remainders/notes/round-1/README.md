### Errata found while drafting the manuscript

- Note 01, Section 5.2: the threshold 1.08·10⁵ (even, p = √5) is below the
  passing end of the record, 108400 (record H). A retest at 108000 fails
  by 4.3·10⁻⁴. The other thresholds, 4.89·10⁴, 1.32·10³ and 6.6·10², were
  rounded below or near the record's passing ends (48920, 1316 and 661.2)
  but pass on retest. The manuscript prints 1.084·10⁵, 4.892·10⁴, 1.32·10³
  and 6.62·10². The summary's 1.1·10⁵ and 4.9·10⁴ are safe.
- Note 01, Section 6.2: at x = 10³, p = √5 and t = 0.02 the second-order
  term is 0.0047 and the measured deviation 0.0048 (record C); the note
  interchanges them.
- Note 01, Lemma 3.2 (zeta): the step |s| ≤ x needs p ≤ x. The manuscript
  states the lemma for 1 < p ≤ x, with |s| ≤ 2x. Every use has p ≤ 6.
- Note 01, Section 5: the integrals are cut at 16τ to the right of σ_*,
  at 40τ to the left, and in the reflected region at
  σ_* + τ²ℓ_* + 30τ + 2, not uniformly at 40τ.
- Note 02, Corollary 2.1: the hypotheses on the disk should include
  ℓ_0 > 0 at Re w′ (family), 2Re w′ − 1 ≤ 2Im w′ (zeta) and τ²a_* < 1.
- Note 02, summary item 2 and Section 1: the preprint's Proposition 7
  (triangle estimate) does not share the main term; Propositions 8 and 11
  do.
- Note 02, Section 4.2: the preprint does not say how its λ_i and μ_i were
  chosen, so "as the paper's rows are" is unsupported. The C_i and G_i
  carry a rounding margin of at least 2·10⁻⁶, and q_{i+1} adds 10⁻⁶ after
  rounding up.
- Note 02, Section 4.3: k_0 = 7.78093, so the range is 7.780 < k_i < 10.592.
- Note 02, Section 4.4: the dependency list should also name the
  preprint's fixed-cutoff argument around (16), the elementary inequalities
  of its Section 8.2 that the certificate does not check, and Polymath's
  equations (37)–(41).
- Note 02, Section 11: Polymath's barrier is a published, refereed
  computation; it was not independently verified or replayed.
