16 October 2026, America/New_York. Prepared for Edward Baker with substantial
LLM assistance. Model: **gpt-6-astra (Codex)**; reasoning effort **ultra**.

**Status: initialized, higher-risk analytic proposal.** No new exterior
theorem, evaluated remainder or certified domain is established here. The
[portfolio plan](../../research-overview/01_PORTFOLIO_RESEARCH_PLAN_20261016.md)
coordinates this project with the finite-sum and family investigations.

## Objective and the present quantitative gap

Obtain one complete remainder estimate that makes a specified exterior
statement effective at a height reachable by its finite consumer. The
consumer may need complex nonvanishing rather than four derivative
inequalities. Choose that statement before retaining the stronger machinery.
An improved formal asymptotic threshold is not useful coverage until its
starting height, constants and finite overlap are supplied.

The existing [carrier calculation](../../../../papers/investigations-2/active/21_quaternion_theta_geometry/notes/01_weighted_outer_remainders/58_EXPLICIT_CARRIER_PHASE_AND_CONDITIONAL_HEIGHT_20261015.md)
gives a complete normalized four-jet error
\(1728B_C(L)+409/L\), where \(L=\log(x/(4\pi))\), with a sufficient
sign condition at error at most \(1/8\). Its domain already requires
\(L\ge100002\), \(0<t\le1/2\), and \(tL\ge61/24\), and its arithmetic
package retains an unevaluated exponent-pair constant and possible
validity threshold.

The [four-range refinement](../../../../papers/investigations-2/active/22_theta_manifold_geometry/notes/06_effective_outer_bridge/01_LOW_INDEX_BUDGET_REFINEMENT_20261015.md)
reduces a low-index constant by a factor exceeding 3974. Nevertheless,
its majorant needs \(L-1\) around \(31{,}424{,}688\) before paying the
sign budget. Practical comparison at physical height \(x\) around
\(6\times10^{12}\) has \(L\) around \(27\). These numbers describe a
specific proof budget, not a lower bound on the true sign onset.
Evaluating the remaining imported constant cannot by itself bridge the gap.

## First bounded task

Write a contract for one attainable \((t_0,X,y_0)\), specifying the exact
exterior region and conclusion used by the global certificate. Reconcile
physical height \(x\) with zeta height \(T=x/2\). Retain the full
verification, barrier and exterior obligations in
\(\Lambda\le t_0+y_0^2/2\); do not report a formal
\(\theta/\log(X/(4\pi))\) projection as a bound.

Then choose one materially different error estimate. Candidates include
direct complex nonvanishing that avoids unnecessary differentiation, a
better joint treatment of the complete oscillatory remainder, or a source
reconstruction preserving cancellation before absolute values are taken.
For that choice, produce a complete budget at the selected overlap height,
including analytic branches, cutoff matching, lower endpoints, tails,
derivative payments actually needed and every imported constant.

Begin with a table of unavoidable positive costs. If one already exceeds
the admissible margin, stop that budget before further algebra or parameter
sweeps. The first deliverable can be a rigorous obstruction to the chosen
estimate. It should explain which new information would be needed to repair it.

## Cancellation and localization have a defined role

The positive primitive, signed averages and disk identities remain useful
tools, as recorded in
[summary 14](../../../../result-summaries/14_theta_identities_and_arithmetic_bookkeeping.md)
and [summary 16](../../../../result-summaries/16_hypercomplex_reductions_and_geometric_realizations.md).
Use one only when it improves a named term of the complete budget.
Separate absolute bounds on primitive components lose the small reconstructed
field. A translated or localized average must pay its entire boundary and
weight cost.

A window shrinking with a hypothetical collision may expose its local mass,
but does not provide a prescribed certificate by itself. Conversely, failure
of a fixed-width window is not an exclusion of every localization method.
Keep this work inside a concrete remainder estimate; do not open another
sequence of equivalent sign criteria. Any resurgence or late-term proposal
needs a checked theorem, hypotheses and explicit remainder before numerical use.

## Stopping, completion and reopening

Continue only for a new applicable estimate, a complete improvement in
effective coverage, or a faithful obstruction to the declared class.
Stop repeated reallocations of the existing budget when its structural
loss remains dominant. Stop localization experiments whose complete cost
erases their apparent gain.

Reopen a stopped design only with an independently specified cancellation,
sharper uniform remainder, weaker sufficient consumer statement, or new
finite coverage that changes its quantitative comparison. Apply
[avoid 01](../../../../avoid/01_restating_the_target.md),
[avoid 07](../../../../avoid/07_scoped_exclusions_read_as_universal.md),
[avoid 12](../../../../avoid/12_term_by_term_matching_and_separate_bounds.md)
and [avoid 20](../../../../avoid/20_volume_without_a_gain.md).
Record the final theorem's precise hypotheses or the failed design's scope,
update status and history, and keep numerical evidence separate from proof.
