## Angular gluing has a sharper gate after genuine progress

The [new brief, Sections 1–3](../../active/02_angular_jacobi_gluing/reviews/CONTINUATION_AND_BRAINSTORM_BRIEF_20261013.md)
adds two substantive results: stable Volterra recovery of the complete
source and

\[
P_t(x)>\frac{1}{3000(1+x^2)},\qquad 0\le t\le1,\quad x\in\mathbb R.
\]

The earlier negative-origin result concerns the auxiliary source
\(\Phi-R\); it does not contradict positivity of the correction transform
\(P_t\). The source recovery estimate also has a different meaning from
observability at one frequency. These distinctions are handled correctly
in the latest brief and should govern the status of 02.

The remaining obstruction is substantial. Writing \(H_t=A_t+P_t\), the two
channels have opposite algebraic tails, whereas the physical sum and its
derivatives decay faster than every inverse power. Consequently
\((H_t^2+(H_t')^2)/P_t^2\) has no constant or polynomial global lower
margin. The complete outward witness at \((t,x)=(0,32)\) also disproves
\(|A_t|\le P_t\). Nearby positive collision sources retain stable recovery
and the correction bound while violating full imaginary Jacobi preparation.
These results close particular transfer strategies; they leave a precise
need for quantitative information from that preparation.

The proposed compact-domain inequality

\[
\left|1+\frac{A_t}{P_t}\right|^2+
\left|\partial_x\frac{A_t}{P_t}
+\frac{P_t'}{P_t}\left(1+\frac{A_t}{P_t}\right)\right|^2
\ge\varepsilon_{\mathcal R}>0
\]

is an exact reformulation of the physical observation gap. The brief
explicitly acknowledges this and does not claim to have proved it. A
successful continuation must supply an independently derived estimate on a
declared domain, or the two complete stationary signs, and explain why the
retained collision controls violate its hypotheses. The backward heat
equation for the quotient supplies no forward maximum principle.

I therefore agree with a pause and the ban on further unguided scalar
transport. I also consider the brief's single focused search for a
full-gluing multiplier a legitimate reopening task. It should return either
a specific candidate with a bounded test or a recorded failure, rather than
an indefinite new identity search.

The closure memo's statement that the remaining lattice law simply now
lives in 18 as Hecke relations goes beyond the demonstrated transfer.
[Jacobi quasi-periodicity](https://dlmf.nist.gov/20.2.ii) constrains the
elliptic variable; 18 derives useful identities for integrated pair
observations. Related arithmetic origins do not prove that these
observations preserve every useful quantitative consequence of full
imaginary gluing. The documents provide no such equivalence theorem.

One small qualification belongs in the nearby-control construction: choose
large displacement points where the genuine pair \((H_T,H_T')\) is not
already zero. This ensures the solved modulation is nontrivial and hence
fails genuine preparation. Such points exist arbitrarily far out because
\(H_T\) is a nonzero entire function. Without this selection, a zero
modulation could return genuine theta itself. This repairs the wording of
[Note 3](../../active/02_angular_jacobi_gluing/notes/3_COMPLETE_CHANNEL_VOLTERRA_CONTROLS_20261013.md)
without changing the control mechanism or the allocation decision.

Similarly, consolidating 07 and 15 under 18 is a reasonable organizational

## The arguments for 14 and 16 do not establish broad closure

The [14 program](../../inactive/14_theta_volterra_integrability/notes/INITIAL_RESEARCH_PROGRAM_20261013.md)
already separates universal Volterra evolution from the nonpolynomial theta
score and a proposed source-preserving Darboux or Lax relation. Its bounded
moment/string diagnostic was never run. Universality of the evolution
rejects an inference from generic integrability; it does not reject that
unperformed task. With no specific new relation in hand, dormancy is still
a sensible allocation.

The [16 program](../../inactive/16_radial_sine_geometry/notes/INITIAL_RESEARCH_PROGRAM_20261013.md)
seeks a signed reflected sine-channel estimate retaining the nonlocal
generator. Its positive radial realization requires
\(R_{3,t}(r)=-m_t'(r)/(2\pi r)\ge0\), a condition beyond generic
one-dimensional positive-definiteness. The linked earlier work proves
theta-specific dimension restrictions, including an obstruction in
dimension 11 and above on the stated time interval. Positivity alone does
not exclude collisions, but it is inaccurate to dismiss all of this
geometric information as generic. Preserve it without allocating further
work until a concrete signed estimate appears.

## Limit the next work in 18 and correct the interpretation of 19

18 has useful identities but no new complete sign. Its own A4 is a
reasonable final bounded test if its time and height domain, tail payments,
stationary constraints and comparator are specified. A passing first mode
does not settle the signed sum, and progress on \(\mathscr L_0\) alone
does not settle the separate \(\mathscr L_1\) obligation. A new signed
inequality could also justify continuation; a positive-definite
factorization is not the only possible acceptable outcome.

The literature comparison should remain secondary. [Young's Theorem 1.2](https://arxiv.org/html/1306.1554v1)
concerns weighted geodesic integrals of \(|E|^2\). It does not directly
give the signs of the inserted, heat-averaged moments in 18. The latest
18 note correctly recognizes this mismatch. Here “cusp kernel” denotes
the nonconstant Fourier part of an Eisenstein series, not a newly supplied
positive cuspidal spectral form.

19 is useful as a shared falsification test. The finite-prime argument
has the following scope: finitely many local relations, **together with
other hypotheses shared by the controls**, cannot force the desired
conclusion. A proof may instead use another input that those controls
fail, such as a quantitative evaluation of the genuine function. Thus
the claim that only all-prime relations can be load-bearing is too strong
without that restriction. Passing the control test is necessary for a
proposed implication with those hypotheses; it supplies no positive proof.

Two corrections in the [19 first note](../../active/19_euler_factor_controls/notes/1_EXACT_FLOW_WITNESSES_AND_ASYMPTOTICS_20261013.md)
should be carried into any allocation or summary:

1. The computed lowest-pair collision time is \(T_0\). It is a numerical
   candidate for a lower bound on \(\Lambda_{c,p}\), not an established
   equality. The note acknowledges that only the next two pairs were
   tracked. Its formal expansion should therefore be labelled as an
   expansion for that collision branch, with identification with the global
   constant conditional on controlling all other zeros. The next sound
   deliverable is the specified outward negative witness at a fixed point.
2. The claim that the certification margin grows near a collision confuses
   the normalized ratio with the quantity being certified. For an isolated
   pair, write locally
   \(H(x)=((x-X)^2+y^2)g(x)\), with \(g(X)\ne0\). Exactly,

   \[
   \mathscr L_0(H;X)
   =-2y^2g(X)^2+y^4\mathscr L_0(g;X).
   \]

   Thus the raw negative margin tends to zero, although
   \(\mathscr L_0(H;X)/H(X)^2=-2/y^2+O(1)\) diverges negatively when
   the remaining factor stays regular. The negative-witness criterion is
   valid, but approaching the collision does not automatically make its
   interval certification easier. Use an absolute error budget at a fixed
   test point, or justify the conditioning of a different normalization.

The isolated-pair qualification also belongs beside the near-collision
witness argument: its bounded remainder assumes other zeros do not join
the same collision point.
