## The alternative lift

Use the manuscript's Cayley–Dickson convention

\[
(a,b)(c,d)=(ac-d\bar b,\bar a d+cb),\qquad
G_m=h_m+\mathbf jh_{m+1},
\]

where off the physical slice the complex jets have arguments \(z=x_0+i x_1\), \(\tau=t+x_2+i x_3\), and \(D_qG_m=0\). For a field independent of the extra quaternion coordinate \(r\),

\[
\mathcal D_8(A,B)=(D_qA,\bar D_qB).
\]

Define

\[
\widetilde{\mathcal O}_m(t,q,r)
=\bigl(G_m(t,q),G_{m+2}(t,\bar q)\bigr).
\]

Conjugation reverses the three imaginary coordinate derivatives, so
\(\bar D_q[B(\bar q)]=(D_qB)(\bar q)\). Thus the field is globally real analytic and left monogenic. Reflection commutes with \(\partial_{x_0}\) and the external time derivative, giving

\[
\partial_{x_0}\widetilde{\mathcal O}_m=\widetilde{\mathcal O}_{m+1},
\qquad\partial_t\widetilde{\mathcal O}_m=-\widetilde{\mathcal O}_{m+2},
\qquad\widetilde{\mathcal O}_m(t,x,0)=(G_m(t,x),G_{m+2}(t,x)).
\]

For \(m=0\), field values use derivatives through order three, evaluated at the two indicated points. The original correction uses order four. Differentiation still generates higher jets in either construction; neither closes a four-jet heat system.

The original lift
\((G_m-\tfrac12G_{m+3}\bar r,G_{m+2})\) remains preferable if the two components must share the same off-slice complex spectral and heat arguments. In the reflected lift the second component instead uses \((z,\tau)=(x_0-i x_1,t-x_2-i x_3)\), and the field is constant in \(r\). A nonzero reflected field consequently does not belong to whole-space \(L^2(\mathbb R^8)\); bounded-domain spaces are unaffected by this observation.

## Physical dictionary and cutoff choice

Set \(v_j=(h_j,h_{j+1})\), with physical jets real. Then

\[
E=|v_0|^2,\qquad\mathcal L_j=-\det(v_j,v_{j+1}),
\qquad\tfrac12E_t=-v_0\cdot v_2.
\]

For \(a=A+\mathbf jB\), \(b=C+\mathbf jD\), and \(\mathbf u=\ell\mathbf j\),

\[
[a\mathbf i,b\mathbf i,\ell]=-2\mathbf u(AD-BC),\qquad
[a\mathbf i,b\mathbf k,\ell]=2\mathbf u(AC+BD).
\]

The physical trace lies in the associative quaternion subalgebra generated by \(\mathbf j\) and \(\ell\). Fixed transverse twists produce the displayed associators, whose only variable datum is one real oriented coefficient. Their norms forget its sign. Calibration/Gram identities recover squared magnitudes; they do not recover the missing orientation.

For the original staircase cutoff, define

\[
B_N=-v_0\cdot v_2^{(N)}=-h_0h_2^{(N)}-h_1h_3^{(N)}.
\]

On \(S_0=\{h_1=0\}\), \(B_N=J_{0,N}\). On \(S_1=\{h_2=0\}\),
\(B_N-J_{1,N}=h_0(h_2-h_2^{(N)})\). Everywhere on the physical slice,

\[
\left|B_N-\tfrac12E_t\right|
\le\sqrt E\,|v_2-v_2^{(N)}|.
\]

This is the complete square-root-energy error scale already compatible with Young's inequality in the manuscript's transfer. It vanishes at \(E=0\). The separate \(J_{j,N}\) can be sharper on \(S_1\), because \(B_N\) pays an extra second-jet tail. The common observation is optional notation, not a strengthened sign criterion.

The rank-three scalar form is indefinite:

\[
\mathcal L_j=h_{j+1}^2+\tfrac14(h_j-h_{j+2})^2
-\tfrac14(h_j+h_{j+2})^2.
\]

Its inertia is \((2,1)\). Shared moment features can reduce computation, but an invertible change of coordinates cannot remove the negative direction.

## Ambient log energy and phase

The vector \((H,H_z)\) is holomorphic in \((z,\tau)\). For \(\widehat E=|H|^2+|H_z|^2\), direct differentiation gives on the physical slice, where \(E>0\),

\[
\Delta_q\log\widehat E
=\frac{4\{\mathcal L_0^2+(h_0h_3-h_1h_2)^2\}}{E^2}.
\]

Its nonnegativity is compatible with collisions: it squares away the determinant orientation and concerns ambient variables, not monotonicity in external heat time. It does not extend the formula across a presumed zero by assuming nonvanishing.

Generic octonionic monogenicity does not imply logarithmic subharmonicity. The Cauchy kernel \(\bar p/|p|^8\) has norm \(|p|^{-7}\), hence \(\Delta_8\log|p|^{-7}=-42/|p|^2\) away from zero. The special identity above comes from the holomorphic two-component structure.

On intervals where \(G_j\ne0\),

\[
\partial_x\arg(h_j+i h_{j+1})
=-\frac{\mathcal L_j}{h_j^2+h_{j+1}^2}.
\]

This phase interpretation is useful for exposition and diagnostics. It neither licenses division at a candidate collision nor supplies a Hermite–Biehler hypothesis.

## Applicable tools and stopping rule

Left multiplication by imaginary octonions provides an associative real-matrix Clifford representation for differential calculations; it is not a multiplicative embedding of the octonion algebra. See [Baez §2.3](https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/octonions/node6.html). Keep the true associators for the physical signed observations.

Hardy/Bergman projections and Teodorescu right inverses can assist a specific boundary or defect calculation. They do not convert absolute error into an energy-relative signed estimate. Slice regularity, quaternionic-slice Fueter regularity, and Dirac monogenicity have different equations; verify the class before transferring any theorem. The literature and exact applicability boundaries are recorded in Review 25 rather than duplicated here.
