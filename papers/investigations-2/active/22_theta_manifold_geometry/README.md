# Theta manifold geometry and signed collision control

15 October 2026. Prepared for Edward Baker with substantial LLM assistance. Recorded model **gpt-6-astra**, reasoning effort **ultra**.

Investigation 22 studies whether the geometry of the arithmetic theta source can control signed turning and prevent degeneration of the physical jet surface. It was opened at the author's request as a separate continuation of [Investigation 21](../21_quaternion_theta_geometry/README.md).

The [current research overview (02)](research-overview/02_CHORD_CRITERION_AND_ARITHMETIC_BRIDGE_20261015.md) gives the strongest results, ranked next steps and new-chat handoff. The [integrated manuscript](manuscript/theta_manifold_geometry.tex) and [31-page PDF](../../../../output/pdf/theta_manifold_geometry.pdf) develop the proofs. The [integration review](reviews/04_MANUSCRIPT_INTEGRATION_REVIEW_20261015.md) and [build record](manuscript/BUILD_RECORD.json) state their verification scope.

The principal new result is a starting-energy chord criterion: a uniform error o(sqrt(E)) excludes every covered finite collision multiplicity. The manuscript also includes its cutoff transfer, the complete signed source bridge, ASD metric freedom, a validated weighted-square collision control, and full-reflection rigidity. The theta-specific signed inequality and global coverage remain open. Earlier geometry and signed-form constructions supply the foundation.
