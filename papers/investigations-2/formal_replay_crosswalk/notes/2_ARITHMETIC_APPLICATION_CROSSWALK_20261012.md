The prime cubic declaration retains the primary-generator hypothesis `lambda^2 ∣ p-1`; this represents the same primary convention because `(lambda^2)=(3)`.

The following qualifications are necessary on every application:

1. **Column and row indices.** Columns are ideals represented once by primary generators. Rows are elements and retain unit factors and arbitrary prime powers. Dividing element rows by the six units without recording multiplicity is not the stated moment.
2. **Zero extension.** A character power of exponent zero or divisible by six retains its zero on nonunits. Thus `chi_n(p^6)=1_(p∤n)`. A cancelled or principal local phase cannot silently become the constant one. O5 [lines 2888–2901](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/The-Quasi-Riemann-Hypothesis-October-5-2026/build/paper2.tex#L2888-L2901) and S30 [lines 625–640](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/The-Quasi-Riemann-Hypothesis-September-30-2026/build/paper.tex#L625-L640) make this explicit.
3. **Fixed exclusions.** O5's `S` contains the primes above 2 and 3 and the conductor of `nu` ([lines 663–678](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/The-Quasi-Riemann-Hypothesis-October-5-2026/build/paper2.tex#L663-L678)). S30 also includes all defining-modulus zeros and the fixed ray group's support, including redundant zeros ([lines 590–604](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/The-Quasi-Riemann-Hypothesis-September-30-2026/build/paper.tex#L590-L604)). To match a nonprimitive presentation, use its complete zero support; conductor support alone is insufficient. Moving punctures remain moving data.
4. **Profiles.** Both sources allow smooth complex annular tests on a fixed compact interval bounded away from zero. Extending such a test by zero to `R` gives a smooth compactly supported Schwartz profile for the Lean declarations. On a fixed annulus the ordinary `C^J` and logarithmic-derivative seminorms are equivalent, but a fixed-test constant is not automatically a bound homogeneous of degree two in `C^J`.
5. **Signs.** O5's displayed family uses `+1`; S30 permits one common sign `epsilon_chi=±1`. The positive case is the direct specialization. For the opposite orientation, conjugate the entire coefficient sequence, fixed character and test together. The exact norm transport is `OAI.SevenEighths.HeckeInverseAmplification.polynomial_inverse_norm`, [InverseAmplificationConjugation.lean:42–46](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/DirichletL/Hecke/InverseAmplificationConjugation.lean#L42-L46). Partial conjugation is not covered.
