## What each project must establish

**Arithmetic source audit.** The October 5 argument already includes a
fixed-character extraction from a family mean square. The audit must reach
the proof of that mean square, track one nonempty cube-tail branch through
both Poisson transformations, and check that its inputs return to the
admissible class with a strictly decreasing recursion parameter. Earlier
local reflection and sieve-interface checks should be reused with their
stated scope. A successful branch does not validate the entire theorem,
and a failed proof step does not refute the theorem's conclusion.

**Newman certificate audit.** Pin a public source version and its analytic
criterion before replaying numerical cells. Begin with 20–50 difficult
barrier or final-time cells and compare the original approximation with the
corrected center under the full error budget. The complete objective is
`t0 + y0^2/2`; the initial region, time barrier, final-time nonvanishing,
and unbounded analytic tail all need their actual hypotheses. A local
benchmark can justify more work without certifying the complete bound.
The [Gomila package](https://github.com/judegomila/dbn-lambda-01787854-candidate-audit)
is the first proposed target; its public claim is unreviewed, and its
[review scope](https://github.com/judegomila/dbn-lambda-01787854-candidate-audit/blob/main/REVIEW_SCOPE.md)
distinguishes numerical replay from the mathematical audit.

**Arithmetic covariance targets.** Existing quadratic and sextic notes
already contain much of the exact bookkeeping. Consolidate their weights,
masks, diagonal, coherent contributions, and signed remainder, then choose
one target. Distinguish an estimate contradicted by a lower bound, one
equivalent on restricted rows, a stronger family estimate, and one supplied
by an independent theorem. A proposed improvement must be supported by a
new applicable estimate rather than another change of notation.

**Function field covariance.** Match the integer family's structure before
invoking equidistribution: specify the field, nonprincipal base twist,
modulus degree, row degrees, masks, and limit. The untwisted principal
Möbius sum in degree at least two vanishes, so it is not a useful stand-in
for a nonzero coherent signal. A theorem in the large-field, fixed-degree
regime does not automatically control growing degrees or sparse families.

**Weil two packet detector.** Work at a declared displacement threshold
`a >= a_min > 0` and height range `|gamma| <= T`. Evaluate the full
functional and its error and conditioning costs, using the established
Weil-depth machinery where applicable. A negative contribution from one
hypothetical zero does not establish negativity of the whole form.
Quasi-RH supplies an upper bound on displacement, whereas a uniform finite
detector of this type needs a positive lower bound.

**Heat counterkernel features.** Specify exactly what theta-like counting
means, including its time-dependent main terms, remainder, and uniformity,
and what eventual reality and simplicity of high zeros means at positive
times. Then test whether a positive even kernel can retain those properties
while having a positive-time collision. Numerical positivity on a bounded
grid is insufficient, and failure of one finite-zero surgery does not
prove that the feature conjunction excludes collisions.

## Mathematical guardrails

An independently proved moment estimate may legitimately imply the desired
individual bound on coherent rows. That implication measures its strength;
it is not itself circularity. The decisive question is how the estimate is
proved and whether its assumptions are independent of the desired bound.

A counterkernel rules out only implications whose hypotheses it actually
satisfies. In particular, a positive-time collision counterkernel cannot
satisfy the collision-excluding stationary inequalities throughout the
required predecessor neighborhood. Generic positivity and identities must
be distinguished from those sufficient inequalities.

Keep diagonal lower bounds and exact normalizations visible. The original
universal D5 target is false for a full character family in its stated
normalization; the project therefore uses family-specific statements.
Likewise, finite RH verification and a heat barrier above positive
imaginary height do not automatically certify every zero in the entire
low-height heat box. Use each certificate in its proved domain.

The weakness of the direct strip-to-heat conversion is not a theorem that
every arithmetic use of quasi-RH in heat flow is exhausted. Any revived
route must identify its additional information explicitly.

external archive lookup when the first large dataset is produced. No
third-party PDFs belong in this tree.

## Chat histories

The base [chat-histories folder](chat-histories/README.md) holds shared
planning conversations, including the initial review and this setup request.
Every project has a separate history folder and numbering registry for its
own research chats. Project history READMEs link back to the shared context;
the initial conversation is stored once in the base archive.

Use the base [record_chat.py](record_chat.py) to capture completed public
Codex text messages or import one selected ChatGPT-style conversation.
The default destination is the base history; `--project PROJECT_NAME`
selects a project's history. Refreshing the same conversation retains its
number. The [recording guide](chat-histories/README.md) explains the commands,
timestamp provenance, size limit, exclusions, and refresh checks.

A capture taken while a response is in progress is a snapshot through that
moment. Later messages, including the final delivery of the capture task,
enter only on a subsequent refresh; missing text is never invented.
