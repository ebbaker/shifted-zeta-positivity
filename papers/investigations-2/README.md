## Background from the exponent descent brainstorms

The earlier program explored two routes: improving the exponent in a
claimed zero-free strip by a new arithmetic estimate, and excluding
positive-time collisions in the de Bruijn–Newman heat flow. The October 5
and September 30 manuscripts supplied different claimed boundaries and
family hypotheses. A zeta-only strip must not be substituted for a
Hecke-family hypothesis, and a source audit must pin the version and
statement it actually checks.

The original brainstorm contains overbroad claims; the corrected scopes

Several existing lanes explain the choice of projects. Fixed-scale
descent encounters an edge-mode obstruction, and an extremal strip need
not contain a zero at its boundary. Quadratic and sextic reductions

does not prove those moments. Mixed-family covariance and character

aggregate covariance. More parameter tuning of the same envelopes does

## Exact arithmetic targets and supporting experiments

**Short-row arithmetic.** The existing extraction refinement retains the
actual Möbius coefficients and deletion identity. Over
\(K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})\), fix the character \(\nu\), exclusions
\(S\), and smooth annular profile \(W\) as in that refinement, and use
the original sextic sum

\[
A_u(D)=\sum_{(n,S)=1}\mu_K(n)\nu(n)\chi_n(u)W(Nn/D),
\]

with every character deletion zero retained. For fixed \(0<h<1\) and
\(a\ge0\), a proposed estimate for every \(\varepsilon>0\) and all
sufficiently large \(D\),

\[
\sum_{0<Nu\le D^h}|A_u(D)|^2
\ll_{\nu,S,W,h,a,\varepsilon}D^{1+a+h+\varepsilon},
\]

would yield the exponent \((1+a)/2+5h/12\) by finite reuse of the same
moment. The missing input is that moment, not another extraction scheme.
The original scale formulas diagnose small cofactor shells, including
\(B=1\), as the region without the positive gap used in the old
induction. Any extension must justify the reduction in the new range and
control the surviving unit descendants. Keep the original Möbius moment,
canonical Gauss coefficient energy, and auxiliary compensated responses
distinct. A zero-free deduction also needs a suitable class of smooth
profiles; one fixed-profile estimate gives its own sum bound. Start with
the
[short-family refinement](../quasi-rh-exponent-descent/short_families/notes/1_SHORT_FAMILY_DESCENT_REFINEMENT_20261008.md)
and
[audited return ledger](inactive/arithmetic_source_audit/notes/TWO_POISSON_RETURN_AND_TERMINATION_20261012.md).

**Covariance ledgers.** Record row weights, coefficient energy, masks,
exact diagonal, coherent mass, and signed remainder. State whether the
target is a whole positive moment, a centered moment, or an off-diagonal
form, and pay every comparison term. The quadratic checkpoint at
\(h=7/5\) leaves a one-sided separated-prime bound of exponent
\(17/10\), with comparison error exponent \(67/40\). Entrywise
absolute values still cost order \(X^2\). The sextic auxiliary-response
ledger shows that scalar coherent-mean subtraction leaves local deletion
modes and the full complement unpaid. Reuse the
[quadratic checkpoint](../quasi-rh-exponent-descent/integer_quadratic_lift/notes/3_SEPARATED_PRIME_PAIR_CHECKPOINT_20261009.md)
and
[sextic deletion-mode ledger](../quasi-rh-exponent-descent/short_families/notes/29_DELETION_MODE_COVARIANCE_AND_FULL_RESPONSE_LEDGER_20261009.md).

**Function fields.** Translate the chosen integer family's coefficients,
character orders, row and modulus degrees, zero extensions, and limit.
Use a nonprincipal base twist or another nontrivial base L-function: the
untwisted polynomial Möbius sum in degree at least two vanishes. Compute
the same diagonal, coherent mass, and covariance in a small exact example.
A large-field fixed-degree theorem does not automatically control growing
degrees or a sparse family. Normalized Frobenius degree sequences can be
quasiperiodic; vertical periodicity in the complex variable does not supply
a common period for them.

**Weil packets.** Two separated packets can produce an exact negative
contribution from a hypothetical off-line zero. The complete functional
still includes other zeros, pole and archimedean terms, the prime side,
truncation, and conditioning. Declare \(a\ge a_{\min}>0\) and
\(|\gamma|\le T\), and compare the whole certified cost with the
existing basis. Quasi-RH supplies an upper displacement bound, not the
positive lower bound needed for that finite target. Increasing packet
separation increases the prime support exponentially.

**Counterkernel features.** The existing positive counterkernel already
matches the leading logarithmic zero density after rescaling. Distinguish
the full counting law, linear term, remainder, simplicity, and uniformity
in time from that leading term. State solidification as eventual reality
and simplicity of high zeros at every fixed positive time, with stronger
location and uniformity requirements listed separately. One finite-zero
surgery must prove global kernel positivity and the declared heat-time
properties. A positive grid scan or failed surgery attempt proves neither
the conjunction nor its impossibility.

## Corrections and decision rules that carry forward

1. A sufficient moment can legitimately imply its coherent-row target.
   Classify the estimate as impossible by a lower bound, equivalent on the
   restricted rows, stronger than that restriction, or supplied by an
   independent theorem. Equivalence alone is not circularity.
2. The original universal D5 formula is false for a full character family
   in its stated normalization. For prime modulus \(q\) and length
   \(N<q\), orthogonality gives
   \((q-1)\sum_n|a_n|^2\). Family size, weights and diagonal cannot be
   suppressed in a common exponent formula.
3. Model tests must preserve the hypotheses they challenge. Randomizing
   Möbius signs destroys its exact convolution identity. A counterkernel
   excludes only implications whose full hypotheses it satisfies; it
   cannot satisfy collision-excluding stationary inequalities throughout
   a neighborhood of its positive-time collision.
4. A zero-free barrier above positive imaginary height does not prove
   reality and simplicity throughout the whole low-height heat box.
   Finite RH verification at time zero and later barrier coverage have
   different domains. The broad historical fact (D) in project 17 reviews
   12 and 13 must be read with the October 11 correction, not used as an
   unconditional low-height theorem.
5. The Newman objective is \(t_0+y_0^2/2\), with initial region,
   intermediate-time barrier, final-time nonvanishing, and infinite tail
   all supplied. The smaller residual belongs to the corrected center;
   derivatives, normalization, cutoffs, and endpoints must move with it.
6. Consequence checks must match hypotheses and aspects. The positive-slot
   fourth moment at \(\kappa=3/4\) already assumes the relevant family
   boundary. It cannot independently test that boundary. An unexpectedly
   strong consequence calls for inspecting the proof, not automatic
   rejection; a weak direct strip-to-heat consequence does not exhaust
   every possible arithmetic use of the strip.
7. Continue only for a new applicable estimate, complete certified gain,
   faithful diagnostic, or precise obstruction. Stop a target below an
   exact lower bound, a proof assuming its conclusion, or a recurrence
   that returns to its target without a paid decreasing parameter.
