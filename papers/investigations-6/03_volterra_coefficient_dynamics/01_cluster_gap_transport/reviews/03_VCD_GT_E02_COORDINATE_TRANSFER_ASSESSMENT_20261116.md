## Collar and coordinate comparisons

- With \(H=\log(F/G)\) holomorphic on a collar,
  \(H'=F'/F-G'/G\). The log-series example in §1 uses the paid
  \(\delta_N/m_N<1\) on the compact circle, so the same ratio is
  strictly below one on a neighborhood there for the fixed time.
  The required bounded log and the model count remain separate
  obligations; the strict argument-principle comparison transfers
  a count with multiplicity. E02's conditional contour estimates
  have no intrinsic strip restriction. When E01 supplies log
  separation or the relative-error payment, that payment must be
  justified in its stated time and spatial domain.
  [Manuscript §10.4][ms] [E01 §4][e01]
- Integrating by parts in the \(k\)-th contour moment gives
  \(kL\rho^{k-1}\ell_N/(2\pi)\), since \(\Gamma\) is a closed
  positively oriented circle and the logarithm is single-valued
  there. The two roots' distances from \(a\) are at most \(\rho-b\),
  so the same holds for both center offsets. The contribution of
  the difference of the center-offset squares is at most
  \(2(\rho-b)e_N\). The resulting constants are
  \(e_N=L\ell_N/(4\pi)=\rho\ell_N/2\) and
  \(d_N=L\rho\ell_N/(2\pi)+2(\rho-b)e_N=\rho(2\rho-b)\ell_N\).
- For either pair polynomial \(P=Q\) or \(P=Q_N\), the rational
  term \((P'/P)/Q_*\) has all poles inside the circle and decays as
  \(O(z^{-3})\), with \(Q_*\) either denominator used in §2.
  Its contour integral vanishes by enlargement. This accounts
  for \(G'/G\) even when evaluated at physical coordinates whose
  roots differ from or coincide with the model roots. The remaining
  holomorphic unit term gives the divided difference by residues,
  or its derivative at a repeated root by Cauchy's formula.
  Reality follows from conjugation symmetry. [Parent Note 28 §2][n28]
- The common-coordinate term is the integral of \(-H(1/Q)'\).
  Each distance to the two physical roots is at least \(b\) on the
  contour, giving \(|(1/Q)'|\le2/b^3\) from the product rule and
  \(L\ell_N/(\pi b^3)\) after integration. This factor agrees with
  the inherited collar comparison under the unit derivative
  convention. [Rodgers–Tao branch Note 02 §4][rt]
- Subtracting kernels gives \((Q_N-Q)/(Q Q_N)\) with
  \(Q_N-Q=(c-c_N)(2z-c-c_N)+(D-D_N)\). Each denominator costs
  \(b^2\) and the center factor is at most \(4\rho-2b\) because
  \(|z-a|=\rho\) and both centers have offset at most \(\rho-b\).
  Multiplying by \(M_N\) and integrating gives exactly
  \(LM_N[(4\rho-2b)e_N+d_N]/(2\pi b^4)\). This includes the center
  and discriminant changes with both centers and discriminants
  computed for their own fields. Repeated roots leave all boundary
  denominators and constants valid.

## Signed time and costs

At a simple physical root, the normalized scalar term in the heat
equation vanishes and \(r'=F_{zz}/F_z\).
For \(r_\pm=c\pm s,\ s^2=D\), this gives
\(r_\pm'=\pm1/s+2h(r_\pm)\) and
\(D'=s(r_+'-r_-')=2+4KD\). At a physical double root the centered
second moment has derivative \(4\) by the heat equation and residue
at the double, and the centered first moment is zero at that time.
Consequently \(D'=2\) there. The regular contour formula for \(K\)
then covers the double with no inverse gap. Only the physical field
is differentiated in time. The proof takes the model as a sequence
of snapshots with the needed measurable data.
[Parent Note 27][n27] [Manuscript §10.4][ms]

Integrating \(|K-K_N|\le\kappa_N\) over \([t,v]\) gives the explicitly
nonnegative error \(4\int_t^v\kappa_N\). The displayed integrability
premise includes \(\kappa_N\) itself, particularly the product of the
model derivative allowance and the coordinate allowances. The
exponent bounds in (6) follow by monotonicity for real \(S\) and \(S_N\).
The model evaluation error \(4\int_t^v\eta_K\) and primitive error
\(\eta_S(t)\) belong inside the upper exponent as in (7). The final
quadrature and arithmetic error \(\eta_{\mathcal C}\) bounds the
remaining error in evaluating the upper integral itself. Point samples
give no continuous-time enclosures. The proof preserves the sign of the
model contribution throughout.

Differentiating \(S(t,v)\) in its lower limit gives \(-4K(t)\),
so \((e^{S(t,v)}D(t))'=2e^{S(t,v)}\). The incoming gap and exact
cost have equality at \(u=T\) in the checked quadratic \(K=0\),
and the cost strictly exceeds the gap when \(u<T<v\).
The shifted model has \(K_N=0\) even across either discriminant's
zero, since both units are constant. The check distinguishes exact
cost saturation from possible slack in the generic upper allowance.
If \(L_v\ge B\), strict reality at a later time follows by deleting
a strictly positive portion of the cost integral for \(u<v\).
The endpoint comparison allows \(D(u)=0\).

A physical Jacobi model and its boundary \(z\)-derivative use the
same links through \(\beta_{N-1}\). Its model count and moments have
the same coefficient input and still require their actual contour
evaluation. If the log payment uses the E01 directed prefix bound,
it needs no next link. An exact leading amplitude costs \(\beta_N\),
which also specifies the next physical prefix. These input counts
buy neither a model count automatically nor a proved gain over
\(D_N-d_N\) or over root jets where slope floors are available.

## Control verdict and application boundary

The quadratic check verifies the time orientation and shows that
geometric transfer can hold on a tube whose exact incoming margin
vanishes. The positive weighted-square, reflected and smooth
shape/primitive events provide the same scope check where the local
counts and geometry are met. They fail their stated complete
reflection, coefficients or square arithmetic premises, which
the local transfer leaves unused. The Euler threshold alone pays
no fixed event tube. The finite-prime and finite-head controls
restrict finite-data inferences and retain their different location
and threshold qualifications. Finite-profile and atomic controls
lack the full canonical profile, and the RH-conditional normalized
endpoint controls retain RH while losing the ordinary arithmetic.
Changed observations fail the physical readout premise, and the
profile relaxation supplies no prescribed physical field carrying
a counted pair. No canonical source inequality was tested on these
families. [Control catalog][catalog] [Parent Note 28 §5][n28]

The proof transfers a signed quantity on one fixed, already paid
two-root geometry. The canonical count, collar bounds, positive
buffer, derivative and time-evaluation allowances, and independently
acquired incoming gap remain necessary for any proposed application.
The integration result has no claimed improvement over the
same-information direct comparison. No numerical certificates were
rerun, and the proof files were not edited in this assessment.
