## 1. Fixed tube, relative remainders, and signed profiles

The limit varies model degree \(N\) and evaluates that field and its truncations

Note06 give the two-zero count, outer modulus floor and common buffer. Contour
moments keep the signed half-gap \(D\) and center \(c\) regular through real,
conjugate and double pairs. The exact heat law gives \(D'=2+4KD\), and the
actual model equation has the additional term \(r_N\).
[Coordinate transfer][n04] [Residual law][n05] [Fixed event][n06]

For centered moments about \(c(t)\), contour integration by parts gives
\(D_j-D=-\langle(z-c)\log(G_j/F)\rangle-(c_j-c)^2\).
Expansion on the fixed collar yields the sign and linear term in Note09 (5).
The identity \((z-c)/Q=\tfrac12[(z-z_+)^{-1}+(z-z_-)^{-1}]\)
takes the average of the regular numerator values, including a repeated root.
The remainder on the circle is \(O(p_j(t)^2 Z_*^{4j})\).
At \(t=T+s/N^2\), division by \(p_j(T)|a|^{2j}\) leaves
\(O(p_j(T)(Z_*^2/|a|)^{2j})\) for \(j=N,N+1\). The logarithmic norm
flow pays the single-time ratio and the factorial upper envelope makes
this remainder smaller than every inverse power. This works with arbitrarily
small positive links. In particular, \(e_0=e_N(T)=D(T)-D_N(T)\) has eventual
sign \((-1)^{N+1}\operatorname{sgn} A_*\). [Phase and norm laws][n03] [Note09 §2][n09]

The physical expansions \(D(T+s/N^2)=2s/N^2+O(s^2/N^4)\) and
\(c(T+s/N^2)=a+O(|s|/N^2)\), the factorial-small model coordinate errors,
and \(|\sqrt{x}-\sqrt{y}|\le\sqrt{|x-y|}\) for real \(x,y\) on the chosen
real and imaginary branches give \(N(z_\pm-a)=\pm\sqrt{2s}+O(N^{-1})\)
for both pairs, including the model double. The local logarithm of \(z/a\)
gives the stated \(O(N^{-1})\) monomial phase error on each fixed compact
real \(s\)-interval. The unit values equal \(A_*+O(N^{-1})\).
Together with the inherited \(O(\log^2(N+1)/N)\) estimates for the two phase
factors this yields Note09 (2)'s rates. The proof uses exact time equations
and pointwise phase estimates. [Note06 §§1–4][n06] [Note09 §§2–3][n09]

Put \(C=C_a\), \(\alpha=4/a^2\), \(B(s)=\alpha\int_0^s C(q)\,dq\), and
\(H=C-1-B\). The negative model-root average for \(R_N/A_N\) has leading

\((-1)^{N-1}\) in \(R_N\) and \(-(-1)^N\) in \(e_0\).

## 2. Deflated curvature and agreement of both drift calculations

In the local field \(Q_d-\epsilon E\), with \(Q_d=x^2-d^2\), \(d^2=2s\)
and \(E=e^{kx}\), \(k=2/a\), the contour moments give the monic pair
polynomial variation \(-I_d\). Here \(I_d\) interpolates \(E\) at the pair
and becomes \(1+kx\) at \(d=0\). Hence the unit varies by \(-V_d\), with
\(V_d=(E-I_d)/Q_d\). The additional first-order denominator term in the
unit-log-derivative representation vanishes because the original unit is
constant. In the full field-log-derivative representation, the term from
moving the polynomial is proportional to \(Q_d'I_d/Q_d^3\). Its integral
vanishes since all its poles are enclosed and it decays faster than \(1/|x|\)
at infinity. Integrating by parts leaves
\[
 \kappa_a(s)=-\frac1{2\pi i}\oint \frac{2x e^{kx}}{(x^2-2s)^3}\,dx .
\]
Expansion on the fixed circle gives the entire series in Note10 (5).
At \(s=0\) the value is \(-2k^4/4!=-4/(3a^4)\), equal to \(-V_0''(0)\).
The divided difference gives \(8s\kappa_a(s)=C'(s)-\alpha C(s)\).
Perturbations of degree at most two keep the deflated unit spatially
constant, in agreement with the functional check. [Note10 §§1–2][n10]

For the physical transfer, normalization by \(N^2/A_*\) and
\(\partial_tF_T(a)=-2A_*\) give convergence to \(Q_d\) on fixed scaled
disks. Division of the signed field error by
\(\epsilon_N=N^2L_N(T,a)/A_*\) gives convergence to \(e^{kx}\) by the norm
ratio and phase laws. The two auxiliary fixed circles in \(x=N(z-a)\)
give common boundary floors and two-zero counts for the fields on a
common sufficiently small complex perturbation-parameter disk.
Spatial Cauchy estimates then control the contour integrands, including
the moment polynomials. Cauchy's formula in the perturbation parameter
gives uniform convergence of the first derivatives and a uniform quadratic
Taylor remainder, which at \(\epsilon_N\) is \(o(|\epsilon_N|)\).
The curvature scales as \(K_z=N^2K_x\) and
\(-\epsilon_N/(N^2e_0)\to1\). This proves the sign and the \(N^4e_0\)
normalization. The auxiliary circles lie inside the original fixed
physical tube and select its pair by equality of counts. Their role
is spatial. [Note10 §3][n10]

Making the substitution \(t=T+q/N^2\) in the normalized drift gives the
uniform integrand limit
\[
 4W_{N,v}(T+q/N^2)
 \frac{(K-K_N)(T+q/N^2)}{N^4e_0} N^2 D(T+q/N^2)
 \ \longrightarrow\ 8q\kappa_a(q).
\]
The oriented integral is \(8\int_0^s q\kappa_a(q)\,dq=C(s)-1-B(s)\).
This proves agreement with Note09's exact error identity. The
quantitative error rate in Note09 comes from its fixed-contour argument.

For any nonzero real sequence \(s_N\to0\), the same integrand on the
segment between \(0\) and \(s_N\) equals \(8q\kappa_a(0)+q\,o(1)\)
uniformly. In detail, \(N^2D(T+q/N^2)=2q+O(q^2/N^2)\),
\(W=1+O(|s_N|/N^2)\), and \(\kappa_a(q)=\kappa_a(0)+O(|s_N|)\).
The uniform additive \(o(1)\) in Note10 is thus multiplied by \(q\).
Either orientation gives
\(\mathfrak H_N(s_N)/e_0=4\kappa_a(0)s_N^2+o(s_N^2)
=-16s_N^2/(3a^4)+o(s_N^2)\).
This derived relative asymptotic uses both notes. Note09 §4's integrated
error-identity proof alone leaves the relative subcritical remainder open.
Using \(\mathfrak R_N(s_N)/e_0\sim-4s_N/a^2\) gives the displayed ratio
\(\mathfrak H_N(s_N)/\mathfrak R_N(s_N)\sim4s_N/(3a^2)\).

## 3. Cancellation and common target
