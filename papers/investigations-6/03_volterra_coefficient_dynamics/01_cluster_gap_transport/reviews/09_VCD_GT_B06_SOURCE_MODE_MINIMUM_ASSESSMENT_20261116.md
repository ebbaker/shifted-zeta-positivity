Lower weights give a lower envelope with a nonnegative lower initial
anchor. In reverse, the actual numerator is at least
\(N_+=L_v-2\int_t^v W_{k_+}(s,v)\). When \(N_+\ge0\), dividing it by
the upper weight gives a lower bound. When \(N_+<0\), the lower weight
gives the valid lower bound. In the scalar example \(K=0\) on \([-1,0]\)
gives \(D(-1)=-1\). Then \(D'=2-D\) on \([-2,-1)\) yields
