## Analytical findings

[Note27][mass] correctly minimizes the joint conjugate weight. With
\(s=x^2,u=y^2,v=s-u-D>0\), its derivative in \(u\) has negative numerator
\(v^2-4s(s-D)\). The derivatives controlling \(D\) and \(s\) have no
interior minimum on the stipulated positive domain. The minimum therefore
occurs at \(y^2=\delta^2\) and one of the four \(x,D\) corners. The
hypothesis \(L^2>\delta^2+d\) pays every denominator and sign step,
including the degenerate endpoint cases.

The maxima of the old and sharp per-occurrence allowances are respectively
\(5/(44+4\sqrt{66})\) and \(1/[4(1+\sqrt{6/5})]\). The calculations of
their stationary points and the check of the other \(D\) corner are correct.
The sharpness assertion concerns an abstract horizontal rectangle, explicitly
distinguished from a conjugation-invariant count-one region. Adding the
fixed mirror allowance leaves nine old-weight occurrences below 0.59 and
four sharp-weight occurrences below 0.59. These are upper bounds on the
**specified lower allowances**, not on actual exterior mass. Ten and five
are necessary counts, not sufficient canonical manifests. The proof
correctly retains the width-one initial slice and states that stronger
coordinate, vertical or correlation information changes the estimating class.

The singleton implication is valid: a conjugation-invariant region with
exact multiplicity count one has a real simple zero. Its positive contribution
is at least \(1/(U^2+d)\). This proves reality only of a selected zero with
a paid count; it neither assumes an all-real remainder nor supplies that count.

[Note28][fourier] correctly obtains the natural difference-frequency matrix
by reflecting the second spectral variable. Its displayed principal
determinant is negative for two distinct positive radii. The note carefully
limits the conclusion to that unchanged matrix: no pointwise negative value,
uniqueness of representation, or exclusion of other Fourier groupings follows.

The finite absolute allowance is exactly
\(10\sum_j\omega_j^2x_j^6-3m_2m_4\). Its maximum-weight sufficient failure
test and degree-six moment requirement are correct. For a nondegenerate even
absolutely continuous positive measure, the weighted pushforward under
\(u+v\) has no atom at zero and has total mass \(3m_2m_4\). The same
constant-mode/absolute-remainder allowance is consequently negative. This
does not assert negativity of the canonical numerator. The normalized
double-point target, vertical two-point average, full-time requirement and
