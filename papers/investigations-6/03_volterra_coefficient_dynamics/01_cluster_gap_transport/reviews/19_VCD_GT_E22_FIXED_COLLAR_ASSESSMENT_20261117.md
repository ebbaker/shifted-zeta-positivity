## 1. E20: retained dissipation and fixed-collar occupation

**Stage A proof pass complete.** [Note40 §§1–2][e20] differentiates at
simple relevant zeros using \(z'_j=H_{zz}/H_z\). Pairwise
symmetrization puts the two mixed Hessian terms at opposite signs.
An active unordered conjugate pair \(x\pm iy\) has constant \(x\)
along its chord and contributes \(-2a_U(x)g'(y)/y\). This is
one negative contribution per nonreal occurrence. At a real root the
retained summand is defined as zero. This also covers a double at an
event time. The remaining vertical chord numerator is nonnegative by
convexity. The exterior imaginary series converges absolutely, and
its conjugate-pair numerator before the force's minus sign is
\(2y(s^2+y^2-v^2)\). The horizontal separation \(|s|\ge U/4\ge2\)
and the width-one strip give it the sign of \(y\). A real exterior
root has the same sign rule, so the exterior vertical term is
nonpositive.

The exterior horizontal field retains its full opposite-paired
convention at \(w=x+4i\); the difference to \(z\) is bounded by
\(5|x-u|^{-2}\) per exterior occurrence. Both height directions enter
the closed-unit shell bound. Integrating the decreasing shell majorant
from \(m-1\) pays the remainder
\(10A[2\log U+\log(m+1)+1]/(m-1)\). The finite safe-line subtraction
costs \(12A(\log U)^2\), and the complete field bounds
\(\#\mathcal C_U\le6AU\log U\), \(|E_U|\le100A(\log U)^2\) give the
unchanged horizontal allowances \(36A^2\|\chi''\|_\infty G(\log U)^2\)
and \(1200A^2\|\chi'\|_\infty G(\log U)^3\). Hence the coefficient in
Note40 (7) is exactly
\(D_\chi=A^2(36\|\chi''\|_\infty+1200\|\chi'\|_\infty)\).

Local multiset continuity across support crossings and the padded
compact-box Hermite splitting give a continuous \(Q\) and finitely
many relevant multiple-event times, including slab endpoints.
The chord and field give a common finite two-sided derivative bound
on the complementary intervals for each fixed \(U,g\). Joining by
continuity gives absolute continuity and zero collision atoms.
The retained sum is Borel and bounded using the population bound and
\(|g'(y)/y|\le\|g''\|_\infty\) away from real roots. The auxiliary
regularity constants do not enter the integrated one-sided inequality.

For [Note40 §§3–4][e20], \(r_0=a/L\) is fixed in time. The test is
\(C^2\) at zero with \(g''(y)=1+2|y|/r_0\) and
\(G=1/2+L/(3a)\). The three plateaus cover \([T/2,5T/2]\), the sum
of their weights is at most three, and their joint support fits in

strict transverse thresholds includes \(|y|=1\) and multiplicities,

\((B_b/8)(279/b^2+837/(ab^3))T/L\). The three transport logarithm
cubes sum to \(3L^3+6L(\log2)^2\le4L^3\).

The [E19][e19] center separation applies to disjoint occurrence pairs,
including a counted double. In the paid collar \(D\le r_0^2/16\),
so a negative summand requires \(y^2>15r_0^2/32\), which implies
\(|y|>2r_0/3\). Its possible centers are within \(|y|+r_0/4\) of
the exterior real coordinate \(u\), and their count is at most
\(5/3+8|y|/(3r_0)\le(8/3)(1+|y|/r_0)\). The argument includes both
signs of \(D\), preserves multiplicity, and uses the plateau cover
at each witness. The omitted terms have positive real numerator
because \(|\Re(\rho-c)|>R\ge3\), \(|\Im\rho|\le1\) and

At \(\tau\asymp L^{-2}\) the averaged allowance thus has order

## 3. E21: exact full interaction and its time derivative

All six pins in the root-issued [Stage B addendum][addendum] matched.
[Note41 §§1–2][e21] uses \(H=QA\), \(Q=(z-c)^2-D\). The fixed circle
centered at \(c(t_*)\) with radius \(r_0/2\) lies between the targets
at distance at most \(r_0/4\) and the exterior at distance at least
\(r_0\). The argument-principle first and second moments give
\(c=J_1/2\) and \(D=J_2/2-c^2\), analytic through a double. The
factor \(A\) has a joint analytic extension inside from the
zero-free contour data. In the deflated PDE the moving numerator
is \(2u c'+D'-2-4u g(c+u)\), \(g=A_z/A\). Since \(Q\) divides that
numerator, the linear interpolant of \(u g(c+u)\) gives
\(c'=g(z_-)+g(z_+)\) and \(D'=2-4DM\). The same interpolation is
confluent at \(D=0\) and analytic for the vertical pair when \(D<0\).
The exterior term \((z-\rho)^{-1}/Q(z)\) gives the interior residue sum
\(-1/Q(\rho)\). Removing \(Q_z/Q^2\) changes the integral by zero.
Thus \(M=-(2\pi i)^{-1}\oint_\Gamma (H_z/H)Q^{-1}\,dz\)
with full occurrence multiplicity. This also fixes the sign of \(M\).

For simple finite roots, writing \(P=\sum w_jm_j\) and
\(V=\sum w_jm_j^2\), the center moves with \(c'=-2P\). The exterior
relative velocity has target term \(4w_jm_j\), center term \(2P\)
and exterior term \(2\sum_{k\ne j}(w_j-w_k)^{-1}\).
A constant drift in the log derivative would translate every root
and the center equally. For the antisymmetric velocity contribution,
\((w_jm_j-w_km_k)/(w_j-w_k)=-(D+w_jw_k)m_jm_k\).
Multiplication by \(m_j+m_k\) and subtraction of \(m_jm_k\) gives
the quotient used in Note41 (5). Summing the paired terms adds
\(2(M^2-S_2)+4D(MS_2-S_3)+4(PV-S_2-DS_3)\). The \(P V\) and
\(D M S_2\) cancel with the velocity and gap terms, leaving
\(M'=2M^2-12S_2-16DS_3\). The finite multiple-root limit perturbs
the root factors at the reference time, fixes the contour count,
and passes through the analytic moment and derivative formulas.
It includes both target and exterior repetitions.

The canonical cosine-integrand envelope gives joint time and space
holomorphy and order at most one on the slab, as in the pinned
[GT Note21 §1][gt21] and Note41. The strip and unit counts give
an absolute inverse-square sum outside a bounded set. Complete
opposite-paired Hadamard products converge locally with spatial
derivatives. Their evenness and that of \(H\) remove the residual
linear exponential. The truncation at each \(t_*\) keeps whole
opposite and conjugate groups and every occurrence at a repeated
location. At that fixed time the polynomial heat equation gives
\(\partial_t(F_z/F)=-F_{zzz}/F+F_zF_{zz}/F^2\) on the same contour.
Uniform spatial convergence there and the strict boundary clearance
give convergence of \(c,D,c',D',M,M'\), retaining
\(Q_t=-2(z-c)c'-D'\). The retained terms of \(S_2,S_3\) use the
same \(c,D\) once the target factors have entered the truncation.
Their remaining inverse-power tails vanish absolutely. The passage
uses only each polynomial's time slice and derivative at \(t_*\).
The collar protects every exterior denominator, and all target
derivatives are contour observables through a double.

## 4. E21: signed forcing, comparison and negative visibility

For \(p=1,2,3\) in [Note41 §3][e21], the near shell costs at most
\(4C_{\rm u}(16/15)^p a^{-2p}L\). The two closed unit intervals
on each far shell \(n\le|\Re\rho-c|<n+1\) have combined count
\(2C_{\rm u}(2L+\log(n+1))\), covering both height directions.
The bound \( |\mu_j|\le (16/15)L^{-2}n^{-2}\), together with
\(\sum n^{-2p}\le2\), \(\sum n^{-2p}\log(n+1)\le3\), pays the
far sum \(10C_{\rm u}(16/15)^p L^{1-2p}\).
Using \(a\le L\) gives \(B_p=14C_{\rm u}(16/15)^p/a^{2p}\).
The independent safe-line subtraction bounds \(g\) on the inner
disk by \(8C_{\rm u}L^2/a+95C_{\rm u}L\). For the normalized
forcing set \(B=B_1\), \(K=12B_2+16dB_3\), so that
\(|m|\le BL\), \(m_\sigma=2m^2-\mathcal F\) and
\(|\mathcal F|\le KL\). The scale derivative is
\(m_\sigma=\ell^4 M'\). The square and cube sums are real by
conjugation, and their signed combination is bounded using
absolute allowances. The square and cube near powers are
\(L/a^4\) and \(dL/a^6\), respectively.

With \(h=\sqrt{KL/2}\), \(k=\max(BL/h,2)\) and
\(u_0=\operatorname{arccoth}k\), the finite barrier
\(v=-h\coth(2h\sigma+u_0)\) has \(v(0)\le -BL\).
The integrating factor for
\((m-v)'-2(m+v)(m-v)\ge0\) preserves \(m\ge v\).
Monotonicity of \(\coth\) yields \(m\ge-h\coth(2h\sigma)\)
for \(\sigma>0\). A fixed positive elapsed scaled buffer gives
the one-sided \(O(\sqrt L)\) negative allowance from \(O(L)\).
Integrating \(-v\) gives the half logarithm of the hyperbolic-sine ratio
in Note41 (16), bounded by \(hs+\tfrac12\log[(k+1)/2]\) since
\(e^{u_0}/(2\sinh u_0)=(k+1)/2\). With
\(\int M\,dt=\int m\,d\sigma\) over corresponding intervals,
this pays \(e^{4hs}((k+1)/2)^2=O(L)\exp(O(s\sqrt L))\)
for the backward transport multiplier, compared with the raw
count allowance \(e^{4BLs}\). The incoming value of \(D\) is
separate from the initial count bound on \(m\). The lower
barrier still allows each fixed adverse normalized margin at
large \(T\).

The physical modulus is bounded by
\(L^2(2B^2L^2+KL)\le(2B^2+K)L^4\). A margin \(m(t_*)\le-\epsilon\)
gives \(m\le-\epsilon/2\) on a chosen side of length
\(\epsilon/[2(2B^2+K)L^4]\) contained entirely in the paid tube.
For several pairs the window must be common. The local counted
contour enforces uniqueness of each continuation, so distinct
eligible pairs at the snapshot remain distinct on that window.
Strict negativity supplies a slice margin but no uniform duration
independent of its size. For a guaranteed negative window of
length \(\Delta\), dividing E20's occupation allowance costs
\(O(T/(L\Delta))+O(L^4)\). The modulus window thus yields
\(O(TL^3/\epsilon)+O(L^4)\), and independently paid negative
visibility of order \(\ell^2\) gives \(O(TL)+O(L^4)\). These
displayed upper allowances furnish no improved fixed-collar
snapshot order over the existing \(O(TL)\) cap. For every
\(\Delta\le1/2\) the explicit initial term alone in the displayed
division is at least \(2B_b(93/b^2+279/(ab^3))T/L\).
The upper-allowance test strictly below one therefore fails as
\(T\) grows for this fixed class and these bounds. The calculation
does not bound actual occupation, exception counts or lifetimes
from below.

\(O(L^{-2})+O(L^3/T)\) occupation allowance and the loss of that
aggregate power saving at duration comparable to \(\ell^2\).
It states the conditional \(O(\sqrt L)\) negative allowance and
the transport multiplier bound with their independently paid tube
and anchor obligations. Mere tube survival provides less information
than a paid window of negative visibility. The reduced short
tube in Audit18 does not acquire the original E21 tube or its
elapsed microscopic buffer. The synthesis comparisons use the
same complete source, count, collar, signed interaction, heat
identities, and incoming anchor for any transport application on
both sides. They establish no computational or total-cost advantage.

The control screens correctly distinguish inherited canonical inputs
from the local structural heat identities, including finite families
that can satisfy coarse upper counts. The source theorem is reused
under its existing internal assessment. The synthesis records
the requested model dispatches with their provenance limit, and
uses B12 only as a proposed conceptual next gate for the actual
signed forcing at fixed collar. It activates no numerical question,
source campaign or execution task. The present outputs are
analytical estimates on their specified classes. All-pair sign,
new reality coverage and independently earned specialist validation
remain outside their conclusions.
