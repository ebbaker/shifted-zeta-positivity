# B11: fixed-collar occupation and the cost of time visibility

17 November 2026, America/New_York. Prepared for **Edward Baker** with
OpenAI Codex LLM assistance. Conceptual collaborator dispatched
**gpt-6-astra / ultra**. Actual root model/effort are unavailable;
dispatch records are not independent serving-backend attestations.

**Decision and proposed analytical contracts; not yet accepted execution.**
Select a retained transverse-dissipation estimate for the time occupation
of negative isolated-pair interactions. At a fixed normalized collar it
has a genuine two-logarithm gain on a fixed physical-time interval.
Compare it with the exact interaction evolution on an already paid
count-two tube. Its Riccati inequality improves the interior normalized
negative allowance from O(log T) to O(sqrt(log T)); its local modulus
pays a short persistence interval. Their combination does not resolve
one exception: the occupation gain disappears on spacing-squared time. This is a scoped
failure of that combination, not a no-go for conditional arithmetic or
other signed estimates.

## 1. Fixed source, scale and target

Keep the complete canonical theta field, physical time
\(0\le t\le1/2\), and \(\partial_tH=-H_{zz}\). Use
\(T\ge64\), \(L=\log T\), \(\ell=L^{-1}\), fixed across the height band
and throughout time. Fix constants \(d\ge0\) and
\(a\ge\max(2,4\sqrt d)\), with \(a\le L\), and \(3\le R\le T/4\).
The principal comparison keeps **a fixed as T grows**.

At each time the eligible pair class is exactly E19's: unordered,
multiplicity-counted conjugation-invariant pairs with
\[
 c\in[T,2T],\qquad D\in\mathbb R,\quad |D|\le d\ell^2,
 \qquad |\rho-c|\ge r_0:=a\ell
 \quad\hbox{for every exterior occurrence}.                 \tag{1}
\]
Count-two and the exterior collar are independent premises. Both signs of
D and D=0 remain allowed. Let \(N_-(t)\) count the pairs with \(S_R<0\);
\(\{M<0\}\) is a subset by the paid nonnegative far contribution.
The physical law \(D'=2-4MD\) and any incoming anchor remain separate.
No lower bound for the eligible-pair population is assumed.

E19's center separation and negative-kernel incidence imply
\[
 N_-(t)\le\sum_{\substack{\Re\rho\in\mathcal B_T\\|\Im\rho|>2a/(3L)}}
 \left(\frac53+\frac{8|\Im\rho|}{3r_0}\right),
 \qquad \mathcal B_T=[T/2,5T/2].                            \tag{2}
\]
The existing slice bound is
\(N_-(t)\le C_bTL e^{-2ba/3}\), fixed \(0<b<1/2\).
It gives only \(C_b\tau TL e^{-2ba/3}\) after integration over an
interval of length \(\tau\). No common-time dynamics was consumed in
that last integration.

## 2. Proposal I: retain the conjugate dissipation already present in the full-field identity

The extra information is the **common physical heat flow**, used through
an unretained nonnegative term of [TND-O Note21 §§3–4][o21]. It is not
another fixed-slice density assumption. For its fixed broad window
\(a_U(x)=\chi((x-3U/2)/U)\), put
\[
 Q_{U,g}=\sum_j a_U(x_j)g(y_j),\qquad
 \mathcal D_{U,g}=\sum_{j:y_j\ne0}a_U(x_j)\frac{g'(y_j)}{y_j}.
                                                                    \tag{3}
\]
For any fixed even convex \(g\in C^2([-1,1])\), \(g(0)=0\), the
proposed sharpened inequality is
\[
 Q_{U,g}(\beta)+\int_\alpha^\beta\mathcal D_{U,g}(t)\,dt
 \le Q_{U,g}(\alpha)+D_\chi\|g\|_\infty(\beta-\alpha)(\log U)^3.
                                                                    \tag{4}
\]
Here \(D_\chi\) is precisely the established complete-field boundary
constant. The retained term counts nonreal occurrences only. Real roots
are **not** assigned the limiting value \(g''(0)\).

The proof route is fixed. In the predecessor's chord kernel, a conjugate
pair \(x\pm iy\), \(y\ne0\), has zero horizontal displacement and
contributes
\(K=a_U(x)g'(y)/y\). Its term in \(Q'=-2\sum_{j<k}K+\cdots\)
is \(-2a_U(x)g'(y)/y\), exactly minus the two occurrence terms in
(3). Keep this subset of the favorable vertical terms. All remaining
vertical terms and the complete exterior vertical field have the same
favorable signs as before. The horizontal Hessian/exterior allowances
are unchanged. No inverse gap or root velocity is added to (4).

On simple relevant-root intervals this proves the differential version.
The predecessor's compact-box Hermite splitting supplies finitely many
multiple-event times and absolute continuity of Q. For fixed U,g, the
new integrand is bounded by a finite population times
\(\sup_{0<|y|\le1}|g'(y)/y|\). Integrating across the finite event set
therefore pays collisions, including real crossings, without an atom,
a simplicity hypothesis, or a continuous choice of root labels.
E20 must verify this assembly rather than simply cite the weaker (6)
of the predecessor.

Select the fixed test
\[
 g(y)=\frac{y^2}{2}+\frac{|y|^3}{3r_0},\qquad
 \frac{g'(y)}y=1+\frac{|y|}{r_0}\ (y\ne0),\qquad
 G:=\|g\|_\infty=\frac12+\frac{L}{3a}.                    \tag{5}
\]
It is C² and convex. The cubic term pays the large-transverse-mark
factor in (2); replacing it by a strip maximum would lose a logarithm.
There is no claim that this test is optimal for growing a.

Use exactly the three broad profiles \(U=T/2,T,2T\), and set
\(A_T=a_{T/2}+a_T+a_{2T}\). Then \(A_T\ge1\) on \(\mathcal B_T\),
\(0\le A_T\le3\), and its support is contained in \([T/4,5T]\).
The five dyadic bands with lower endpoints
\(T/4,T/2,T,2T,4T\) cover that support. T>=64 guarantees every density
input is at a scale >=8 and \(\log U\ge(2/3)L\) on these bands.
The established exponential transverse density implies, for k=2,3,
\[
 \sum_{U\le x_j\le2U}|y_j|^k
 \le\frac{k!B_b}{b^k}\frac{U}{(\log U)^{k-1}}.             \tag{6}
\]
Layer cake over the strict transverse tail justifies (6), including
multiplicities; real roots contribute zero. Closed dyadic endpoints can
only overcount. Consequently one admissible explicit initial allowance
for \(Q_A=Q_{T/2,g}+Q_{T,g}+Q_{2T,g}\) is
\[
 Q_A(\alpha)\le\frac{B_b}{8}
       \left(\frac{279}{b^2}+\frac{837}{ab^3}\right)\frac{T}{L}.
                                                                    \tag{7}
\]
The factor3 from overlapping profiles is included. This numerical
constant is deliberately conservative and must be checked in E20.
The sum of the three transport logarithm cubes is at most \(4L^3\).

For all \(0\le\alpha\le\beta\le1/2\), \(\tau=\beta-\alpha\),
(2), (4) and (5), dropping only the nonnegative final Q, give the target
\[
 \boxed{
 \int_\alpha^\beta N_-(t)\,dt
 \le B_b\left(\frac{93}{b^2}+\frac{279}{ab^3}\right)\frac{T}{L}
   +\frac{32}{3}D_\chi
        \left(\frac12+\frac{L}{3a}\right)\tau L^3.}
                                                                    \tag{8}
\]
Intersect (8) with the original \(C_b\tau TL e^{-2ba/3}\) slice
allowance and the integrated packing allowance. The same bounds apply
to full negative interactions. These are occupations of eligible pairs,
not counts of event times or distinct trajectories. Pair membership may
change; no tracking is used.

For fixed a and fixed positive \(\tau\), (8) yields
\[
 \frac1{TL}\int_\alpha^\beta N_-(t)\,dt
    =O_{a,b}(L^{-2})+O_{a,\chi}(L^3/T).                   \tag{9}
\]
This is the selected fixed-collar improvement over the O(1) normalized
slice allowance. The constants and finite onset remain unevaluated.
On a spacing-squared interval \(\tau\asymp L^{-2}\), however,
(8) divided by its duration is only \(O(TL)\). More generally the
averaged count allowance is
\[
 \frac1{\tau TL}\int_\alpha^\beta N_-(t)\,dt
 \le\min\left\{C_{a,b},\,
       \frac{C_{a,b}}{\tau L^2}+\frac{C_{a,\chi}L^3}{T}\right\}.
                                                                    \tag{10}
\]
The gain requires time intervals substantially longer than \(\ell^2\).
Equation(9) is an aggregate rate, not a conditional law for a chosen pair,
a fraction of eligible pairs, or pointwise positivity.

## 3. Proposal II: use the exact common-time interaction evolution

This second proposal concerns the actual signed interaction, not a raw
moment surrogate. It is conditional on an independently paid count-two
tube satisfying (1) throughout the time interval. No contour/collar
acquisition from a snapshot is part of this contract. The exact evolution
and its Riccati bound strengthen the same time-visibility route; they do
not introduce a third source mechanism.

Write \(H=QA\), \(Q=(z-c)^2-D\), \(g=A_z/A\). Define, for every exterior
occurrence,
\[
 w_j=\rho_j-c,\qquad m_j=(w_j^2-D)^{-1},\qquad M=\sum_jm_j.
\]
Conjugation makes the sum real and equal to the interaction already used.
The proposed exact physical-time identity is
\[
 \boxed{M'=2M^2-12\sum_jm_j^2-16D\sum_jm_j^3.}             \tag{11}
\]
Its square/cube sums are real after conjugate grouping; neither is
asserted termwise nonnegative. At D=0 this is
\(M'=2M^2-12\sum_jw_j^{-4}\). For one exterior occurrence it reduces
to \(-10M^2-16DM^3\), a useful algebra check, not canonical evidence.

The proof route is the complete deflated PDE. Set u=z-c, s²=D,
\(h(u)=u g(c+u)\), and use confluent divided differences. Then
\[
 \frac{A_t}{A}=-(g_z+g^2)-4h[-s,s,u],\qquad
 M=-g[z_-,z_+],\qquad z_\pm=c\pm s.                       \tag{12}
\]
The first identity follows by inserting
\(c'=g(z_-)+g(z_+)\), \(D'=2-4MD\), and dividing only after the
quadratic numerator vanishes at both target roots. Its apparent poles
cancel. Differentiate the second identity with all c,D motion retained,
then express the surviving logarithmic-derivative differences through
the complete exterior reciprocal sums. This should give (11) without
individual exterior velocities at collisions. A finite-polynomial
calculation is a diagnostic, not a substitute for the infinite-divisor
passage. E21 must pay that passage or explicitly report its failure.

For D<0 the divided differences use the vertical segment; for D=0 they
are ordinary confluent derivatives. A logarithmic derivative is
single-valued; no global choice of log A is needed. The complete
safe-line resolvent and the collar bound g itself by O(L²) on a fixed
inner disk; its higher derivatives have absolutely convergent reciprocal
sums. These facts pay analytic interpolation and continuity through
multiple exterior events. The count-two tube excludes an additional
zero colliding with a target root.

Normalize physical time by \(\sigma=(t-t_0)/\ell^2\), and write
\(m=\ell^2M\), \(\delta=D/\ell^2\), \(\mu_j=\ell^2m_j\). The fixed
scale is not differentiated. Equation(11) becomes
\[
 m_\sigma=2m^2-12\sum_j\mu_j^2-16\delta\sum_j\mu_j^3.
                                                                    \tag{13}
\]
The collar gives \(|\mu_j|\le(16/15)\ell^2/|w_j|^2\).
The complete unit count has O(L) occurrences in the fixed physical
near band, and the far reciprocal-power shell sums converge. Thus the
bounded proof must supply constants \(B,K>0\), depending only on fixed
a,d and the accepted source count constants, such that
\[
 |m|\le BL,\qquad
 \left|12\sum_j\mu_j^2+16\delta\sum_j\mu_j^3\right|\le KL,
 \qquad m_\sigma\ge2m^2-KL.                              \tag{14}
\]
The leading near allowances are of orders \(L/a^4\) and
\(dL/a^6\) for the square and cube terms respectively. This is a
complete-divisor bound, not a conditional distribution of exterior marks.

Put \(h=\sqrt{KL/2}\). The scalar Riccati comparison with an unrestricted
finite initial lower value yields, at every positive elapsed scaled time
inside the paid tube,
\[
 \boxed{m(\sigma)\ge-h\coth(2h\sigma),\qquad\sigma>0.}     \tag{15}
\]
For an elapsed physical buffer \(\asymp\ell^2\), this improves the
coarse negative allowance \(-BL\) to \(-O(\sqrt L)\). The statement
is one-sided: no corresponding improved positive upper bound is claimed.
It includes D=0 and both signs of D, provided the tube hypotheses persist.
There is no fixed O(1) lower allowance or sign conclusion.

The known initial bound in (14) also pays an integrated version. Set
\(k=\max(BL/h,2)\), \(u_0=\operatorname{arccoth}k\). The finite lower
comparison \(-h\coth(2h\sigma+u_0)\) gives, for every elapsed scaled
length s inside the tube,
\[
 \int_0^s(-m)_+\,d\sigma
 \le\frac12\log\frac{\sinh(2hs+u_0)}{\sinh u_0}
 \le hs+\frac12\log\frac{k+1}{2}.                        \tag{16}
\]
Consequently the upper allowance for the backward transport exponential
\(\exp(-4\int M\,dt)\) on a fixed microscopic scaled length is
\[
 \exp(4hs)\left(\frac{k+1}{2}\right)^2
       =O_{a,d}(L)\exp(O_{a,d}(s\sqrt L)).                 \tag{17}
\]
This improves the raw count allowance \(\exp(O(sL))\), while still
diverging at fixed positive s. It supplies no incoming gap value or
comparison margin. The initial bound here concerns M and is not a
replacement for the independently paid D anchor.

The same equation gives the simpler physical-time modulus
\[
 \left|\frac d{dt}(\ell^2M)\right|\le K_1L^4.             \tag{18}
\]
A fixed margin \(\ell^2M(t_*)\le-\epsilon\), \(\epsilon>0\), therefore
persists with \(\ell^2M\le-\epsilon/2\) on either available side of
length \(\epsilon/(2K_1L^4)\), provided that length is inside the paid
tube. Strict negativity without a margin supplies no uniform duration.
For counting several pairs at one snapshot, require their independently
paid tubes over the same visibility interval. Their collars keep the
continued eligible pairs distinct. No count of repeated episodes along
one trajectory is inferred.

The matched fixed-collar success test divides the occupation allowance
(8) by a paid visibility length. With (18) this is
\(O_{a,b,d}(TL^3/\epsilon)\), worse than the existing \(O_{a,b}(TL)\)
snapshot count. Even an independently supplied natural lifetime
\(\asymp\ell^2\) only recovers O(TL). To exclude one pair the allowance
would have to be strictly below1. Its T/L budget already grows when
divided by the maximal physical duration1/2. The Riccati improvement
(15) is real but still permits every fixed negative margin for sufficiently
large T. These statements concern the displayed upper allowances, not
lower bounds for actual occupation, lifetime or exception count.

**Disposition:** select (11), (15) and (16) as the substantive exact
evolution and quantitative interior estimates, with (18) as a corollary.
Stop the proposed inference of single-exception exclusion from the
occupation budget plus these persistence allowances. A smaller
conditioned inventory or additional signed control of the actual square
and cube sum would be new information; neither is asserted, selected as
a third proposal, or hidden in an E request.
