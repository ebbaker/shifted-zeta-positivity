# B09: source phase screening and a transverse collar estimate

17 November 2026, America/New_York. Prepared for **Edward Baker** with
OpenAI Codex LLM assistance. The conceptual collaborator was explicitly
dispatched **gpt-6-astra / ultra**; the lead's actual model and effort are
unavailable. Dispatch records are not independent serving-backend
attestations. This is the B09 decision and its fixed proof contracts,
not an accepted execution result or a manuscript change.

**Two proposals, with distinct dispositions.** Screen the literal
positive-phase source-block allowance by an exact negative asymptotic
(E16). Select a source-derived transverse second-moment estimate for an
unconditioned center average (E17): its dependence on an enlarged
microscopic collar improves from the count-only (a^{-1}) allowance to
(a^{-3}). At a fixed spacing-scale collar both remain order one; neither
pays the selected-pair near sign. The source estimate does not acquire
that collar, its incoming gap, or the conditioning on an isolated pair.

## 1. Unchanged consumer and the additional information

Keep the complete cosine-theta field, physical time (0\le t\le1/2),
and (\partial_tH=-H_{zz}). On an independently paid conjugation-invariant
count-two tube use real (c,D), target roots (c\pm\sqrt D), and the
zero-free deflated factor (A=H/((z-c)^2-D)). The inherited quantities are

\[
 M=\sum_{\rho\text{ exterior}}\Re\frac1{(z_1-\rho)(z_2-\rho)},
 \qquad D'=2-4MD.
\]

For (x=\Re\rho-c,y=\Im\rho), the near term is

\[
 S_R=\sum_{\rho\text{ exterior},\ |x|\le R}
 \frac{x^2-y^2-D}{(x^2-y^2-D)^2+4x^2y^2}.
\]

Multiplicities and imaginary marks are retained. Note33 pays the far
term (T_R=M-S_R), including time zero and both signs of D. Its normalized
size at (R=\sqrt L,\ell=1/L) is (O(L^{-3/2})). Note34 gives identical
horizontal counts and opposite near signs in its fixed-slice
analytic-divisor class. Its positive-source and common heat-tube premises
remain unpaid. An additional transverse source input is therefore
substantive information, rather than another horizontal count.

For E17 use (L=\log T), (\ell=1/L), frozen across the center band
([T,2T]). This differs from Note33's (\log(c/(4\pi))) by a bounded
additive quantity when (c\in[T,2T]); no scale is differentiated. The
source-specific input below is the complete canonical **dyadic transverse
second moment**, already proved in [TND-O Note21, (8)][density]:

\[
 \sum_{T\le\Re\rho\le2T}(\Im\rho)^2\le C\frac T{\log T},
 \qquad 0\le t\le1/2.                                      \tag{1}
\]

Its canonical density input is stronger than strip and horizontal
counts. The source contains the proof, uniform time-zero endpoint, and
the status qualifications. The present work imports (1), not an ensemble
law or a selected-pair conditional distribution.

## 2. Proposal I: retain all favorable source phases

This extends the specific allowance left open by Note28: instead of
retaining only a zero-frequency atom, retain a neighborhood of the
anti-diagonal, or every positive cosine lobe, before paying the rest
absolutely. It is one proposal; convex time averaging below is a
robustness check of the same allowance, not a second mechanism.

On a fixed time slice normalize the **complete** source to an even
probability density (f_t) and write

\[
 F_t(z)=H_t(z)/H_t(0)=\int_{\mathbb R}e^{izu}f_t(u)\,du,
 \qquad m_k(t)=\int u^k f_t(u)\,du.
\]

Normalization is used only slice-wise; no heat equation is assigned to
the time-dependent normalized field. The full theta reflection identity
at the origin and its double-exponential tails must pay the uniform
smoothness/decay claims on (0\le t\le1/2).

Let (a_{k,t}(u)=u^k f_t(u)). Note24's exact numerator has density

\[
 \begin{split}
 \Pi_t(c)&=2F_t'''(c)^2-3F_t''(c)F_t''''(c)
             =\int_{\mathbb R}\nu_t(s)\cos(cs)\,ds,\\
 \nu_t&=3(a_{4,t}*a_{2,t})-2(a_{3,t}*a_{3,t})\ge0,\\
 W_t:=\int\nu_t&=3m_2(t)m_4(t)>0.                       \tag{2}
 \end{split}
\]

The sign in (2) follows from the original joint polynomial

\[
 \tfrac12u^2v^2(3u^2-4uv+3v^2)
   =\tfrac12u^2v^2\{u^2+v^2+2(u-v)^2\}\ge0,
\]

not from a positive-semidefinite convolution matrix.

For a measurable retained set (E\subseteq\{s:\cos(cs)\ge0\}), the
specified lower allowance is

\[
 B_{t,c}(E)=-W_t+\int_E(1+\cos(cs))\nu_t(s)\,ds.       \tag{3}
\]

It retains the exact signed contribution on E and substitutes (-1)
for every discarded cosine. Since (1+\cos(cs)\ge0) on E, the largest
allowance in this class takes **all** positive lobes. Define

\[
 g(\theta)=\begin{cases}\cos\theta,&\cos\theta\ge0,\\-1,&\cos\theta<0,
 \end{cases}\qquad
 \mu=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}g(\theta)\,d\theta
      =\frac1\pi-\frac12<0.
\]

The E16 quantitative test is

\[
 \sup_E B_{t,c}(E)
 =U_t(c):=\int\nu_t(s)g(cs)\,ds
 =\mu W_t+O(c^{-1}),                                  \tag{4}
\]

uniformly in (0\le t\le1/2), with a fixed positive lower bound for
(W_t). One integration by parts against a bounded periodic primitive
of (g-\mu) pays (4), once
(\sup_t\|\nu_t'\|_1<\infty) is proved. The requested error must display
the derivative norm and primitive norm; a finite height threshold is not
claimed from unevaluated constants.

The narrower anti-diagonal block, (|s|\le b/c), (0<b\le\pi/2), has
the sharper predicted allowance

\[
 B_{t,c}([-b/c,b/c])
 =-W_t+\frac{2(b+\sin b)\nu_t(0)}c+O(c^{-3}),\qquad
 \nu_t(0)=5\int u^6f_t(u)^2\,du.                       \tag{5}
\]

Uniform second-derivative control and evenness of (\nu_t) pay the
remainder for fixed b. Thus thickening the anti-diagonal to its first
nonnegative lobe cannot save this absolute-complement payment; even the
optimal positive-lobe union retains a strictly negative limiting
allowance. This is an allowance failure for the **literal complete theta
source**, not an assertion that (\Pi_t) is negative.

At any stipulated nondegenerate real double, Note24 gives

\[
 M(c)=\frac{\Pi_t(c)}{18F_t''(c)^2}.                    \tag{6}
\]

The negative allowance in (4) cannot establish a nonnegative normalized
curvature there, much less a positive spacing-scale margin. This already
defeats a certificate intended to include D=0; it does not need a claim
that the canonical field actually has such a double. Formula (6) is not
the two-point formula away from D=0. For D<0 the correct consumer is the
vertical average in Note24, (3); no real-axis numerator estimate is
substituted for it.

Any nonnegative normalized time average of (U_t(c(t))), with
(c(t)\ge C\to\infty), inherits the same negative upper allowance
uniformly. This is only an allowance statement. A persistent tube of
doubles is impossible because (D'=2) at D=0, and an averaged numerator
is not an estimate of the actual integrated M cost. Genuine signed
cross-time cancellation remains outside this class.

## 3. Proposal II: retain transverse marks and pay their radial loss

For one exterior occurrence put (r^2=x^2+y^2). The exact decomposition
to be verified in E17 is

\[
 q(x,y,D)=\frac1{r^2-D}
 -\frac{2y^2(r^2+D)}{(r^2-D)((r^2-D)^2+4Dy^2)}.         \tag{7}
\]

This is regular at D=0. It separates a positive radial term from a
nonnegative transverse correction provided the **independently paid**
exterior collar and pair size satisfy

\[
 r\ge a\ell,\quad |D|\le d\ell^2,\quad a^2>d,\qquad
 \delta=d/a^2<1.
\]

The reverse triangle inequality gives

\[
 0\le\frac{2y^2(r^2+D)}{(r^2-D)| (x+iy)^2-D|^2}
 \le C_\delta\frac{y^2}{r^4},\qquad
 C_\delta=\frac{2(1+\delta)}{(1-\delta)^3}.              \tag{8}
\]

Consequently, on such a counted pair,

\[
 \ell^2 S_R\ge
 \ell^2\sum_{\rho\text{ exterior},|x|\le R}\frac1{r^2-D}
 -C_\delta\,\Xi_{a,R}(t,c),                            \tag{9}
\]

where the source observable is defined for **every fixed real center X**,
without selecting a pair:

\[
 \Xi_{a,R}(t,X)=\ell^2
 \sum_{\substack{\rho\text{ any zero occurrence}\\
       |\rho-X|\ge a\ell,\ |\Re\rho-X|\le R}}
 \frac{(\Im\rho)^2}{|\rho-X|^4}.                       \tag{10}
\]

On the paid tube the two target zeros lie inside the excluded disk
because (a^2>d); thus (10) is exactly the transverse loss of its nearby
exterior. Equations (7)–(9) retain D>0, D=0 and D<0 without an inverse
internal gap. A uniform (o(1)) bound for (10) at the selected centers
would be a sufficient new input, but is **not** assumed or acquired.

E17 instead has a concrete, available source estimate. For sufficiently
large T, (L=\log T), (\ell=1/L), (1\le a\le L),
(3\le R\le T/2), prove

\[
 \boxed{\quad\frac1T\int_T^{2T}\Xi_{a,R}(t,X)\,dX
          \le \frac{C_{\rm src}}{a^3},\quad 0\le t\le1/2.\quad} \tag{11}
\]

The complete proof route is short and quantitative. For every real y,
and every (r_0>0),

\[
 I(y,r_0):=\int_{x^2+y^2\ge r_0^2}
                 \frac{y^2\,dx}{(x^2+y^2)^2}
 \le\frac\pi2\min\left\{\frac1{r_0},\frac{y^2}{r_0^3}\right\}. \tag{12}
\]

For (s=|y|/r_0\le1), the exact value is
(I=r_0^{-1}(\arcsin s-s\sqrt{1-s^2})/s), with the removable value
zero at s=0. The identity

\[
 \frac{\arcsin s-s\sqrt{1-s^2}}{s^3}
  =2\int_0^1\frac{v^2\,dv}{\sqrt{1-s^2v^2}}\le\frac\pi2
\]

pays both bounds. For (s\ge1), (I=\pi/(2|y|)), which pays them
as well. The constant is attained at (|y|=r_0).

The horizontal cutoff restricts all contributing zero occurrences to
([T/2,5T/2]). Three dyadic applications of (1), with their endpoint
conventions, give total transverse second moment (O(T/L)) on that
band. Apply Tonelli, extend each center integral to its whole line,
use the second bound in (12), and substitute (r_0=a\ell):

\[
 \frac1T\int\Xi\le
 \frac{\pi\ell^2}{2T(a\ell)^3}\sum y^2
 \ll\frac{\ell^2}{(a\ell)^3L}=a^{-3}.                 \tag{13}
\]

No local zero tracking, smooth cutoff derivative, sharp-cutoff time
derivative, root simplicity, or sampled truncation is used. The estimate
is slice-wise uniform on the complete closed time slab.

## 4. Quantitative gain and its exact limitation

The direct/count comparator must be averaged over the **same** center
band. Applying the first bound of (12) and the inherited total count
(O(TL)) gives

\[
 \frac1T\int_T^{2T}\Xi_{a,R}(t,X)\,dX\le C_{\rm count}/a. \tag{14}
\]

Comparing (11) to a pointwise coarse (O(L)) allowance would be an
unfair change of quantifier. For fixed a, (11) and (14) are both order
one in height. The new source use improves the dependence on **growing
normalized collar size** by two powers of a. For
(a=L^\eta,\ 0<\eta<1), the physical collar
(a\ell=L^{\eta-1}\to0), while the respective proved allowances become
(O(L^{-3\eta})) and (O(L^{-\eta})). The larger counted collar is not
acquired; (11) is still an unconditioned average.

This is a source-specific quantitative intermediate result if E17
returns the proof. It is not a full near-field lower bound, not a
selected-pair conditional average, and not a strict incoming passage
allowance. In particular an exceptional selected center can carry a
large value while respecting (11). An initial probability profile,
conditional denominator, or a lower bound on the spatial/time visibility
of an exception would be additional inputs.

The size required at a fixed microscopic collar is not artificial.
For Note34's inserted conjugate pair (x=2\ell,
y=\pm2\sqrt3\ell), its D=0 transverse contribution is
(\Xi=3/32), its radial contribution is (1/8), and (7) gives
(1/8-2(3/32)=-1/16) exactly. The real inserted pair has
(\Xi=0) and radial contribution (1/2). These are the inserted groups;
the source's remaining full-divisor payments retain their stated errors.
Their difference shows what transverse information (10) records. It does
not show that the canonical source attains either value or that those
controls satisfy (1) uniformly in any common heat tube.

[TND-O Note21, (29)][density] already gives all-order averaged transverse
moments and records that its all-order allowance exceeds the one-pair
signal. [Discussion12][predecessors] also locates the missing initial
profile and selected-pair conditioning in the OC/OG interfaces. E17 does
not repeat those results as new acquisitions; it contracts the paid
second moment against the **specific** exterior kernel, with the explicit
collar parameter and the matched count comparison (14). Positive blur,
source-node covariance, and Vandermonde carrier positivity are not
substitutes for (1) or for conditional zero data.

## 5. Control-premise replay and frozen execution boundary

The following is a documentary/analytic screen against [controls12][controls],
retaining the more detailed order/strip/count verdicts of Notes21,33–34.
No new collision scan or canonical event is constructed.

| Control or relaxation | Premise verdict for the two proposed uses |
| --- | --- |
| Quadratic and even quartic | Can share the local pair law and collar, including both D signs; finite divisor fails the canonical growing count. A finite positive Fourier-density representation needed by E16 is absent. E17's complete canonical second-moment premise is not assigned to them. |
| Euler-factor translates | Positive source and heat evolution survive, so the source-block failure may also apply where its smoothness hypotheses hold. The unchanged theta source fails; the canonical endpoint count discrepancy fails as recorded in Note33. Canonical transverse density (1) is unproved for this changed source. |
| Weighted-square, finite-source, finite-score and finite-prime controls | Full unit-weight reflected theta source fails, and the documented endpoint-defective classes fail the fixed strip. Finite scores/endpoints are not the source input for (1). |
| Reflected super-Gaussian | Positive reflected source survives, but order at most one fails in the documented class. It is not assigned canonical counts or (1). |
| Smooth shape and positive-primitive controls | Can share even smooth positive source assumptions of E16; arithmetic is changed. Their strip/count and transverse estimate (1) remain untested, rather than asserted false. |
| Fixed-event atom and compact-profile controls | Atomic measures fail E16's absolutely continuous smooth-density premise. Compact smooth variants may satisfy that premise, which only yields an allowance failure. Their exponential-type counts fail the canonical growing count, and full theta structure is absent. |
| RH-conditional endpoint mergers | Retain their RH assumption, changed ordinary Dirichlet arithmetic and untested full count/density transfer. D=0 is allowed; no simplicity or positive incoming margin is inferred. |
| Varied observation kernel / fractional-profile relaxation | Respectively lack the literal cosine/Newman readout and complete integer zero divisor. They do not supply E16's exact numerator or E17's zero-source input. |
| Note34 identical-count controls | Pass the stated order/strip/count/collar inputs and explicitly change transverse loss. Unchanged theta equality fails along the specified sequence; positive Fourier source, canonical (1) on a common time tube, and that tube itself remain unpaid. |

Literal theta equality alone is not a proof that a changed control
violates (1), (11), or any future derived inequality. E16 is an adverse
allowance calculation on the unchanged source itself. E17 uses an
independently established canonical density estimate and retains the
selected-center limitation. These are the exact classes of the negative
and positive conclusions.

Freeze E16 and E17 as analytical requests, **Sol6.1 / ultra**, each with
one proof and one correction pass, followed by a pinned internal
assessment. E16 must pay full-source origin regularity, uniform derivative
norms, the positive lower mass, (4)–(5), and the numerator/transport scope.
E17 must verify (7)–(14), all cutoff endpoints, the dyadic cover, both D
signs, uniform time zero, and the growing-a versus fixed-a distinction.
No new source samples, known-pair scan, model covariance, altered
observable, manuscript work, or larger-cluster extension is authorized by
these contracts. A new source/conditioning mechanism is a B escalation,
not a routine repair.

Give the direct evaluator every input: the full source, normalized jet
identity, canonical count and transverse moment, contour/collar and pair
coordinates, radial loss formula, far-tail estimate, and any independently
paid incoming value. It obtains the same average bounds. No transport-only
information, computational advantage, new zero coverage or Newman bound
is claimed.
