multiplicity, in the two real-part bands |Reρ−c|≤3 and |Reρ+c|≤3. The
removed finite divisor is invariant under conjugation and negation. Its
cardinality is O(log c) by the inherited complete unit-count theorem.
Call the resulting entire quotient B_c. At ±c insert target doubles.
Compare the two new factors at positive real part c+r and their mirrors:

    F_+(z)=B_c(z)(z²−c²)² (z²−(c+r)²)²,
    F_−(z)=B_c(z)(z²−c²)²
             [(z−(c+r))²+3r²][(z+(c+r))²+3r²].

An irrelevant nonzero real normalization is allowed. Prove both fields
are real even entire, order at most one, with width-one zeros for large c,
exactly two zeros in the target disk |z−c|<ℓ and a common zero-free collar
through radius 3ℓ/2. They have **identical entire real-part counting
functions**, including all multiplicities. Each retains the canonical
cumulative main term with O(log(2+X)) error and unit-count upper bounds,
with constants uniform in c. The entire tails and zero counts, not just
the inserted polynomial, must be checked.

With M defined by the same deflated-divisor formula, prove

    ℓ²M_+(c) = 1/2 + O(1/log c),
    ℓ²M_−(c) = −1/16 + O(1/log c).

The common remaining divisor costs O(log c), and mirror groups O(c^−2).
