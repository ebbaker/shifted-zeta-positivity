## B01 and E01 prepare the analytic contract

B01 compares a compact Cauchy evaluation followed by a controlled
deformation against an actual Mellin–Barnes or inverse-Mellin kernel.
For each, state the reconstruction, function class, branches, local
time-law requirement, and intended quantitative consumer. The compact
Cauchy evaluation starts within \(\Re p>1/2\), with \(Z\) holomorphic on
and inside the contour. The inverse-Mellin route needs its integrable
kernel and convergence strip, or explicit regularization and a correction.
A pure inverse power on the entire positive ray has incompatible
ordinary Mellin conditions at zero and infinity. Continued validity to
the left of \(\Re p=1/2\) is an obligation of a route that needs it.
A compact contour can serve the research question if its estimate pays
the entire infinite divisor and any order growth it claims.

E01 checks \(M=-K\), both time normalizations, the weights at \(p=2,3\)
and the separate evaluation at \(p=1\). Check the inherited convergence
and multiplicity scopes and principal-power gamma passage with the
actual sector and interchange conditions. Distinguish checked DLMF
passages from a proved application to this divisor. E01 supplies an
audited premise table to B01. It does not select a contour.

B01 freezes one analytic choice using E01. Specify the complete divisor
and grouped cutoff, exact tube and time domain, near/far boundary
conventions, branches at adverse or moving denominators, kernel,
vertical or compact contour and orientation, required time regularity,
and one signed consumer. Specify what equation E02 must establish
to supply E03's domain. Name the spatial companion or complete-field
input if required for the time law. Define the remainder and exact
acceptance/failure test, plus the expected order of limits. Retain the
other starting route's scoped disposition and use B03 for a structural
repair before B02 when needed.

## B01 and E08 investigate symmetries with a bound in view

Symmetry analysis is an explicit subgoal, not an assumption behind the
contour formula. B01 considers at most two candidates per pass. Start
with the proposed relation \(S(p,t)=\overline{S(\bar p,t)}\), checking
conjugation-paired logarithms of every exterior occurrence. Negative-real
denominators need explicit boundary arguments, multiplicities and any
branch defect. A second candidate can be a relation under
\(p\mapsto\kappa-p\) for a specified completion or regularization only
when there is a concrete mechanism and a common domain to test. Field
reflection in the spatial variable does not by itself give a functional
equation in \(p\); track the changed center and target exclusion. Do not
presuppose \(p\mapsto1-p\), analytic continuation or a beneficial gamma
completion. Classify recurrences and normalization changes accurately.

Use E01 to freeze one useful E08 identity/defect calculation, with exact
inputs, transformation, branch rules, domain, remainder and consumer.
If neither candidate has a justified next step, record that scoped
finding and continue the viable contour tasks. E08 may run alongside E02
when its hypotheses are slice-wise. It must prove the grouped infinite
sum identity and every needed extension, retaining near, subtraction,
branch and residue terms. State how an S relation transforms to Z and
how it acts on the kernel, contour orientation and the entire weighted
integrand. A time-uniform use must pay branch-chart compatibility and
exterior mergers; a relation only on one slice stays on that slice.

An E03 formula relying on E08 waits for its return. E04 assesses any
symmetry before B02/E05 use it, including a focused assessment of a later
E08 return under a newly frozen E04 request. Unrelated work continues.
B02 compares the resulting joint bound with the equally informed direct
method: folding a contour or proving reality alone is not a sign, decay
or rate improvement. E05 pays the defect and both vertical tails, with
N/Y/P/T uniformity wherever relevant. Preserve cancellations until the
joint estimate. Use the existing controls at the quantitative premises;
a failed reflection identity excludes only the specified transformation
and branch class. The [amendment note][symmetry] records this addition.

## E02 through E04 establish and assess a complete evolution formula

E02 proves the selected complete-divisor complex-power time law on the
paid tube. Justify convergence in \(p,t\), the time derivative or
the exact weak/integrated replacement specified in B01, and behavior
at exterior mergers with multiplicity counted. If using local branch
charts, show agreement or account for the jump where they meet.
Control moving \(c,D\), denominator phases and any moving cutoff
boundary. A fixed reference-slice product limit must also pay the
limiting derivative. Do not assume differentiable root labels through
mergers or denominator-only autonomous closure.

E03 uses exactly E02's proved domain. Justify the sum/integral or
time-derivative interchange used. Restore the finite near terms, branch
jumps, residues and all connecting segments and contour tails. Keep the
local \(2M^2\) term. If represented by contours, account for the full
double-contour or convolution expression and its cross terms. State
the initial-layer and endpoint interpretation of the time formula.
Give an explicit error ledger with \(N\) for the divisor truncation,
\(Y\) for contour height when unbounded, \(P\) for moment/residue order
when present, and \(T\) for physical height. For each used limit give
uniformity, summable or vanishing error, constants and admissible order.
If \(Y\) or \(P\) is absent, say which contour/expansion choice makes
that limit unnecessary. A claimed deformation cannot discard its
horizontal or connector integrals by citing compact convergence.
E04 checks each assertion against the frozen contracts and marks what
B02 may use. Any missing domain or branch mechanism goes to B03.

## B02 through E06 measure the bound and its transport consequence

B02 compares at most two explicit candidate estimates for an upper
bound on the complete real forcing \(F\), an integrated adverse cost,
or a direct comparison of the exact multiplier \(W\). State the
quantitative new input or joint cancellation and the tested class of
every failure. Compare against the best applicable direct estimate
using the same signed observable, tube, and source and error information.
Include the cost of acquiring any extra information for either method.
Use Note41 §§3–4 as the inherited baseline and cite its assessment.
Compare actual constants, rates, scope or integrated cost. A prettier
representation or a comparison with separately bounded terms alone
does not establish a gain.

E05 proves the one selected estimate and the precise advertised
infinite-index consequence. Include uniform \(N,Y,P,T\) dependence
for the relevant parameters, the exact limit order and any justified
coupled growth regime. A sector rotation must retain its phase.
On \(p=u+iv\), compare the kernel size with
\(|a|^{-u}\exp(v\arg a)\) in the chosen branches, for both signs of \(v\).
Bounded-real-part gamma asymptotics are available only on that stated
range. An all-order claim needs a bound with actual order dependence
and a summable or otherwise controlled remainder through the limit.
An analytically complete branch/sector or tail theorem can be an
intermediate milestone if it materially helps this later estimate.

E06 checks whether the full claim improves the inherited \(C_F L\) forcing
allowance, \(O(\sqrt L)\) interior negative allowance or
\(O(L)\exp(O(\sqrt L))\) multiplier on fixed scaled intervals, with
the forcing constant \(C_F\) and geometry from Note41. Use the exact edge-inclusive
backward identity when claiming a gap consequence. Preserve the paid
initial layer and independent terminal/incoming gap and tube. Equality
can yield \(D=0\), including at the initial time. No acquired reality
margin follows from a transfer estimate alone. Record a precise,
class-scoped limit if the result merely reaches the direct comparator.
