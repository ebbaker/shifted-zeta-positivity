## 1. Joint identity and barrier

**Accept.** With complete moments on the real-simple stopped regime,
V=1−2DM and K=2D²F give

    V_u=4M−4DM²−2DF=(1−V²−K)/D,       D_u=−2V.

The equation is in physical backward time. If K>=1−eta² and
v>=max{V(0),eta}, then w=V−v satisfies

    w_u+[(V+v)/D]w <= (eta²−v²)/D <=0.

On every compact pre-collision interval the coefficient is continuous,
so its integrating factor is positive; its sign is irrelevant. This
proves V<=v and D>=D(0)−2vu. For v=0, D is nondecreasing in this regime.
For v>0, the strict margin 2vDelta<D(0) survives passage to a hypothetical
first collision by continuity. The proof does not continue positivity
past a different first loss of the global real regime, nor use a
nonzero denominator at the collision itself. No finite moment closure
or differentiated cutoff is introduced.

The rate target correctly pays the square root of the K deficiency:
V(0)_+=o(L^−2) and eta²=o(L^−4), together with D(0) comparable to L^−2,
suffice for any fixed available physical duration. Mere K=1+o(1) is not
claimed to suffice.

## 3. Growing canonical head and full exterior payment

**Accept.** The fixed positive slab is essential. The published uniform
clock input, already checked for E21/E22, permits a common onset after
paying both the index and height thresholds. Explicit clock motion is
bounded by pi/4; therefore a fixed high label with reference height X
stays in X+O(1) through the slab. Its disjoint simple-root disks patch
by the implicit-function theorem, so no label permutation or moving
window omission is hidden in the construction.

The radius R=L² is o(X). For all zeros within radius 2R, localization
has clock-value error O(L X^(−ct0)). Taylor curvature of G costs
O(R²/X), giving the uniform absolute normalized position error

    E_X=L X^(−ct0)+L^4/X.

This argument is uniform over the growing window because Theorem1.5's
localization is a height-uniform assertion. It does not extrapolate a
fixed-window constant to a growing window without control. The clock
curvature is bounded throughout [X−O(R),X+O(R)].

The fixed head size J=floor(RL/(64pi)) is safely below the available
clock count on each side; G_x>=L/(8pi) provides substantial slack for
source displacements and the one-label asymmetry. J=O(L³) is negligible
compared with the initial index of order XL, retaining the left-index
onset. Every fixed head label remains covered at every time.

The normalized denominator factors are k+e_k and k−1+e_k−e_1. For the
exterior indices their integer reference factors have magnitude at least
one. Uniform small E_X therefore changes each positive K contribution
by relative O(E_X), summable against its finite reference sum at most
one. Omitting the remaining positive forcing gives
K>=1−C(E_X+L^−9) on the globally real stopped regime. That positivity
hypothesis is not silently extended to arbitrary nonreal low divisors.

The complete M estimate pays a different tail. Its near index endpoints
are comparable to RL; truncating the lattice sum costs O((RL)^−1).
The entire exterior unit-shell sum from R onwards is bounded by
C[L+log(R+1)+1]/R. This includes negative zeros, all low-height occurrences
and shell boundaries; noninteger R only changes an absolute constant.
Multiplication by d²=O(L^−2) pays O((RL)^−1)=O(L^−3). Thus
|1−2DM|<=C(E_X+L^−3) is a complete estimate, not a finite-head equality.

The barrier parameter is O(L^−3+sqrt(E_X)). At fixed t0>0, E_X is smaller
than every fixed negative power of L, so v_X=O(L^−3). With D(0)~4pi²/L²,
2v_XDelta/D(0)=O(Delta/L), and the algebraic duration budget is bounded
below by a positive constant times L. It is valid only on the specified

    |g(t)−g(s)|<=C epsilon/L+C|t−s|/(X²L²),
