## Laurent normalization and shared annular information

The inherited strict positive-real-part bounds on sigma G(a+w)/w^2
extend from the two boundary circles to their annulus by the harmonic
minimum principle. The quotient is holomorphic there; its pole at zero
lies outside the annulus. Thus the same principal logarithm, with the
same real centering constant, is analytic throughout the annulus and
continuous on its boundaries. The inherited boundary M values apply to
this one centered logarithm, not independently chosen functions.

For f_h=(w^2-hw)L, the full N-node mean selects precisely the Laurent
indices divisible by N. Subtracting the angular mean leaves

    sum_(m!=0) [l_(mN-2)-h l_(mN-1)] rho^(mN).

The old moment formulas have p1=-Re mean(wL) and
p2/2=-Re mean(w^2L). Consequently this alias combination is exactly
eta2/2-h eta1, up to the harmless common sign/real-part convention.
There is no extra factor2. N>2 removes centering-constant aliases and
ensures the two coefficient ladders are distinct. The65 stored
conjugation-reduced nodes retain the full128-node weights.

On each boundary Parseval bounds the squared norm of the sequence
l_n r^n by M_r^2. On the outer boundary, the positive-m ladders have
weights r_+^2 q_+^m and -h r_+ q_+^m; on the inner boundary the
negative-m ladders have the analogous weights r_-^2 q_-^|m| and
-h r_- q_-^|m|. Their squared norms give exactly

    (r_sigma^4+h^2 r_sigma^2) q_sigma^2/(1-q_sigma^2).

Cauchy--Schwarz on each boundary, followed by triangle inequality
between boundaries, proves Note52's A_joint. It retains a common L2
constraint across coefficients but still bounds the two boundaries
separately. It neither asserts coefficient independence nor sharpness
for the entire analytic-source feasible set.
