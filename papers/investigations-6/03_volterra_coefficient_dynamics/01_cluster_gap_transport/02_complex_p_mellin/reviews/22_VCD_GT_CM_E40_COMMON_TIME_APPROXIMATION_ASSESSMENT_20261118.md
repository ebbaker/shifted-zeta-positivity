## 1. Field proof and initial information

    E_N <= 2K [log(2+N)+1]/N.

No lower population asymptotic was used. The finitely many factors of

product by the same `N`-independent order-one envelope `G`.

The permitted telescoping refinement in Note47 (14) is valid. At a finite
larger complete cutoff, each added-factor difference is bounded by
`|z|^2 |rho|^(-2) G(|z|)` because every partial absolute product is bounded
by the full one. Summing and then taking the product limit proves
`|H0-P_N0| <= |z|^2 G(|z|) E_N`, including zeros. No factor was divided
out. This strengthens the frozen exponential bound without changing the
approximation; it removes the small-tail restriction needed only by the
unsharpened estimate.
