## Required local scope and exposition corrections

1. [Abstract][main], lines 43–48. Qualify Schwarz symmetry as a
   compatible-chart identity. State that the regular description is analytic
   through \(D=0\) even when an individual raw split coordinate has a genuine
   square-root cusp. The quartic shows that its singular derivative is real
   in the stated finite class, and calling it merely apparent is misleading.
2. [Scope of the forcing comparison][bounds], lines 57–61. The two
   displayed height allowances have the same \(L\) order **at fixed collar
   parameters and fixed damping**. When \(a\) varies, their explicit factors
   are different: the absolute contour bound uses \(B_1L\) while the direct
   one has the extra \(\gamma\) powers in \(C_F\). Keep the accepted
   fixed-parameter comparison and its larger closed-form coefficient.
3. [Static proof][bounds], lines 129–175 and 184–190. Say that the inherited
   unit-count and safe-line estimates used on \(H(0,z)\) hold at time zero,
   independently of whether the canonical tube \(I\) contains zero. The
   background proof states them on the closed slab. Explicitly assign
   principal logs to the entire noncore: the retained background and all
   opposite insertions, including the reflected double, have
   \(|\Re w|>3,\ |\Im w|\le1\) and \(\Re(w^2)>8\). This justifies the
   \(\pi/2\) phase in the displayed noncore bound and its Schwarz property
   without an implicit citation. For the transverse bound say that
   \([U,2U]\) meets the inserted positive nonreal projection \(c\)
   (equivalently \(c\in[U,2U]\)), including an endpoint cut. The text's
   “containing that band” omits dyadic intervals containing the projection
   but only part of the erased band. The same bound with
   \(L/2\le\log U\le2L\) works on every interval in question.
4. [Slice branch comparison][complex], lines 153–167. Say explicitly
   that the comparison with \(Z_+\) is (the algebra of) the transition
   formula on **one slice**, grouping finitely many occurrences with the
   same logarithm shift. The differentiated transition theorem concerns
   pairs of local analytic charts. The resetting slice prescription does
   not supply such a chart, and needs no chart for that value identity.
