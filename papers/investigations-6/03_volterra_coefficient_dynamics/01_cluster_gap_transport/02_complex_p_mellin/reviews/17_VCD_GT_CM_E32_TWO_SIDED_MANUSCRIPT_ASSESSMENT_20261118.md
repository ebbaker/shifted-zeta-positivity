## 5. Exact continuous residual and adjoint pairing

Accept. F(t) is the complete forcing along the same actual source path,
not an independently adjustable control and not a function closed by two
moments. The reference forcing is separate; its full difference deltaF
retains all source and state dependence.

Expanding the quadratic pair equations gives exactly

    e' = Ahat e + (-4 e_D e_M, 2 e_M^2-deltaF) - r.

The linear matrix is the derivative with forcing held as the explicitly
separate input. There is no missing derivative of the complete forcing:
its entire change is in deltaF. The quadratic error terms are retained.
With p'=-Ahat^T p, the product rule cancels only the linear terms. The
remaining Psi has the stated signs, including both `-p_M deltaF` and
`-p dot r`. Integrating from either endpoint gives two representations
of the same target error with the correct minus sign on the right-end
integral.

The manuscript assumes continuous reference forcing, giving a classical
identity. Note35's integrable reference-forcing version is also valid
with the residual equality understood almost everywhere and the same
integral identity; its coefficient matrix remains continuous because
the reference path is C1. No division by D enters either version.
Merger validity still requires the common pair contour and regular
complete observables; it does not extend all neighboring edge charts.

Intersecting independently justified two-end interval enclosures is
sound because both enclose the same error, not because they are
independent measurements. Empty intersection is an inconsistency check.
The absolute sufficient payment includes all quadratic, source and
reference-residual terms; its potential loss of useful cancellation is
stated. Computed endpoint and adjoint errors, source/tail/parameter
uncertainty and uniformity in the target time must be paid additionally.
Finite weighted sums give linear empirical error identities, not a
lower-tail or zero-exception theorem without further majorants and
coverage. No strict canonical interval shrinkage or recursive rate is
acquired by these identities.
