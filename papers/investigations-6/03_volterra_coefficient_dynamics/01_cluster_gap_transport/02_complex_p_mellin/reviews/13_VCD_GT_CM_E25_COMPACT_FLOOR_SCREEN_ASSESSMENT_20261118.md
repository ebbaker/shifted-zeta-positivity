## 1. Trial-coordinate identities and complete passage

**Accept.** Let f=Q^(-p), p>=1, with both trial roots inside the fixed
cleared target circle. For an actual interior occurrence rho, every pole
of f(z)/(z-rho) is inside the circle and the function decays as
O(z^(-2p-1)). Its contour integral is therefore zero. This remains true
when an actual pole coincides with a trial root, or either has
multiplicity. For an exterior occurrence, the residue outside the circle
is f(rho), so the interior contour integral is -f(rho). The genus-one
constant terms integrate to zero since the integral of f is zero. Thus
Note27 (1) extracts the complete exterior relative to the fixed counted
circle even when the trial quadratic differs from the actual one.

The canonical passage requires the actual complete product and its
locally uniform logarithmic derivative on the cleared contour. The
inverse-square tail gives absolute convergence of the extracted p=1
sum and stronger convergence for higher p. This is the passage in
[Note09 §3][hierarchy], not evolution of an arbitrarily selected finite
divisor. An enlargement of the time domain must pay these bounds there;
Note27 explicitly retains that obligation. The old numerical certificate
alone is not used to furnish it below zero.

The centered moments give c=a+A1/2 and D=A2/2-(A1/2)^2. Their definition
uses the two actual occurrences, including a double or conjugate pair.
At these actual coordinates, the combined kernel is exactly

    K=24D²S2+32D³S3=2D²(12S2+16DS3).

For the annular logarithm, B=L'+2/(z-a). The rational second term has
zero integral against Q^(-p), and integration by parts gives

    S_p=-p/(2pi i) integral L Q'/Q^(p+1) dz.

Differentiating the combined rational kernel gives the coefficients
48D² and 96D³ in Note27 (5), with the displayed negative sign. A fixed
real centering of L changes none of these integrals because each remaining
rational kernel is a derivative with zero contour integral. No derivative
of sampled source values is needed for the time-zero screen.
