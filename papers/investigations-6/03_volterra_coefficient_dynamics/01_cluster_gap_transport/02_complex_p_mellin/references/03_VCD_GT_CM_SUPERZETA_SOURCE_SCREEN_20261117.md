**André Voros, *Zeta functions over zeros of Zeta functions and an
exponential-asymptotic view of the Riemann Hypothesis*.**
[arXiv:1403.4558v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.4558v2), 26 July 2014,
17 PDF pages, report IPhT14/031. Inspected definitions (16)–(19), §2.1.1
(20)–(22), and §2.2 (24), PDF pp. 4–8. Initial v1 metadata/definitions
were seen before the version-pinned v2 passage inspection; no full-paper
reading is claimed. The first-kind continuation retains a Mellin integral
of zeta's logarithmic derivative, trivial-zero term, pole term and cut
shift domain. The second kind uses squared ordinates with an auxiliary
shift, and has a different continuation structure. That shift is not
Newman heat time. These passages provide no ready uniform theorem for
CM's moving center, removed target pair and heat interval. Any transfer
needs a checked normalization, branches, complete corrections and a
quantitative signed consumer. This last statement is the lead's scope
assessment, not a nonexistence claim by Voros.
