## Finite rational Burgers dynamics

Bernard Deconinck, Yoshifumi Kimura and Harvey Segur, **The pole dynamics
of rational solutions of the viscous Burgers equation**, Journal of
Physics A: Mathematical and Theoretical **40** (2007), 5459–5467.
Published 30 April 2007.
[Publisher and DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8113/40/20/014).

Retrieved 18 November 2026. Publisher metadata and abstract inspected;
the full article was not successfully retrieved. The abstract reports
complete integrability of the finite pole system via conserved quantities.
This establishes relevant prior work, not an infinite-divisor theorem.
The finite heat-coefficient formulas in the brainstorm are derived there
directly, with the physical sign H_t=-H_zz; no uninspected theorem from
this paper is used. Publisher/CiteSeer PDF retrieval failed, and no local
PDF copy is claimed.
