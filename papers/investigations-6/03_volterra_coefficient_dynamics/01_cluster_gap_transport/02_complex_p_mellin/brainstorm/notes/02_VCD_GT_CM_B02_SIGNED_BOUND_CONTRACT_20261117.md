## 1. Two estimates considered

1. Apply the absolute branch-angle and count envelope to the entire damped
   integrand on both retained lines. At fixed epsilon, collar and strip,
   E03 supplies a finite coefficient times A_b=O(L). This controls the
   representation but supplies no better height exponent than the direct
   C_F L allowance. The Gaussian damping is not a signed cancellation estimate.
2. Preserve complete conjugate groups and the signed combination
   F=12Z(2)+16delta Z(3), including the information m=Z(1)<0 if available.
   Before claiming an improvement from this joint information, extend the
   already assessed GR static comparison to its complex-p sum. This tests
   exactly the slice-estimating class. It does not test the full canonical
   time law, theta arithmetic or a common paid heat tube.

Select (2) with (1) as its quantitative baseline. [GR E06 §§3–4][grproof]
and [GR E07][grreview] already construct real-even order-one changed divisors
with complete tails, width-one strip, a counted collar, growing count and
broad transverse bounds with fixed enlarged constants. Reuse that construction;
no new numerical search or new counterexample family is needed. The new work
is to check complex-p holomorphy, compatible Schwarz symmetry and Gaussian
p-integrability for its **complete** sum. If those properties coexist with
m<0 and positive F of order L, they cannot by themselves imply a uniform
o(L) upper bound in that estimating class. This is not a lower bound for the
canonical forcing or an exclusion of all contour methods. [Scope07][scope]

## 2. Frozen E05 objective

For the E03 kernel on b=1,r=4 with fixed epsilon>0, prove an explicit
full-contour envelope using A_1 and Theta and compare it with C_F L. Keep
both lines, the whole signed residue combination and its sharper N remainder.
A triangle inequality before aggregating occurrences or specified conjugate
groups has absolute contour norm at least the absolute value of that group's
exact direct residue combination. Prove the summation justification. Do not
turn this elementary comparison into a claim that all joint signed methods fail.

Use the GR example with c=T=e^L, L>=16, a=4,d=1,D=0,b_core=8 and
J=floor L. Its local core has J occurrences at each real displacement
+/-b_core ell and J+1 at each imaginary displacement +/-i b_core ell.
With real logs for positive denominators and opposite +/-pi logs for the
negative pair,

    Z_core(p)=b_core^(-2p)[2J+2(J+1)cos(pi p)].

Include the retained canonical background, every reflected insertion and the
reflected target in the remainder. Verify the resulting complete p-properties
and quantitative remainder from the inherited physical-power tails. Establish
m=-2/b_core^2+O(L^-1)<0 eventually and
F=12(4J+2)/b_core^4+O(L^-3), with the inherited error constants. Identify
precisely the enlarged-constant static class excluded from a uniform o(L)
forcing upper estimate. These snapshots have no claimed common canonical heat
family; E02's differentiated law is not among the tested premises.
