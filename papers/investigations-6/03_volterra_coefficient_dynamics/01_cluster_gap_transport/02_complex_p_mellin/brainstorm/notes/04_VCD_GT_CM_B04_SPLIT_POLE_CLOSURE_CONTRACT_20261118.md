Define P_{p,q}=sum_j (w_j-s)^(-p)(w_j+s)^(-q), p,q nonnegative integers,
p+q>=2, with all exterior multiplicities. On a D!=0 time chart choose s
analytically. This family contains S_n=P_{n,n} and
T_n=(P_{n-1,n}+P_{n,n-1})/2 for n>=2. Include pure powers with total degree
at least two because differentiation and partial fractions can require them.
The inverse-first-power endpoint sums must cancel or appear only as a
convergent difference; do not silently promote their absolute convergence.
The coefficient in the elementary split w=(w-s)+s is s, not D.

The closure question is whether each time derivative is a finite algebraic
expression in this infinite family and the target coordinates. A finite
truncation is a separate question. No finite-dimensional closure is assumed.
Use finite polynomial heat fields first, symmetrize exterior pair interactions,
cancel first-power sums, then pass through the accepted slice-product/heat-jet
argument in GR Note02. The canonical input remains the unchanged theta heat
field and the independently paid tube. Multiple exterior roots are handled
through contours. A moving square root is singular at a target merger, so its
behavior must be checked rather than inferred from smooth c,D.
