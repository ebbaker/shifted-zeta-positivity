## 1. Comparison and selected route

The complete canonical source, paid count-two tube, frozen scale, both
signs of D, all multiplicities and physical time remain those of the
[overview][overview]. The [parent law and allowances][n41] have their
[internal assessment][a19]. The [GR synthesis][gr] and [assessment][gra]
already rule out claiming a gain from a small Gaussian complement alone.

1. **Selected: Cauchy with an entire damping factor.** Fix epsilon>0 and
   1/2<b<2<3<r<infinity. Use
   K_epsilon(p,delta)=12 exp(epsilon(p-2)^2)/(p-2)
   +16 delta exp(epsilon(p-3)^2)/(p-3).
   The residues remain 12 Z(2) and 16 delta Z(3). On either vertical
   line the damping is exp(-epsilon v^2), which can pay every finite
   branch-argument allowance exp(Theta |v|). Deform a finite rectangle,
   retaining its horizontal edges, and prove their vanishing. The result
   uses the right upward line **minus** the left upward line. The right
   line is retained; sending it to +infinity at fixed epsilon is not
   justified because exp(epsilon r^2) grows. This is a controlled Cauchy
   deformation, not an ordinary Mellin transform of a pure power.
2. **Deferred: inverse Mellin/Mellin–Barnes for the bare polynomial in mu.**
   The real-ray integral of x^(s-1)x^(-n) needs Re s>n at zero and
   Re s<n at infinity, so has no strip. A shifted resolvent, cutoff or
   subtraction could supply a genuine kernel, but adds an observable and
   remainder with no identified signed benefit over the selected route.
   The rejection concerns this bare-power implementation, not all
   Mellin–Barnes methods. No p-plane continuation is assumed.

The DLMF passages [recorded here][dlmf] were reinspected at launch:
2.5(i), (2.5.1)–(2.5.7), (5.9.1) and (5.11.9). Their Mellin strip,
remaining-contour and gamma-sector conditions remain mandatory. The

imports no gamma asymptotic or growing-order Stirling estimate.

## 2. E02: the exact local time-law contract

At each reference time choose two cleared vertical lines Re w=R_+ and
Re w=-R_-, R_+,R_->5, and the safe horizontal lines Im w=+/-4. Choose
the cuts in bounded windows using the unit count; shrink the real-time
neighborhood so their finite connectors stay zero-free. Far right and
left domains have Re(w^2-D)>0. Use their principal log and all their
infinite divisor occurrences. Enclose the remaining finite exterior
clusters by disjoint contours, each avoiding Q=w^2-D=0, and use one
analytic logarithm of Q on each cluster neighborhood. All occurrences
merging at a location share that branch. Cluster contours include full
multiplicity. They exclude the selected pair.

Write h(t,w)=H_z/H(t,c(t)+w), f_p=ell^(2p) exp(-p Log Q).
The required exact law is a sum of oriented cluster and unbounded-tail
boundary integrals for Z and its physical derivative, retaining

    h_t=-h_ww-2 h h_w+c' h_w,       (f_p)_t=p D' f_p/Q.

An integrated-by-parts form may use
[(f_p)_t-(f_p)_ww-c'(f_p)_w]h+(f_p)_w h^2,
provided every endpoint cancels or vanishes. This keeps the full field,
including the spatial information visible as +4T2^2 in GR's integer
next-moment law. It claims no denominator-only closure.

Prove holomorphy in p for Re p>1/2 and local real-time C1 with uniformity
on compact p sets and compact subcharts, including exterior mergers.
Finite covers of the paid time interval give an atlas, **not** an assumed
single global logarithm. On chart overlaps retain the finite difference
sum (exp(-2 pi i k p)-1) Z_cluster(p); the common far principal branches
agree. These differences vanish at integer p. Do not assume globally
smooth root labels or differentiate a sharp moving root cutoff.

State explicit complete-divisor value tails and safe-line integral/time
derivative tails. At fixed T the expected forms are
N^(1-2u) log N and N^(1-2u) log^2 N, for u>1/2, with all branch,
scale, tube and safe-line constants displayed or named. Track the
condition N>4T+constant, compact real parts and branch-angle growth.
Derivative convergence is supplied by boundary integrals, not asserted
from the scalar value-tail estimate.

## 3. E08: conjugation and exact branch defect

Only the required first candidate is selected: Schwarz conjugation.
Choose conjugate cluster contours with conjugate logarithms; at real
exterior clusters Q>0 and use the real branch. Purely imaginary
displacements with negative-real Q occur in distinct conjugate clusters,
where opposite boundary arguments are available. Prove the complete
sum's conjugation identity and merger compatibility locally. Also record
the exact defect when every negative-real denominator is instead assigned
the +pi principal boundary value. Track scale factors, the full weighted
integrand, vertical/closed-contour orientation and overlap corrections.

No second p->kappa-p candidate is issued: no specified completion with
a common domain and a reconstruction mechanism has been identified.
Evenness maps c to -c and the excluded pair to its reflected pair; it
does not reflect this exponent. This is a scoped disposition, not a
nonexistence theorem for possible completions.

E03 uses E02's established domain and E08 if invoked. Prove the full

## 5. Control screen and failure conditions

The [overview's premise table][overview] and [Controls12][controls] are
applied to the actual selected ingredients: a heat equation, cleared
counted tube, integer divisor, strip/unit upper count and local analytic
branches. Polynomial collisions share the contour algebra and local
regularity; a coarse count upper bound need not exclude them. They lack
the canonical growing divisor. Euler modifications change theta and its
endpoint discrepancy, but those facts alone are not a cancellation
inequality here. Endpoint-defective weighted-square/finite-source/prime
classes lack the full reflected source and can fail the strip. Reflected
super-Gaussian fails the inherited order-one product input. Smooth and
positive-primitive sources change arithmetic; strip/count transfer is
untested. Atomic/finite-profile controls lack the canonical population.
Conditional endpoint mergers retain RH and changed Dirichlet arithmetic.
Observation variants lack the physical cosine law; fractional profiles
lack integer multiplicities. GR changed static slices can pass these
slice contour premises but lack an unchanged canonical common-time tube;
its scalar equilibrium is not a divisor. None of the selected generic
contour ingredients is advertised as distinguishing all these controls.

Missing local chart regularity or a failed tail law returns to B03 before
quantitative use. Completed proofs do not themselves acquire tube,
terminal gap, initial-layer control or improved signed forcing. Execution
requests and returns are separate, with frozen hashes and disjoint files.
