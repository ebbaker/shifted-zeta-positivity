## 2. One calibrated omitted-product comparison

Let E=E_N be the actual omitted inverse-square tail, with one occurrence
per opposite pair. Retain the initial strip |Im rho|<=1. Require

    N>2R+1,  L_N=(N/2-R)/v>8,
    F_N(w)=product_omitted(1-w^2/rho^2),  A_N=F_N(a).             (3)

The auxiliary comparison is G versus g A_N P_N. A_N is a fixed positive
real scalar; the divisor, c_N,D_N,M_N,W_N and heat equation of P_N
are unchanged. It is not a spatial or time-dependent normalization.
Every field/jet error in one Note48 quotient must use this same calibration.

For |r|<=L_N and w=z+2i sqrt(t)r, |w|<=N/2. The straight-segment
logarithmic estimate in [Note50 (12)][n50] therefore yields

    |log F_N(w)-log F_N(a)| <= E p(|r|),
    p(u)=c0+c1 u+c2 u^2,
    c0=(4/3)d0(2a+d0), c1=(8/3)(a+d0)v, c2=(4/3)v^2=1/192.     (4)

Indeed |w^2-a^2|<=(d0+v|r|)(2a+d0+v|r|), and the omitted-factor
denominator is at least 3/4. As before, retained zeros cause no division:

    G_0(w)-g A_N P_N(0,w)
       =G_0(w)[1-exp(log F_N(a)-log F_N(w))].                   (5)

There is also a convenient global calibration payment. For an omitted
rho, Re(rho^2)=|rho|^2-2(Im rho)^2>N^2-2, so
2 Re(rho^2)>a^2. Thus |1-a^2/rho^2|<1. Conjugation and the real
factors give A_N>0; the convergent logarithmic series gives

    0<A_N<=1,  -log A_N<=a^2 E/(1-a^2/N^2)<=(4/3)a^2 E,
    |1-A_N|<=(4/3)a^2 E.                                      (6)

The positivity of the infinite product follows from its convergent real
logarithm, not from an infinite product of unspecified positive factors.

## 3. Evaluated central and middle allowances

Use |r|<=8 as the central region. Then |w-a|<=d0+8v=1.0265<1.05,
so its field bound is Bc. On 8<|r|<=L_N use (2). Define, for A>0,

    J(A,B;l)=integral_l^infinity exp(-A u^2+B u) du
       =sqrt(pi)/(2 sqrt(A))*exp(B^2/(4A))
          *erfc(sqrt(A)l-B/(2sqrt(A))).                        (7)

Set E_star=1/(16a) and for now assume 0<=E<=E_star. All Gaussian parameters
below are positive. Equations (2), (4), (5), and the exact imaginary
Gaussian formula give the following evaluated near-path allowance:

    Acent(E)=(2Bc/sqrt(pi))[
          exp(Ec0) J(1-Ec2,Ec1;0)-J(1,0;0)]
       +(2C/sqrt(pi))[
          exp(Ec0) J(alpha-Ec2,beta+Ec1;8)-J(alpha,beta;8)].      (8)

The first actual integral is only over [0,8], and the second only over
[8,L_N]. Extending their **nonnegative majorant integrals** as in (8)
is an upper bound. This does not assert the local Bc source bound or
the product-ratio estimate outside their proved domains. Keeping the
first integral finite would sharpen (8), but does not remove the
failure in Section 5.

This has a paid linear rate. Define

    I0(A,B;l)=J(A,B;l),
    I1(A,B;l)=[exp(-A l^2+B l)+B J(A,B;l)]/(2A),
    I2(A,B;l)=[l exp(-A l^2+B l)+J(A,B;l)+B I1(A,B;l)]/(2A).

These are the integrals of 1,u,u^2 against the same Gaussian, by
integration by parts. Since exp(Ep)-1<=E p exp(E_star p),

    Acent(E)<=E Kcent,
    Kcent=(2 exp(E_star c0)/sqrt(pi))*[
       Bc sum_(j=0)^2 cj Ij(1-E_star c2,E_star c1;0)
       +C sum_(j=0)^2 cj Ij(alpha-E_star c2,beta+E_star c1;8)].            (9)

Here E_star is the fixed threshold, not the variable E. This explicit
constant has no origin-centered
exp(R^2/[4(1+Delta)]) factor. It retains its possibly unfavorable
absolute amplitude and local variation costs.

## 4. The far path, input errors and the complete bound

On |r|>L_N, do not extend the omitted-product logarithm. Instead use
Note47's absolute-product envelope mathcal G and (6):

    |G_0(w)-g A_N P_N(0,w)|
       <=g E[|w|^2+(4/3)a^2] mathcal G(|w|).                  (10)

This includes the calibration mismatch. Put lambda=1/[8(1+Delta)]
and M=M_lambda from [Note47 (18)-(19)][n47], so mathcal G(s)<=M exp(lambda s^2).
All near-origin, normalization and supplied complete-count constants
remain in M. Set b=1-8lambda Delta=1024/1025 and

    J0(b,L)=b^(-1/2) erfc(sqrt(b)L),
    J2(b,L)=L exp(-bL^2)/(sqrt(pi)b)
              +erfc(sqrt(b)L)/(2b^(3/2)).

Since |w|^2<=2R^2+2v^2 r^2, exact Gaussian integration yields

    Kfar(N)=g M exp(2lambda R^2)*[
        (2R^2+(4/3)a^2) J0(b,L_N)+2v^2 J2(b,L_N)].             (11)

The complete fixed-time-interval bound is therefore

    epsilon_pad <= Acent(E)+E Kfar(N)+epsilon_pad^data,
    epsilon_1 <=16[Acent(E)+E Kfar(N)+epsilon_pad^data]           (12)

before separately incurred certified evaluation errors. Cauchy applies
to the analytic field/coefficient difference, not to arbitrary pointwise
rounding errors. All fields and data errors here use the G gauge.

Unlike Note50's tail beginning at a fixed small shift, (11) starts at
L_N tending to infinity with N. This suppresses the old radial constant.
For an explicit sufficient test put

    B=log(gM)+2lambda R^2,
    Dfar=[2R^2+(4/3)a^2+v^2/b+2v^2]/(sqrt(pi)b).

For L=L_N>=8 the elementary erfc tail bound gives

    Kfar(N)<=Dfar L exp(B-bL^2).

Since log L<=bL^2/2 for L>=8 and b=1024/1025, it is sufficient that

    L_N>=max{8, sqrt((2/b) max(0,B+log Dfar))}                     (13)

to obtain Kfar(N)<=1. The test explicitly retains the supplied constants
inside M. It is not an acquired cutoff bound for an unspecified C_u.
For fixed inputs it eventually holds; then (12) is at most
(Kcent+1)E+epsilon_pad^data. This gives a complete evaluated centered
O(E_N) allowance, preserving the actual one-initialization polynomial.

For finite coefficient uncertainty, use [Note48 (27)][n48] or Note47's
weighted Gaussian sum in the same gauge. Exact latent A_N can use (6)
without acquiring its value; shape errors are still multiplied and paid.
If a numerical Ahat is instead used, add |Ahat-A_N| times a bound for
the whole evolved polynomial and its required jets. Exact real symmetry,
complete integer counts, modulus ties and multiplicities remain required.
The representable chart-center error and every-subinterval integration
reserve in Note50 are unchanged. None of these data were acquired here.

## 5. The absolute core still fails the full consumer

The outer repair does not justify acquisition. This conclusion can be
proved without evaluating (7), optimizing (13), acquiring C_u or using
Note50's optional conservative gate (6).

Use the same count-tail allowance

    Ebar_N=2K[log(2+N)+1]/N>=10/N,  K=5C_u>=5,
    2R+1<N<=2^40.                                             (14)

If Ebar_N>E_star, the stated small-tail linear contract is unavailable.
In the eligible cases, insert Ebar_N into the positive core allowance.
Even the **tighter** core integral restricted to [-8,8] is bounded below
by Bc c0 Ebar_N times its Gaussian mass. Here c0>19000 and that mass
exceeds 2/3: on [-1,1], exp(-r^2)>=1-r^2, and sqrt(pi)<2. Hence

    epsilon_core > (2/3)*5200000*19000*10/2^40 > 1/2.           (15)

This is a lower bound on the chosen positive error allowance, not on
|G-g A_N P_N|. The exact source may cancel strongly in the Gaussian
integral. Neither (14) nor (15) is a lower bound on the true tail E_N.

Apply the actual Note48 formulas with the frozen supplied h=35625,
K1=10400000, r=.4 and q=.100625. If epsilon_0>=h/2, its first contour
test fails. Otherwise, grant the optimistic values epsilon_1=0 and zero
data, arithmetic and complementary errors. Its quotient allowance still
satisfies

    b1 >= K1 epsilon_0/[h(h-epsilon_0)]
       > epsilon_0/128 > 1/256,

because K1/h^2>1/128. The complete moment allowance contains the
positive summand (2r/q)b1, and 2r/q>7. Consequently

    e_M > 7/256,
    4 Delta e_M > 7/65536 > 1e-6.                              (16)

If the separate quadratic-denominator test fails, the transfer is already
unavailable; if it passes, (16) defeats its sufficient log-W error test.
No derivative or quadrature accuracy can cancel these positive budget
terms. The conclusion is for the displayed absolute envelope, count-tail
relaxation and fixed quotient constants. Sharper relative, derivative,
correlated-contour or acquired-tail information is outside that class.
It is not a claim that the actual multiplier error exceeds 1e-6.

This is the requested consumer test after the whole-path repair. It is
stronger than merely observing that Note50's conservative field gates
fail, and it does not recommend raising the 2^40 analytical screen into
an acquisition budget. No finite source/precision/runtime contract follows.
