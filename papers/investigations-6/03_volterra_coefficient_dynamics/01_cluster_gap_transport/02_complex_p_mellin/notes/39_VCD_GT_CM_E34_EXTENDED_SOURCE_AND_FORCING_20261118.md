### Complete product and regular laws on the enlarged interval

The old nonnegative-time strip is not silently extended. The complete
source directly supplies the product passage needed here. Its inherited
envelope has the form `Phi(u)<=C exp(9U-pi exp(4U))`, `U=|u|`, with
finite C. Since `exp(4U)>=8U^2`, `t U^2<=exp(4U)/80` for every t in I.
For `|z|<=R`, the absolute Fourier integral is consequently bounded by

    C integral_0^infinity exp((R+9)U-(pi/2)exp(4U))dU
      =C/4 integral_1^infinity v^((R+9)/4-1) exp(-pi v/2)dv.

With `n=ceil((R+9)/4)`, enlarge this by
`(C/4)n!(2/pi)^(n+1)`. Thus the logarithmic growth is
`O(R log(2+R))`, uniformly for t in I, and the entire order is at most
one. Moreover `H_t(0)>=H_(-.0003)(0)>0` by positivity of the source.
Jensen's formula therefore gives the uniform disk count
`N_t(R)=O(R log(2+R))`, hence, by summation by parts,
`sum_(|rho|>R)|rho|^-2=O(log(R)/R)` uniformly on I.

Hadamard factorization for an entire function of order at most one gives
the genus-at-most-one product. The field is even, nonzero at zero and
conjugation symmetric. In its genus-one product the linear exponential
coefficient equals `H_t'(0)/H_t(0)=0`; grouping opposite occurrences
gives the convergent paired product. Its logarithmic derivative and all
fixed spatial jets converge locally away from the complete divisor.
The uniform inverse-square tail just obtained pays this convergence
without a strip assumption or a real exterior. Hadamard factorization
and Jensen's formula are the classical complex-analysis inputs here.

In particular the contour sum passage of Note27 is valid throughout I:
interior-pole contributions to `integral B/Q^p` cancel by the rational
residue identity; exterior occurrences give `-Q(rho)^(-p)`, with
absolute tails and every multiplicity retained. The regular pair laws
also extend without asserting a common-time finite-polynomial model.
At any fixed reference time, truncate the opposite-paired product and
use the resulting polynomial as the initial slice of its finite heat
flow. Its spatial jets converge to the true field's jets; its first time
jet is minus its second spatial jet. Counted contour coordinates and

denominators are cleared. The finite identities of the

    D_t=2-4DM,       M_t=2M^2-F.

The finite algebra extends through multiple roots by continuity of
