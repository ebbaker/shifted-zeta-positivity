## 1. Fixed quantities and quantitative comparison

Retain the unchanged complete theta field, physical `H_t=-H_zz`, fixed
`ell=1/log T`, both signs of D, integer multiplicities, strict exterior
collar, and the independently established count-two tube and gap anchors
of [Note09][hierarchy] and [manuscript Section 1][framework]. Put

    s_n=ell^(2n) S_n,   v_n=ell^(2n−1) T_n,
    m=s_1,   delta=D/ell²,   sigma=(t−t0)/ell²,
    F=12s_2+16delta s_3.

Primes below mean sigma derivatives. The accepted equations give

    m'=2m²−F,       delta'=2−4m delta.

The current complete allowance is `|F|≤C_F L`, `L=log T`, at fixed
collar constants. Its Riccati consumer gives an interior negative allowance
of order `sqrt(L)` and, on fixed scaled duration, a multiplier bounded by
`O(L) exp(O(sqrt(L)))`, with the finite initial layer retained. A candidate
milestone must improve an actual constant, rate or domain in this allowance
or its full cost. Merely renaming F or assuming its improved bound is not a
milestone. No RH equivalence is claimed for any local estimate here.

Use the existing sign convention

    W(x,y)=exp(−4 integral_x^y m),
    W(x,y)delta(x)=delta(y)−2 integral_x^y W(u,y)du.          (1)

The physical inhomogeneous cost for D is ell² times the normalized cost.
An estimate does not supply either anchor or the tube.

## 2. Route A: the joint forcing derivative

The fixed n=3 row of Note09, after the constant normalization, is

    s_3'=12m s_3+6s_2²+12v_2 v_3+12delta s_2 s_3
           −60s_4−72delta s_5.

Differentiate F once, using this row, the accepted s_2 row and delta'.
Collect the terms proportional to m before estimating. The result is

    F'−8mF=R,
    R=48v_2²+192delta v_2v_3+144delta s_2²
      +192delta²s_2s_3−352s_3−1440delta s_4−1152delta²s_5.  (2)

Every quantity is real by conjugation. Fixed-order complete convergence
and merger regularity are inherited directly from Note09; no sharp cutoff
is differentiated and no arbitrary-sequence solution is asserted. The
product rule in this finite collection of established analytic observables
needs no order-to-infinity limit. In particular the coefficient of s_3 is
`12(−32)+16(2)=−352`, and the m terms are exactly `8mF`.

At delta=0 the remainder is `48v_2²−352s_3`. Positivity of v_2² does
not help an upper bound; s_3 is real, not known positive. Completing the
odd terms gives `48(v_2+2delta v_3)²−192delta²v_3²`, again with no
one-sided conclusion. The shell estimates give `|s_n|≤B_n L`. If
`q0=16/15` and `gamma=q0/a²`, then

    |v_n|≤sqrt(q0) gamma^(n−3/2) B_1 L,  n≥2,              (3)

because each normalized displacement u=w/ell satisfies
`|u| |mu|^(1/2)≤sqrt(q0)` for `mu=ell²/Q` and `|mu|≤gamma`.
Thus the direct termwise estimate of R is at best an `O(L²)` allowance
at fixed parameters; (2) alone has acquired no smaller forcing scale.

The precise integrated formula is

    F(y)=W(x,y)^(−2)F(x)+integral_x^y W(u,y)^(−2)R(u)du.   (4)

This follows by the integrating factor `exp(−8 integral_x^sigma m)`.
A canonical signed estimate of this jointly weighted remainder could be
useful, but neither its sign nor an independently useful bound for its
weights and entry value has been established. Using the desired W bound
as input would leave the transport problem unpaid. The equally informed
direct-residue computation has the same identity and moment information.

**Exact premise replay.** Set ell=1 solely for a finite polynomial test.
At a double target at c=0, take exterior occurrences at `±ib`, b>0, in
the heat evolution initialized by `P(0,z)=z²(z²+b²)`. This evolution is
the finite polynomial `P(0,z)−tP_zz(0,z)+t²P_zzzz(0,z)/2`, so it has
the required heat sign and a local isolated count-two tube. At t=0,

    m=−2/b², s_2=2/b⁴, s_3=−2/b⁶, v_2=0,
    F=24/b⁴, R=704/b⁶, F'=320/b⁶.

Replacing the exterior by `±b` gives `R=−704/b⁶`. Thus `R≤0` does not
follow from the finite hierarchy, conjugation and collar, even with
v_2=0. These examples lack the unchanged theta source, its nonzero value
at the origin and its growing complete population. They are not canonical
counterexamples or a no-go theorem for source-specific estimates.

## 3. Route A: integrated gap energy and its precise limitation

Eliminating m from the two accepted consumer equations without division
by delta gives the regular identity

    2delta delta''−(delta')²+4=8F delta².                  (5)

For example, differentiating delta' gives
`delta''=8m²delta−8m+4Fdelta`; substitution proves (5), including
delta=0. Integration by parts on any closed subinterval of the tube gives

    8 integral_x^y F delta²
      =2[delta delta']_x^y−3 integral_x^y (delta')²+4(y−x)
      ≤2[delta delta']_x^y+4(y−x).                         (6)

This is a genuine upper bound for the *weighted signed* integral shown.
Its boundary value is `delta delta'=2delta−4m delta²` and is not free.
The weight vanishes at mergers; moreover, with F of unknown sign an upper
bound for a weighted signed integral cannot simply be divided by a positive
lower weight to bound the unweighted integral. Negative contributions would
need payment as well. The target consumer (1) uses neither this weight nor
this boundary data alone.

The failure is exact in the **scalar consumer class only**. For h>0 set

    m=−h, F=2h², delta=−1/(2h).

Both consumer ODEs hold, and `|delta|≤d` whenever d>0 and `h≥1/(2d)`.
Equation (6) is saturated with `integral F delta²=(y−x)/2`, independently
of h, whereas

    W(x,y)=exp(4h(y−x)),
    2 integral_x^y W(u,y)du=(exp(4h(y−x))−1)/(2h).         (7)

Thus this weighted-energy allowance alone permits arbitrarily large
transport in that scalar class. Choosing `h=sqrt(C_F L/2)` also retains
the baseline forcing scale for large L and retains its exponential scale.
No realization by a divisor, full hierarchy or canonical theta heat field
is claimed. This screen does not rule out exploiting their extra structure.
The equivalent linearization `q=exp(−2 integral m)`, `q''=2Fq`, merely
gives `W(x,y)=(q(y)/q(x))²` and supplies no new signed information.

**Decision for A.** The displayed derivative and weighted-energy routes
stop at joint signed acquisition and degeneracy, respectively. Neither
provides a routine proof contract for an improved canonical estimate.
A future premise must pay its actual entry/endpoint data and must bound
(1), not just the weighted quantity in (6). No broader obstruction to the
complete S/T dynamics is asserted.

## 4. Route B: exact deflation identifies the missing signed derivative

Let

    G(t,w)=H(t,c(t)+w)/(w²−D(t)),
    U_n(t)=sum_ext w_j(t)^(−n),  n≥2.

The quotient is entire in w and zero-free in `|w|<r0`; target factors are
removed with their full multiplicity, also at D=0. Locally choose a log
of G. The genus-one regularizers disappear after two derivatives, giving

    U_n=−partial_w^n log G(t,0)/(n−1)!.                    (8)

Since `|D/w_j²|≤1/16`, the binomial expansion yields

    S_n=sum_(k≥0) binom(n+k−1,k) D^k U_(2n+2k),
    F=4ell⁴ sum_(k≥0) (k+1)(2k+3) D^k U_(2k+4).          (9)

For n≥1 the first expansion converges absolutely and uniformly in real
time on the fixed paid tube: dominate the k series by its geometric
majorant and sum the absolute inverse powers using the collar and complete
unit count. Thus (9) holds for both D signs and at a merger. A truncation
through k=K in the F formula has explicit residual at most

    4ell⁴ (sum_ext |w_j|^(−4))
       sum_(k>K) (k+1)(2k+3) q^k,  q=|D|/r0²≤1/16.        (10)

This is a paid representation tail, not a signed improvement. At D=0,

    F=12ell⁴ U_4=−2ell⁴ partial_w^4 log G(t,0).            (11)

Analytic continuation to the left of Re p=1/2 is unnecessary for this
integer consumer. Direct residues receive exactly (8)–(10) as well.

### Source transfer and what the inspected paper actually supplies

The manuscript normalization is `H(0,z)=xi((1−iz)/2)/8`. Write
`s_c=(1−ic)/2`. With Voros's first-kind notation
`mathfrak Z(n|tau)=sum_rho (1/2+tau−rho)^(−n)`, and s²=D≠0,

    U_n(0)=(i/2)^n mathfrak Z(n|−ic/2)−s^(−n)−(−s)^(−n). (12)

Indeed `s_c−rho=i w_j/2`. For even n=2r the target subtraction is
`2D^(−r)`. The separate terms become singular at D=0; only their exact
deflated combination (8) is the regular transfer. Independent absolute
estimates of the two singular pieces lose this cancellation. Formula (12)
applies only when the independent tube contains time zero; it is no anchor
for a positive-time tube.

Define `Xi_t(s)=8H(t,i(2s−1))`. Then `Xi_0=xi` and

    partial_t Xi_t=(1/4) partial_s² Xi_t.                  (13)

The auxiliary translation tau in the fixed-xi superzeta is not this heat
time. Defining a heat-deformed superzeta at positive integer n returns the
same derivatives in (8), and does not estimate them.

The version-pinned [Voros paper, 1403.4558v2][voros] was checked again in
definitions (16)–(19), (20)–(23), Table 3 and §2.2 (24)–(27), using the
existing [source-use record][source]. Its first-kind continuation includes
the pole/trivial-zero corrections and a Mellin term of zeta's logarithmic
derivative. Positive integers retain completed-function log derivatives.
The second kind uses symmetry-centered squared ordinates and an auxiliary
shift. These inspected passages supply no moving-center, removed-pair,
heat-interval signed estimate for (9). This is our transfer assessment,
not an impossibility assertion attributed to Voros. No new theorem is
imported into the manuscript and no third-party PDF is added to the tree.

**Decision for B.** The exact missing quantitative input is a joint upper
bound for (9) that improves `C_F L`, or a signed estimate paying (1) directly,
uniformly on the given tube with all source and endpoint costs. Equation
(9) equals F; asserting such a bound as a new hypothesis merely restates
the open estimate. No checked acquisition mechanism makes this a ready E
task. Meromorphic continuation by itself is insufficient in this comparison.
