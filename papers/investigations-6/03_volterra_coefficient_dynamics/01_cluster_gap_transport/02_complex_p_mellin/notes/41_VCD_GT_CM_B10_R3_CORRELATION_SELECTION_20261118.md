## 3. The quantitative estimate before execution

Write <.> for the real weighted nodal mean, w=z-a, and

    p1=-Re<w L>+eta1,   p2=-2Re<w^2 L>+eta2,
    gamma=p1/2,        D=p2/2-gamma^2,
    |eta1|<=epsilon1,  |eta2|<=epsilon2.

At each stored log box choose its exact representable midpoint ell_j and
write L_j=ell_j+e_j, with |Re e_j|<=a_j, |Im e_j|<=b_j. Any conversion
rounding must enlarge these same boxes for both methods. Retain the
half-circle weights omega_j (1 at the two endpoints, 2 otherwise).
For a complex kernel K define its rectangular support

    S(K)=sum_j omega_j (|Re K_j|a_j+|Im K_j|b_j)/128.

Then gamma has nominal center gamma0=-Re<w ell>/2 and radius
r_gamma=(S(w)+epsilon1)/2. The p2/2 radius is
r2=S(w^2)+epsilon2/2. If gamma0>r_gamma, the exact independent-moment
discriminant width is

    W_sep=2r2+4 gamma0 r_gamma.                            (1)

Freeze a reference h as the exact representable midpoint of the computed
nominal gamma0 enclosure; this is a rule, not a fitted search. The identity

    J_h=p2/2-h p1+h^2=D+(gamma-h)^2
       =-Re<w(w-h)L>+h^2+eta2/2-h eta1                    (2)

combines the shared nodal error. Its support is

    R_h=S(w(w-h))+epsilon2/2+|h|epsilon1.                 (3)

With J0=-Re<w(w-h)ell>+h^2 and a sound interval Gamma for gamma, obtain

    D in J0+[-R_h,R_h]-(Gamma-h)^2.                       (4)

The square is the full real interval range, not an independently signed
linear error. All nominal arithmetic, kernel rounding and tail payments
remain outward enclosed. For exact h=gamma0, (4) has width
2R_h+r_gamma^2, and triangle inequality gives
R_h<=r2+2|gamma0|r_gamma. Strict improvement is established when

    2(r2+2|gamma0|r_gamma-R_h)>r_gamma^2.                 (5)

The executed midpoint h may differ by a paid arithmetic amount; use the
fully outward version of (4) and the lower enclosure of (1) for the test.
This prevents comparing against an artificially inflated baseline width.
The finite E34 origin offset is nonzero, so first-order cancellation can
exceed the small quadratic payment; its size is to be measured, not assumed.
