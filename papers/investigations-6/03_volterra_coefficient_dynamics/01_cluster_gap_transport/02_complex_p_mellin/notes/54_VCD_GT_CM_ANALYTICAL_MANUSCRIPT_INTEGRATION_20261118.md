## What the integration clarifies

The finite-head negative screen and the common-time theorem concern
different finite objects. Independent unresolved moment rows may have a
large propagated absolute allowance even with a small boundary residual.
One initialized canonical divisor constrains every higher moment through
the same polynomial heat flow. Its explicit convergence does not prove
stable short-head closure or acquire the required divisor and tube data.

Likewise, a global sufficient allowance is not a lower bound on true
approximation error or necessary complexity. The centered-path proof
pays the previously missing Gaussian complement. Its tested absolute
core still fails the selected Note48 transport budget under the specified
cutoff cap. The manuscript records that scoped failure without turning it
into an impossibility statement about relative or correlated estimates.

The affine pair identity retains the intrinsic clock term and complete
quadratic remainder. Independent error payment returns the direct
allowance; joint support returns the same direct functional on identical
information. The cardinal, shared-log and annular improvements instead
sharpen successive outer relaxations of that direct estimator. They add
no source information, and an equally informed direct method receives
the same improvement.

The existing measured 6.4031% and greater-than-33.9494-fold width gains
are distinguished from Note52's analytical greater-than-2.4% reduction of
the complete outward-parsed old width. The latter is not a newly
serialized interval. All three statements retain their original
comparator, inherited data and error accounting.
