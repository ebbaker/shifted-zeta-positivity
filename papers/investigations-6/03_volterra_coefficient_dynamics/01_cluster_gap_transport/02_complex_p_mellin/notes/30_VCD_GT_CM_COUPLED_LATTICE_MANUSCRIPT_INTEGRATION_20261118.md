## What the coupled lattice adds

For adjacent real simple zeros a=x_n,b=x_(n+1),
M_n(p,q)=sum_{rho!=a,b}(rho−b)^(-p)(rho−a)^(-q). The overlap identity
M_n(r,0)+(−g_n)^(-r)=M_(n+1)(0,r)+g_(n+1)^(-r), r>=2,
and the common deflated resolvent make the zero-index constraint explicit.
Subtracting regularized node forces gives

    sum_{n=l}^r g_n M_n(1,1)
      = 2 sum_{n=l}^r 1/g_n − U_(r+1)+U_l.

A common force approximation therefore pays two boundary errors when the
gaps are exact. Uncertain reciprocal gaps have their own explicit sum.
This is an actual algebraic improvement over treating identical interior
node errors independently, but it is not a canonical minimum-gap estimate.
A block aggregate need not prevent one interior gap from shrinking.
The merger limitation is also explicit: a regular S/T chart for one pair
does not preserve neighboring edge collars at that merger.
