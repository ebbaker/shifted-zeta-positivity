## What is now proved

of [E16 r4][e16], with its full [r1 method][e16full]. One canonical
opposite-paired product is initialized at t0, including all cutoff ties,

strong-collar application, `I⊂[0,1/2]`; no negative-time extension is made.

With one representative per omitted opposite-pair occurrence,

\[
 E_N=\sum_{|\rho|>N}^{\text{opposite pairs}}|\rho|^{-2}
 \le {2K\{\log(2+N)+1\}\over N},\qquad K=5C_u,
\]

where C_u is the supplied complete unit-count constant. The initial
absolute-product envelope has the explicit form

factors sharpens the originally proposed exponential remainder to

\[

\]

The exact Gaussian imaginary-shift formula and a paid Gaussian majorant

\[
 \sup_{t\in I,\ |z|\le R}|H(t,z)-P_N(t,z)|

\]

Note47 gives C in closed form, not merely as an unevaluated integral.
Cauchy estimates on a padded disk give spatial derivatives through order

N≥1; no small-tail restriction is needed for the field estimate itself.
