## 1. Fixed domain and useful outputs

The canonical inputs remain the complete unchanged theta heat family
H_t=-H_zz, an independently paid compact count-two tube in [0,1/2], both signs
of D and all multiplicities. Write L=log T, ell=1/L, w_j=rho_j-c,
mu_j=ell^2/(w_j^2-D), delta=D/ell^2, m=ell^2 M and sigma=(t-t0)/ell^2.
Here T>=64, c in [T,2T], |D|<=d ell^2,
max(2,4 sqrt(d))<=a<=L, and exterior |w_j|>=a ell, |Im rho_j|<=1.
The parent and GR proof/assessment pairs are inherited by reference; no inherited proof
or manuscript was changed. The fixed-scale integer law remains

\[
 m_\sigma=2m^2-F,\qquad F=12Z(2)+16\delta Z(3),\qquad
 \delta_\sigma=2-4m\delta .
\]

The new outputs are:

- **[Note03: full local complex-power time law][time].**
  Z(p,t)=sum exp(p(2 log ell-Log(w_j^2-D))) is holomorphic for Re p>1/2
  and locally C1 in real time on a finite atlas of compact subcharts.
  Inward derivatives are included at tube endpoints. Every merging spatial
  cluster has one analytic logarithm; far tails use
  their principal logs. With h=H_z/H(t,c+w), f_p=ell^(2p)(w^2-D)^(-p),
  and the complete oriented spatial contours C,
  \[
  Z_t=\mathscr C\big([(f_p)_t-(f_p)_{ww}-c'(f_p)_w]h+(f_p)_w h^2\big).
  \]
  All finite connectors and the full h-squared term remain. Independent
  boundary-integral domination pays the time derivative through mergers;
  it is not inferred from a value tail or a smooth labeling of roots.
- **[Note04: symmetry and exact branch defect][symmetry].** Compatible
  conjugate logs give Z^sharp=Z. Assigning +pi to every negative-real
  denominator instead gives
  Z_+-Z_+^sharp=-2i ell^(2p) sin(pi p) A_-(p), where A_- counts all such
  occurrences. The note gives the full weighted-contour and half-line
  corrections with orientation. Local atlas changes have finite factors
  exp(-2pi i k p)-1; integer observables and their derivatives glue.
  There is no asserted global smooth principal branch or reflected-exponent
  functional equation.
- **[Note05: complete damped two-line evolution][contour].** For any fixed
  epsilon>0, use
  \[
  K_\epsilon(p,\delta)=\frac{12e^{\epsilon(p-2)^2}}{p-2}
             +\frac{16\delta e^{\epsilon(p-3)^2}}{p-3},
  \quad
  F=\frac1{2\pi i}\left(\int_{4-i\infty}^{4+i\infty}
                   -\int_{1-i\infty}^{1+i\infty}\right)K_\epsilon Z\,dp.
  \]
  The distinct p=1 evaluation of m uses real edges 3/4 and 3/2.
  The nonlinear term 2m^2 remains outside the contour. Both lines are
  retained; Gaussian decay in the imaginary direction does not permit
  dropping the right line or taking epsilon to zero.

These identities are **representations**, and their tail/domination statements
are intermediate estimates under [classification01][class]. No RH-equivalent
criterion or numerical evidence for one is introduced.

## 3. Quantitative outcome and its exact limit

[Note06][comparison] gives a full absolute contour envelope C_env A_1 with
C_env=2 C_epsilon sqrt(pi/epsilon) exp(Theta^2/(4epsilon))/pi.
It has order L at fixed collar and damping and is larger than the stated
direct coefficient. For any prescribed finite occurrence/conjugate groups,
the group's absolute full-contour norm is at least the absolute value of its
exact signed residue combination. This remains true after taking the
right-minus-left difference and, for compatible groups, real Schwarz folding.
The complete sum and its integrals interchange absolutely.

A stronger test preserves m<0 and the new complex-p properties. The existing
[GR changed static divisor construction][grproof] is extended to the complete
sum, not just its inserted core. At c=T=e^L, a=4,d=1,D=0, b_core=8 and
J=floor L,
\[
 Z(p)=b_{\rm core}^{-2p}[2J+2(J+1)\cos(\pi p)]+R(p),\quad
 |R(u+iv)|\le(10C_u+5)L^{1-2u}e^{\pi|v|/2}\quad(1\le u\le r<\infty).
\]
It includes the entire retained background and all reflected insertions.
The complete Z is holomorphic on Re p>1/2, Schwarz symmetric and integrable
against the selected damped kernels, yet
\[
 m=-2/b_{\rm core}^2+O(L^{-1})\le-1/64\ \text{eventually},\qquad
 F/L\longrightarrow 3/256>0.
\]
Thus **no uniform o(L) signed forcing upper bound follows in the specified
fixed enlarged-constant static class**, even with those p-properties and a
negative m margin. The family changes the canonical source and supplies no
common paid canonical heat tube. E02's full time law is not a premise of
this exclusion. It establishes no canonical lower bound, optimal canonical
constant, or obstruction to every contour method. [Scope07][scope]

## 4. Transport, controls and continuation

The canonical baseline stays F<=C_F L, C_F=12B_2+16dB_3. Its finite-initial-value
Riccati comparison gives an O(sqrt L) negative allowance after a positive
scaled buffer, and O(L)exp(O(sqrt L)) transport allowance for fixed scaled
duration. The exact identity is still
\[
 W(x,y)\delta(x)=\delta(y)-2\int_x^y W(u,y)\,du,\qquad
 W(x,y)=\exp\left(-4\int_x^y m\right).
\]
The initial layer, independently paid tube and independent incoming or terminal
gap anchor remain. Equality with the exact full cost permits delta(x)=0,
including the initial endpoint; the physical gap cost carries ell^2.
No new collision exclusion or reality coverage follows.
