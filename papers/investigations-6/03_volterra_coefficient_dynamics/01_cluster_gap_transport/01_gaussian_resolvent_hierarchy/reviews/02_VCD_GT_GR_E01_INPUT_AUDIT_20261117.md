## Ready domain and algebra for E02

Use the physical \(H_t\), \(\partial_tH=-H_{zz}\), with the complete
integer divisor for \(0\le t\le 1/2\), the unchanged theta source and
the independently enclosed count-two tube. Freeze \(T\ge64\),
\(L=\log T\), \(\ell=L^{-1}\), \(d\ge0\),
\(\max(2,4\sqrt d)\le a\le L\) and \(r_0=a\ell\). On the whole tube,
\(c\in[T,2T]\), \(|D|\le d\ell^2\le r_0^2/16\), the targets lie
within \(r_0/4\) and the exterior has \(|w_j|\ge r_0\). Thus
\[
 |a_j|=|w_j^2-D|\ge (15/16)|w_j|^2,\qquad
 \eta_0=(15/16)r_0^2.
\]
A fixed real tube can have one-sided physical derivatives at its
endpoints, supplied by the local contour extension. The
[strip/product record][s03], [count record][s05] and manuscript
[§15][height] supply the uniform strip and unit count, including
the separately paid \(t=0\) argument. The [density record][s06] and
[§16][transverse] give broad unconditioned bounds. They have no role
in justifying an individual derivative via this rational contour limit.

In the finite calculation let \(h_3=(uA^3-vB^3)/(u-v)\),
\(A=(u^2-D)^{-1}\), \(B=(v^2-D)^{-1}\).
Algebra gives \(h_3=-AB(A+B)-(D+uv)AB(A^2+AB+B^2)\).
For \(j<k\) the three corresponding sums before the minus signs are
\[
 MS_2-S_3,\qquad
 D(MS_3+S_2^2/2-3S_4/2),\qquad
 PT_3+T_2^2/2-3(S_3+DS_4)/2 .
\]
The last diagonal uses \(w^2m^4=m^3+Dm^4\). Substituting in
Note44 (14) cancels \(PT_3\) and \(DMS_3\) and yields
\[
 S_2'=8MS_2-32S_3+4DS_2^2-40DS_4+4T_2^2 .
\]
In the finite nonclosure comparison the stated derivative difference
is \(10838016/121550625\), which is
\(4[(80/441)^2-(1136/11025)^2]\). The comparison uses the positive
branch for both exactly equal denominator multisets. Continuity of
the contour moments and their derivatives under a simple-root
perturbation at the reference slice proves the finite rational law at
multiplicities while keeping the independent count and separation.

For the canonical step, let \(\langle f\rangle_\Gamma\) be the
positively oriented normalized integral on a local fixed circle
in the physical \(z\) plane that encloses the two targets. Set
\(u=z-c(t)\), \(Q=u^2-D\) and \(B=F_z/F\).
For \(f=Q^{-p}\), \(p\ge1\), and \(f=uQ^{-2}\), the exterior sum of
\(f(\rho)\) equals \(-\langle fB\rangle_\Gamma\). The integrals of
\(f\) and \(fQ_z/Q\) are zero by rational decay at infinity and
containment of all their poles in \(\Gamma\). Thus constant prefactors,
Hadamard linear drift and individual \(1/\rho\) regularizers give
zero, while the exterior log-derivative pole gives the negative of
its kernel value on the inner contour. On \(\Gamma\),
\[
 \dot B=-F_{zzz}/F+F_zF_{zz}/F^2,\qquad
 \dot Q=-2u c'-D',
\]
\[
 S_p'=-\left\langle Q^{-p}\dot B-pQ^{-p-1}\dot Q B\right\rangle_\Gamma,
 \qquad
 T_2'=-\left\langle
     uQ^{-2}\dot B-c'Q^{-2}B-2uQ^{-3}\dot Q B
                         \right\rangle_\Gamma .
\]
Track \(c',D'\) by differentiating the first two argument-principle
moments on that same circle. These stationary-\(z\) derivative formulas
retain the moving center and discriminant. A centered moving contour
can instead hold \(u\) fixed and use the corresponding chain rule.

At each reference time, truncate the opposite-paired product by modulus,
keeping all equal-modulus, opposite and conjugate occurrences. The
constant is \(H_t(0)>0\). The product tail has
\(\sup_t\sum_{|\rho|>N}|\rho|^{-2}=O(\log N/N)\) from [§15][height].
For a fixed compact \(z\)-set this gives a uniform multiplicative tail
error tending to zero, and Cauchy's formula gives uniform convergence
of the required spatial jets on a slightly smaller compact set. The
polynomial heat solution initialized at each reference time has its
first time jet there determined by these spatial jets. On the compact
paid real-time tube a finite cover by local fixed circles and closed
time subintervals supplies positive minima for \(|H_t|\) and \(|Q|\)
on their boundaries. The moment derivatives therefore converge
uniformly there. For sufficiently large \(N\), the truncated slices
contain the exact targets and use the same \(c,D\); the remaining
\(S_1,\ldots,S_4,T_2\) tails tend uniformly to zero by the shell bound
and the fixed height band. No single polynomial evolved from one
reference time is being asserted to converge on a common time
neighborhood. \(P=\sum w_j/(w_j^2-D)\) has only inverse-first-power
decay; use the canceled formula for \(S_2'\) when passing to the
limit. If desired, the displayed \(T_2'\) contour is already an exact
first derivative in terms of a paid field. An autonomous reduced
odd-companion law requires its own algebra and convergence payment.

The uniform series
\(\mathcal R(\eta,t)=\sum_{n\ge0}\eta^n S_{n+1}(t)\) holds on compact
subdisks of \(|\eta|<\eta_0\), by
\(\sum |a_j|^{-1}/(1-|\eta|/\eta_0)\).
For a direct contour kernel \(1/(Q-\eta)\), enclose the shifted poles
as well as the targets. In centered coordinates the radius \(r_0/2\)
works only if \(|D+\eta|<(r_0/2)^2\).
For \(|\eta|\le b<\eta_0\), a radius strictly between
\(\sqrt{r_0^2/16+b}\) and \(r_0\) works uniformly.

## Ready domains and obligations for E03

The radial scale derivative in Note44 (7) integrates to one per
occurrence because \(w\ne0\). The count and collar give uniform
absolute tails at fixed \(T\). Finite zero clusters vary continuously
as multisets, so the full radial sum is continuous on the paid tube.
Uniform radial scale endpoints follow by first paying a common tail
and then treating the finite near contributions with \(|w|\ge r_0\).
Time differentiation of the raw occurrence weights cannot be assumed
at exterior mergers. In particular, for a real exterior double with
no other exterior cluster except its two occurrences, let its centered
separation be \(\pm\sqrt{E(t)}\), where \(E'(t_*)=2\) by the
independent local pair law. Write \(f(w,\bar w)=e^{-|w|^2/R^2}/(w^2-D)\).
At its real displacement \(u\), the two-term Taylor sum contains
\(2 f_{w\bar w}|E(t)|\). The other quadratic terms are proportional
to \(E(t)\). Here
\[
 f_{w\bar w}(u,\bar u)
 =-R^{-2} f(u,\bar u)
       \left(1-\frac{u^2}{R^2}-\frac{2u^2}{u^2-D}\right)>0
\]
under the collar, so the slopes differ at the double. The even
quartic with two separated real doubles supplies this finite class
of examples. This regularity check places no claim on whether such
mergers occur along a particular paid canonical tube. A radial
time law would need a specified weak or integrated formulation,
with control of its differentiated tails as well as its slice tails.

For \(s=R^{-2}\) in a compact positive interval, the holomorphic
kernel \(k_s(w)=e^{-s w^2}/(w^2-D)\) is bounded at large
\(w=x+iy\) by the Gaussian in \(x\) times \(e^{s y^2}\)
and the rational tail. Its exact radial conversion per occurrence is
\[
 \frac{e^{-s(x^2+y^2)}-e^{-s(x^2-y^2)-2isxy}}{a_j}.
\]
The numerator has modulus at most
\(2s|wy|e^{-s x^2+s y^2}\), by integrating the exponential along
the segment from \(|w|^2\) to \(w^2\). The radial conversion and
\(M-\sum k_s(w_j)\) are full signed terms.

The manuscript [safe-line premise][transverse] supports the outer
contour proposed for B01. For each fixed real \(t\), choose the two
vertical abscissae independently in distant unit windows, a distance
\(\gg1/\log(2+|x+c|)\) from the projected divisor. The closed-unit
count pays this choice by excluding short intervals. Subtracting
the log derivative at \(x+c+4i\) cancels its regularizers. The near
unit shells on those edges cost \(O(\log^2(2+|x+c|))\), the distant
shells \(O(\log(2+|x+c|))\). The Gaussian makes both vertical integrals
vanish. Residue subtraction of the target circle \(|w|=r_0/2\)
leaves the exterior sum from the positive outer contour on
\(\Im w=\pm4\). The two edges need only be chosen on the slice
used to establish this representation.

In the resulting horizontal integrals, \(h(t,w)=H_z/H(t,c(t)+w)\)
and its first two spatial derivatives are
\(O(\log(2+|x+c|))\), by the safe-line premise and the absolutely
convergent inverse-square and inverse-cube shell sums. On the inner
circle joint holomorphy and clearance bound the derivatives.
Use \(h_t=-h_{ww}-2h h_w+c'h_w\) and
\(\dot k_s=D'e^{-s w^2}/(w^2-D)^2\) at fixed \(s,w\).
The horizontal derivative integrands have an integrable Gaussian
majorant uniform in real \(t\) and \(0<s_-\le s\le s_+<\infty\)
for the fixed tube. An evolving \(s\) adds \(-s' w^2 k_s\).
This pays the premises for real-time \(C^1\) in E03 without a
uniform complex-time strip. Constants include the safe-line weight
\(e^{16s_+}\); growth-uniform gains at microscopic scales require
a separate estimate. Finite scales retain the complete complement.
The small-length endpoint is unqualified only for the radial weight.

For complex powers, take a specified logarithm on each near root
cluster's denominator neighborhood avoiding zero and the principal
branch on the positive far sector. At a merger a local analytic
cluster sum uses a single compatible branch at every coalescing
denominator within that root cluster. The collar excludes zero, while
the strip gives \(\Re a_j\gg|\Re w_j|^2\) on a uniform far tail.
Shell convergence is normal for \(p\) in compact subsets of
\(\Re p>1/2\) at fixed branches. For real \(p\) a conjugate pair
with negative-real denominators needs opposite boundary arguments
to produce conjugate terms. The [gamma passage record][gamma] pays
the principal-branch scalar identity for \(\Re a>0,\Re p>0\).
For the positive integration ray, the absolute interchange is
precisely paid by
\[
 \int_0^\infty\sum_j s^{\Re p-1}|e^{-s a_j}|\,ds
   =\Gamma(\Re p)\sum_j(\Re a_j)^{-\Re p}<\infty.
\]
The far sector's shell bounds and strictly positive real parts for
each retained near term suffice at a slice. For uniform interchange
on a compact tube require a uniform positive lower bound for the
near real parts on the integration sector. The complete near
exterior can violate this condition. Keep any excluded terms as
actual powers, or justify a separate contour and branch prescription.
