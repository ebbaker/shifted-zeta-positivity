George Csordas, *Fourier transforms of positive definite kernels and the
Riemann xi-Function*, arXiv:1309.0055v2, 21 February 2014.
[Primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/1309.0055v2), Theorem 2.4 and
Theorem 3.7. These connect generalized Laguerre inequalities with Fourier
correlation kernels. Theorem 3.7 retains its admissible-kernel and strict
log-concavity hypotheses; its full hierarchy is a characterization, not
a new sufficient source estimate. No such theorem is applied to an atomic
measure here. The finite identities and constants in Note24 are derived
directly. Existing half-line normalization caveat retained.
