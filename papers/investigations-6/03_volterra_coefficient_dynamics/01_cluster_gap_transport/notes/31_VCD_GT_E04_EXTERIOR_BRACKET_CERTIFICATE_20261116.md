## 1. Literal groups and preflight before new source work

The frozen target is the same complete canonical physical heat field and
counted pair centered at \(a=140101/10\), on \(I=[0,1/10]\).
Each Manifest16 group specifies exact rational midpoint offset \(m_j\),
slope \(v_j\), and half-width \(w_j>0\), with boundaries

\[
 \ell_j(t)=a+m_j-w_j+v_j(t-1/20),\qquad
 u_j(t)=a+m_j+w_j+v_j(t-1/20).
 \tag{2}
\]

All 176 groups remain in their frozen order, are pairwise disjoint on
every slice, and exclude the real diameter of the radius-\(8/5\)
target-only disk. Every boundary remains in \([13840,14180]\).
These facts were checked exactly at both time endpoints; the relevant
differences are affine, so the endpoint checks pay the whole interval.
The total offset envelope is
\([-80417/512,81165/512]\), and \(\max_j|v_j|=125/32\).
The full list of rational groups is the manifest itself, rather than
rounded location tables. Its SHA-256 is
`c068fda3ec1b36fd1b14e949e484fbfe32f4f8f6670d10f90cc6be93c0932cff`.

The [Stage A diagnostic][diagnostic] supplied candidate locations, not
proof of full-time signs or matched identities. The certificate uses
neither its three-time signs nor its ambiguous planning matches as count
premises. Its planning uses of old floating gap/drift values do not enter
the certificate arithmetic.

Before generating any new boundary source values, the program verified
the manifest and [input pins][pins], the Note30 proof, both old consumers,
and both hashes of the original complete-source array. It replayed the
target-only auxiliary disk, original collar, tensor errors and **512
closed-cell enclosures of \(c,D\)**. Only the geometry/moment interfaces
were called; no target \(K\) evaluation or gap-law reconstruction was used.

For each cell, the hypothetical retained weight was computed with every
manifest group assumed to supply its real occurrence:

\[
 M_T=\kappa_0+\sum_{j=1}^{176}\frac1{U_{j,T}^2-D_{-,T}},
 \qquad \kappa_0=\frac{5000}{1962848636289}.
 \tag{3}
\]

Here \(U_{j,T}\) bounds the largest distance between the affine bracket's
cell endpoint extrema and the entire independently enclosed target-center
interval; \(D_{-,T}\) is the same cell's lower gap enclosure. Every
denominator was strictly positive. The preflight minimum exceeded
\(0.59375580810\), so new source generation was allowed under the
[frozen Stage B contract][request]. This was a conditional mass gate at
that stage, not yet a certified count.

The record also recomputes two comparisons for these same brackets:

| --- | --- |

The first two failures concern those allowances, not actual curvature.
Using each time cell's joint location/gap information is quantitatively
material. The global and cellwise positive gap inputs already establish
target-pair reality; the new output is its exterior-derived curvature
magnitude. The direct comparator receives those same inputs.

## 2. Complete source and uniform boundary signs

For each of the 352 affine boundaries, use its own constant positive
gauge \(e^{\pi B/8}\), where \(B\) is its position at \(t=1/20\).
The source is Note30's exact \(\xi\) Gaussian convolution, with 224-bit
Arb arithmetic, spacing \(1/3\), and cutoff 12. There are 13 degree-12
Lobatto time nodes on the full interval. Time zero is evaluated directly
through \(\xi\); at positive nodes, conjugation pairs the two real-lattice
halves. The complete lattice and omitted-tail errors are paid separately
from finite arithmetic.

The constant-gauge sample balls are interpolated with a real Chebyshev
polynomial. Interval coefficients retain the finite arithmetic and archive
reconstruction errors. Note30's \(25\)-fold source-error allowance and
full affine-chain-rule time remainder are then added. In particular, the
velocity-dependent derivative sum and factor \(e^{\pi|v_j|/160}\)
are included; the fixed-point heat derivative alone is not substituted.
The largest complete analytic interpolation allowance was less than
3.86534038 in the respective boundary gauges.

Closed sign cells use the certified Chebyshev derivative bound
\(\sum_{m\ge1}m^2|a_m|\), not point sampling alone. All boundaries passed
the fixed schedule:

| Number of closed sign cells on the full interval | Boundaries accepted at that level |
| --- | ---: |
| 16 | 344 |
| 64 | 8 |
| 256 or 1024 | 0 additional levels required |

The smallest accepted strict boundary-sign floor exceeded 225.29586666
in its own constant gauge. Both boundaries succeeded with opposite signs
for every group. Consequently the fixed omission rule removed **zero**
groups. Selecting one real occurrence from each interval leaves all
unselected conjugate groups favorable, as proved in Note30. No exact
count, simplicity or continuous root labeling was needed.

After the signs were certified, (3) was recomputed for the retained
manifest on all 512 coordinate cells. The minimum occurred on cell 511
(zero-based), the final closed time cell, and its outward lower endpoint
exceeded

\[
 0.5937558081007126288347775431878456.
\]

Thus the margin above the frozen target exceeds 0.00375580810, proving
(1). A convenient exact-rational consequence, checked against the outward
recorded lower endpoint with Arb, is
\(-K>19/32=0.59375\), whose margin above \(59/100\) is exactly
\(3/800\). Only the target's reflected pair is added in (3); negative reflections
of the 176 selected occurrences are omitted, along with the remaining
favorable divisor. No global exterior-reality premise is imposed.

## 3. Execution, source preservation and replay

The two-row benchmark was reused in the acquisition and projected
94.41298 seconds for exactly 156,640 \(\xi\) calls. Actual generation used
156,640 calls and 94.42562 seconds; complete execution took 99.23591
seconds. These are observed timings, not proof inputs. They were below the
200,000-call, 360-second source and 420-second total caps. The runtime was
Python 3.10.0, python-flint 0.9.0 and FLINT 3.6.0 on macOS arm64.

uses integer group IDs, while the initial loader required strings. The

acquisition reported here; no source, grid, precision or scientific target

## 4. Controls and interpretation

The source-free checks exercised the same strict-sign routine on a
constant-positive polynomial, zero, and a polynomial whose sign changes
inside the time interval. The latter two were rejected. At the exact
quartic double slice, every literal exterior boundary has strictly positive
\((z^2-a^2)^2\); the corresponding quadratic has strictly positive
\((z-a)^2\). They cannot provide the claimed additional opposite-sign
brackets, despite the target geometry they share. Wrong-digest and missing
archive checks were rejected by the identity guards. DCT normalization
and decimal-ball enclosure checks were also performed before acquisition.

The full source/control premise screen remains [Note30 §7][screen30] and
[Note21 §6][screen21]. Endpoint-defective finite square/prime changes do
not inherit a global strip; Gaussian and reflected super-Gaussian controls
fail the stated order premise; other families retain their specifically
untested local signs/mass or changed physical observation. A source's being
different from theta is not itself a failed quantitative inequality.

This certificate answers the fixed finite question: these explicit
brackets have independently verified real lower counts and locations
sufficient to pay \(-K\ge0.59\) over the whole slab. It does not claim a
height-uniform theorem, arbitrary source stability, exact exterior counts,
or a general prohibition of collisions. The previously accepted measured
bound \(K\le-0.599329616\) is stronger numerically; it was not an input
to (1). The direct endpoint/minimum result also remains stronger for the
already settled reality question. No evaluation-cost advantage or new
endpoint enclosure is claimed. Final acceptance of this proof/code/record
batch awaits the separate internal assessment and its bounded replay.
