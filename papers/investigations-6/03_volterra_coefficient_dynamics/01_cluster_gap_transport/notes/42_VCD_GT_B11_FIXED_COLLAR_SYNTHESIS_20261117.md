## 1. What is fixed and what remains independently paid

Keep the unchanged complete canonical field, physical heat law
\(\partial_tH=-H_{zz}\), full integer zero divisor and \(0\le t\le1/2\).
Let \(T\ge64\), \(L=\log T\), \(\ell=L^{-1}\), fixed in height and time.
Take fixed \(d\ge0\), \(\max(2,4\sqrt d)\le a\le L\) and
\(3\le R\le T/4\). The main comparison holds a fixed as T grows.

Eligible conjugation-invariant unordered pairs have real center
\(c\in[T,2T]\), real \(|D|\le d\ell^2\), and every exterior occurrence
satisfies \(|\rho-c|\ge a\ell\). Count-two and this empty collar are
application premises. Both signs of D and a double D=0 are included.
No lower bound for the number of eligible pairs is assumed. For E21 the
same pair and geometry must persist on a separately paid time tube.
E20 instead counts eligible pairs on each slice and needs no labels.

The full real signed interaction is
\[
 M=\sum_{\rho\text{ exterior}}\frac1{(\rho-c)^2-D},
 \qquad D'=2-4MD.                                             \tag{1}
\]
All sums use complete multiplicities and conjugate grouping; the displayed
M sum is absolutely convergent. The incoming D anchor remains separate.

## 2. A fixed-collar gain for total time occupied by bad pairs

Let \(N_-(t)\) count the eligible pairs with negative nearby sum \(S_R\).
[Note38][e19] gives \(N\{M<0\}\le N_-(t)\) and the slice allowance
\(N_-(t)\le C_bTL e^{-2ba/3}\), with one fixed \(0<b<1/2\).
At fixed a this is O(TL). Its direct integration over duration \(\tau\)
gives O(\(\tau TL\)).

[E20 / Note40][e20] retains the self-conjugate contribution discarded in
the accepted broad-window convex-moment identity. For each nonreal
occurrence it contributes \(a_U(x)g'(y)/y\) to dissipation. Real roots
are not counted by the limiting value at y=0. With
\(g(y)=y^2/2+|y|^3/(3r_0)\), \(r_0=a/L\), this weight is
\(1+|y|/r_0\). It pays Note38's negative-kernel incidence weight
\(5/3+8|y|/(3r_0)\), up to a factor 8/3. Source second and third moments
pay the initial energy. The full boundary flux and collision assembly
are part of the proof, not unrecorded favorable terms.

For \(0\le\alpha\le\beta\le1/2\), \(\tau=\beta-\alpha\), the result is
\[
 \begin{split}
 \int_\alpha^\beta N_-(t)\,dt
 &\le B_b\left(\frac{93}{b^2}+\frac{279}{ab^3}\right)\frac{T}{L}\\
 &\quad+\frac{32}{3}D_\chi
       \left(\frac12+\frac{L}{3a}\right)\tau L^3 .           \tag{2}
 \end{split}
\]
Intersect this with the old slice allowance times duration and the
packing cap times duration. Constants are symbolic inherited constants;
no numerical finite-height onset is certified.

For fixed a and fixed positive physical duration, (2) is O(T/L) with
the lower-order boundary term displayed. Relative to TL it is
O(\(L^{-2}\))+O(\(L^3/T\)), a two-logarithm improvement. This measures
time occupation, not the number of event times, distinct trajectories,
or a fraction of an independently uncounted eligible population.
For \(\tau\asymp\ell^2\), division by duration gives only O(TL).
The useful aggregate gain therefore needs time longer than the
spacing-squared scale. It does not control an arbitrary selected slice.

## 3. Exact signed dynamics and interior control on a paid tube

For \(w_j=\rho_j-c\), put \(m_j=(w_j^2-D)^{-1}\) and
\(S_p=\sum_jm_j^p\). [E21 / Note41][e21] proves the exact law
\[
 \boxed{M'=2M^2-12S_2-16DS_3.}                              \tag{3}
\]
It includes both D signs, target doubles and multiple exterior roots.
The power sums are real after conjugation, not termwise nonnegative.
Finite-polynomial algebra is extended to the complete canonical field
at a fixed time using paired Hadamard truncations, the physical heat
derivative and the counted-pair contour. This avoids differentiating an
infinite list of singular individual root velocities.

Write \(\sigma=(t-t_0)/\ell^2\), \(m=\ell^2M\),
\(\delta=D/\ell^2\), \(\mu_j=\ell^2m_j\). The unit count and paid collar
give constants B,K, depending only on the fixed geometry and inherited
source constants, such that
\[
 |m|\le BL,\qquad
 m_\sigma=2m^2-\mathcal F,\qquad
 \mathcal F=12\sum_j\mu_j^2+16\delta\sum_j\mu_j^3,
 \qquad |\mathcal F|\le KL.                                \tag{4}
\]
The scale ell is frozen, so there is no omitted scale derivative.
With \(h=\sqrt{KL/2}\), Riccati comparison gives
\[
 \boxed{m(\sigma)\ge-h\coth(2h\sigma),\qquad\sigma>0.}      \tag{5}
\]
After a fixed positive elapsed scaled time, the negative allowance
improves from O(L) to O(\(\sqrt L\)). This is a conditional interior
magnitude estimate; it does not acquire the tube, give O(1), or fix the
sign of M. It is compatible with every fixed negative margin at large T.

The initial count bound also pays the logarithmic boundary layer. Set
\(k=\max(BL/h,2)\). For any scaled length s inside the paid tube,
\[
 \int_0^s(-m)_+\,d\sigma
 \le hs+\frac12\log\frac{k+1}{2},\qquad
 \exp\!\left(-4\int_{t_0}^{t_0+s\ell^2}M\,dt\right)
 \le e^{4hs}\left(\frac{k+1}{2}\right)^2.                  \tag{6}
\]
At fixed s>0 the allowance is O(L)exp(O(\(\sqrt L\))), improving
exp(O(L)) from raw counting. It still diverges and pays no incoming D
value or gap-comparison margin. The direct evaluator has the same gain.

## 4. Why these two gains do not remove one exception

Equation (4) also gives the physical modulus
\(|d(\ell^2M)/dt|\le K_1L^4\). A fixed adverse margin
\(\ell^2M(t_*)\le-\epsilon\) persists with half that margin for a
one-sided length \(\epsilon/(2K_1L^4)\), if that entire side is inside
the independently paid tube. A strict sign without a margin pays no
uniform duration. Several pairs require simultaneous paid tubes before
converting occupation into a snapshot count.

Dividing (2) by that visibility length produces
O(\(TL^3/\epsilon\))+O(\(L^4\)), worse than the old O(TL)
fixed-collar slice allowance. Even a separately paid duration of negative
visibility comparable to ell² only recovers O(TL)+O(\(L^4\)),
whose leading fixed-a allowance is O(TL); survival of the counted tube
alone is a weaker premise. The original slice cap remains available. The T/L occupation
allowance grows when divided by the largest permitted physical duration,
so it cannot force the displayed count allowance below one. These are
failures of these upper-bound arguments, not lower bounds on true
occupation, a theorem that exceptions exist, or a universal obstruction.

Audit18's optional short-tube argument from a snapshot pays only time
O(\(L^{-4}\)) with a reduced collar and altered pair-size constants.
It does not supply the unaltered E21 tube over ell² and is not used to
claim automatic access to (5) after a full microscopic time buffer.
