## 2. Why the two literal Fourier proposals stop here

The natural difference-frequency coefficient matrix of the deflated
double-point numerator has a negative 2-by-2 principal determinant for
any two distinct positive spectral radii. That unchanged matrix therefore
has no positive Gram factorization. This does not rule out alternate
representations, redistribution at coincident frequencies, or local
phase-dependent estimates.

Keeping the atomic constant mode and paying the rest by absolute values
gives exactly 10 sum(omega_j^2 x_j^6)-3 m2 m4. In the complete absolutely
continuous even source, the constant-frequency atom is absent and this
allowance is -3 m2 m4<0. It cannot pay even the sign, before normalization,
much less the positive magnitude at a stipulated double. This is failure
of the allowance, not a negative canonical numerator. The finite sufficient
failure condition and its moment assumptions are proved in Note28.

Any new Fourier proposal must preserve phase correlations and must pay
the normalized deflated two-point expression on the full time interval.

real-axis double numerator. Finite-model proposals still require the
complete error, coordinates, collar and physical heat-defect payments.
