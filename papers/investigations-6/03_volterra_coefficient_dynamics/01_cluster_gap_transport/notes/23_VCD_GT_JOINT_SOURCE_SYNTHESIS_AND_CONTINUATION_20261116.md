## 1. What the joint estimate pays

Keep the complete canonical field \(H_t\), physical equation
\(\partial_tH_t=-\partial_z^2H_t\), \(a=140101/10\) and
\(0\le t\le1/10\). Let the two multiplicity-counted zeros in the
original radius-\(9/10\) contour be \(z_1,z_2\), with
\(c=(z_1+z_2)/2\), \(D=(z_1-z_2)^2/4\). Both coordinates are real,
including before a possible double. The complete-source growth and the
classical strip contraction give order at most one and
\(|\Im\rho(t)|\le\sqrt{1-2t}\le1\). The precise source normalization,
primary passages and proof hypotheses are recorded in [Reference03][sources].

The deflated Hadamard product yields the absolutely convergent identity
\[
 -K=\sum_{\rho\ {\rm exterior}}
       \frac{1}{(z_1-\rho)(z_2-\rho)}.
\]
For \(\rho=c+x+iy\), grouping each nonreal zero with its conjugate
before estimating gives two copies of
\[
 q(x,y,D)=\frac{x^2-y^2-D}
 {(x^2-y^2-D)^2+4x^2y^2}.
\]
A real occurrence contributes one copy. Absolute convergence makes this
grouping legitimate, including multiplicities and a double target zero.

The new acquisition proves that the disk \(|z-a|<8/5\) contains exactly
the original pair throughout the interval, and pays
\(|c-a|\le7/100\), \(|D|\le1/5\). Thus every exterior zero satisfies
\[
 |x|\ge\sqrt{(8/5)^2-1}-7/100>113/100,\qquad
 x^2-y^2-D>769/10000>0.
\]
All conjugate groups are favorable. The unselected infinite remainder
can therefore be discarded in an upper estimate of \(K\), with no
separate absolute-tail debit. This is the gain over the stopped
one-mode/Cauchy-tail allowance in [Synthesis20][previous].

Evenness supplies the reflected target pair, whose exact contribution
to \(-K\) is \(1/[2(c^2-D)]\). Consequently the new bound is
\[

 \kappa_0=\frac{5000}{1962848636289}
           \simeq2.547318171946818\times10^{-9}.}

endpoint gap to establish this sign.

The positive-group proof gives a specific finite interface. For disjoint

\[
 K\le-\kappa_0-\sum_j n_j m_j,\qquad

 {(U_j^2+1+1/5)^2+4U_j^2}>0.
