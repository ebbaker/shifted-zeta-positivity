For the physical equation \(\partial_t H=-\partial_z^2H\), determine what passes up and down a spatial tree when two adjacent zero clusters become one block. Start on a symbolic time interval \(I\) with a cleared family of contours around finite clusters. Allow the counted pair to coalesce inside its contour. Use three comparisons, in order.

1. **Exact evolution.** Compare evolving the children and merging with merging first and evolving the parent. The same physical exterior acts in both calculations. Exhibit every term through which it enters. If one local state cannot determine its own derivative, identify sufficient additional information or exhibit two compatible states with the same retained data and different derivatives.
2. **A reduction that changes an estimate.** Seek control of the signed gap drift \(2-4DM\), the joint forcing \(\mathcal F=12S_2+16DS_3\), or a specifically named downstream moment equation. Measure what remains when the spatial resolution or the retained channels are truncated. State scale, time, gap and exterior-separation dependence. Compare with direct evaluation or bounds for the identical kernel using identical input. A rewritten equality is a foundation. An estimate earns its scope through a closed law, a sharper bound, a decay rate for neglected terms, or a proved limitation of a specified state. [AGC §3](../../manuscript/sections/03_hierarchy.tex) and [§4](../../manuscript/sections/04_continuation.tex)
3. **Passage to the tree limit and AGC.** Distinguish refinement in one bounded spatial region, exhaustion of the full exterior, and increasing the number of retained moments. State the topology and the order of any limits. Justify time differentiation and assembly separately. If a joint estimate survives, propagate it to the stopped backward-time pair law with the required initial and clearance data. Only then select explicit examples to test sharpness, sensitivity or a hypothesis left open by the general argument. Algebraic collision controls apply from the start.
