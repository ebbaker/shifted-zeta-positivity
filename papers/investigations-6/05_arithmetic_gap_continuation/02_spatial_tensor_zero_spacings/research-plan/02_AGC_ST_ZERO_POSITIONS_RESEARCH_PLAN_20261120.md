A precise question for a proposed reduction is whether its derivative is determined by the information it retains. Let \(X\) be the exact state, \(h\) the allowed outer boundary input, \(\dot X=\mathcal V(t,X,h)\) its generator and \(\pi X\) a differentiable, time-independent choice of reduced variables. Closure would require a function \(v\) satisfying
\[
 D\pi_X\,\mathcal V(t,X,h)=v(t,\pi X,h)
\]
on the stated class, as well as consistent contraction of the projected states. Two admissible states with the same \(t,\pi X,h\) and different projected derivatives identify a missing datum. If the identity holds only up to a residual, carry that residual into the particular gap or moment row to be estimated. A time-dependent projection also contributes \(\partial_t\pi\).
