Write \(a=c-\sqrt D,\ b=c+\sqrt D\) on a separated chart, \(P=(z-c)^2-D\), and \(R=\partial_z\log(H/P)\) near the pair. In forward physical time the inherited equations give

\[
V_c=c_t=R(t,a)+R(t,b),\qquad
D_t=2-4DM,\qquad M=-R[a,b].
\]

Here \(R[a,b]\) is the divided difference in \(z\), with its derivative value at a double. The symmetric equations extend through \(D=0\) on a cleared count-two chart. A single simple root has \(x_t=H_{zz}(t,x)/H_z(t,x)\). The target is an enclosure for the **signed** displacement \(c(t)-c(t_0)=\int_{t_0}^t V_c(v)\,dv\), then for the two positions after combining this with an independently justified \(D(t)\) enclosure. For backward elapsed time \(u=t_0-t\), \(dc/du=-V_c(t_0-u)\) and \(dD/du=-2+4DM\). An uncertainty in the initial height remains in the final positions.
