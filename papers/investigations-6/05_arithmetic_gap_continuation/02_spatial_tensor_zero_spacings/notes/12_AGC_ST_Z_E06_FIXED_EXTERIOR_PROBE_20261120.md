## Complete exterior and the fixed observation

At each fixed physical time and for \(0<x\le X\), put
\[
 K_\rho(x)=\Re\big[(\rho-x)^{-3}-(\rho+x)^{-3}\big],
 \qquad S(x)=\sum_{\operatorname{Re}\rho>46}K_\rho(x).
\]
Every summand includes one positive-half-plane representative with multiplicity. For \(\rho=q+iy\), let \(f_y(u)=\Re(u+iy)^{-3}\). Then \(f_y'(u)=-3\Re(u+iy)^{-4}\), where
\[
 \Re(u+iy)^{-4}=\frac{u^4-6u^2y^2+y^4}{(u^2+y^2)^4}>0
 \qquad\text{if }u>(1+\sqrt2)|y|,\ u>0 .
\]
Here each of \(q-x\) and \(q+x\) is larger than \(5/2>(1+\sqrt2)|y|\). Consequently \(K_\rho(x)=f_y(q-x)-f_y(q+x)>0\) and \(K_\rho'(x)=-f_y'(q-x)-f_y'(q+x)>0\). The complete count and cubic tail ensure absolute convergence, so termwise inequalities give \(K_d(x)\le S(x)\le S(X)\). For the real occurrence \(d>X\), \(K_d(x)\) decreases as \(d\) increases since its \(d\)-derivative is \(-3(d-x)^{-4}+3(d+x)^{-4}<0\). Thus \(K_{50.1}(\underline x)\) supplies a lower bound from any certified positive lower endpoint \(\underline x\) of a target box.
