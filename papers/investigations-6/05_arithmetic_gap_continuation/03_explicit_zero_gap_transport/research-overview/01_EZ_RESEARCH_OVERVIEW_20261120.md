Thus \(\dot\gamma_n=\gamma_{n+2}+\sum_{j=0}^n\binom nj\gamma_{j+1}\gamma_{n-j+1}\) for \(n\ge1\). Even cumulants enter transport of odd cumulants. Use this or positive reweighting at one anchor to pay a time remainder; an arbitrary finite cumulant truncation supplies no tail control.
