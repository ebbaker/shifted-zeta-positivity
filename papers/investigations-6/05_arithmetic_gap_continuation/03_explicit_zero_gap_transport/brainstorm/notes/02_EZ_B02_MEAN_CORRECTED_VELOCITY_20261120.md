# B02 · which cancellation to measure

20 November 2026. Prepared for Edward Baker with OpenAI Codex assistance. Conceptual lead gpt-6.2-astra/ultra. Bounded estimates and comparison design by gpt-6.2-sol/ultra. The [register](../../research-progress/01_EZ_RESEARCH_PROGRESS_20261120.md) records the decision at this stage.

The finite target is the first squared half-gap \(D(999)\) carried from 1000 with its count and physical-time derivative sign. The mean-corrected derivative is a representation. Interval bounds on its common coefficients and its residual are intermediate estimates. The comparison is the complete width from the same source observations. This is a local finite question inside known high-time coverage. **Quartic curvature with an absolute sixth derivative.** Write \(a=E_tU,\ w=m_2^*\), \(C_4=m_4^*-3w^2\), \(\beta=1-X^2w/2\). For \(h=\log j\), the opening [quartic identity](../../manuscript/frontier.tex) yields \(G=(\log(ag))_t=C_4\langle x^2\rangle/2+e\), \(|e|\le B_6\langle x^4\rangle/24\). To bound \(B_6\), majorize \(|j^{(r)}|\) for \(r=1,\ldots,6\) by \(A=(Xw,w,Xm_4^*,m_4^*,Xm_6^*,m_6^*)\), by \(|\sin z|\le |z|\) on the real line. A logarithmic Taylor expansion about each real \(x\) bounds \(|h^{(6)}(x)|\) by
\[
6![z^6]\sum_{\ell=1}^{6}\frac1\ell
  \left(\sum_{r=1}^{6}\frac{A_r z^r}{\beta\,r!}\right)^\ell .
\]
The opening cubic tilt extends to \(d_7\) with remainder \(s^4 e^{b^2}\sqrt{E[P^6W^4]E[P^8W^4]}/24\), where \(P=U-b,\ W=U^2-b^2\). The two even factors come from Cauchy–Schwarz and fit in the degree-28 budget. A preliminary 16-cell calculation estimated a \(2.545\,10^{-7}\) width in \(D\), of which \(2.390\,10^{-7}\) came from the endpoint mean factor. Its sixth-derivative term alone reached about \(2.13\,10^{-9}\) in \(G\). These are design estimates from a helper, not the validated E03 return.

**Common velocity divided difference.** If one first adds \(wxj\) to \(j'\), the term of degree one vanishes identically, and the coefficient of degree three is \(C_4/6\). All terms through degree seven cost at most moment eight of the weighted difference. Moment ten pays the remainder. The same signed coefficient is used at both moving endpoints. This retains cancellation beyond fourth order and makes the endpoint mean error separately measurable. One change in how the paid data are used also removes the cubic tilt bottleneck for *both* arms. Expand the tilt and, in the direct arm, the tilted cosine, as single functions of the centered frequency; the absolute remainder costs the already available positive 28th moment. In particular a comparison against the opening six tilted moments alone cannot decide endpoint accuracy at this budget.

**Decision.** Issue the common velocity difference with an all-28-moment endpoint comparator and a direct implicit-velocity path control. Keep the opening measurement and the exploratory quartic comparison for context. Freeze the attempt at one slab before considering any extension.
