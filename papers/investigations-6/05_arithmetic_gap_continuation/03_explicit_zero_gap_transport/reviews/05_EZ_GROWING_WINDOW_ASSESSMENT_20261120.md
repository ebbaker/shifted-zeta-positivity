## Complete source and logarithm

With \(\zeta=\xi+i\eta\), multiplying the central error by \(e^{-\eta w}\) shifts the real Gaussian and costs \(e^{\eta^2/2}\). Dividing by the modulus of \(e^{-\zeta^2/2}\) leaves \(e^{\xi^2/2}\); applying an independent bound for each factor would overpay. The exact shifted moment of \(1+w^4+w^6\) is \(19+51\eta^2+16\eta^4+\eta^6\). At \(|\zeta|\le r\), the relative remainder is therefore \(O(\kappa^{-7/8}(\log\kappa)^3)\). On the far-left part \(rL\sqrt\kappa=o(\kappa)\), which leaves exponential decay after dividing by the Gaussian. Both estimates include the discarded Gaussian model tails.
