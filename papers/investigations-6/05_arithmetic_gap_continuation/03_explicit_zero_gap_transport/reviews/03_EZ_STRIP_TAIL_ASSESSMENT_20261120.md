**Accepted as proved in-note on the stated strip.** At \(0<\tau\le t\le1/2\), \(1/5\le y\le1\), and \(x\ge4\pi e^{32/\tau}\),
\[
\left|\frac{H_t(x+iy)}{\mathcal B_t(x+iy)}-1\right|
<\frac{15}{64}+\frac7{1000}=\frac{1931}{8000}<\frac14.

The normalizer \(\mathcal B_t(z)=M_t((1-iz)/2)\) is the published nonvanishing factor, with the sign of \(iz\) giving the carrier of real \(s\)-part \((1+y)/2\). Its argument is off the cut for \(x>0\). This is an explicit sufficient cutoff for the published pointwise approximation and gives a normalized modulus strictly above \(3/4\). ## Premise screen

The arithmetic premise is the complete enclosure (13)–(24) for the literal theta field, including its exact Dirichlet coefficients and cosine observation. Euler-factor translates change the arithmetic terms. Weighted-square, finite-source and finite-prime controls change the full coefficient sequence even where finite measurements agree. Reflected, smooth-shape and positive-primitive sources lack the canonical square expansion. Atomic threshold and finite-profile controls lack the complete theta arithmetic. The RH-conditional endpoint control fails ordinary Dirichlet series arithmetic. Fixed-amplitude observation controls change the literal cosine. Reflection rigidity identifies theta in its stated normalized integer-square class, with the quantitative budget still supplied by Theorem 1.3. These checks locate the missing premise for transfer to each class. The estimate is sufficient for one strip and its proof uses the published theta-specific enclosure directly.
