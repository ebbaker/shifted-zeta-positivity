20 November 2026. Prepared for Edward Baker with OpenAI Codex assistance. Separate contributor settings are unavailable. - **Labelled zero positions.** D. H. J. Polymath, arXiv:1904.12438v2 (4 August 2019), Theorem 1.5(i)–(ii), defines a clock location \(a_j\) by (26). For \(0<t\le1/2\), all zeros of sufficiently large real part \(x\ge e^{C/t}\) are real and close to a clock location. For \(j\ge e^{C/t}\) a disk of radius \(c/\log a_j\) counts one zero. The theorem states error \(O(x^{-ct})\). The proof after (104) gives \(O(a_j^{-ct}/\log a_j)\) for the nearest clock. Subtracting adjacent bounds gives a same-time gap comparator once both counted labels are matched. Theorem 1.3, (5) and (13), supplies an explicit normalized-field comparison for \(x\ge200,\ 0\le y\le1,\ 0<t\le1/2\). A comparison with the branch's large-\(T\) inverse first needs a proved common domain and consistent labels. [Polymath, §1](https://arxiv.org/html/1904.12438v2#S1) · [Proof of the position error, §9](https://arxiv.org/html/1904.12438v2#S9)
- **Finite-height count and Newman bound.** Dave Platt and Tim Trudgian, arXiv:2004.09765v1 (21 April 2020), Theorem 1, verifies the critical-line count through zeta ordinate \(3{,}000{,}175{,}332{,}800\). Polymath (1) gives \(x=2\gamma\) for the corresponding positive \(H_0\) zero. Section 2 describes refining brackets as needed for counting. The comparison here is with complete finite-height coverage at heat time zero. Section 3.4, Corollary 2, combines this verification with Polymath's Table 1 to give \(\Lambda\le0.2\), improving Polymath's Theorem 1.1 value of \(0.22\). A proposed lower-time gain must specify how it supplies reality and complete zero counts, or the terminal and intermediate nonvanishing conditions if using Polymath's Theorem 1.2. [Platt–Trudgian, Theorem 1 and §2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.09765v1#page=2) · [Corollary 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.09765v1#page=5) · [Polymath, Table 1](https://arxiv.org/html/1904.12438v2#S10)
