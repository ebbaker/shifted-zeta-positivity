## Established statements

**Complete forced integer hierarchy.**
[Note02](02_AGC_JH_FORCED_INTEGER_HIERARCHY_20261119.md) gives the
residual correction to every fixed-order integer trace, including both
axes and collision-regular S/T. For \(f=R/Y\),
\(\gamma=-\langle f\rangle/2\),
\(\delta=-\langle(z-c)f\rangle\) and
\(\tau[k]=-\langle kY_z/Y\rangle\), the added force is
\[
\varepsilon[k]=\langle k_zf\rangle+
                 \gamma\tau[k_c]+\delta\tau[k_D].
\]
The moving-kernel terms are essential. The finite exponential readout
has the required complete even product with fixed-degree zero count
\(O(\|J_N\|R)\); its Fourier zero set is still infinite. This is
infrastructure, not finite closure or a canonical sign estimate.

**Finite-prefix two-degree formula and complete joint time payment.**
[Note03](03_AGC_JH_DEGREE_TIME_INTERFACE_20261119.md) derives
\[
G_{N+2}-G_N=h_N^*\frac1{2\pi i}\oint_\Sigma
       \frac{\xi e^{iz\xi}}{P_N(\xi)P_{N+2}(\xi)}\,d\xi .
\]
It uses links through \(\beta_{N+1}\); the actual two-head heat defects
need \(\beta_{N+2}\). The combined-node simplex law has an explicit
finite variance and phase allowance. Finite-to-finite acquisition
therefore need not treat two full-source error laws as independent
unknown functions. No canonical coefficient table was acquired here.

With \(\Delta D=D_+-D_-\), \(M=S_1\),
\(r_j=-\langle(z-c_j)R_j/G_j\rangle\) and
\(W_-(s,v)=\exp(-4\int_s^v M_-)\),
\[
\int_u^v W_-(\Delta r-4D_+\Delta M)\,dt
       =\Delta D(v)-W_-(u,v)\Delta D(u).
\]
This holds for every subinterval of the common counted chart. The
cancellation applies to the whole signed combination, not to either
part separately. Direct evaluation with the same endpoints and weight
can use the same identity.

**Complete signed gap estimate with explicit constants.**
[Note04](04_AGC_JH_SIGNED_GAP_REMAINDER_20261119.md) is
“Proved in-note under the stated chart and input hypotheses.”
Let the common circle have radius \(r\), pair buffer \(b\),
\(q=(Z-Y)/Y\), \(\|q\|\le\eta<1\), supplied \(q_0\) with
\(\|q-q_0\|\le e\), and \(J=-\langle(z-c_Y)q_0\rangle\). Then
\[
|D_Z-D_Y-J|\le r(2r-b)[e-\log(1-\eta)-\eta]
       +\frac{r^2}{4}[-\log(1-\eta)]^2 .
\]
For \(\eta\le1/4\), this is at most
\[
r(2r-b)e+
\left(\frac{16r^2}{9}-\frac{2rb}{3}\right)\eta^2 .
\]
It pays the nonlinear center square, needs no inverse internal gap,
and gives the matching endpoint remainder for the full weighted time
force. Exact polynomial tests distinguish both signs from a double
reference: the actual remainder is \(1/16384\), below \(13/3600\).
Those are algebra-control values, not theta margins. Source-phase
specializations retain their inherited strip/time restrictions.

**Quantitative obstruction to generic parity monotonicity.**
[Note05](05_AGC_JH_PARITY_OSCILLATION_CONTROL_20261119.md) constructs
a continuous even probability density on \([-2,2]\), with an infinite
Jacobi chain and isolated simple pair \(\pi\pm ia\),
\(a=\operatorname{arcosh}2\). For
\(A_N=h_N(\pi^2+a^2)^N/(2N)!\), on each parity,
\[
\limsup\frac{D_{N+2}-D_N}{A_N}\ge\frac{a(\pi^2+a^2)}{2\sqrt3},
\qquad
\liminf\frac{D_{N+2}-D_N}{A_N}\le-\frac{a(\pi^2+a^2)}{2\sqrt3}.
\]
Both signs occur infinitely often even on a two-degree subsequence.
The proof retains the actual \(h_N\) in the perturbation error and
requires neither a lower bound on it nor an irrational-angle assumption.

states “proved in-note as a time-zero control for the continuous compact
positive-source class.” This control is not theta or a new positive-time
collision. Theta-specific and more flexible contraction estimates
