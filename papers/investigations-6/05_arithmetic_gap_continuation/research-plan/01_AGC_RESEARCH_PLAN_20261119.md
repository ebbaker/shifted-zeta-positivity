## Operating contract

On a research prompt, read the live register, choose the next ready task,
and carry a coherent sequence through decision, derivation, checks,
comparison, revision and integration. Continue to the next authorized
ready task without renewed permission. A routine E completion, a scoped
failed estimate or a helper return is not a stopping condition for the
lead. Resolve further in-scope conceptual choices as B tasks, record them
before execution, then issue their E contracts. Preserve each failed
attempt and the reason for changing approach.

Use Astra/ultra for B choices and explicitly dispatch Sol 6.1/ultra for
bounded E derivations, calculations and numerical checks when useful.
A helper gets a fixed source, domain, observable, input version, permitted
files, evidence standard and finite return condition. Helpers return to
the lead when their work unit ends; the lead continues the research chain.
Do not silently change the physical source or target inside an E packet.
A new in-scope B decision can resolve such a choice without asking the
author again. Only a genuinely new scientific objective, missing external
input or user decision requires a new authorization/clarification.

Prefer analytical estimates and exact derivations. Supporting numerics
must test a named uncertainty and compare complete quantities. A packet
may allow a declared finite set of cells and at most two stated precision
or mesh refinements; larger work needs a new documented B decision, not
an unrecorded scan. Each proof packet permits one complete derivation and
one correction/alternative pass before reporting the remaining obstacle.
These limits bound helpers and local attempts; they do not cap the number
of useful packets completed by the lead in a research turn.

End a research turn after a substantial coherent milestone with the
current evidence integrated and a concrete continuation, an actual
external dependency that blocks further useful authorized work, an author
stop, or a genuine execution limit. Do not use context compaction or a
single bounded-task boundary as the reason to stop. No recurring
scheduler, new user-owned chat or unattended process is implied.
