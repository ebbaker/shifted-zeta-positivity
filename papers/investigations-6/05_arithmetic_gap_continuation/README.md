On a research continuation, proceed through a substantial chain of ready
conceptual decisions, derivations, bounded checks, comparisons and
integration. Completion or failure of one bounded subtask is a handoff
inside the turn, not an automatic end to the research turn. The lead may
resolve subsequent in-scope B decisions and dispatch further fixed E work
without renewed permission. The plan defines dependencies and genuine
stopping conditions. New user chats, unattended work and unrelated
investigations are not part of that permission.

Use **Astra/ultra** for consequential mechanism choices and **Sol 6.1/ultra**
for bounded derivations, calculations, numerical checks and assessments.
State actual metadata rather than infer it from that preference. The
current request authorizes setup and manuscript preparation; it does not
claim that the prospective research estimates have been acquired.

The analytical goal leads; numerics support a specified proof question.
Compare a complete gap-drift, forcing or nonvanishing margin with an equally
informed direct calculation. Smaller intermediate residuals, extra moment
rows and checks inside known coverage do not establish new protection.
