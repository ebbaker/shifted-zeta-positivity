## 2. Independent physical-phase estimate

Put P*={7,11,13,17,19,23,29,31} and C_*(x,u)=sum_(p in P*)R_p(x,u).
For any frozen observation a, the accepted Fourier-mean argument and
exact eight-prime witness give

    mean_u C_*(a,u) > (21/20)*(1-g(a)).                    (3)

By itself (3) has no actual-height consequence. The additional estimate
keeps coefficient variation and pays it across a real phase traversal.

For T=x/2 and w=v+iT with2/5<=v<=3/5, differentiate the explicit alpha:

    alpha'=-1/(2w^2)-1/(w-1)^2+1/(2w),
    alpha''=1/w^3+2/(w-1)^3-1/(2w^2).

The elementary bounds |alpha|<6, |alpha'|<51/(100T),
|alpha''|<1/T^2 imply |L_t''|<3/(5T), where
L_t=L_0+alpha^2/40. The sharper first derivative estimate
|alpha'|<=1/(2T)+3/(2T^2) also gives

    |sigma'|<1/(39x),  |tau'+1/2|<1/(39x).               (4)

Schwarz conjugacy of the fixed carrier gives
log g=Re[L_t(2/5+iT)−L_t(3/5+iT)]. Integrating L_t'' over that horizontal
segment and using the accepted g<3/5 yields

    |g'|<9/(125x)<1/(10x).                               (5)

There is no assumption that g or sigma is constant along the physical
path. At fixed y, beta/a are constant inside each fixed-N chart and
change at the declared seams.

For the eight prime cores, the only possible original indices n=p*2^k
with n<=48 are

    7,14,28; 11,22,44; 13,26; 17,34;
    19,38; 23,46; 29; 31.

m<=2N<=96. Since log n<4, sigma>3/5, and b_2*2^(-sigma)<3/4,

n^(1/5)*b_n*n^(-sigma)<n^(-3/10)<3/5. At most16 terms multiplied by

they are not lower bounds for the physical field.

Use Q_p=(1-g)*p^(-sigma)*M_p, so R_p=||Q_p||_2. Each harmonic is
