## QTT-B01 — Select the observation norm and first quantitative estimate

**Type B; recommended GPT-6 Astra.** The consequential decision is which
rank/accuracy statement can help a signed Newman observation after its
cancellation and normalization costs, rather than merely compress a smooth
source. This choice remains open despite the explicit MPS formulas.

**Readiness and dependencies:** ready and authorized for continuation. Read the
[overview](../research-overview/01_QTT_RESEARCH_OVERVIEW_20261113.md),
[source crosswalk](../notes/02_QTT_SETUP_AND_SOURCE_CROSSWALK_20261113.md),
[reference-use record](../references/README.md), and the opening note's
equations (3)–(16), pilot scope and control table. The setup manifest pins
the inherited inputs. No favorable rank law or signed field bound is assumed.

**Decision and finite work limit:** one conceptual round; compare at most
three QTT formulations from the overview against one common non-tensor
baseline. Select one first analytical estimate and one parameter domain.
The clock remains \(\tau=1/2-\lambda\), and the complete source/readout
remain the target. A restriction of the initial time range or observation
domain must be explicit. B01 itself does not execute a numerical campaign
or manuscript. Parallel workers and ready E successors are authorized by
the continuation instruction above, within their separately frozen scopes.

**Deliverable:** a decision in `brainstorm/notes/`, with an argument for
the selected formulation or a scoped reason to park it. If a tractable
derivation is available, prepare `research-progress/handoffs/QTT-E01-r1.md`
with all of the following fixed before execution:

- Exact source versions, representation, any transformed generator,
  normalization, observation and comparison baseline.
- Height/time/transverse domain; tensor ordering, cutoff/resolution policy;
  error norm and tolerance, including height dependence when used.
- A specific bound or allowance to derive, its proof method and required
  regularity/analytic constants. Name unproved inputs explicitly.
- Source, quadrature, evolution, compression, normalization and arithmetic
  error payments relevant to that consumer.
- Success test, permitted routine refinements, finite work limit and a
  scoped failure/stop condition, with the collision-control premise screen.

**Evidence and completion:** complete when the decision supplies one usable
derivation contract with an interpretable quantitative output, or a justified
scoped failure of the tested formulation. Merely restating the desired
Green sign or asserting low rank is not completion. An unfavorable but
precise comparison is an allowed outcome.

**Routine choices:** notation, a finite derivative order, compatible tensor
gauges and an explicitly justified normalization can be selected inside
the comparison. A new physical source, different heat flow, change of
scientific consumer, or a need for an unfrozen arithmetic theorem triggers
a recorded B01 scope decision; it does not silently enter E01. Record
this round, then begin the declared E follow-through on Sol 6.1 / Ultra
if ready, under the standing continuation authorization.
