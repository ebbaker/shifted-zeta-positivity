# QTT manuscript source passages, 14 November 2026

Prepared for Edward Baker with substantial Codex assistance. Recorded
drafting dispatch gpt-6.1-sol / ultra. Separate backend attestation
unavailable. The [manuscript](../manuscript/main.tex) uses these
passage-level checks.

- NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions,
  [20.2.3](https://dlmf.nist.gov/20.2.E3) and
  [20.7.32](https://dlmf.nist.gov/20.7.E32), reproduce the
  \(\theta_3\) series and the inversion with lattice parameter
  \(-1/\tau\) and the principal square root. At argument zero and
  lattice parameter \(\mathrm i t\) for \(t>0\), these give exactly
  \(\vartheta(t)=t^{-1/2}\vartheta(1/t)\). This reuses the
  [inherited source citation](../../../investigations-5/09_green_potentials_newman_flow/manuscript/sections/02_source.tex).
- NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions,
  [5.11.9](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.11.E9), gives the leading
  \(|\Gamma(a+\mathrm ib)|\) formula as \(|b|\to\infty\) with
  bounded real \(a\). Here \(a=1/4\), \(|b|=x/4\), and the quadratic
  factor in \(\xi\) yields the \(x^{7/4}\exp(-\pi x/8)\) motivator
  at time zero on the real axis. The proof uses the explicit \(S(x)\)
  with its elementary finite-box bound.
- Lindsey, [arXiv:2311.12554v3, §4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.12554v3),
  Lemma 12 and Proposition 13, were rechecked in the primary HTML.
  They concern interpolation cores on a binary grid in \([0,1]\)
  and yield exactness for polynomials up to the interpolation degree.
  The draft cites this as context and proves the midpoint translation
  formula directly. The existing [canonical record](README.md) remains the
  source for the version and earlier PDF-retrieval status.

DLMF was consulted as a linked online reference. No external reading
copy was acquired for this integration.
