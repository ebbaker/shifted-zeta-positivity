# Exact infinite tensor products

13 November 2026, America/New_York. Prepared for **Edward Baker** with
OpenAI Codex LLM assistance. Actual backend model and effort are unavailable
in recorded configurations.

Explore exact infinite networks assembled from factors approaching identity, and seek useful recursions across scale or height. The starting examples are binary phase factors and ratios of successive source averages; other architectures remain open.

This branch owns exact refinement products, their convergence and full-source assembly. Branch 01 owns finite QTT rank/error questions; branches 02–03 organize the source through orthogonal polynomials.

source, inherited identities, mathematical domains, evidence and open
questions. See the [program overview](../research-overview/01_TENSOR_NETWORK_PROGRAM_OVERVIEW_20261113.md)
for the common purpose and relationships among all four branches.

a signed-field consequence remains open.
