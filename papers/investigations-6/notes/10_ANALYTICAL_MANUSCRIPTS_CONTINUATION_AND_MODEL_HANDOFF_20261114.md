## What the development now supplies

with physical factor 1/2, H_0(z)=xi((1-iz)/2)/8 and reversed time
tau=1/2-lambda. Each ends its scientific development with outstanding
challenges. The [cross-review](../reviews/03_TN6_ANALYTICAL_MANUSCRIPT_CROSS_REVIEW_20261114.md)
records the exact assessed sources and review limitations.

- **QTT:** constructive polynomial tensor ranks, heat/phase structure and
  a complete fixed-box absolute-error ledger. Rank at most 641 and scaled
  error below 2^-36 are formal exact-input bounds. Direct integration of
  the same polynomial is the comparison to retain.
- **Jacobi chain:** self-adjoint spectral realization, exact height/heat
  evolution, finite scalar quadrature and its Hermite-Gaussian remainder,
  an explicit sufficient depth, and coefficient uncertainty transfer.
  Scalar accuracy and a state-prefix approximation are distinct. The
  existing N=16 certificate is supporting evidence at its issued scope.
- **Volterra:** moment and basis differentiation, the finite-sequence
  Lax identity, complete-source confinement, and the uniform logarithmic
  coefficient envelope. The general confinement lemma explains exactly
  which assumptions yield the bound; weighted-square collisions can meet
  those assumptions.
- **Infinite products:** compatible refinements and Hilbert-space limits,
  weighted source approximation, exact cell phase and the uniform strip
  estimate. The all-height obstruction is for fixed point-phase cells;
  it does not exclude exact cell integration or other discretizations.

These are analytical approximation, representation and coefficient
results. Manuscript integration and routine supporting derivations do
not establish a new arithmetic separator, a computational speed law, or
a signed lower margin at zeros.
