\begin{abstract}
A specified Loewner evolution generates a curve, and a fixed quantum field
theory assigns Wilson observables to that curve. We investigate the relation
between geometric growth and expectation-value evolution without requiring
a closed-form path integral. For a genuine growing trace in four-dimensional
Yang--Mills theory, completed by a straight return chord, the moving-tip
connection terms cancel. We derive the first two curvature-insertion
hierarchy equations and, for a linear driver, a short-time response with
an explicit smooth-field remainder bound. The expectation statements use
a fixed positive field-flow resolution and stated regularity and moment
assumptions; a closed Makeenko--Migdal reduction remains open. The longer-term
realization question asks whether a boundary observable can reproduce the
exact shifted-zeta evolution
$\partial_\omega V_{\omega,L}=-G_{\omega,L}V_{\omega,L}$ and admit a
positive-norm identity for its cumulative defect. Such contraction along
$L\to\infty$, $\omega\downarrow0$ would imply Weil positivity. Earlier
Gaussian readout and free-localization calculations constrain particular
candidates; they do not exhaust the observables of a fixed interacting
theory. The companion gives the derivations, quantum assumptions and
separate arithmetic context. No matching physical transfer, cumulative
norm identity or all-depth continuation is established.
\end{abstract}
