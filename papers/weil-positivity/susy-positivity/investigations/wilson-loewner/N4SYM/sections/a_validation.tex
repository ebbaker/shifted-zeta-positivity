(byte-identical on repeated runs on a given platform), with records under
\pathfile{N4SYM/numerics/records/}. Every case is a floating diagnostic of an analytic statement; the $SU(2)$
backgrounds are classical test connections, not samples of the quantum measure; no zeta function is evaluated
anywhere.

All 472 cases pass. The programs are not yet registered in a validation replay; registration belongs to the pass
that records a reviewed build. Traps recorded in the research notes include: the implicit linear-driver relation is
ill-conditioned for $s<0.05$ (use the exact series); $\cos\theta-\cos\varphi$ must be written as
$-2\sin\frac{\theta+\varphi}2\sin\frac{\theta-\varphi}2$ near the corner; the classical corner string is not thin;
fillet rounding and flow are separation-based regulators and give $|s|\log|s|$, vertex excision does not; the
Zarembo-stadium control has an identically vanishing integrand; the oscillatory depth integral of
Section~\ref{sec:finite-mass} should be evaluated on a rotated contour, or through its antiderivative.

\subsection{Provenance of literature inputs}

Publication data were taken from the INSPIRE API. The CHMS normalizations and near-BPS formula, the Drukker--Forini
two-loop cusp, the Chernicoff--García--Güijosa equations, the Giombi--Roiban--Tseytlin four-point functions and the
Beccaria--Giombi--Tseytlin and Ferrero--Meneghelli dimensions were read through automated fetch summaries of the
arXiv versions. Each formula used in a derivation was validated before use: the DF cusp by three limits, the CGG
equations by their nonrelativistic limits against an independent derivation, the GRT $\Delta=1$ functions by crossing
in two channels and by the OPE, the GRT $\Delta=2$ functions likewise (and rejected). Equation numbers cited from these
summaries should be confirmed by eye before publication.
