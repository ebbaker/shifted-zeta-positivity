\begin{abstract}
We examine a construction in which Loewner evolution supplies a contour and
a supersymmetric gauge theory supplies an open Wilson observable on it.
The motivation is a positivity program for the Weil quadratic form, but
the results concern the geometric and field-theoretic prerequisites of
that program. We give an exact constant-driver description of the
inherited semicircles and prove their rigidity for one fixed scalar
coupling and bulk supercharge. A smooth transport variation identifies
the insertions produced by a regular inverse Loewner map. Allowing a
tangent-dependent scalar coupling restores contour freedom: a full
chiral D3--D5 projector calculation gives a common complex Poincare
charge, including one charge for all defect directions. Within this
constant-map ansatz, however, the scalar endpoints killed by the same
charge have zero mutual contraction and a charged, vanishing vacuum
pairing. Ordinary time reflection instead gives a nonzero free Gram
kernel, with a different scalar map on the reflected branch. We construct
a smooth reference junction and compute a finite, nonzero direct bulk
response to a polynomial contour deformation. This is one perturbative
sector, not the complete interacting response. The paper supplies the
arithmetic target, relevant background, proofs, controls, and reproducible
diagnostics. Interacting reflection positivity, a protected nonzero
pairing, and the identification with the full Weil form remain open.
\end{abstract}
