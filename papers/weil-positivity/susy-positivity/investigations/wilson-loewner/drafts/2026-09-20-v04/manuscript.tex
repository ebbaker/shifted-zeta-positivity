\begin{abstract}
The shifted completed-zeta quotient determines an exact causal evolution
$\partial_\omega V_{\omega,L}=-G_{\omega,L}V_{\omega,L}$ on a finite
boundary interval. We propose using this equation to prescribe the
effective evolution of a normalized Wilson observable on
Loewner-generated contours. The equation is known arithmetic input;
its realization by fields, contours and endpoint states is the research
hypothesis. The desired physical identity expresses the cumulative
defect $I-V_{\omega,L}^*V_{\omega,L}$ as a positive norm. Contraction
along a sequence with $L\to\infty$ and $\omega\downarrow0$ would then
imply the classical Weil positivity criterion. We explain the
normalization, the required closure of Wilson variation, the distinction
between instantaneous and cumulative positivity, and the separate
gluing problem when the interval grows. A fixed-window test excludes a normalized regular Wilson readout,
while an auxiliary boson--fermion product reproduces the full
prime-free gamma and pole factor. This supplies a concrete
localization question, not a physical derivation. These results,
together with the earlier endpoint and reflection tools, are proved
and qualified in the companion \emph{Supplementary information}. No matching physical transfer,
cumulative norm identity, or all-depth continuation is established here.
\end{abstract}
