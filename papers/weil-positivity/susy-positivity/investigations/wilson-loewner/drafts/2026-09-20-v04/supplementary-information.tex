\begin{abstract}
This companion to \emph{Shifted-zeta evolution and Wilson lines}
collects the detailed tools used to formulate that research program.
It gives the causal arithmetic factorization, the free defect kernel
and auxiliary gamma model, the constant-driver contour dictionary,
the exact smooth Wilson variation, chiral defect projectors, endpoint
selection rules, and ordinary reflected pairing. A smooth junction
and its direct Gaussian bulk response provide a controlled
deformation calculation. We also give the algebraic cumulative-storage
gluing and simultaneous depth--shift criteria used in the exposition.
A fixed-window Wilson readout has exact identity normalization and
causal support but fails the full arithmetic comparison by boundedness
and compactness obstructions. An auxiliary boson--fermion product
reproduces the complete prime-free factor and specifies a localization
mode-counting test. These results do not identify a physical arithmetic
transfer or prove interacting reflection positivity. The four
preserved diagnostic programs contain 299 finite cases.
\end{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{How to read this companion.}
The program, its physical realization hypothesis and the route to RH
are developed in the main exposition~\cite{Exposition}. Sections
S1--S8 retain the technical calculations of the preceding unified
manuscript, with arithmetic conventions repeated for independent
reading. Section~S9 records the inherited continuation algebra;
Sections~S10--S11 give the fixed-window response test and localization
product, and Section~S12 describes the diagnostics.
The two documents have separate
numbering and bibliographies and can be compiled independently.
References to numbered sections below are local unless explicitly
attributed to the exposition.
