\begin{center}
{\LARGE\bfseries Arithmetic ground-state geometry\par}
\vspace{6pt}
{\Large Physical caps, arithmetic sources and finite-dimensional residuals\par}
\vspace{10pt}
{Working draft, September 2026\par}
\end{center}
\begin{abstract}
We investigate positive supersymmetric theories and explicit auxiliary responses as sources of the finite-interval Weil form. Interacting prime models retain Euler dependence, while a genuine shape deformation produces a three-state sector inside a nine-vacuum theory. Compact physical caps and opposite-twist reflection fix the residue normalization and a homogeneous middle-class Euler metric. We derive both metric endpoints, including the contributions of escaping vacua, and prove uniqueness conditional on the stated physical chiral equation. Separately, an explicit gamma preparation is closed on the full logarithmic source domain. Robin gluing, coherent delays and positive prime-return graphs expose the contact terms and residuals that their positive realizations require. A joint response, using an independently proved Neumann high-mode bound, replaces an infinite-rank compact error by an explicit positive finite-rank error while retaining all mixed terms. The complete arithmetic sign at each fixed support length is thereby equivalent to a finite matrix inequality. Its positivity and compatibility under unbounded support enlargement remain open; the positive completion is not a proof of Weil positivity or of the Riemann hypothesis.
\end{abstract}
