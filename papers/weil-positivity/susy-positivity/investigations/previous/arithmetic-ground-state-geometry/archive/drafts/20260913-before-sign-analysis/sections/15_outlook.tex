\section{What the remaining physical-pairing problem now requires}
\label{sec:outlook}
The investigation now has two substantial constructions. The
finite-dimensional one gives genuine physical caps, their
residue normalization, a normalized interacting Euler source and
controlled endpoint geometry. The infinite-dimensional one gives
a closed preparation for the complete arithmetic form plus an
explicit finite-rank positive error. The bridge between them is
still incomplete: the unit quartic cap can label the response
channels isometrically, but the resulting norm identities remain
the same if that label is suppressed.

The exact remaining arithmetic question at fixed support is
\[
G-D_N\ge0
\]
in the finite cosine sector of Section~\ref{sec:joint}.
Both matrices arise from explicit sources and stable responses.
Their positivity separately is proved; their ordering is not.
Further iteration of the already solved high-sector series
cannot determine this ordering. A useful continuation would
bound the mixed response $B^*H^{-1}B$ using the arithmetic
structure of the ordered return expansion, or derive a physical
constraint that identifies the finite difference as a positive
Gram pairing without assuming its sign.

A verified enclosure of the matrix at one length would be a
finite-support result. The larger problem requires control under
support enlargement. The compatible restriction law
\eqref{joint:envelope} works inside one fixed envelope; rebuilding
on a larger envelope changes the finite error. No all-support
limit or cancellation rule for those errors has been proved.
The divergence of unrenormalized all-prime output power remains
an additional constraint on such a law.

On the physical metric side, the outstanding identification is
the exact chiral equation for the harmonic family in the chosen
source frame. Once that identification is established, the
endpoint theorem gives uniqueness and implicitly selects the
remaining homogeneous scale. Computing that scale or the
radial metric could illuminate the geometry, but would not by
itself settle the arithmetic finite-matrix sign. The periodic
gamma sector also still requires the global deck or flavor data
for its multivalued insertion and a calculation of the relevant
physical arithmetic pairing.

The scoped obstructions remain useful tests. Scalar transforms,
finite ambient-holomorphic vacuum enlargement, bounded or
closable finite-range source maps, the specified finite-delay
class and finite Schatten-class source corrections each fail
under their stated assumptions. The joint source avoids the
last obstruction by a compact infinite-rank correction with
singular values asymptotic to $\alpha/(2\sqrt{\log n})$.
Its finite-rank form error must still be retained.
The complete positive Weil norm, an all-support positivity
theorem and a proof of the Riemann hypothesis remain open.

\subsection{Reproducibility and review scope}
Nine small standard-library programs now replay 49 labelled
exact algebra checks. They cover the common-core supercharge
relations, quartic and shape period elimination, residue and
reflection normalization, endpoint matrices, Robin and delay
identities, prime scattering, collective feedback, noncommuting
joint-response blocks and the rational high-mode cutoff.
The programs do not certify analytic domains, Hodge theorems,
asymptotic limits, or the sign of the arithmetic Schur matrix.
Those arguments and hypotheses are stated separately here.

The source package contains a coverage map from each investigation
note to this integrated manuscript, the TeX build record,
the exact numerical records and a replay validator.
The preceding 25-page draft is preserved byte for byte with
its TeX sources and build record under
\texttt{archive/drafts/20260913-pre-integration/}.
The integrated version is also saved with its complete sources under
\texttt{archive/drafts/20260913-integrated/}.
The earlier draft's historical claims and records have not been rewritten to
make them appear to contain the later results.
The package manifest records file identities; it is not a
mathematical proof certificate.

This is an AI-assisted working manuscript. Internal analytical
review and exact algebra replay do not replace independent
specialist review or establish a priority claim.
