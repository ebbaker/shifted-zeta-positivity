# Candidate bulk theories: first investigation

**12 September 2026 — research package, not a proof of full Weil positivity.**

The best candidate found is a **relative auxiliary Hilbert complex with coherent arithmetic transport in its channel derivatives**. Its undeformed boundary states survive cohomology and reproduce the complete gamma *kinetic* pairing, including the contact term, for every support length and every required complex test input. A positive deformation of the infinite tower produces the first-prime delta translations at first order, with a nonzero continuous remainder calculated explicitly. Product composition of two labels removes the leading mixed delta but leaves another explicit remainder. At finite coupling, the simple product also gives incorrect repeated-prime coefficients. This is a concrete candidate and a defined next calculation, not a full arithmetic realization.

No model here realizes the gamma normalization, both poles, and all prime delays as one positive norm. The exact full target is also organized as a difference of two independently positive channel norms; the required comparison between them remains unproved. All conclusions are separated below by status. No positivity of the target, zero data, or spectral square root of the target was used.

**Conditional/speculative:** coupling the relative complex and prime histories so that residuals, normalization, and poles are forced by one positive construction. No such cancellation or arithmetic selection law has been proved.
