# Failed constructions, exact scopes, and controls

12 September 2026. A failure below rejects the stated construction or inference. None rejects all positive bulk realizations of the central Weil form.

| Construction or inference | Calculated failure | Scope and positive control |
|---|---|---|
| Quotient the amplitude by its own factorization differential | $(0,Af)=\mathsf q_{\rm fac}(f,0)$ is exact, so its class is zero even if its norm is positive. | Applies to that differential. The relative complex uses $D_au=(u,u'/a)$, and the class of $(f,0)$ survives. |
| Treat raw heat preparation as a protected norm | Its multiplier contains $(2/a)[a^2/(a^2+\tau^2)]e^{-t(1+\tau^2/a^2)}$. | Finite $t$ changes the pairing. Projected harmonic states have constant full Hermitian pairing for every $t\ge0$. |
| Infer invariance of the positive norm from invariance of cohomology | Changing $a$ changes the explicitly computed gamma pairing; even a fixed cohomology class need not be isometric across metrics. | Same-range auxiliary reparametrizations *do* preserve the quotient metric because the ambient metric stays fixed. |
| Make tangential translation cohomologically exact without defects | If $\partial_x O=[\mathsf q,R]$ and both-insertion Ward identities hold without contact terms, the correlator is constant away from coincidences. It cannot generate the gamma exponential or discrete arithmetic separations. | Auxiliary symmetry may leave $x$ untouched, as in the relative channel model. Defects could alter the Ward identity but must be specified. |
| Closure only modulo a tangential derivative suffices for arbitrary smearing | $\int f\partial_xR=-\int f'R$ generally does not vanish. | Pointwise distributional closure of both ket and adjoint, or harmonic preparation, covers arbitrary $f$. |
| A bounded rotation of one graph range creates the prime at first order | Its variation on $\chi e^{iNx}$ is $O(N^{-1})$; the prime has order-one subsequences. | One channel or fixed finite tower, bounded rotation, fixed injection, tangent at the undeformed model.
