## Assessment

The review's main qualification is valid. Convergent numerical enclosures do not necessarily identify the smallest cutoff defined by a non-strict inequality, because they need not decide exact equality. This did not invalidate the mathematical definition or the RH equivalence. It did require separating that definition from the claim of an effective construction.

The revised integer family uses a search for a certified strict margin, with deterministic rational enclosure routines fixed in advance. At each stage the search enlarges both the candidate set and the precision. The companion proves termination and explains why prime enumeration and scalar enclosure are effective at integer lengths. Different fixed routines may select different cutoffs; every such admissible family has the same equivalence with RH. No claim is made to compute the optional exact minimum, and no arithmetic response matrix has been numerically certified.

The normalization bridge and projected-source clarification are also correct and useful. I checked the proposed refinements algebraically and against the domain and positivity assumptions. The changes below retain the theorem statements about RH and the unresolved arithmetic sign.

## Editorial and bibliographic recommendations

The opening now gives the boundary representation and controlled response equal prominence with the equivalent conjecture. The optional positive-matrix comparison is retained in the companion, allowing the main paper to proceed directly from exact elimination to its enclosures. The main article no longer closes with a submission memo or an unexplained physical chiral-equation reference.

The high operator is consistently described as a form compression, with its domain specified after the boundary theorem; it is not presented as a restriction to an invariant high subspace. Its inverse is called well defined and bounded, rather than already known or evaluated. The finite cosine projection and the gamma form with the lowest mass removed now have explicit definitions. A bibliography entry for the companion supports precise section citations, and the length-one example points directly to its expanded proof.

I verified the review's comparison with [Connes--Consani--Moscovici, Proposition 3.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.22755v1#S3.SS1). The introduction now distinguishes exact response elimination and its certified bounds from the cited finite restrictions. This remains a focused comparison, not a completed originality audit.

The main introduction uses Suzuki's overview for general historical context instead of retaining individually attributed Yoshida and Bombieri claims without direct entries. The original investigation's author line is still absent from the source inspected here; no author name or affiliation has been inferred. Final author metadata and the archival DOI remain for the author's publication preparation.
