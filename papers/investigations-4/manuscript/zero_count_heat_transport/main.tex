\begin{abstract}
For a holomorphic function with nonzero boundary values, the
derivative-weighted energy over the region $|f|<\varepsilon$ is exactly
$4\pi\varepsilon$ times its zero count, provided $\varepsilon$ lies below
the boundary amplitude. The formula includes every multiplicity and is
the energy of the exact differential $2\,dw$ on the normalized cover
$w^2=f$. We give a general power-energy and root-cover formulation,
construct compact target-space regularizations of the divisor, and prove
explicit field-error, boundary-bias and localization estimates. Harmonic
reproduction gives second-order bias; complete disk and unit control
give a weighted-divisor rate $O(\eta^{\min(1,2/m)})$ at an
$m$-fold zero, with matching exponent examples.
For analytic backward heat flow we prove the complete real cap-and-wall
balance with event weight $\lfloor m/2\rfloor$, distinguish it from
conserved complex multiplicity, and identify the complex discriminant
residue $\binom m2$. We then derive finite inverse-square pair-matrix
bounds and the complete forced root dynamics. An elementary concentration
argument for the full theta source proves clock limits on explicit
growing rank windows and fixed-population cumulative exterior cancellation. Finally, weighted pair-law
transfer and exact physical cap formulas specify the additional
small-gap, analytic-unit and phase information that such data require.
The proofs are analytic and finite-domain; a numerical certificate is
used only in a supporting appendix. No assertion of RH or endpoint
simplicity is made.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Acknowledgements, provenance and status}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Acknowledgements, provenance and status}
This manuscript was drafted for Edward Baker with substantial assistance
from OpenAI's Codex, recorded model \texttt{gpt-6-astra}, reasoning effort
\emph{ultra}. LLM assistance was used for derivation, checking, exposition
and document preparation. Separate agents of the same model reviewed the
transport, localized-energy and theta-concentration arguments. These are
internal checks, not independent specialist or human validation.
The divisor-stability and growing-window continuation used the recorded
model \texttt{gpt-6-astra}, reasoning effort \emph{max}; its proof
audit is a same-assistant self-review, not a separate review.

The work consolidates and extends the author's research archive on
physical jet geometry, relative Chern--Hopf charges, reciprocal Hodge
energy and late heat caps~\cite{ResearchArchive}. In particular the real
wall/birth identity and the complex event charge were established in
earlier notes; their proofs are included here. The exact low-value count
is a refinement of the same area formula used for the earlier full-energy
count bound. Classical ingredients are identified in the text, and no
priority claim is made. The manuscript is a working analytic treatment
with the stated hypotheses and limitations. Its supporting numerical
appendix imports an existing certificate rather than reporting a new
computation. Detailed internal reviews and a source-to-result crosswalk
are retained with the manuscript's research records.
