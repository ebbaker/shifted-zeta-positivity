\section{A supporting certified finite disk}
\label{app:numerics}

This appendix illustrates the analytic identity using existing numerical
data; it is not used in any theorem above. The recorded finite-fiber
calculation~\cite{RHErecord} uses the canonical identity
\[
 H_0(z)=\frac18\xi\left(\frac{1+iz}{2}\right)
\]
and outward-rounded complex enclosures. It certifies exactly two zeros
in $\Omega=\{|z-35|<8\}$ and the full boundary margin
\begin{equation}\label{app:margin}
 \min_{|z-35|=8}|H_0(z)|>6.6759\times10^{-7}.
\end{equation}
The count is certified by a complete boundary winding calculation;
the supporting record also isolates two simple real roots. Decimal
approximations to the roots alone are not the certificate.

If these recorded count and boundary-margin enclosures are accepted,
Theorem~\ref{en:power} gives the exact consequence
\begin{equation}\label{app:example}
 W_{<\varepsilon}(H_0;\Omega)=8\pi\varepsilon,
 \qquad0<\varepsilon\le6\times10^{-7}.
\end{equation}
For example the value at $\varepsilon=10^{-7}$ is exactly
$8\pi\times10^{-7}$. This is the localized derivative energy, not the
unweighted reciprocal energy $2\int|H_0|^{-1}\,dA$. The equality
uses the already certified count; it does not independently certify it.

There is also a source-defined positive continuation disk. With the
complete theta source of Section~\ref{th:section}, put
\[
 M_2=\int_0^\infty u^2e^{u^2+8u}\Phi(u)\,du,\qquad
 h=\min\{1,3\times10^{-7}/M_2\}.
\]
The source decay proves that $M_2$ is finite. On $|\tau|\le1$ and
$|z-35|=8$, differentiating the defining integral gives
$|H_\tau(\tau,z)|\le M_2$. Integration along the time segment therefore
gives $|H(\tau,z)-H_0(z)|\le3\times10^{-7}$ for $|\tau|\le h$.
The boundary margin is then greater than $3.6759\times10^{-7}$,
and Rouch\'e's theorem preserves count two. Consequently
\eqref{app:example} holds throughout $|\tau|\le h$ for
$0<\varepsilon\le3\times10^{-7}$. No simplicity of every continued
fiber is required, and no useful numerical size for $h$ is claimed
without evaluating the moment.

No new numerical replay or acquisition is claimed in this manuscript.
The analytic proofs, including the separate large-time theta result,
do not depend on \eqref{app:margin}.
