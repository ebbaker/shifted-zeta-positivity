## Precise import limits

- **dobner-newman:** Its fixed-negative-time results must not be silently treated as the uniform shrinking-time Euler-region estimate proved in this round. Match normalization and parameters before transferring a statement; no simplicity theorem is imported.
- **rodgers-tao-newman:** The conclusion Lambda>=0 does not imply simple zeros at time zero. Root-motion identities require the paper's regularity, completeness and time-domain conditions; a local cluster argument cannot discard exterior forces.
- **cccm-three-integrals:** The imported upper bound optimizes to the 4/3 multiplicity-moment bound in this round's calculation. The conjectural asymptotic is not an RH-only estimate; cumulative upper bounds cannot be subtracted to assert the same local constant.
- **ge-log-derivative:** Theorem 1.1 assumes RH plus ES(2K). That hypothesis counts distinct labels in microscopic tuples, so repeated zeros contribute already; importing the moments as RH-only simplicity information would be circular.
- **bui-heath-brown-simple:** This is an asymptotic proportion, not every-zero simplicity, and is not a consequence supplied by the present heat estimates. No exhaustive up-to-date priority claim is made by citing it.
- **chirre-goncalves-delaat-pair:** N* sums m over repeated labels and therefore equals the sum of m^2 over distinct zeros. The resulting C<=0.3208N is cumulative; subtracting cumulative upper bounds does not localize its constant.
- **lamzouri-simple-critical:** Its unconditional simple-critical-line proportions must not be conflated with the stronger RH-conditional 19/27 comparison. This catalog does not verify the entire proof, formal certificate, or priority narrative.
- **polymath-effective-heat:** The computational bound Lambda<=0.22 is not RH. A theorem for each fixed positive time need not be uniform as time tends to zero; retain all approximation errors and the stated height cutoff.
