- **Earlier nonreal mass:** even negligible inflow leaves N_nr(−ε) to be bounded independently.
- **Larger width:** distinguish an enlarged admissible parameter range from a sharper completed bound. Compare the same endpoint target and source information.
- **Global assembly:** pay omitted labels, overlap, recrossings and any height-graph joins. A finite band or an o(N) error does not exclude every exceptional multiple zero.
