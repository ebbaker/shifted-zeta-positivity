The [control register](../../../../result-summaries/12_collision_controls_and_screening.md) and [premise rule](../../../../avoid/02_shape_only_arguments_and_the_controls.md) govern this logical screen. | Control | What the manuscript does and does not obtain |
| --- | --- |
| Quadratic and cubic heat fields | The contracting double-scale tube contains the quadratic pair. The cubic enters and pays two labels, attaining the sharp multiplicity constants. Dropping relative inflow fails this explicit control. |
| Euler-factor controls | They can satisfy positivity and coarse anchors. Their modified source is not the unchanged unit-coefficient theta source. No new global estimate has been proved that separates their event budget from theta's. |
| Weighted-square and finite-tail controls | They share square ordering, and positive weights preserve the new decreasing-ratio lemma. They fail the complete canonical coefficients and full reflection. The lemma is an estimator improvement, not collision exclusion or an all-real claim for the finite reference. |
| Reflected super-Gaussian control | It shares symmetry, positivity and full reflection, but lacks theta's arithmetic square expansion. Generic chart and current bounds may hold; useful discrimination is not claimed from those bounds. |
| Smooth shape and positive-primitive controls | Strong decay, concavity and primitive endpoint data do not give the unchanged square expansion. The paper does not use those shared properties as a sign premise. |
| Finite-prime orbit controls | Matching finite primes and finitely many measurements does not match the full unchanged source/reflection. No finite selection of such data is asserted sufficient here. |
| BW-01 atomic threshold and exact finite-profile controls | Complete moments and selected profile floors may coexist with their events. They fail the full canonical source and growth structure; their generic finite budgets are not forced below the event cost. |
| RH-conditional close-source endpoint controls | Closeness, completion and reflection can survive while ordinary Dirichlet arithmetic fails. A small error alone pays neither earlier nonreal population nor the complete flux threshold. |
| Reflection rigidity | This identifies the canonical source within its precise square-comb class. Identification alone is not a small quantitative event budget; the manuscript still needs the latter. |

The controlling quotation is: “The ratio ordering also survives positive reweightings of the same square terms; it does not by itself distinguish theta from weighted-square collision controls.” The paper also states: “The arithmetic tail bounds and signed determinant have no proved useful height-uniform size here.” These qualifications are part of the result, not a universal obstruction to better arithmetic estimates.
