## Object and inherited boundary

For a smooth finite cover of a declared spatial domain,
\[
 X_t:\ w^2=H(t,z),\qquad\omega_t=dz/w,\qquad
 \mathcal E_t={i\over2}\int_{X_t}\omega_t\wedge\bar\omega_t
             =2\int_\Omega {|H(t,z)|^{-1}}\,dA_z.
\]
The factor two counts both sheets. A simple branch point is regular in its square-root coordinate. Near an m-fold zero the base integral behaves like \(\int r^{1-m}dr\): finite for m=1, logarithmically divergent for m=2, stronger for m>2. Reciprocal integrability therefore rephrases simplicity for a nonzero holomorphic field. This is classified once; a separately proved source estimate is the research objective.

[BW10 C4][bw10] and [BW13 Note 01, (9), (11)][period] already retain the reciprocal cycle's finite nodal residue. BW13's first regular-period correction is exactly existing event four-jet information. The new candidate consumer is **full energy or dual-period pairing**, not either known cycle calculation. A source-independent recoding of those data does not shrink the admissible set.

## Exact formulas and complete geometry

For compact smooth X, holomorphic ω, and symplectic cycles with a_j·b_j=+1, put A_j=∮a_j ω and B_j=∮b_j ω. The classical relation is
\[
 {i\over2}\int_X\omega\wedge\bar\omega
       =\sum_j\operatorname{Im}(\bar A_jB_j).
\]
See [Riemann Surfaces and Integrable Systems, §3.1.2][bilinear]. On smooth compact y²=J_(2g+1)(x), g≥1, dx/y is holomorphic. The finite physical cover has boundary instead. On a fully cut domain with dF=ω,
\[
 \mathcal E={i\over2}\int_{\partial X_{\rm cut}}F\bar\omega.
\]
Retain paired cut edges, every unpaired outer arc and its orientation; meromorphic extensions need pole/excision payments. No compact finite-genus model of the full entire theta cover is assumed.

For a pair chart H=(z²−a²)U, U holomorphic and nonvanishing, |U|≤M, 0<2|a|<R, the two-sheet annulus yields the inherited screen
\[
 \mathcal E\ge {16\pi\over5M}\log{R\over2|a|}.
\]
It follows from |z²−a²|≤5|z|²/4 on 2|a|≤|z|≤R. An independent upper \(\mathcal E\le B\) would imply
\[
 |a|\ge (R/2)\exp[-5MB/(16\pi)].
\]
Endpoint use needs fixed R>0 and uniform M,B. No such theta upper estimate is available. Capacity supports this same task: mod A(r,R)=log(R/r)/(2π), cap A(r,R)=2π/log(R/r), using Dirichlet energy without 1/2. Topology alone supplies neither upper modulus nor upper energy.
