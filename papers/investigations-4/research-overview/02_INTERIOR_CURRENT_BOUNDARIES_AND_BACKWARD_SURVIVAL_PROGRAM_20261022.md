## 1. Objectives and logical scope

There are two distinct objectives: simplify the topological boundary
integrals inherited from BW10/BW12, and seek endpoint noncollision through
backward survival. Exact boundary removal is useful independently of an
exclusion theorem; the dedicated task and parent mapping below make this
explicit.

The local noncollision objective is a source-derived guarantee that two distinct
scalar-energy current paths survive inside the same real-root gap while
moving backward from a fixed T>0 toward t=0. The desired missing input is
backward survival, not the already proved separation lemma.

Use the complete canonical field
\[
 H(t,x)=\int_0^\infty e^{tu^2}\Phi(u)\cos(xu)\,du,
 \qquad H_t=-H_{xx},\quad p=H_x/H,\quad Q=H_x^2-HH_{xx},
 \quad q=Q/H^2=-p_x.
\]
Ordinary diffusion runs toward decreasing t. In a declared all-real regime,
the [paired-product hypotheses][product] give q=sum_r(x-r)^(-2)>0 in a
simple gap. A real-valued heat solution need not have this sign. For an
RH-conditional simplicity application, RH and the positive-time real-root
regime remain explicit assumptions. No RH proof follows from assuming them.

Let a(t)<b(t) be consecutive simple real roots on 0<t<=T. If two solutions
of w'=2p stay strictly between them for that whole interval, then
\[
 b(t)-a(t)>w_+(t)-w_-(t)\ge w_+(T)-w_-(T)>0.
\]
This excludes a finite endpoint collision of this pair. A theorem covering
all heights and multiplicities requires additional quantified coverage;
ordinary-double arguments alone do not supply it. Survival on [epsilon,T]
with an uncontrolled epsilon-dependent constant is not endpoint survival.

## 2. Keep the three currents distinct

| Observable | Cancelling velocity | Required nonvanishing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scalar density H² | v_0=2H_x/H | H!=0 |
| Jet density H²+H_x² | v_J=2H_x(H+H_xx)/(H²+H_x²) | First jet nonzero |
| Phase of H+iH_x | v_theta=Q_x/Q | Q!=0 and first jet nonzero |

The scalar balance retains the bulk source 2H_x². Only its moving-wall
flux vanishes. Its velocity derivative is -2q<=0, so two surviving paths
separate as time decreases. This is the primary route.

Phase walls H_x=cH can remain smoothly inside a gap while their first jet
vanishes at an ordinary double. They do not generally cancel scalar-energy
flux. Bounded speed, relative interiority, forward trapping and a single
surviving path do not replace the two-path backward-survival hypothesis.
The full complex Hopf boundary and the Weil test-function seam are also
different objects; no sign or cancellation is transferred between them.

## 5. Alternative: a paid interior amplitude estimate

For a phase wall p=c, define
\[
 B_c(t)=e^{-cw_c(t)+c^2t}|H(t,w_c(t))|,
 \qquad (\log B_c)'=q(t,w_c).
\]
A uniform finite upper bound for integral_epsilon^T q, together with a
bounded wall, preserves positive endpoint amplitude. For an ordinary
double q=1/t+O(1); therefore an upper bound
\[
 \int_\varepsilon^Tq\,dt\le\kappa\log(T/\varepsilon)+C,
 \quad\kappa<1,
\]
would exclude that ordinary-double endpoint. This weaker logarithmic budget
has no asserted higher-multiplicity extension. Both budgets are unproved
for the required theta application. The identity is exactly a normalized
amplitude ratio, so its reformulation alone offers no gain.

The simplest source interface is the critical wall c=0. With complete
moments C_j=integral u^j exp(tu²)Phi(u)cos(xu)du and
S_1=integral u exp(tu²)Phi(u)sin(xu)du, one has
\[
 S_1=0,\qquad q=C_2/C_0,\qquad
 q\le k(t)\ \Longleftrightarrow
 \sigma(C_2-k(t)C_0)\le0,\quad\sigma=\operatorname{sign}C_0.
\]
Any new restriction must retain unchanged theta coefficients, complete
reflection, joint moments and all tails. An unproved lower bound for C_0
is not a payment of the denominator. This is a candidate consumer of the
[theta-source program][source-home], not a separate duplicate source effort.

complete bound in the direction required by the chosen observable: for example, a smaller integrated-q upper bound,
a subcritical logarithmic coefficient, a stronger survival/separation bound,
or a strictly contained complete enclosure on the declared class. A proved acquisition
benefit is useful separately. Neither a smaller unsigned error nor a
finite-time numerical trajectory establishes endpoint exclusion. Exclusion
requires the whole source error and continuation/limit argument to close.

boundary term or reduction to paid cap/label data is a valid deliverable.

claim is required. Preserve the unreduced topological contributions.

forward trapping, transfers phase cancellation to scalar flux, drops a
source tail, or calls the regularity of a wall a regularity bound for its
first jet. Preserve [scoped failures][a07], [noncircular hypotheses][a14]
