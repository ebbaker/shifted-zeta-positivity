This matches the real event weight locally, while both regular complex cap fibers still have the same topology. The original differential dz/w and its periods retain amplitude and unit information not contained in pair distances.

For the exact derivative differential H_z dz/w=2dw, [Note 08][n8] sharpens the existing RHE area formula into

\[
2\int_{\Omega\cap\{|H|<\varepsilon\}}

\qquad 0<\varepsilon<\inf_{\partial\Omega}|H|. \tag{1}

This counts all multiplicities with no root separation requirement. These are corollaries of the acquired count and margin, not a new certificate or a gain over the strongest direct method.
