## Concrete analytical result

On the actual root cap, with epsilon_T=r_T/L_T, S_T=L_T^2Y_T and gamma_T=epsilon_T^2/(1+epsilon_T^2),

\[
 I_{T,j}=\frac{\gamma_T}{2\pi}\Delta\arg H_z,
 \qquad I_{T,j}^{\rm ref}=\frac{\gamma_T S_T}{\pi},
 \qquad |I_{T,j}-I_{T,j}^{\rm ref}|
       \le\frac{\gamma_T}{\pi}\min\{S_TD(T),b(T)\}.
\]

All theta modes, tails, source normalization, common phase and physical H_z scaling are retained. This is the new analytical estimate. The exact complete assembly remains

\[
 I_T-I_b+E_T=0,\qquad B_0=q-I_b,\qquad I_T+B_0+E_T=q,
 \qquad q=\binom m2.
\]

The local and tube imaginary-time faces are retained in these formulas. For shrinking Y_T their complete values approach q/2 each. For fixed Y and epsilon_T=r_0/L_T, m root caps approach m r_0^2Y/pi, and the tube imaginary faces together approach its negative. This determines the location and size of the closure payment; it is not a contradiction of the candidate.
