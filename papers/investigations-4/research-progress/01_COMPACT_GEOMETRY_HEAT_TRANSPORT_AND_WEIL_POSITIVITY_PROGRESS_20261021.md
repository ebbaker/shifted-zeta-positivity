flux from the current cancelling real-jet phase. Under the inherited
all-real product sign Q>=0, **two** scalar-current paths surviving inside
the same gap throughout 0<t<=T have separation at least their separation
at T. Their existence would exclude collapse of that pair. One surviving
path does not suffice. In the exact quadratic every noncentral scalar path
