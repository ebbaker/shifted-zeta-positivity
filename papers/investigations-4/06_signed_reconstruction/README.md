and its signed linear-plus-quadratic effect on a two-root discriminant,
with the entire cubic remainder. This home consumes joint source information;
it does not duplicate the historical FTSR ledger.

then [Notes 13][n13], [14][n14] and [15][n15]. The conditional window pays
both root counts and an outer floor without an internal-gap assumption.

common-grid range enclosure with extra moments and full source/acquisition
errors; direct use of those same moments gives the identical improvement.
FTSR-04 is Direct refinement proved; positive-time advantage unestablished;
FTSR-06 is Gated; FTSR-07 is Gated; not achieved.

**Exact missing estimate.** A complete signed finite-T error or acquisition
contract that improves a declared direct endpoint method given the same
information, or a new theta-specific constraint on the response itself.
A positive complete lower endpoint is an additional requirement for exclusion.
At a hypothetical double, the [head–tail cancellation][n11] remains compatible
with the full discriminant being zero.

**First bounded task: I4-R01 — Gated.** Prefer I4-S01’s joint-source task
first. For any genuinely new approximation P(t,y), specify finite T, the
full real-line terminal residual, heat defect, spatial/source tails,
integration-by-parts boundaries, endpoint contour allowance and every
acquisition payment. [Note 16][n16] supplies the accounting template.
Return one full signed interval and a matched direct comparison, or a
scoped failure. No growing-T experiment starts from this setup.

**Dependencies and comparison.** [02][p2] supplies canonical constraints;
[BW12 Note 13][n13] supplies the window. Retain the same F,E,contour,rho
and cubic error for both methods. Distinguish the specified common-grid
range comparator from adaptive or equal-cost methods. Price any extra
moments; distinguish a sharper interval from a measured cost advantage.
A changed shifted reference must pay a new residual/window contract.
For the signed-enclosure milestone, require a strictly higher complete lower
endpoint or strict containment in the matched direct interval and report
both endpoints. Reduced width alone is insufficient. A separately measured
acquisition benefit does not by itself meet that FTSR milestone.

**Relevant controls.** Exact canonical-cell evolution reconstructs the
same endpoint cells; Gaussian Hermite projection reconstructs the endpoint
Taylor polynomial. The covariance method improves both endpoints of the specified
common-uniform-grid exact cell-range enclosure as a direct refinement with
shared moments. No superiority over Hermite or all direct methods follows,
and no time advantage is proved. Generic
heat and arithmetic collision controls retain their separate preservation
lists, and complete mixed terms must remain in every control.

**Stopping condition.** Stop if reconstruction reproduces the same data
and bound without a new estimating benefit, the full-line/defect error is
unpaid, or generic precision is presented as a positive endpoint sign.
No completed candidate is rerun as evidence of time advantage. All-height,
higher-multiplicity and growing-time claims retain their historical gates.
