## Research round 2: theta-specific coupling and the fair comparison

The [paired-source note](../notes/02_PAIRED_THETA_RESIDUAL_AND_MATCHED_DIRECT_COMPARISON_20261022.md)
records four completed analytic outcomes:

1. Full reflection is applied to the complete source before splitting its
   paired transform. The original Laplace law, with its exponential
   moment, is valid on both shifted rays. Shifting the real-ray cutoff
   indicators would be invalid; individual connectors are retained.
2. Atom subtraction gives the sharper canonical cap
   Mhat=2B_k+A(1+6/D)r/(1−r). It is strictly below (4177/1967)A<3A,
   whereas the old coarse cap is 4E_m−A>3A. Relative to the remaining
   source mass its budget tends to two. It also beats the stronger inherited
   strip cap by exactly 6A alpha/(D beta), using the same source data.
   This is a source allowance gain,
   not a complete physical-error or runtime gain.
3. The signed correction uses the same law at every spatial point and in
   both residual copies. Event matching and a reflection sample enter a
   finite dual. At fixed positive moment multiplier the new moment cap
   strictly lowers its upper certificate. The optimized gap and actual
   1/100 physical margin remain unproved.
4. Direct density has exactly the same integrated first and second
   variations. Giving it the same law, constraints, source cap and cubic
   payment yields exactly the same upper certificate. This settles the
   declared residual-linear/quadratic estimating class as a tie.
