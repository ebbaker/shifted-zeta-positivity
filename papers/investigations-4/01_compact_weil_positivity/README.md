fixed-test compact target and exact seam control. [CCM][ccm] supplies finite
realizations, while [round 9][ccm-obstruction] obstructs a bounded endpoint
in the bare archimedean energy norm. Its one-sided inequality is open;
automatic fixed-L enlargement remains suspended. Ground overlap and
Xi determinant-limit identification are not assumed.

**Exact missing estimate.** For every fixed compact test f, justify
Q_0[f]>=E_j[I_jf]-epsilon_j[f], E_j>=0, epsilon_j[f]>=0 and
epsilon_j[f]->0. A uniform positive gap is unnecessary. Positivity cannot
be defined by presupposing Q_0>=0 or by discarding a scalar shift.
