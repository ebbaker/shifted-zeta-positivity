## Why test the arithmetic joint field first

[Ansatz Note26](../01_heat_density_asymptotics/ansatz/notes/26_RETAINED_ARITHMETIC_MOVING_POSITIVE_BAND_20261026.md)
states an explicit finite model F_N, retaining both arms, every mode up to
the fixed chart cutoff, and its completion. Its longer positive band has
normalized allowance (1+log X)^2 X^(-3/4), with derivative scales retained.
The [time-zero J contract](../02_heat_flow_rh_applications/rh_simple_zeros/notes/02_TARGET_BOUNDS_FOR_RH_TO_SIMPLICITY_20261026.md)
uses the sharper microscopic crossover bound C X^(-3/4) for
(h_X,h_X')-(f_X,f_X'). These are different stated domains/allowances.

The useful question is whether the actual arithmetic terms and their
derivatives can cancel simultaneously within that error. A model joint
norm floor c X^(-beta), beta<3/4, would transfer at time zero. That global
polynomial floor is a final-strength quantitative input, stronger than
qualitative simplicity; writing it down is not progress. The research
content must be a genuine lower estimate on the complete signed sum.
Positive diagonal terms alone are not a lower bound for the squared sum;
mixed terms can cancel them. This route does not bypass that difficulty.

The first bounded analytical task should compare a paired-leading-term
joint estimate with the **complete** remaining arithmetic and derivative
allowance on one declared time-height band. Retain carrier derivatives
and fixed normalization. Establish whether correlated terms yield any
improvement over the inherited exterior theorem, before creating another
representation or increasing approximation order.

The comparison baseline matters: [Polymath v2, Theorem1.5](https://arxiv.org/html/1904.12438v2#S1)
already controls high zeros beyond exp(C/t) for 0<t<=1/2. A derivation
recovering only this range is not a new exclusion. Accept a stronger proved
domain, a useful explicit onset/margin/cost improvement, or a precisely
scoped failure of the tested estimate. A finite known-simple sample and
a renamed nonvanishing condition do not satisfy that acceptance test.
This recommendation agrees with the bounded arithmetic test in
[04 Review08](../04_three_regime_heat_flow_dynamics/reviews/08_PROGRESS_ASSESSMENT_AND_NEXT_STEPS_20261027.md).

For RH, the eventual consumer concerns H(T,.) at every possible positive
threshold T and every possible event height. The fixed-positive-time
exterior leaves a finite candidate region for each T, but its size grows
as T approaches zero. A time-zero J result under RH proves conditional
simplicity; it does not itself prove RH. Time-zero charts are a testbed,
not a replacement for the shifted canonical application.

## Why P remains the main alternative

For a complete real population and y=sqrt(t), the existing endpoint
argument gives a multiple-zero signature D>=2/3. A full upper bound below
that value would be decisive in its stated RH-conditional application.
The observable combines all pair terms and therefore offers an integrated
arithmetic question that differs from a pointwise lower bound.

But [03's current summary](../03_heat_flow_collision_bounds/summary/CURRENT_SUMMARY.md)
and [Note07](../03_heat_flow_collision_bounds/notes/07_HIGH_ORDER_ENDPOINT_PROBES_AND_UNIFORM_COUNT_GATE_20261026.md)
are explicit: uniform endpoint detector inequalities, integer counts,
effective constants/onsets and the favorable second moment are not acquired.
The m=16 example has count one and is a finite acquisition comparison.
Conservative degree/width transfer remains costly; the recorded binomial
fallback fails in its stated n>=6 large-height regime. An n+o(n) aggregate
estimate can conceal a single collision. Thus any P continuation should
seek an actual integrated arithmetic gain with absolute error below one
event, while comparing with J on the same available information.

## Why the other priorities are lower or serve a different goal

The [Rodgers–Tao register](../02_heat_flow_rh_applications/rodgers_tao/research-progress/01_RODGERS_TAO_RESEARCH_PROGRESS_20261026.md)
records the known-core pilot's complete contour allowance about309 times
larger than the direct allowance, with source error negligible. This is a
budget comparison on that family, not a runtime or universal-method claim.
The original [Rodgers–Tao v5 abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.05914v5)
also makes the proof orientation explicit: a negative threshold would
force excessive regularity at the actual zeta slice. Moving the clock to
a positive threshold does not import that arithmetic endpoint premise.

[04 Review08](../04_three_regime_heat_flow_dynamics/reviews/08_PROGRESS_ASSESSMENT_AND_NEXT_STEPS_20261027.md)
records E(t_c+u)=kappa_m/u-2 kappa_m gamma+O(u). Its bounded exterior
corrections have a finite total over shrinking dyadic annuli. Refining
them within that tested class does not create the required fixed saving
on every annulus. This is not an exclusion of all energy methods.

[05 Note29](../05_radial_theta_collision_geometry/notes/29_POSITIVE_CARRIER_COMPARISON_AND_QUANTITATIVE_REGULARITY_20261028.md)
converts a direct first-jet margin into feedback regularity; the carrier
does not produce the margin. Note31 stops its fixed zero-residual source
relation variant. [Note36](../05_radial_theta_collision_geometry/notes/36_DIMENSION_TRANSFORM_AND_ZERO_DENSITY_COMPARISON_20261028.md)
separates faithful representation from an independent multiplicity estimate.
These scoped limits justify deprioritizing additional auxiliary structure
until an actual estimate motivates it.

The constructive exception is [04's Dobner summary](../04_three_regime_heat_flow_dynamics/notes/06_CHAT0001_RESEARCH_SUMMARY_20261027.md):
the documented witness envelope improved from exp exp(C_d tau^(-3)) to
exp exp(C_d/sqrt(tau)). The next specified bottleneck is simultaneous
prime-phase recurrence, with seed, tail, wall and coordinate-map payments
retained. A better complete upper envelope would be a concrete theorem,
but still not a lower escape rate or a regime exclusion. The source
framework is [Dobner v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2005.05142v2); the quantitative
envelope is the repository's internally assessed deduction, not a bound
being attributed to Dobner's paper.
