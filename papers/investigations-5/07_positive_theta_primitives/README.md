# Positive theta primitives and indirect sign arguments

31 October 2026, America/New_York. Prepared for **Edward Baker** with substantial LLM assistance. Recorded model **gpt-6-astra**, reasoning effort **xhigh**.

Investigation07 studies the classical Riemann theta primitive and the additional structure that turns positive or integrated kernels into sign and zero theorems. It begins with convexity, Hardy’s modular boundary argument, and the Csordas–Norfolk–Varga integrated-kernel theorem. It is a separate continuation of the primitive-centered discussion in [Investigation05](../05_radial_theta_collision_geometry/README.md).
