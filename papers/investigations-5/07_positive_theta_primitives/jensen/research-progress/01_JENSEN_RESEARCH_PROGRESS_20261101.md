## Evidence and status rules

Ready does not mean proved. A task becomes Active when work starts and
Complete only when its bounded return is saved, including a scoped failure
when that is the outcome. Record the mathematical objective separately:
a failed request or a useful identity does not prove the target inequality.
Finite observations, exact algebra, interval certificates and universal
proofs retain distinct labels.

Each return records date, actual model/effort, request revision and
input versions, evidence paths, corrections, failed cases and the next
declared B/E task. Assessments identify their review relationship; a
model change alone is not independent specialist review. Preserve previous
returns and reread this register before changes.

[control premises][shape], [joint estimates][joint] and

claims and domains before sufficient estimates are promoted.
