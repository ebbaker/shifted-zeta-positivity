The common objects are
\[
D_{r,k}=\det[c_{k+j-i}]_{i,j=0}^{r-1},\quad
c_n=\frac1{(2n)!}\int_0^\infty u^{2n}\Phi(u)\,du,
\quad \gamma_n=n!c_n.
\]
Retain the complete canonical source at physical heat time zero, except in branch05 where the explicitly identified source path is itself the object under assessment. Do not interchange raw moments, ordinary coefficients and exponential coefficients, or confuse the triangular D_{r,0} anchor with all-degree Jensen hyperbolicity at shift zero.

For the flow branch, the author's proposed target is
\[
\int f_s=M_{r,k}=F_{r,k}D_{r,k},\qquad
N_{r,k}(s)=\int f_s^-\longrightarrow0.
\]
Pointwise decay is insufficient. Pure invertible conservative transport preserves negative mass; ordinary heat has limiting negative mass max(-M_{r,k},0). An estimate that assumes M_{r,k}>0, global regularity equivalent to the desired sign, or a sign-dependent inverse coefficient does not supply the missing proof. A new argument must identify its independent quantitative theta input.

Analytical derivations, exact identities, selected primary-source inspection and bounded symbolic checks directly needed for a declared implication fit this evaluation. Before any supporting calculation, state its finite expression and purpose. No unbounded derivative escalation, coefficient scan, precision campaign, PDE simulation or empirical root search is part of this packet. If an indispensable numerical question remains, provide a fixed E contract and complete the branch assessment at that boundary. Continue the other branches and the comparative review.

Every proposed sign mechanism must identify its actual source hypotheses and screen them against the relevant controls in [the control cluster][controls].
