### Decisive checks

**Thermal and fermionic constructions.** The half-trace factor in MC01 accounts for the two nonzero circle modes, and its temperature-dependent filter cannot be treated as a fixed Gibbs observable. The exact single-mode value `−157375/99127` concerns a missing-reflection comparison class, not the complete integral. The Gaussian residual is affine in R and gives h3=1. In MC02, the backward-shift series converges in operator norm and its exterior powers give exactly the arbitrary ordered minors with a strictly positive scaling. The all-minors quantifier, not a rectangle-only replacement, is what permits the inherited PF-infinity/RH bridge. The finite invariant block has a nonzero upper entry; the positive metric condition would force its positive first diagonal entry to vanish. This excludes only the proposed self-adjoint realization.

**The useful Mellin reduction.** In MC03, each Vandermonde supplies three powers of sigma and volume supplies three, giving sigma^9 rather than a vanished rank-one determinant. Divided differences pay collision faces. The exact centered-score identity is algebra at a fixed s, not differentiation of a moving center. With `s=2πa exp(4a)`,
\[
\mathcal C_3(s)=\frac{2}{s(1+4a)}
\left(\frac{I_2}{I_0}-\left(\frac{I_1}{I_0}\right)^2\right).
\]
The separately checked implication `I0≥1/2, I2≤6, s≥4πe^8 ⇒ C3≤κ3` is correct. Its threshold is conditional; the hypotheses are unproved for the full integrals. Exact Gaussian moments validate constants, not theta localization.

**Ward and coefficient evolution.** MC04's undivided product retains Xh and yields `(s+1/2)M′+3M+N=0`. The source-tail ray defeats only a pointwise fixed finite polynomial relation `J=P(T)h`. MC05's highest coefficient in the derivative of `log η_k` is
\[

\]
Compact source perturbations preserve the specified finite prefix and strict source signs but vary that derivative; they need not reverse its sign. They fail literal canonical arithmetic. The oscillator norm is paid by a complete double integral with cosh(uv), not by assuming a formal series is a state.

**Supersymmetry.** MC06 retains the two graded states at every energy and the harmonic subtraction. The degree-fugacity trace is `(1−y)(theta−1)`, so its derivative yields the full excited spectrum even though the protected value is zero. `[Q,O]=Q` produces the nonzero thermal Ward term. A fixed even Q-closed energy-block-preserving insertion cancels the excited pairs and reconstructs zero; it already fails the r=1 match. This is an exact model-specific obstruction, not a claim that all topological methods fail.
