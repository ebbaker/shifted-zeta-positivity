## Problem and normalization

Keep the complete canonical theta source at physical time zero and use
\[
c_n=\frac{1}{(2n)!}\int_0^\infty u^{2n}\Phi(u)\,du,
\qquad D_{r,k}=\det[c_{k+j-i}]_{i,j=0}^{r-1},\qquad c_n=0\ (n<0).
\]
The exponential coefficients are \(\gamma_n=n!c_n\); raw moments are different coefficients. The [Jensen program](../README.md) explains the connection to real-rooted Jensen polynomials and coefficient total positivity, with their full quantifiers.

Desnanot–Jacobi gives
\[
D_{r+1,k}D_{r-1,k}=D_{r,k}^2-D_{r,k-1}D_{r,k+1}.
\]
Given the positive lower-order inputs, a sufficiently strong comparison of neighboring indices raises the order. Restating this identity does not supply the estimate. The triangular anchor \(D_{r,0}=c_0^r\) leaves the first substantive index \(k=1\) to be treated.

For the order-three pilot, let \(q=\prod_{j=1}^3u_j^2\), \(T=\log q\), \(w_s=q^s w_0\), \(d\mu_s=w_s/Z(s)\), and \(h=h_3\). The positive carrier \(w_0\) and signed observable are defined in [Note14](../notes/14_HIGHER_ORDER_SIGNED_AVERAGING_AND_RADIAL_FLOW_FEASIBILITY_20261101.md). Put
\[
A(s)=E_s h,\qquad M(s)=Z(s)A(s),\qquad
M(k)=(2k)!(2k+2)!(2k+4)!D_{3,k}.
\]
The accepted signed-averaging argument extends to real nonnegative tilts and gives \(A(s)>0\). This justifies the logarithm without assuming a noninteger Toeplitz endpoint identity.

## The estimate that is missing

The complete curvature is
\[
(\log M)''(s)=\operatorname{Var}_s(T)
+\frac{\operatorname{Cov}_s(h,(T-E_sT)^2)}{A(s)}
-\left(\frac{\operatorname{Cov}_s(h,T)}{A(s)}\right)^2.
\]
The candidate bound is
\[
(\log M)''(s)\le\kappa_3(s)
:=\sum_{j=0}^{2}\sum_{\nu\ge0}(s+j+\tfrac12+\nu)^{-2},
\qquad s\ge0.
\]
It would imply, for \(k\ge1\),
\[
\frac{M(k-1)M(k+1)}{M(k)^2}\le\frac{2k+5}{2k-1},
\qquad D_{4,k}\ge\frac{3}{k+3}\frac{D_{3,k}^2}{D_{2,k}}>0.
\]
The pointwise curvature bound is stronger than the integrated Gaussian comparison, which is stronger than the exact next-order sign condition. Its failure would not close order induction. A successful order-four pilot would test a quantitative mechanism within known positivity; it would not prove arbitrary-order positivity or RH.

Positive association does not control the covariance with the centered square. The [two-point relaxation](notes/01_INITIAL_BRANCH_EVALUATION_20261101.md#4-exact-failure-of-the-association-only-comparison) demonstrates that association and increasing signed averages alone are insufficient. It is not a theta or Toeplitz counterexample. Gaussian equality checks normalization; it does not establish the theta bound. Keep all three curvature terms together and retain the negative part of \(h\).
