## RGD-E03 r1: exact discriminating question

At exactly **k in {1,4}**, does the sufficient lower allowance
\[
L_{k,2}=q_{k,2}-R_{k,2},\quad
q_{k,2}=\mathscr P_k(1/16,x^{[2]}),\quad
R_{k,2}=\mathcal R_k(x^{[2]},E+B(2))
\]
have a stable positive floating margin? Here
\[
x_j^{[2]}=\frac1{8(2j)!}\int_0^2u^{2j}e^{u-\pi e^{4u}}du,
\quad j=0,\ldots,7,
\]
and E_j and B_j(2) are exactly Note01 (6) and (18).
The sums over all omitted modes n>=2 and the entire spatial exterior
u>2 are paid by these bounds. There are no other source or spatial
cutoffs. The canonical source is unchanged; only the reference moments
are acquired on a compact interval.

This asks whether a specified conservative error allowance can be useful.
It does not test the already known low-order determinant sign as a new
discovery. Do not compute additional indices or a parameter sweep.

### Fixed calculation

1. Construct C_j=b*delta_(j0)+z_(j-1)-z_j with negative indices zero;
   b=1/16 is an exact rational. Construct the three order-three
   determinants and P_k=F_k^2-F_(k-1)*F_(k+1). Collect this polynomial
   exactly before applying any absolute-value payment.
2. Check P1 against Note01 (16), and check both P_k identities against

   exact algebra checking, not an extra theta experiment.
3. Expand P_k(b,x+z)-P_k(b,x) using exact rational algebra. Evaluate its

   degrees 1,...,6. At most 462 monomials including the constant occur
   for k=1, and 3003 for k=4; do not replace the joint remainder by

4. Acquire the same eight moments for both cases, using arbitrary

   [0,1/8,1/4,1/2,1,2]. Use Python mpmath (or the already available
   equivalent implementation of this same quadrature); record actual
   library versions. Evaluate the explicit E and B at the same precision.

5. Report q, R, L and S=q/R for each case, plus normalized margin
