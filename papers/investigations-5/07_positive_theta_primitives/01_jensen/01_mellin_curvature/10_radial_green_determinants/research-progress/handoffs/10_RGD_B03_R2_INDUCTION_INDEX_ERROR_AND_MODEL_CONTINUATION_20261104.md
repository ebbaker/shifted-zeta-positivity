## The research question

Determine how the overall argument could close: which quantitative
hypothesis H_r follows from canonical theta, how it propagates to H_(r+1),
and why the complete signed error remains below a justified determinant
margin as k increases. The first task is an analytical investigation of
this chain. The low-order comparison is a candidate input and source of
diagnostic lessons; it does not dictate a sequence of new certificates.

Use the combined expression Q_(r,k)=D_(r,k)^2-D_(r,k-1)D_(r,k+1).
Its factorization as D_(r+1,k)D_(r-1,k) gives the consumer, not the missing
sign. Specify what quantitative information persists after each step,
without assuming the desired next-order positivity. Track margin decay,
remainder growth and determinant sensitivity together. Reference
positivity must be justified separately if a reference is used.

Cover fixed small k, k near r and unbounded k. If an analytical split at
K(r) is proposed, state its r-dependence, the joining argument and how
the portion below K(r) is handled as r grows. A growing table of isolated
finite checks is not a general argument. Keep the complete canonical
source, b=1/16, negative-index convention and all boundary contributions.
