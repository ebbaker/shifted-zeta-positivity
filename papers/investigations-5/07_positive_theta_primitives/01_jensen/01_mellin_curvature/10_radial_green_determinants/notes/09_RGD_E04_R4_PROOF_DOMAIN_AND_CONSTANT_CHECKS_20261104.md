## 1. Old payment and new tail are different mathematical objects

In Note07 (5), replacing only the homogeneous contribution of
\(z_{k-1}\) by \(t\) leaves diagonal \(t\) and subdiagonal \(-t\).
Consequently the coefficient of \(t^{2r}\) in the already combined
product is exactly one. Constant coefficients cannot enter that degree.

For \(k\ge r+2\), all entries in orders \(r+1,r-1\) have index at least
two. A permutation has \(\sum\ell_i=nk\), so convexity applies before
summing all \(n!\) permutations. This proves the bound by \(n!V_k^n\)
even without assuming a reference determinant sign. Since
\[
\frac{E_{k-1}}{V_k}
=\frac{K}{aa_0}\frac{(2k-2)!}{(aU)^{2k-2}}=A_k\longrightarrow\infty,
\]
the exact old payment dominates the absolute reference margin for each
fixed \(r\). This is a failure of that independent primitive-moment
allowance, not a lower bound on the true signed remainder.

For the replacement coefficient tail, the complete mode sum gives
\(64e^{-3\pi e^{4u}}\). In Note07's tilted-mass split, the low region
has mass at most \(e^{-7j/(4\log j)}\); the high-region mode ratio is at
most \(64e^{-3j/(16e\log j)}\). Thus
\[
\frac{c_j-c_j^{[1]}}{c_j^{[1]}}
\le64e^{-7j/(4\log j)}+64e^{-3j/(16e\log j)}
\le128e^{-j/(16\log j)},\qquad j\ge e^{16}.
\]
The threshold makes the split intervals positive. No spatial exterior
is discarded. The conditional determinant consumer uses only coefficient
indices \([k-r,k+r]\), so it never invokes this estimate at index zero.
Its separate reference signs and condition number remain assumptions;
\(e_++e_-+e_+e_-\le9/16\) includes the full product error.

## 2. Complete boundary and the strict-minor bridge

The full-half-line finite-mode reference is
\(C_N(z)=\tau_N+\int\Phi_N\cosh(u\sqrt z)\,du\), with
\(\tau_N>0\). The Fourier integral tends to zero, so only finitely many
negative real zeros remain; positive coefficients exclude nonnegative
real zeros. For \(s=(\sqrt{|z|}+1)/4\), the gamma upper bound proves
\(\log M(R)=O(\sqrt R\log R)\) and entire order at most one half.
A nonpolynomial entire function of order below one cannot have only
finitely many zeros: its Hadamard exponential factor would be constant.
Thus infinitely many zeros are nonreal.

The exact import in [Katkova v1][katkova] is Theorem C together with
Lemma 3. For the latter, positivity and summability hold because
\(C_N(1)=b\). Its consecutive-column minors beginning at zero are
\(D_{n,0}=c_0^n>0\), so the one-sided starting column is covered too.
If every other contiguous minor were strictly positive, Lemma 3 at each
finite order would imply PF-infinity, contradicting the nonreal zeros.
No negative minor, failing location, or failure along \(k=1\) is located.
Neither the compact reference nor references varying with \(r,k\) are
excluded by this theorem.

## 3. Domain clarification for propagation

Note07 (26) follows from the exact telescoping identity
\[
t_n=nt_1+\sum_{r=1}^{n-1}(n-r)\mathcal Lg_r,
\qquad t_0=0,
\]
where \(D_{0,k}=1\) supplies the zero initial difference. The constant
\(C(a,b,c)\) must bound every intervening order, and every interpolation
interval \([k-1,k+1]\) must lie in its stipulated domain \(x\ge r\).
For simultaneous use through \(n-1\), this requires \(k\ge n\), with
\(k>1\). Then the triangular second-difference kernel has integral one,
and summing \(n-r\) gives precisely \(n(n-1)/2\).

These conditions unpack “if these budgets persist”; they do not repair
an incorrect formula. The benchmark recurrence and the four partial
derivatives in Note07 are exact. No theta initialization, common
interpolation, or preservation of its derivative budgets is established.
In particular the displayed estimate cannot be applied to fixed small
\(k\) while \(n\) grows. Closing derivative propagation still requires
additional regularity beyond the finite package printed there.

## 4. The three contour thresholds pay three different losses

With Note08's \(\delta^2=An^2/(cv)\), its three terms in \(G(n)\) give
\[
\delta\le\delta_0,\qquad
\delta^2\le\frac{3}{4n^2}<1,\qquad
\tau=B(A/c)^{3/2}\frac{n^4}{\sqrt v}\le\frac14.
\]
The middle inequality also gives
\(n(n-1)\delta^2/3\le(n-1)/(4n)\le1/4\).
Gaussian polynomial norms times the determinant-integration factor
\(n!\) yield exactly
\[
Z_n(v)=(2\pi)^{n/2}v^{-n^2/2}\prod_{j=1}^n j!.
\]
Neither the factor \(n!\) nor the variance power is missing.

On the whole complement, one variable outside the local cube already
implies \(\sum\theta_i^2\ge\delta^2\). The single Gaussian exponent
split therefore covers every mixed exterior box without a union factor.
Writing \(A=8+2\log(2/c)\) gives
\[
E=e^{-2n^2},\qquad
\log T=\frac{n^2}{2}\log2-\frac{An^2}{4c}\le-2n^2.
\]
The local phase and sine losses consequently leave a normalized margin
strictly greater than \(61/224>1/4\). The theorem accepts any positive
entire coefficient series satisfying H1/H2 at its saddle; it does not
prove those hypotheses for theta.

For the canonical consumer, \(k\ge k_0\ge\mu(x_0)\) places the unique

index threshold. Local H1 would give the local part of H2 after shrinking
