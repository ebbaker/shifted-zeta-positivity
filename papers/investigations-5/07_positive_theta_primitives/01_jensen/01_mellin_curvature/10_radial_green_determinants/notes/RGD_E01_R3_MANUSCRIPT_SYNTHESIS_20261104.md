## Assessment of the results

The results warrant a coherent methods paper. Their value is in exact
boundary-preserving comparisons, finite paid margins, and quantitative
limits on specific attempts to make those comparisons uniform in dimension.
**This draft records a comparison-methods milestone; it does not add a
canonical positivity range.** It is not an RH proof, a new all-index
induction or a priority claim. Publication readiness and novelty need
further author/specialist and literature assessment.

| Result | What is established | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green representation | Complete inner-boundary terms, positive primitive, exact coefficient/determinant transfer, joint residual payment | The signed probes are not made positive by p>0 |
| Three finite certificates | Q3,1>7e-23, Q3,4>1e-74, Q4,1>3e-31; Q=D(r+1,k)D(r-1,k); last relative allowance <1/15 | No new positivity coverage or all-order induction; no new integration in this task |
| Reciprocal reference | Exact rectangular duality and a positive finite-pole margin that contracts in r at fixed k | Fixed N-pole reference vanishes for r>=N,k>N; chosen triangle allowance fails in specified proportional regimes, not every residue method |
| Gaussian reference | Both C endpoints, p'(0) and primitive integral match; every reference rectangle is positive | Actual target/reference determinant ratio tends to zero on 1<=r<=M k; excludes fixed uniform slack, not shrinking slack or theta positivity |
| Pair-product reference | Exact endpoints and bounded-factor full growth match, positive rectangular reference margins, complete scalar coefficient allowance | Its finite-order sector margin is classical; no new unbounded-r canonical step |
| Diagonal controls | For each q>=26, an algebraic completed product has O(q^-2) complete scalar coefficient defect and a negative (q,q) determinant | Family varies with q; no positive smooth source/primitive, theta arithmetic or actual finite-prefix match is established |
| Complete contour | An explicit conditional Gaussian/exterior margin with order-eight sufficient variance budget | H1/H2 and canonical constants remain unacquired; no joined complementary region |

The pair-product construction removes the earlier Gaussian growth
mismatch. The diagonal control then shows why matching growth and making
a scalar residual small cannot alone carry the determinant sign in a
growing dimension. A stronger dimension-dependent estimate, signed branch
compensation, or additional canonical arithmetic information remains open.
**A negative diagonal minor in the algebraic control family is not a
negative theta determinant.**
