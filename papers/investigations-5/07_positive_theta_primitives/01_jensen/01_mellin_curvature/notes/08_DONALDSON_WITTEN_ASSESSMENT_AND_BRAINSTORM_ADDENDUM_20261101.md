## What the four-dimensional assessment found

The selected theory is Donaldson–Witten theory on the complex projective plane, with SU(2) gauge group and its standard surface observable. Two mathematically different operations must be distinguished.

**Before the full modular integration**, a normalized response of the theory's flux sum recovers the entire ordinary theta series on a specified electromagnetic-coupling line. The established differential reconstruction then gives our complete canonical source, including every integer-square mode. This is a concrete four-dimensional physical interpretation of the source. Its coupling-dependent extraction is not a protected Donaldson invariant.

**For the full protected correlator**, the selected proposal fails. The instanton-moduli degree rule forces its Taylor coefficient of order two to vanish, whereas our normalized theta generating function has a strictly positive coefficient there. Overall normalization and linear source rescaling through zero cannot remove this mismatch. The calculation retains the contact term and the coefficientwise cusp prescription. Other point insertions, bundle classes, manifolds and justified transforms were not assessed.

The short comparison with K3 yields another useful dictionary: its projected Donaldson series exactly realizes the normalized Gaussian comparison function. That is an interpretation of the benchmark, not a theorem comparing it with canonical theta.
