## Dean, Le Doussal, Majumdar and Schehr

David S. Dean, Pierre Le Doussal, Satya N. Majumdar and Grégory Schehr, *Non-interacting fermions at finite temperature in a d-dimensional trap: universal correlations*, arXiv:1609.04366v1, 14 September 2016; Phys. Rev. A **94**, 063622 (2016).

[Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.04366v1), [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1609.04366v1), [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1609.04366v1), [journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.94.063622).

Checked HTML Section IV.1–IV.2, especially equations (55), (59)–(68): occupation-state Slater normalization, canonical partition function, full joint position density, and its heat-kernel determinant with factor 1/(N! Z_N). Lower-order canonical marginals do not inherit a single-kernel determinant automatically. We use this exact finite-N identity, not their large-N ensemble-equivalence or asymptotic claims. Note05 independently derives the ordered-field form by occupation expansion. This was a passage check, not a full paper audit; the publication date is taken from the arXiv record, not the HTML conversion dateline.

## Schönhammer

K. Schönhammer, *Deviations from Wick's theorem in the canonical ensemble*, arXiv:1707.05111v2, 11 August 2020; initial submission 17 July 2017.

[Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.05111v2), [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1707.05111v2), [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1707.05111v2).

Checked HTML Introduction and Sections II–III, particularly equations (1)–(9) and the discussion beginning Section III.3. Fixed particle number constrains occupation correlations; grand-canonical Wick factorization cannot simply be applied to a canonical thermal state. This is consistent with, and does not invalidate, the full-N heat-kernel determinant above. No detailed higher-correlation recurrence or numerical application is imported.
