## PlanatSole260819160v1

Michel Planat, Patrick Solé. *Second-Level Concavity of the Riemann Xi Kernel*. arXiv:2608.19160v1, 19 August 2026. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.19160v1).

**Relevance.** Reports log-concavity of the first differential Laguerre expression of s(t)=Phi(sqrt(t)). Two methods: theta first-term/tail estimates and Jacobi differential dynamics with a cone barrier.

**Inspected.** Theorem 1.1; Sections 2–3; selected Sections 5.1 and 5.5–5.7; Sections 6–8.

**Scope.** Preprint claim, selected arguments inspected; certificates not rerun and full proof not audited. Source-coordinate concavity and double coefficient Turan inequalities, not the spectral first Laguerre inequality or RH.

**Reading copy.** archived; provenance in the receipt manifest. Retrieval date: 1 November 2026.

## LiuMao261004972v1

Yanxin Liu, Jianxi Mao. *Infinite log-concavity of the Taylor coefficients of the Riemann xi-function*. arXiv:2610.04972v1, 4 October 2026. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.04972v1).

**Relevance.** Reports strict infinite log-concavity of ordinary coefficients lambda_n of xi(1/2+sqrt(x)). Uses logarithmic-ratio recurrences, saddle estimates, finite interval verification and closure.

**Inspected.** Introduction; Theorem 1.1; Section 2, equations (2.1)–(2.4); proof architecture and appendix contents.

**Scope.** Preprint claim, not independently verified; code not executed. Ordinary lambda_n must be distinguished from exponential coefficients n! lambda_n. This is not all Jensen hyperbolicity or RH.

**Reading copy.** archived; provenance in the receipt manifest. Retrieval date: 1 November 2026.

## BanerjeeBringmannRolen260914295v2

Koustav Banerjee, Kathrin Bringmann, Larry Rolen. *The Jensen–Pólya program and inequalities for arithmetic sequences*. arXiv:2609.14295v2, 18 September 2026; first submitted 13 September. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14295v2).

**Relevance.** Adjacent framework for transferring asymptotic coefficient information into higher inequalities; partial converses to Hermite–Jensen limits.

**Inspected.** Abstract; Section 1.1 and assumptions C1–C10 in Section 1.2.

**Scope.** Discovery and setup inspection, not a theorem import. Applications discussed concern partition-type sequences; the hypotheses have not been verified for this investigation's primitive moments.

**Reading copy.** archived; provenance in the receipt manifest. Retrieval date: 1 November 2026.

## GriffinEtAl191001227v3

Michael Griffin, Ken Ono, Larry Rolen, Jesse Thorner, Zachary Tripp, Ian Wagner. *Jensen polynomials for the Riemann xi-function*. arXiv:1910.01227v3, 17 December 2020; Advances in Mathematics 397 (2022), 108186. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/1910.01227v3).

**Relevance.** Makes eventual Jensen hyperbolicity effective: n >= c exp(d/2). A Hermite-basis coefficient criterion supplies an explicit sufficient error budget.

**Inspected.** Section 1, Theorems 1.1–1.2 and Corollary 1.3; Section 2, Lemma 2.4 and start of proof.

**Scope.** Version v3 read, journal version not separately compared. Degree-dependent threshold persists; verified-zero implications are one-way, not a new primitive noncollision estimate.

**Reading copy.** archived; provenance in the receipt manifest. Retrieval date: 1 November 2026.

## RibeiroYakubovich230409545v2

Pedro Ribeiro, Semyon Yakubovich. *Certain Extensions of Results of Siegel, Wilton and Hardy*. arXiv:2304.09545v2, 7 May 2023; first submitted 19 April. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.09545v2).

**Relevance.** Extends the theta/Hardy mechanism through Bessel and modified Bessel transformation formulas to specified Hecke-functional-equation Dirichlet series.

**Inspected.** Introduction and Theorem 1.1; Definitions 1.1–1.3; modular relation (1.29).

**Scope.** Selected setup and statements only. Absolute summability, bounded distinct shifts attaining their bounds, and the stated auxiliary-parameter domain matter. Infinitely many critical-line zeros, not all zeros real.

**Reading copy.** archived; provenance in the receipt manifest. Retrieval date: 1 November 2026.

## Ribeiro240102813v1

Pedro Ribeiro. *Estimates for the number of zeros of shifted combinations of completed Dirichlet series*. arXiv:2401.02813v1, 5 January 2024. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.02813v1).

**Relevance.** Quantifies critical-line zero production for a specified class of shifted combinations; the z=0 specialization has a lower bound of order sqrt(T)/log(T).

**Inspected.** Theorem 1.1; discussion of Fekete/Fejer method; Remark 1.2 and equation (1.28).

**Scope.** Selected statements and method description, not a full proof audit. Retain summable real coefficients, bounded shifts, symmetry assumptions and parameter restrictions. Not an improvement to the ordinary zeta critical-zero proportion.

**Reading copy.** archived; provenance in the receipt manifest. Retrieval date: 1 November 2026.

## OSullivan200713582v2

Cormac O’Sullivan. *Zeros of Jensen polynomials and asymptotics for the Riemann xi function*. arXiv:2007.13582v2, 9 December 2020; Research in the Mathematical Sciences 8 (2021), article 46. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2007.13582v2).

**Relevance.** Introduces Hermite-weighted polynomial combinations and detailed xi coefficient asymptotics; useful companion to the effective Jensen result.

**Inspected.** Introduction, equations (1.1)–(1.4) and Theorems 1.1–1.2; section outline.

**Scope.** Selected introductory passages; no new asymptotic bound imported. Its Hermite-weighted P^{d,n} differs from ordinary J^{d,n}. Its citation of an earlier effective threshold should not replace Griffin et al. v3.

**Reading copy.** archived; provenance in the receipt manifest. Retrieval date: 1 November 2026.

## Farmer200807206v2
