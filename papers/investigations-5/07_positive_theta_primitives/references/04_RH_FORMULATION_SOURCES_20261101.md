## CsordasDimitrov2000Conjectures

**Inspection.** Definition 2.2, Theorem 2.4(b), Remark 2.5, Problems 3.2–3.3, and Section 1 total-positivity discussion read in primary PDF text; full proof not audited.

**Relevance.** Original source for source concavity implying double Turan inequalities; distinguishes this sufficient kernel hypothesis from an RH characterization.

**Scope.** Selected source inspection only; preprint claims, where applicable, remain unaudited. No inference that finite-order or tail positivity proves RH.
