## Romik180706130v2

Dan Romik. *The Taylor coefficients of the Jacobi theta constant theta_3*. arXiv:1807.06130v2, 17 October 2018; Ramanujan Journal 52 (2020), 275–290. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/1807.06130v2).

**Inspection.** Introduction: theta convention, modular relation and theta_3(1); Section 6 equation (28) checked in primary arXiv HTML. No full-paper audit.

**Relevance.** Primary attribution for the classical nonlinear third-order theta equation underlying the finite-state reduction. Distinct from Romik's orthogonal-polynomial expansion article.

**Scope.** Selected formulas, not a full reading or a sign theorem for the Fourier transform.

**Reading copy.** archived; [receipt](PDF_RECEIPTS_CLOSURE_20261101.json).

## CsordasVarga1988Moments

George Csordas, Richard S. Varga. *Moment Inequalities and the Riemann Hypothesis*. Constructive Approximation 4 (1988), 175–198. [Primary source](https://www.math.kent.edu/~varga/pub/paper_161.pdf).

**Inspection.** Printed pp. 175, 177–178, 180–181 visually inspected: abstract, heat family, Theorem 2.1, Proposition 2.2 signed double integral, Proposition 2.3 normalized moments, and Theorem 2.4 including all real heat parameters. Section 3 constructive theta proof not audited.

**Relevance.** The stronger log-concavity of Phi(sqrt(x)) and heat-weighted moment inequalities clarify what is already known beyond the 1986 CNV tail-kernel argument.

**Scope.** Full constructive proof not audited; ordinary source-coordinate concavity is not spectral Laguerre positivity.

**Reading copy.** archived; [receipt](PDF_RECEIPTS_CLOSURE_20261101.json).

## CravenCsordas2002Iterated

Thomas Craven, George Csordas. *Iterated Laguerre and Turán Inequalities*. Journal of Inequalities in Pure and Applied Mathematics 3(3) (2002), Article 39; author-hosted manuscript has 18 PDF pages. [Primary source](https://math.hawaii.edu/~tom/mathfiles/iteratedLT.pdf).

**Inspection.** Introduction and Theorem 2.2 in primary PDF text; broader iteration results retained as reading leads.

**Relevance.** Separates the generalized spectral Laguerre hierarchy from iterated differential inequalities and coefficient Turan inequalities.

**Scope.** Theorem 2.2 retains the stated genus/order form. No all-order theta claim imported; full paper not audited.

**Reading copy.** retrieval failed; [receipt](PDF_RECEIPTS_CLOSURE_20261101.json).
