## Lindblad1976Generators

Göran Lindblad. *On the generators of quantum dynamical semigroups*. Communications in Mathematical Physics 48, 119–130, June 1976. [Primary source](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF01608499).

Publisher metadata and abstract inspected; bounded-generator scope identified. Project Euclid web response was a wrapper, not readable article text; original theorem proof not inspected.

Foundational completely positive dynamical semigroup background. Do not import an unbounded or time-dependent infinite-mode theorem from this abstract. Explicit conventions in this packet use the inspected trajectory and divisibility sources.

## CastinDalibardMolmer08054002v1

Yvan Castin, Jean Dalibard, Klaus Mølmer. *A Wave Function approach to dissipative processes*. arXiv:0805.4002v1, 26 May 2008; reproduction of a 1992 manuscript published in AIP Conference Proceedings 275. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/0805.4002v1).

Primary HTML introduction, Section 1 equations (1)-(8), (10)-(11), and Section 2 ensemble-equivalence setup inspected.

Distinguishes reduced density matrices, non-Hermitian no-jump propagation, jumps and averaged master equations. Markovian time-local relaxation form. This is the conference manuscript, not a claim to have read the separate PRL 68, 580–583 article. Infinite-mode/domain extensions require separate work.

## Prosen08011257v3

Tomaž Prosen. *Third quantization: a general method to solve master equations for quadratic open Fermi systems*. arXiv:0801.1257v3, 4 December 2008; New Journal of Physics 10, 043026 (2008). [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/0801.1257v3).

Primary metadata, abstract, introduction and Section 2 equations (1)-(4) inspected. Used v3 metadata date, not the HTML rendering's spurious 2026 title-page date.

Quadratic finite fermion Hamiltonians with linear bath operators; possible efficient operator-space machinery. His equation (1) uses 2L rho L†-{L†L,rho}; convert to this packet's convention with jump operators sqrt(2)L. Full diagonalization proof not audited; finite/time-independent results do not automatically cover the whole v family or infinite Fock space.

## RivasHuelgaPlenio09114270v2

Ángel Rivas, Susana F. Huelga, Martin B. Plenio. *Entanglement and non-Markovianity of quantum evolutions*. arXiv:0911.4270v2, 28 July 2010; Physical Review Letters 105, 050403 (2010). [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/0911.4270v2).

Primary introduction through equations (1)-(2) and surrounding time-dependent generator/CP-propagator discussion inspected.

Separates an endpoint CPTP map, a homogeneous semigroup and time-inhomogeneous CP-divisible propagation. Use explicit CP-divisibility terminology, not a universal definition of every type of quantum Markovianity. No theta criterion, infinite-domain theorem or full non-Markovianity measure audit imported.
