## FominZelevinsky9912128v1

Sergey Fomin, Andrei Zelevinsky. *Total positivity: tests and parametrizations*. arXiv:math/9912128v1, 15 December 1999. [Primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/math/9912128v1).

Primary HTML, Planar networks: definitions, Lemma 1 and proof, Corollary 2 inspected. Noncrossing positive path mechanism for minors; directed acyclic planar network, compatible ordered boundaries, nonnegative edge weights. No construction of the theta network; infinite limits need convergence. No full-paper audit.

## Chin240701618v1

Siu A. Chin. *Simple proof that there is no sign problem in Path Integral Monte Carlo simulations of fermions in one dimension*. arXiv:2407.01618v1, 28 June 2024; Phys. Rev. E 109, 065312 (25 June 2024). [Primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.01618v1).

Primary HTML abstract, introduction, Section II equations (1)-(2), selected Section IV-V determinant passages inspected. Physics background on one-dimensional fermionic propagator signs and closed-loop PIMC weights. Gaussian kinetic propagator/closed-loop context does not establish theta's long-jump ordered amplitudes. General-N proof not fully audited.

## Robinson180309329v1

P. L. Robinson. *Julia operators and Halmos dilations*. arXiv:1803.09329v1, 25 March 2018. [Primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/1803.09329v1).

Primary HTML Theorems 0-2 and displayed proofs inspected, especially Theorem 2 block dilation. Every bounded contraction admits a unitary block dilation; supplies enlarged-system quantum realization of each fixed transfer. Compression is not similarity or a local Hamiltonian construction. Does not produce a common v evolution or signs of minors.
