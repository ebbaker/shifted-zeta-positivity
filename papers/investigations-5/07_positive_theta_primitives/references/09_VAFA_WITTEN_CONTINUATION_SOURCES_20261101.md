Atish Dabholkar, Pavel Putrov and Edward Witten, *Duality and Mock Modularity*, SciPost Physics 9, 072 (2020), DOI 10.21468/SciPostPhys.9.5.072. [arXiv:2004.14387v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.14387v3), 20 October 2020.

Inspected: introduction, printed pp.2–5; Section 3.1, pp.14–15, equations (3.1), (3.3)–(3.6); Section 3.3, especially pp.19–20; Section 4.4, p.33, equation (4.38). These fix the completed SO(3) partition function, eta-three normalization, anomaly, vector-valued modular behavior and physical boundary interpretation. The explicit completion fixes the sign and absolute normalization used in our return; the physical derivations leave an orientation sign unresolved, and the 4D calculation does not determine its measure constant. The 2D trace fixes the magnitude. This is not an imported constructive-QFT theorem. HTML equation numbers differ from the PDF; citations here use the PDF.

Publisher PDF access returned an access-denied page; the public arXiv copy was used without bypassing access controls.
