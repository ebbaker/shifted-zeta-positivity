## AlexandrovZabrodin2013

Alexander Alexandrov and Anton Zabrodin, *Free fermions and tau-functions*, J. Geom. Phys. 67 (2013), 37–80, DOI 10.1016/j.geomphys.2013.01.007. Read [arXiv:1212.6049v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1212.6049v3), revised 24 September 2013. Inspected the ordinary Wick determinant in Section 2.3, the generalized theorem's group-like and nonzero-denominator hypotheses in Section 2.7, Schur-state expansions in Section 2.10, Section 3.4.1 and Appendix A's Jacobi–Trudi convention (A3). Note04's finite empty-vacuum CAR construction is derived directly; it does not import a convergence theorem for an infinite current operator or a theta-specific physical Hamiltonian. The Schur specialization is formal and carries no positivity assertion.

## Borodin1998

Alexei Borodin, *Biorthogonal ensembles*, [arXiv:math/9804027v1](https://arxiv.org/html/math/9804027v1), 6 April 1998. Inspected the introductory comparison with ordinary orthogonal ensembles and Section 2's general two-determinant density and pairing setup beginning at (2.8). The later special-ensemble scaling limits were not imported. The relevant premises are a genuine nonnegative integrable density and the needed nondegenerate pairing/biorthogonal construction. Note04 checks invertibility of its carrier pairing by a positive ordered-chamber integral. A signed target integral alone does not meet the probability-density premise.

## DesrosiersForrester2006

Patrick Desrosiers and Peter J. Forrester, *A note on biorthogonal ensembles*, [arXiv:math-ph/0608052v1](https://arxiv.org/html/math-ph/0608052v1), 23 August 2006. Inspected the Introduction, (1), (3)–(5), for a two-determinant density, invertible pairing matrix and determinantal correlation kernel; Proposition 2 was inspected only as related context. Note04 fixes the inverse-transpose convention from its own pairing and checks integral K(x,x)=r. The displayed v1 marginal definition (2) appears to contain an extra 1/Z after the density was already normalized in (1); the standard normalized-marginal convention is stated explicitly in Note04. No characteristic-polynomial theorem, asymptotic limit or chiral matrix model is imported.

## Witten1988TQFT

Edward Witten, *Topological Quantum Field Theory*, Communications in Mathematical Physics 117 (1988), 353–386, DOI 10.1007/BF01223371. Read the [IAS-hosted journal PDF](https://www.ias.edu/sites/default/files/sns/%5B109%5DCommMathPhys117-1988.pdf). Inspected the opening description of the twisted gauge theory, Section 3 around (3.8)–(3.10), its measure qualification, and selected Section 5 discussion of Donaldson observables and differential forms on instanton moduli space. These passages explain what protected metric independence requires; no constructive-QFT theorem or correspondence to the theta determinants is imported. Smooth-structure dependence and analytical/moduli-space qualifications are retained.
