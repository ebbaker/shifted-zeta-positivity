## Chin, Rivas, Huelga and Plenio

Alex W. Chin, Ángel Rivas, Susana F. Huelga and Martin B. Plenio,
*Exact mapping between system-reservoir quantum models and semi-infinite
discrete chains using orthogonal polynomials*, Journal of Mathematical
Physics 51, 092109 (2010), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3490188),
[arXiv:1006.4507v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1006.4507v2), 30 September 2010.

Inspected PDF §2.1, Theorem 2.3 and equations (2)–(3), and §3, Theorem 3.1
and its recurrence-coefficient construction, on 13 November 2026. This is
the physics precedent for the brainstorm's chain mapping. The full-line
theta application is derived in the note; bounded-support tail results
are not imported for its unbounded source.

## Van Assche

Walter Van Assche, *Orthogonal polynomials, Toda lattices and Painlevé
equations*, Physica D 434, 133214 (2022),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physd.2022.133214),
[arXiv:2202.11017v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.11017v1), 22 February 2022.

Inspected §1.1 equations (1.1)–(1.4), §1.2 equations (1.5)–(1.7), and
§2.3, Theorem 2.4 and its symmetric quadratic-weight specialization,
printed page 10, on 13 November 2026. Used for recurrence conventions,
the Volterra/Langmuir identification and a possible discrete-height
direction. Theta-specific coefficient inequalities and asymptotics are
not supplied by this survey's derivation.

## Verstraete and Cirac

Frank Verstraete and J. Ignacio Cirac, *Continuous Matrix Product States
for Quantum Fields*, Physical Review Letters 104, 190405 (2010),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.104.190405),
[arXiv:1002.1824v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1002.1824v1), 9 February 2010.

Inspected the unnumbered cMPS definition and ordered-particle expansion
on PDF page 1, and the transfer-generator formulas on page 2, on
13 November 2026. A continuous physical coordinate in this construction
differs from a continuous external height parameter. No exact finite-bond
theta representation follows from the definition alone.
