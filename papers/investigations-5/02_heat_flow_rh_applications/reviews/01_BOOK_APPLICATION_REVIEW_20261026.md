## Findings that govern an application

1. **The normalization is compatible with Rodgers–Tao and Polymath.**
   The book uses \(H_t=-H_{zz}\), \(H(0,z)=\xi((1+iz)/2)/8\).
   The standard zeta height is \(x/2\); the local heat-coordinate spacing
   is approximately \(4\pi/\log(x/(4\pi))\). Keeping this factor is
   essential for gap energies and initial verification heights.

2. **The positive hierarchy is strong in a regime already known to be
   real-rooted.** Its \(t\ge32\) analytic range and eventual onsets do
   not cover a Newman-bound candidate below 0.2. The compact arithmetic
   term cannot be dropped merely by increasing saddle order. A smaller
   remainder near time 64 is a genuine approximation gain, but needs a
   separate small-time consumer to improve a Newman bound.

3. **The negative hierarchy and signed crossover have complementary
   scopes.** The exponential-in-\(L^2\) error stops at \(T=1/L\), while
   the weaker crossover reaches zero. This suggests a worthwhile
   microscopic-scale comparison; it does not authorize propagating the
   stronger rate through zero. Outer/gamma norms and frozen chart data
   must remain explicit.

4. **The physical construction is not a zero-location theorem.** Its
   regulator and averaging widths are prescribed. Multiple zeros are
   allowed in the central transfer, and the joined field is
   nonholomorphic in transition bands. Consequently neither arbitrary
   absolute physical accuracy nor its algebraic density supplies
   pointwise simplicity or a holomorphic contour certificate there.
   The Book itself preserves these qualifications.

5. **The clock has a genuine energy interface, with an unpaid evolution
   issue.** Vandermonde and pair-Laplacian comparisons are relevant to
   interaction energies. A finite root factorization also carries the
   exterior unit drift \(2U_z/U\). An application must bound that drift,
   its variation, the smallest gaps, and localization boundaries, rather
   than evolve the clock block as a closed particle system.

6. **One stated open task has since closed in a source supplement.**
   Ansatz Note 23 and Assessment 17 complete the fixed-P4 physical tuple
   on \([63,65]\), with their displayed output-specific rates. The
   Book's earlier open-task wording is a production-baseline statement.
   The brainstorm records the new result separately, retaining its
   same-model status and uninstantiated onset. This review does not edit
   the parent manuscripts or reopen their task registers.

7. **Effectivity and useful cost are distinct.** Polynomial extra-accuracy
   cost at a fixed descriptor/query box does not pay the growth of that
   descriptor, height, inverse regulator scales, or the number of modes
   needed by an RH consumer. A certificate comparison must count those
   costs explicitly.
