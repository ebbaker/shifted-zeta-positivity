## Common normalization and the physical derivative

Throughout, the canonical field satisfies

\[
 H_t=-H_{zz},\qquad H(0,z)=\xi((1+iz)/2)/8.
\]

For a chart centered at X, use

\[
 L=\log(X/(4\pi)),\quad N=\lfloor e^{L/2}\rfloor,\quad
 z=X+u/L,\quad A=E_o(t,X)>0,
\]
\[
 V_H=(H/A,H_z/(LA)),\qquad V_F=(F_N/A,F_{N,z}/(LA)).
\]

The physical derivative freezes X,L,N,A and t. The complete finite model
retains both arithmetic arms for every n<=N and the Riemann--Siegel
completion. Carrier, weight and phase derivatives are all included.
No logarithmic derivative of an oscillating field replaces H_z; no
floor, comparison allowance or algebraic norm map is differentiated.

The useful common map is

\[
 \mathsf T(v_0,v_1)=(v_0,(\alpha v_0-v_1)/\beta_c),
 \quad\alpha=E_z/(LE),\quad\beta_c=\Theta_z/L.
\]

It is invertible after elementary carrier gates, with ||mathsf T||<6.
It converts the n=1 contribution into a circle of radius q=E(t,z)/A.
Thus value and physical derivative complement one another near a zero
of the leading cosine. At chart centers q=1.

Read the [result index][results], particularly [heat flow11][cluster11]
and [controls12][controls], and the [avoid index][avoid], particularly
[target classification][a01], [scope][a07] and [signed payments][a12].
The common algebra supplies no missing arithmetic sign by itself.

## Exterior calibration and its limits

[Note10][n10] first identifies a loss in the ordinary phase-insensitive
Euclidean triangle: the leading ellipse's worst floor tends to 1/4,
whereas even n=2,3,4 have limiting separate allowances exceeding 25/96
at tL=8. This fails that majorant, not every phase-aware Euclidean test.

The map mathsf T preserves the leading pair's joint information. On
6<=tL<=8, splitting the complete n>=2 population at log n=L/12 gives
weighted arithmetic payment <=15/16. Including the actual completion,
the separate value/derivative approximation errors and the norm
condition yields

\[
 \|V_H\|_2\ge1/384\qquad(|u|\le1,\ 6\le tL\le8)
\]

for every sufficiently large X. No RH premise is used. This calibrates
classical exterior behavior; it does not improve the inherited outer
domain tL>=61/24 or supply an evaluated starting height or matched
cost/margin advantage.

At tL=4 a fixed block already defeats the weighted absolute-sum test:
its limiting payment is at least

\[
 \sum_{n=2}^{31}n^{-3/2}\ge\int_2^{32}x^{-3/2}\,dx
 =3/(2\sqrt2)>1.
\]

That stopped comparison cannot be repaired by smaller source errors.
It is not evidence of an actual simultaneous zero.

## The signed exponent pair budget at tL equal to two

[Note12][n12] applies the inherited five-pair maximal-interval estimate
and exact Abel summation to the actual quadratic heat weights. This
keeps cancellation within each block and covers the physical derivative,
all complementary blocks and the shortened terminal interval.

For beta=log M/L the limiting weighted block exponent is

\[
 F_{k,l}(\beta;2)=k+(l-k-1)\beta+\beta^2/2.
\]

At beta=2/5, pair D is best among the five inherited pairs, with

\[
 \min F(2/5;2)=321/9950>1/50.
\]

An actual frozen dyadic block lies O(1/L) from that point. Its allocated
high error grows with power 321/9950+eta, eta>0 fixed, while even the
absolute upper bound for the entire exactly retained low jet is
11q exp(L/50). The power gap is **61/4975**, so the complete selected
lower-certificate expression is eventually negative, even before source
and completion errors.

This is growth of an **allocated allowance**, not the signed arithmetic
remainder. Altering its implied constant cannot remove that power gap.
Removing the positive gap needs a saving of at least 61/4975+eta;
at equality constants matter. Making the offending weighted allowance
decay requires a saving exceeding 321/9950+eta in that framework.

The inherited heat-to-zeta shortcut also loses its original payments:
its low unsigned replacement debit grows as exp(L/50), the effective
real part is approximately one rather than >=9/8, and neither its
ordinary absolute Dirichlet tail nor the fixed-half-plane logarithmic
derivative bounds apply. No replacement or favorable phase bound was
assumed. These failures are applications of the inherited outer package's
limitation, not a universal obstruction to signed arithmetic methods.

## Global physical correlations and the absolute lag loss

[Note13][n13] removes independent dyadic error blocks. For the exact
transformed remainder R=sum_{n=2}^N B_n, M=N-1, define

\[
 D=\sum_{n=2}^N\|B_n\|^2,\qquad
 C_h=\sum_{n=2}^{N-h}B_{n+h}\mathbin{\cdot}B_n.
\]

Each signed C_h contains every pair at that lag, including pairs from
different former blocks. Its physical dot product includes both
log(m/n) difference phases and log(mn) sum phases, with their derivative
weights. A scalar |U_0|^2 estimate would not suffice.

Finite vector Cauchy--Schwarz gives, for every integer K>=1,

\[
 \|R\|^2\le A_K:=\frac{M+K-1}{K}
 \left[D+2\sum_{h=1}^{K-1}(1-h/K)C_h\right].
\]

At every large real center D -> zeta(2)-1. On an unbounded canonical
recurrent sequence the actual correlations satisfy, for each fixed h,

\[
 C_h\longrightarrow\frac1h\sum_{n=2}^{h+1}\frac1n,
 \qquad C_1\longrightarrow1/2.
\]

Replacing C_h by b_h>=|C_h| then forces allocated remainder radius
>=sqrt(17)/4>1 for **every** shift length on that sequence. For the
usual K<=M range, the diagonal alone already loses at all sufficiently
large centers. Neither statement proves a large actual remainder or a
small full jet. They stop the comparison using absolute lag allowances.

## The fully signed estimate and the actual remainder

[Note14][n14] retains all lag signs. The exact sliding-vector variance
shows A_K>=||R||^2, with A_K -> ||R||^2 as K tends to infinity. Hence

\[
 \inf_{K\ge1}A_K=\|R\|^2.
\]

Perfect signed acquisition can remove the earlier allocation loss, but
cannot beat the actual remainder. That actual quantity is estimated by

\[
 Q(T)=\left(\sum_{j=0}^4 2^{-ijT}\right)
       \left(\sum_{k=0}^4 3^{-ikT}\right),\qquad
 P(T)=|Q(T)|^2/25\ge0.
\]

Along center charts x with t=2/log(x/(4pi)), put
Z=e^(-iTheta)(R_0+iR_1). Both exact physical branches in Z are retained.
The complete signed estimate is

\[
 \langle PZ\rangle_{[X,2X]}
 =\frac{12721}{10800}+O_*(1/L_X+X^{-7/8}),\qquad
 \langle P\rangle_{[X,2X]}=1+O_*(X^{-1}).
\]

The mean constant factors exactly as
(129/80)(547/405)-1, exceeding 7/6 by **121/10800**.
Every arithmetic index through the moving cutoff, each entry endpoint
x=4pi n^2, and the whole mirror branch are included. The mirror branch
costs O_*(X^(-7/8)/L_X); the auxiliary center-family derivative used in integration by parts is distinguished
from the fixed-time physical derivative already present in the jet.

Since P is nonnegative, every sufficiently large dyadic interval
contains a center with **Re Z>7/6**, hence actual ||R||>7/6. The
average proves existence of a point defeating a uniform comparison;
it is not used to rule out exceptional collisions.

At these points every perfectly signed Fejer allowance exceeds 7/6,
and the raw leading-pair triangle margin is

\[
 1-\sqrt{A_K}-c_R-c_H<-1/6-c_R-c_H<0
\]

for every K. The same obstruction applies to any valid upper norm
bound for that entire remainder in the declared transformed norm.
This is stronger than Notes12--13: the actual remainder is too large
for that main/error split, even with perfect signed information.

## Complete errors and the auxiliary positive margin

The complete-field comparison remains the inherited CS6 theorem. Its
positive crossover estimate pays the normalized complete-field
source errors separately:

\[
 |\Delta V_0|\le qC_0X^{-3/4},\qquad
 |\Delta V_1|\le qC_1\frac{1+L}{L}X^{-3/4}.
\]

The transformed source cost is c_H=O(X^(-3/4)). The actual completion
and its physical derivative give
c_R=O(X^(-1/4)exp(-t lambda^2/16)). Thus c_R=O(X^(-5/8)) on the
6<=tL<=8 calibration, improves to O(X^(-3/4)) at tL=8, and is
O(X^(-3/8)) at tL=2. These payments apply to the whole field once,
not once per mode, block, lag or resonator coefficient.

No heat evolution is used; the model's nonzero residual is never used
as a substitute for its field error. All source and carrier constants
and starting-height gates remain finite and uninstantiated.

The Note14 witnesses are **constructive**: the remainder's projection
along the leading pair is positive. Once c_R+c_H<=1/24, the transformed exact-field
remainder ||mathsf T V_H-B_1|| exceeds 9/8, while the rotated full exact-jet projection
exceeds 17/8. With ||mathsf T||<6 this gives

\[
 \|V_H\|_2>17/48
\]

at the same unspecified center in every sufficiently large dyadic
interval. This is an auxiliary existential estimate, not a uniform
bound on |u|<=1, an evaluated witness, a finite certificate, or new
coverage for the RH/simplicity applications.

## What remains open

**HCB-05 stays Open.** The acquired obstruction stops uniform domination
of the whole remainder by the n=1 pair in the declared norm at tL=2.
It does not stop other norms, larger justified main blocks, restricted
regions, finite charts or a complete signed-square estimate retaining
the leading/remainder mixed term.

a complete jet floor cX^(-beta), beta<3/4, on every high chart with the
bounded remainder covered. The existing transfer would then imply
simple zeros under RH. The positive-threshold argument separately needs every possible event time
and height, including its bounded remainder. Neither application follows
from the existential good points above. The inherited tL>=61/24 exterior
comparator is unchanged; no matched effective-onset or cost improvement
was acquired.
