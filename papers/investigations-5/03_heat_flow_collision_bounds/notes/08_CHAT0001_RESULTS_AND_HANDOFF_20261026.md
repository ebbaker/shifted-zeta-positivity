## 4. Note06: endpoint transport, count tools and complete wall

[Note06][n06] and [Review04][r04] establish the following generic tools.
For F=h_X(0,·), F(c)≠0, real A_c=F'/F(c), and
\(Q_c=-(\log F)''(c)=\sum_r(c-r)^{-2}\ge0\),
\[
 \left|h_X(\tau,c)/F(c)-e^{-\tau A_c^2}\right|
 \le(1-2\tau Q_c)^{-1/2}-1,\qquad2\tau Q_c<1.
\]

small transport error.
