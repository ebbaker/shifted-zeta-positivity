## 2. Source allowance and the first unpaid input

explicit envelope ratio
\[
 B_X=\sup_{[0,\tau_X]\times\mathcal B}
       \mathcal E_o(\tau/L^2,X+u/L)/A_X.

Its elementary arm formulas give \(B_X=O(1)\) on this family: the time
amplitude exponent is \(O(\tau_X)\), transverse phase growth is
\(O(|\Im u|)\), and the spatial amplitude change is \(O(1/L)\).
For a finite certificate one must bound this explicit supremum and
instantiate the theorem's constants and onset, rather than guess them.

\[
 e_{\rm src}=C_0 B_X X^{-3/4},\qquad
 \|h_X-f_X\|_\infty\le e_{\rm src}.
 \tag{3}

inherited constants. These are approximation bounds, not lower bounds.

## 3. Existing wall-to-coefficient conversion, with all payments

Work first with exact \(f_X\), and put
\(\eta=e_{\rm src}/m_X<1\), \(\nu=-\log(1-\eta)\),
\(\kappa=24/(2\pi R)=12/(\pi\sqrt{20})\).
The following is [HDC Note08 §3][hdc08], also acquired in the finite
canonical box by [HDC Note13 §4][hdc13]. It needs only a logarithm on
a collar of the wall, not a nonvanishing ratio throughout its interior.
On the wall \(\ell=\operatorname{Log}(h_X/f_X)\) is single valued and
\(|\ell|\le\nu\). Integration by parts gives the power-sum differences
\[
 \Delta s_k=-\frac{k}{2\pi i}\oint_\Gamma u^{k-1}\ell(u)\,du,
 \qquad |\Delta(s_k/R^k)|\le k\kappa\nu. \tag{5}
\]
Thus no source derivative or internal root slope is needed for this
same-time comparison.

Let the exact prepared model polynomial at \(\tau_X\) be
\(Q_F(u)=u^n+\sum_{j=1}^n b_jR^j u^{n-j}\), with \(b_0=1\).
Every model root lies within \(|u|\le R\), hence
\(|b_j|\le\binom nj\), even if its roots are nonreal or repeated.
For \(a=\kappa\nu\), define
\[
 h_0(a)=0,\qquad
 h_l(a)=[w^l]\{\exp(a\textstyle\sum_{k=1}^n w^k)-1\}
 =\sum_{q=1}^l\binom{l-1}{q-1}\frac{a^q}{q!},\quad 1\le l\le n,
\]
\[
 C_j(a)=\sum_{v=0}^{j-1}\binom nv h_{j-v}(a).
 \tag{6}
\]
The source contribution to the monic coefficient error is at most
\(R^jC_j(a)\). Using acquired \(|b_v|\) instead of the binomial
bound can improve (6).

Keep continuous wall integration and coefficient encoding separate.
Compute model moments with certified errors, propagate them through
Newton identities, and enclose the resulting normalized coefficients
by a stored nominal vector \(\widehat b_j\) with radii \(q_j\).
One explicit propagation is as follows. If the normalized power sums
are \(\widehat s_k\pm\epsilon_k\), \(q_0=0\), and the nominal Newton
step has certified local rounding error \(\rho_j\), choose the certified radius
\[
 q_j:=\rho_j+\frac1j\sum_{k=1}^j
 \left\{|\widehat s_k|q_{j-k}
 +\epsilon_k(|\widehat b_{j-k}|+q_{j-k})\right\}.
 \tag{7}
\]
Here the displayed right side is an admissible chosen upper radius;
the power-sum and coefficient variables in (7) are normalized by
\(R^k\) and \(R^j\), respectively. The moments themselves come from
\((2\pi i)^{-1}\oint u^k f'_X/f_X\,du\), with the acquired floor
and the entire quotient/quadrature/evaluation error included.
Store the *physical microscopic polynomial*
\(Q(u)=u^n+\sum_{v=0}^{n-1}\widetilde c_vu^v\) with exact dyadic
complex centers \(\widetilde c_v\). In (7), interpret
\(\widehat b_j=\widetilde c_{n-j}/R^j\) exactly; these normalized
centers may be algebraic. Use certified rational enclosures of \(R\)
and charge rescaling and rounding to \(\rho_j\) or the final \(q_j\).
Store outward dyadic error radii. The leading coefficient is exactly
one, not an uncertain field amplitude.

For the monic stored polynomial \(Q\), feed [Note01][n01] the vector
\[
 \boxed{d_{n-j}=R^j\{C_j(a)+q_j\},\qquad 1\le j\le n.} \tag{8}
\]
All exact coefficients are in microscopic coordinates. Normalization
by \(A_X\) cancels in the logarithmic moments. For \(n=2\), the
binomial fallback is explicitly
\[
 d_1=R(a+q_1),\qquad
 d_0=R^2(3a+a^2/2+q_2). \tag{9}
\]

The exterior unit is retained: locally \(h_X=U_HP\),
\(f_X=U_FQ_F\), and
\(P'/P=h'_X/h_X-U'_H/U_H\). The analytic unit has zero contour
power moments, which is why (5)–(8) isolate the cluster polynomial.
This is an exact projection of the unit, not a claim that it is constant.
The density is computed from the resulting polynomial; using
\(-\pi^{-1}\Im(h'_X/h_X)\) directly would retain exterior zeros.

This acquisition route compares fields directly at \(\tau_X\) while
the whole-interval wall tracks membership. It therefore adds no time
transport residual to (8). If coefficient transport is used instead,
[HDC Note12][hdc12] must supply its complete residual and integral
payment, including the correlated numerator
\(h_{uu}f-hf_{uu}\), model defect and unit; such a payment is added
to (8), not inferred from (3) or omitted. No transport advantage is
asserted here.

## 5. Explicit demand on the unacquired wall

\[
 B_0=\sum_{j=1}^nR^jZ^{n-j},\qquad
 B_1=\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}(n-j)R^jZ^{n-j-1},
\]
\[
 q_{\rm cap}=\min\left\{\frac{y^n}{4B_0},
 \frac{\pi y^nE_*}{4(B_1+nB_0/y)}\right\}>0.
 \tag{Qcap}
\]
Require every propagated/encoded radius in (7) to satisfy
\(q_j\le q_{\rm cap}\). This consumes at most half of each coefficient
allowance in (11); contour quadrature and coefficient rounding share
that allocation. It is a tolerance formula, not an executed enclosure.

For prescribed extraction radii \(q_j\), substitute (6)–(8) into (11).
The resulting left sides are monotone finite polynomials in
\(a=\kappa[-\log(1-\eta)]\). Define \(\eta_*\) as the largest
\(\eta\in[0,1/2]\) passing both tests. This is a finite, explicit
tolerance definition using only \(n,y,R,Z,q_j\) and (10).
If the tests already fail at \(\eta=0\), extraction/encoding has used
too much budget; source refinement alone cannot help. Otherwise a
positive \(\eta_*\) yields the sufficient floor demand
\[
 \boxed{m_X\ge e_{\rm src}/\eta_*} \tag{14}
