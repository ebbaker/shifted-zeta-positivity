## 1. What the last comparison settled

Note10 completed the bounded analytical task in Note09. With
\(L=\log(X/(4\pi))\), \(z=X+u/L\), and the positive normalization
\(A=\mathcal E_o(t,X)\) frozen in the spatial variable, it records
\[
\left\|\left(H/A,\ H_z/(LA)\right)\right\|_2\ge\frac1{384},
\qquad |u|\le1,\quad 6\le tL\le8,
\tag{1}
\]
for sufficiently large \(X\), after finite but uninstantiated source and
carrier gates. Its status is **proved in-note from inherited approximation
statements; separate-agent same-model internal review only**. No finite
starting height, numerical certificate or practical cost was acquired.

The useful mechanism is precise: an invertible weighted norm retains the
value/derivative correlation inside each paired arithmetic mode. The
leading pair has a known radius; all other modes, the Riemann–Siegel
completion and both source errors are paid. On this band the arithmetic
payment is at most \(15/16\), and the final separate completion/source
payments yield (1). This is a reusable calibration of the physical jet.

The limits are equally precise:

- The ordinary phase-insensitive Euclidean majorant loses throughout the
  tested \(4\le tL\le8\) band; this is a failure of that majorant.
- The improved weighted absolute-sum comparison loses at \(tL=4\): its
  fixed block \(2\le n\le31\) has limiting total weight at least
  \(3/(2\sqrt2)>1\). Reducing source error cannot repair that payment.
- The result supplies **no demonstrated new zero coverage or matched
  onset, margin or cost improvement**. The [earlier outer budget][outer58]
  already works at the asymptotic scale \(tL\ge61/24\) for
  \(0<t\le1/2\) and sufficiently large height, subject to its imported
  arithmetic package and unevaluated onset. Its quantitative
  error is \(1728B_C(L)+409/L\); the unknown arithmetic constant cannot
  be silently replaced by a number.

Stop the tested \(n=1\) absolute-sum variant. This does not stop all direct
jet methods, establish actual simultaneous cancellation, or rule out a
different coupled arithmetic estimate. HCB-05 remains open at time zero.

## 2. Research objective and what must now change

The common objective remains an estimate that distinguishes a multiple
canonical endpoint from nearby simple roots. The next useful advance
must exploit **relations between different arithmetic modes**, or obtain
a complete integrated estimate that is easier than the pointwise jet.
Another norm transform, more upper approximation terms, or a tighter
constant within the stopped comparison is not the default next task.

This is a judgment about work order, not evidence that the next bound is
accessible. Read the [result index][results], especially [heat flow][r11]
and [collision controls][controls], and the [avoid index][avoid]. In
particular, [target reformulation][a01], [scoped exclusions][a07],
[certifying the known][a11] and [separate absolute bounds][a12] govern
what can be claimed as progress.

There are two distinct eventual applications:

| Application | Required coverage and information |
| --- | --- |
| RH implies simple zeros | At time zero, cover every high real chart and the bounded remainder under RH; a complete model first-jet floor beating its \(O(X^{-3/4})\) error is sufficient |
| Positive-threshold collision exclusion | Obtain canonical information at each possible \(T>0\) and every possible event height, with the threshold argument's hypotheses retained; the bounded remainder grows as \(T\downarrow0\) |

The time-zero polynomial margin remains a final-strength quantitative
target, stronger than qualitative simplicity. A bound on one positive
time-height curve is an intermediate estimate, not either global
conclusion. Time-zero zeta information in Rodgers–Tao cannot be shifted
to an unknown positive threshold by changing the clock.
