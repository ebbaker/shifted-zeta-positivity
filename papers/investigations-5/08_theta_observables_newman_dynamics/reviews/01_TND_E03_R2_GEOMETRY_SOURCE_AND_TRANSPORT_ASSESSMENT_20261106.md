## 5. Heat-generator clarification and bounded correction check

Parent Note02 §7 says ordinary scalar cylinder heat gives e^(−τk²)H, referring to the companion. The actual [book subsection “Ordinary heat on the cylinder”][book] defines that evolution as e^(τΔC). In the canonical angular-constant sector ΔC=∂u², so the sentence is correct.

To make its reusable scope explicit, for the unchanged canonical radial observation T, fixed λ and each finite real τ≥0,
\[
\mathcal T(e^{\tau\Delta_C}G_\lambda)(z)
=e^{-\tau z^2}H_\lambda(z),
\qquad
\mathcal T(e^{-\tau L_{\rm sc}}G_\lambda)(z)
=e^{-\tau(1+z^2)}H_\lambda(z),
\quad L_{\rm sc}=1-\Delta_C.
\tag{R1}
\]
These identities hold first for the paid strip integrals and then by entire continuation of the response. The second follows from e^(−τLsc)=e^(−τ)e^(τΔC); no new evolution is selected. The comparison cannot silently drop e^(−τ) when the generator is Lsc.
