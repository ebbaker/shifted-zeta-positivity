## TND-A-B03: one target, at most two routes

**Input:** the current overview; accepted E01 proof and E02 review;
B02 Decision02 and the unchanged E03 request; the OC program-fit diagnosis;
the inherited auxiliary-flow lesson note; and the root controls/patterns.
The new overview links all of them at point of use.

**First decide the useful statement.** Specify the original canonical
quantity, derivative units, real/complex observation domain, Newman-time
range, height range, event multiplicity and consequence. Choose a bounded
collision restriction or a substantive intermediate estimate. A complete
RH-equivalent assertion may be recorded once as motivation, not treated
as a tested milestone. A known final theorem is not an entry requirement
for this conceptual task.

**Compare at most two routes:** additional canonical information within
the accepted response model, and at most one explicitly motivated
auxiliary extension. The extension may change only a declared ingredient;
do not open a new family of flows, fitted potentials or readouts.
For each route record:

- the independently justified information it would obtain;
- the complete implication from that information to the original H;
- the arithmetic estimate or acquisition assumption still missing;
- the strongest direct comparison with identical information and errors;
- the relevant control's failed premise, including regularity limits;
- the number, bound, cost or structural consequence that would count as
  a gain, and the outcome that would end this candidate.

Keep signed cancellations and correlated errors. A known invertible
factor, a changed coordinate or an auxiliary path with fewer collisions
does not alone improve the canonical conclusion. A positive energy
upper bound is not automatically a lower bound for a local first jet.

**Finite work limit:** one design/route pass, at most two candidates and
one focused clarification. Permitted routine choices are notation,
compact cutoffs within the declared regime, elementary checks and
source/version reconciliation. No numerical implementation, model
dispatch, new manuscript or parameter scan is part of B03.

**Completion and handoff:** return one concise decision in brainstorm/notes,
with any derivation in notes. A useful source-dependent result can
complete the cycle. Alternatively freeze E07 only when the source,
observable, equation/domain, error model, method, success/failure test and
finite work limits are explicit. E07 may prove a genuinely new
intermediate estimate or test a falsifiable acquisition advantage; it
must not be an unspecified request to “find a collision bound.”

If the two routes provide no plausible added information or transfer,
record that precise gap and recommend a representation-only disposition
for the tested auxiliary construction. This is a research decision, not
a theorem excluding all auxiliary methods. Do not create another route
solely to keep the sequence active.

**Escalation:** a proposed change to the physical construction triggers
the conditional B01 r2 gate. A third route, changed target or new broad
research question requires a newly dated B03 revision. Stop dependent
execution until that conceptual change is classified.
