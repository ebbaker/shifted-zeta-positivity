## 2. Clocks, event class and available data

Reserve \(\tau\) for the auxiliary proper time in
\(\mathcal O_{\lambda,\tau}=q(\tau)e^{-\tau z^2}H_\lambda\).
Write the advisory's forward collision heat variable instead as
\[
t=\lambda_{\rm ref}-\lambda,\qquad
h(t,z)=H_{\lambda_{\rm ref}-t}(z),\qquad h_t=h_{zz}.
\]
The two variables must not be identified. For a nondegenerate double
event, the upper zero is born for t greater than the event time, hence
on the smaller-lambda side. Its height is locally of order
\(\sqrt{t-t_*}\), with event-dependent constants. This local statement
does not transport a positive-time event to lambda=0.

The accepted auxiliary response has compact-parameter validity in
lambda, but that is not an acquired arithmetic margin at every lambda.
The present OG arithmetic acquisition and OC B06/E08 statistical
specification are at lambda=0. A consumer elsewhere needs a same-time
estimate or a proved transport preserving its depth, window, multiplicity
and errors. An event-dependent Taylor remainder is not a uniform tube.

A real multiple zero at lambda=0 is compatible with RH; excluding it
also addresses simplicity. A conclusion about a positive Newman threshold
needs its own implication to the selected finite event class. Isolated
double events do not silently cover higher multiplicities or endpoints.

## 3. Gap lifetime: the direction and size of the required bound

Use \(q_{\rm gap}(t)=(r_+(t)-r_-(t))^2\) to distinguish the gap from
the angular attenuation q(tau). For an isolated real pair with a
nonvanishing local deflated factor F on the relevant segment,
\[
q_{\rm gap}'=-8-4q_{\rm gap}B,\qquad
B(t)=\int_0^1(\log F)_{zz}(t,r_-+u(r_+-r_-))\,du.
\]
This is the inherited [collision anatomy, §4](../../03_operator_imaginary_time_evolution/notes/07_TND_O_B02_COLLISION_ANATOMY_20261106.md)
law, in the opposite direction from increasing Newman time.

If \(B(t)\le b\) persists up to a proposed first pair collision on
[0,Delta], together with isolation, define
\[
E_b(\Delta)=
\begin{cases}
(e^{4b\Delta}-1)/(4b),&b\ne0,\\
\Delta,&b=0.
\end{cases}
\]
Then \(q_{\rm gap}(0)>8E_b(\Delta)\) excludes that first collision
through Delta. Indeed, with \(A(t)=\exp(4\int_0^t B)\),
\[
A(t)q_{\rm gap}(t)=q_{\rm gap}(0)-8\int_0^t A(s)\,ds,\qquad
A(s)\le e^{4bs}.
\]
The strict bracket remains positive. This check works for either sign
of b and includes its continuous b=0 limit.

A finite value of B at the event is compatible with
\(q_{\rm gap}'=-8\); boundedness at one time is not a contradiction.
The heat-polynomial model with B=0 attains equality at its collision
horizon, so it does not pass the strict test. This is a local model
check, not a canonical source control. Failure of isolation or of the
uniform bound ends the stated comparison.

## 4. What the selected pair's environment asks of statistics

For a finite polynomial with simple zeros, the signed exterior quantity
is
\[
\mathcal B_{ij}=-\sum_{k\ne i,j}
\frac1{(z_i-z_k)(z_j-z_k)}.
\]
The segment integral of \(-1/(z-z_k)^2\) gives each displayed term.
Accordingly its average weighted by a two-position test is a contraction
of the third factorial measure:
\[
\mathbb E\sum_{i\ne j}\psi(z_i,z_j)\mathcal B_{ij}
=-\int\frac{\psi(a,b)}{(a-c)(b-c)}\,d\alpha_3(a,b,c).
\]
This requires integrability. For a fixed finite number of labels, a
positive separation floor and bounded test suffice. For a random label
count, also control the necessary count moments rather than relying on
separation alone. It is not an unqualified infinite-product formula for theta.

Conditioning on a selected pair requires the appropriate pair mass or
event probability in the denominator. Adjacency and isolation concern
the rest of the configuration and are not encoded by an arbitrary
two-position test. Near a rare event that normalization may amplify the
error. A direct uniform exterior bound or justified closure can avoid a
full three-point acquisition; the plan should state which input it uses.

## 5. Statistical visibility is part of the strict margin

At a time where the positive Green representation is justified, a
scenario forcing an upper zero of depth at least d within distance r
of every center in \(A\subset I\) implies
\[
\frac1{|I|}\int_I J_\lambda(X)^2\,dX
\ge \frac{|A|}{|I|}
 \left(\frac{d\sin(\pi/8)}{\cosh(\pi r/8)}\right)^2.
\]
A fully paid arithmetic upper bound must be strictly smaller than this
quantity to contradict the scenario. The set A and its measure must
follow from the event assumptions. Time averaging also pays the
corresponding space-time visibility fraction.

The exact square uses the one-point self term plus the full factorial
pair contraction. Connected covariance alone is insufficient. Smoothed
marginals require bias, real-zero background, tails, unfolding and
first-/second-intensity errors. A scalar contracted estimate can be enough;
uniform recovery of the full pair function is not required by this
consumer. Neither bound has been acquired here.
