## Research aims

1. Understand what a collision is in the assessed realization: the conditional state, success probability, signed observation, local zero geometry, and full count-output structure, including the distinction between double and higher-order events.
2. Investigate **mechanisms for collision control within the unchanged dynamics**: source-specific relations, invariants or transport restrictions that constrain the relevant signed balances. Mechanism discovery may precede the choice of a single quantity to bound.
3. Investigate quantitative consequences, conditioning bounds and usable identities, comparing every claimed advantage with direct analysis using the same source information.

A mechanism here is an analytical explanation or restriction arising within the frozen construction. A new controller, feedback law, source, clock, observation or physical access assumption changes B01 and cannot be silently introduced under B02. Analytical auxiliary traces of already defined count outputs are allowed; physically observing post-count channels is not presumed by their mathematical definition.

**Phase 2 — mechanism and target decision, later author-selected continuation.** One conceptual decision pass comparing at most two structural approaches, informed by Phase 1.
