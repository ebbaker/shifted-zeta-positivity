## 3. Primary candidate: a signed neighboring-count estimate

For normalized Gaussian weight \(w_v(k-X)\), \(v>0\), and \(\alpha>0\),
the selected diagnostic is
\[
 S_\alpha(X,v)=\int_{\mathbb R}w_v(k-X)
       \left[(d_0'(k))^2+\alpha^{-1}d_0(k)d_1(k)\right]dk
       =A_0(\lambda,X,v)/h_*^2.
\]
Here \(A_0=\int w_v[(H_\lambda')^2-H_\lambda H_\lambda'']\).
The [operator theorem][laguerre] supplies the paid negative signal after a
double event at \((\lambda_*,k_*)\): at \(\lambda=\lambda_*-\varepsilon\)
and \(v=c\varepsilon\), \(0<c<2\), its leading term is
\(H_{\lambda_*}''(k_*)^2\varepsilon(c/2-1)\). Its full error and dependence
on the nonzero curvature remain costs.

The count dictionary and the first-jet error transfer in [brainstorm
section 3][brainstorm] currently have **proof-author checking only**.
That transfer controls A0 using existing H/H' errors, with a value-error
cost proportional to \(1/v\). It does not supply the sign. Use the
inherited complete error, not an unquantified asymptotic, for a consequence.

The remaining B03 decision must do all of the following:

1. Declare the event class and observation domain. Start with double events
   in a specified compact subinterval of \(0<\lambda_*<1/2\) and a finite
   real observation window; choose the permitted \(\varepsilon\), variance
   and normalization. If another scope is justified, record the B decision
   before dependent execution. Higher multiplicities and endpoint events
   are outside this initial target, not ruled out.
2. Identify a complete-theta source relation beyond the generic count
   calculus, tail comparability or the event equations. Propose one signed
   lemma and explain how its premise can be obtained independently of the
   desired collision exclusion. Source identification alone is not a sign
   estimate; global nonnegativity of S is not an independent premise.
3. Retain the mixed term, complete source/index/radial payments, curvature
   conditioning and shrinking-variance error. Carry the event amplitude
   explicitly or prove any lower bound used; do not assume a uniform floor
   that removes the difficult event class.
4. Compare the resulting bound with direct scalar-source analysis using
   identical information. State the numerical or symbolic signed margin
   that would improve, or identify a distinct useful intermediate estimate.

OA's regular deflation is available only if a specified finite-pair
consequence needs it. It still requires its full-box factor floor,

and finite-prime controls lack complete unit weights or full reflection;
smooth/positive-primitive controls lack the canonical arithmetic expansion.

variable kernels fail the literal cosine/Newman law; fractional profile

bound each relevant control fails on a domain containing its witness.

fails a derived inequality. A finite collision-free box may legitimately
pass. Keep every negative conclusion confined to the tested information,
domain, method and comparison.

generic positivity, a change of notation, or reduced absolute errors with
