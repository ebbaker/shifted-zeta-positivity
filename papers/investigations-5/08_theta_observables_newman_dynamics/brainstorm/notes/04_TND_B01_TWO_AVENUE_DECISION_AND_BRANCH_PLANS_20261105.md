## One common missing premise

The free cylinder's angularly damped Green diagonal is translation invariant. It does not by moving the probe produce \(\beta(v)=\pi e^{4v}\), the source readout \((\partial_v^2-1)[e^vQ_1(\beta(v))]/8\), or a canonical spatial field. The common missing construction is:

**A specified source-placement/preparation law that turns the angular-response scan into the canonical position-dependent radial field, with a fixed calibration and declared readout domain.**

That premise may be supplied as external apparatus if the chosen goal is a controlled response interpretation; it must be derived if the chosen goal is an intrinsic source explanation. Direct physical Newman evolution additionally needs its specified clock/controller. The branching decision keeps these unresolved choices visible rather than calling a prescribed profile their solution.
