1. **What should a satisfactory physical model explain?** Clarify whether the first aim is a mathematically complete state/operator/measurement model, a mechanism generating the theta source, a preparation/evolution protocol, or a more experimentally motivated realization. These can be related goals without committing to all of them at once.
2. **What is the useful connection between the two starting models?** Compare the angular Dirac-cylinder response with the radial potential and source-defined Newman generator. Identify their fields, coordinates, measures and observations before suggesting that they describe the same physical system.
3. **What observation carries the mathematical question?** Begin with the energy moments, the signed cosine expectation and its position-weighted sine derivative. Consider what a physical relation could say about them beyond a change of representation. A promising mathematical payoff can be discussed without requiring the author to supply the missing estimate in advance.
4. **How should Newman evolution enter?** Consider a controlled family of preparations, conditioned dynamics or an enlarged system as possibilities, not a preselected construction. Keep physical time, damping, source coordinate and Newman parameter distinct, and state what theta information is supplied externally.
5. **What would be a useful first gain?** Distinguish a better physical explanation, an exact operational relation, a quantitative diagnostic and a new source-sensitive constraint on collisions. The draft overview should reflect the author's priorities after this discussion.
