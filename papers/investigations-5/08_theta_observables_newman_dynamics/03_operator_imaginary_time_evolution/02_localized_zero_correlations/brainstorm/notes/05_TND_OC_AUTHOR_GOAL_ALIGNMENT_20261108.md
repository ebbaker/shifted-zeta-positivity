**Status: author-goal clarification, not a new theorem or research task.**
The author confirms that the intended goal is to generalize a localized
distribution/marginal approach, in the spirit of Montgomery's pair
correlation and its use by Rodgers–Tao, to possibly nonreal zeros.
The completed logarithmic work pursued a narrower route within this goal.

[Further clarification06](06_TND_OC_PAIR_MARGINAL_AND_RODGERS_TAO_SPACING_20261108.md)
makes the author's two-particle marginal precise, including a symmetrized
atomic joint law for actual zeros. The missing ingredient is useful
independent control of the canonical measure, not existence of a joint
measure or validity of marginalization.

The [original author-ideas note](01_TND_OC_AUTHOR_IDEAS_AND_SELECTION_BOUNDARY_20261107.md)
explicitly records spatial weighting before marginalization. The
[overview](../../research-overview/01_TND_OC_RESEARCH_OVERVIEW_20261107.md)
defines genuine ensemble marginals conditional on a justified ensemble,
and deterministic factorial zero measures. Its localized pair object is

\[
\sum_{j\ne k}w_{X,h}(x_j)w_{X,h}(x_k)\delta_{(z_j,z_k)},
\qquad z_j=x_j+iy_j,
\]

with multiplicity copies retained. Marginals and separation statistics
must state which transverse variables survive. A nonreal conjugate pair
must remain distinguishable from coincident real multiplicity copies.
The all-real restriction provides a spacing-statistic benchmark; it is
not assumed for the complex-zero construction. Scaling, normalization,
any center-averaging law and the time regime must be declared.

[B01](02_TND_OC_B01_SELECTION_20261107.md) tested a particular quadratic
projection. Accepted [Note01](../../notes/01_TND_OC_REFLECTION_AND_CONFIGURATION_REDUCTION_20261107.md)
reduces it by conjugation to 2 integral (chi*q_0)dq_2, and to 2N_wQ_w
when chi=1. General chi still retains a density/transverse-mass coupling.
This is not a collapse theorem for the full pair measure or arbitrary
marked kernels. The selected product's logarithm is also a one-point
statistic. E02 paid acquisition errors; B02/B03 then sought signed
smallness for those observables. They did not derive a new full localized
joint law, nonreal pair-correlation asymptotic, or Rodgers–Tao-type
dynamical estimate for that generalized correlation.

**B03's parking decision applies only to its two logarithmic completion
attempts. The original localized-marginal/correlation program remains open.**
The finite count alternative likewise does not replace the research goal.
E02's time-zero theorem should not silently confine the broader program
to that one acquisition method or to Q as its final observable.

Montgomery studies weighted deterministic zero-difference sums under RH,
with a restricted-frequency theorem. Rodgers–Tao assume Lambda<0 for
contradiction, obtain local near-equal spacing of real zeros, and contradict
known spacing information; their implementation uses Conrey et al.'s
positive-proportion small-gap result. Neither requires a finite GUE joint
probability law for actual zeros. A localized empirical measure, with
explicit center averaging if desired, can therefore be the starting point;
a Vandermonde density would need separate identification.
[Montgomery, first theorem and (1)--(4)](https://websites.umich.edu/~hlm/paircor1.pdf);
[Rodgers–Tao v5, Introduction and section9](https://arxiv.org/html/1801.05914v5#S9).
