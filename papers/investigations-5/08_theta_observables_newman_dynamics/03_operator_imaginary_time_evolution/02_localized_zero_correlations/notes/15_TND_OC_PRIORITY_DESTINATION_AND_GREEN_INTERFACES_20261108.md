**Time.** Use \(t\) for forward collision heat:
\(h(t,z)=H_{\lambda_{\rm ref}-t}(z)\), \(h_t=h_{zz}\).
If \(a=h_{zz}(t_*,x_*)\ne0\), \(b=h_{zzz}(t_*,x_*)\), the local double germ is
\[
 z_\pm(t_*+s)=x_*-\frac{2b}{3a}s\pm i\sqrt{2s}+O(s^{3/2}).
\]
Thus the upper-zero signal is on the smaller-Newman-\(\lambda\) side.
The constants are event-dependent. Existing B06/E08 data are at
\(\lambda=0\); neither an event-time estimate nor finite-time transport
has been acquired. An isolated compact double-event result is not a
positive-threshold theorem without an implication to that event class.
A real double at time zero does not contradict RH by itself.

**Gap.** For the isolated real pair, the advisory's integrating factor
checks its sign and strict comparison:
\[
 q'=-8-4qB,\quad A(t)=\exp\!\left(4\int_0^t B(s)\,ds\right),\quad
 A(t)q(t)=q(0)-8\int_0^t A(s)\,ds.
\]
If \(B\le b\) and the isolation/bound persist up to a possible first
collision, \(q(0)>8E_b(\Delta)\) keeps the bracket positive through
\(\Delta\). Bounded \(B\) at the event alone does not do so.
The \(B=0\) quadratic heat model attains equality at its collision
horizon, not the strict inequality. This checks a conditional interface,
not an available theta estimate.

**Selected-pair information.** For finite simple labels,
\[
 {\cal B}_{ij}=-\sum_{k\ne i,j}
       \frac1{(z_i-z_k)(z_j-z_k)},\qquad
 \mathbb E\sum_{i\ne j}\psi(z_i,z_j){\cal B}_{ij}
 =-\int\frac{\psi(a,b)}{(a-c)(b-c)}\,d\alpha_3 .
 \tag{1}
\]
This follows by expanding the ordered distinct triples. For fixed finite
label count, bounded \(\psi\) and a positive relevant separation floor
make it integrable. If the finite count is random and unbounded, a
separation floor alone is insufficient: one additionally needs the
corresponding count/integrability condition (for example
\(\mathbb E[N(N-1)(N-2)]<\infty\) under that uniform bound).
This qualification is retained when adopting the advisory.

Conditioning further divides by pair/event mass. Small mass amplifies
an unconditional error; adjacency/isolation also involves the remaining
configuration. Equation (1) is a finite-cloud identity, not a closed
canonical infinite-zero law. Canonical product normalization, convergence
and all exterior terms remain obligations. A uniform exterior estimate
or justified closure could avoid computing the full third marginal.

**Visibility and the full pair.** For the positive Green potential on
its justified slice/strip,
\[
 J(X)^2=\langle g_X^2,\nu\rangle+
          \langle g_X\otimes g_X,\alpha_2\rangle .
\]
A forced visibility set \(A\subset I\), depth \(d\) and distance \(r\)
give
\[
 |I|^{-1}\int_IJ(X)^2\,dX
 \ge \frac{|A|}{|I|}
      \left[\frac{d\sin(\pi/8)}{\cosh(\pi r/8)}\right]^2.
 \tag{2}
\]
It is the full pair plus the one-point term, not connected covariance
alone. Distinct multiplicity labels stay in the factorial term.
The same positivity argument works over a space-time visibility set
with its space-time fraction; the event must establish that fraction.

A single near-birth upper zero has Green contribution of order
\(\sqrt{s}\), square of order \(s\), with event-dependent remainders.
Other zeros can contribute background. A mere vanishing error is
insufficient without its rate and visibility. Smoothing, real-zero
background, tails, unfolding and first/second intensity errors are
additional payments. An accurate contracted estimate may suffice for
(2); the full-profile objective remains useful on its own.
