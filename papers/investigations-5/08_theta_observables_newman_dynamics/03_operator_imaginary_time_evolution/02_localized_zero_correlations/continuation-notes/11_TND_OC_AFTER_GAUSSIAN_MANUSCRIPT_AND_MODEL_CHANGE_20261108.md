    bar-R_2 - bar-r tensor bar-r = Cov_pi(tilde-r_X,tilde-r_X) - E_pi tilde-d_X.

Each tilde-r_X has the explicit compact source-log integral (6.14).

across X. Choose the center interval/law, horizontal/transverse scales,
smoothing width and tested observation domain before searching for a
