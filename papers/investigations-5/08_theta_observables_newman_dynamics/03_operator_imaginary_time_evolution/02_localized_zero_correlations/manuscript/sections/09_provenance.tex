\subsection{Two sufficient finite error budgets}

$r=2^{-12}$ and $\sigma=2^{-24}$ give
\[
 \frac{(18N^2+5N)r^2+(18N^2+4N)\sigma}{T}+2^{-32}
 \le\frac{1208254589}{536870912000}<\frac1{400}.
\]
Thus an additional arithmetic radius below $1/400$ would suffice for
a total radius below $1/200$. This is an analytical conditional budget,
not a report that every centroid was acquired.
