- **Baluyot, Goldston, Suriajaya and Turnage-Butterbaugh, Pair Correlation
  of Zeros of the Riemann Zeta Function I: Proportions of Simple Zeros and
  Critical Zeros**, arXiv:2501.14545v3, 1 September 2026.
  Reopened [section 3, (3.1)–(3.5)](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.14545v3#S3).
  Its dyadic sum is a function of complex zero differences with the
  displayed analytic weight and frequency range. The source explicitly
  distinguishes corrected dyadic and cumulative statements. Comparing
  that statement with Note11 (5)–(7) shows it does not specify the
  independent planar marks and compact common-center weights required
  here. This is a comparison of interfaces, not a universal impossibility
  theorem. Reuse the existing canonical BGST citation.
- **Fazzari, Gerspach and Minelli, Selberg's Central Limit Theorem weighted
  by Linear Statistics of Zeta Zeros**, arXiv:2507.04150v1, submission
  5 July 2025. Reopened [introduction (1.6)–(1.8), Theorems 1.1–1.2 and
  Proposition 1.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.04150v1#S1).
  Theorem 1.1 fixes the powers and restricts Fourier support; the log
  values are on the critical line. Theorem 1.2 and Proposition 1.5
  explicitly assume RH. These statements do not state the uniform
  disk-product source-moment errors of Note11 (14)–(16). The HTML date
  header remains 24 August 2026 despite the v1 submission label; no
  inferred version is substituted. Existing BibTeX remains canonical.
