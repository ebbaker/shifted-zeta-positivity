**Objective.** Decide a mathematically precise and scientifically useful
operator realization of Newman evolution, understood as imaginary time.
The known source and scalar generators are baselines, not missing formulas
to rediscover. A physical/geometric explanation may be the selected
consumer; a collision estimate is not a prerequisite for opening this inquiry.

**Inputs.** Read [parent Note02](../../notes/02_TND_E01_R1_GEOMETRIC_HAMILTONIAN_AND_COMPLETE_SPECTRAL_COMPARISON_20261105.md) §§5–8, especially (35)–(40),
and [shared Review01](../../reviews/01_TND_E03_R2_GEOMETRY_SOURCE_AND_TRANSPORT_ASSESSMENT_20261106.md) §§4–5. Use the
[background manuscript](../../manuscript/README.md), [original Hamiltonian priority](../../brainstorm/notes/02_DIRAC_CYLINDER_HAMILTONIAN_PRIORITY_20261105.md),
[two-branch comparison](../../brainstorm/notes/04_TND_B01_TWO_AVENUE_DECISION_AND_BRANCH_PLANS_20261105.md) and current sibling registers for context.
[Direct Continuation08](../../01_direct_newman_realization/notes/08_TND_D_RESULTS_AND_B03_BRAINSTORM_CONTINUATION_20261106.md) and the
[auxiliary proof/assessment](../../02_hamiltonian_auxiliary_dynamics/reviews/01_TND_A_E02_REALIZATION_ASSESSMENT_20261106.md) identify comparisons already made.
Use [the reference index](../references/README.md) and the exact local versions frozen at activation.

**Candidate scope.** At most two approaches:

- A source/scalar operator formulation beginning from X², −∂²_k and
  B=L⁻¹X²L, with a declared space, inner product, domain and useful
  operator/semigroup or comparison question.
- One geometrically motivated cylinder operator/encoding/intertwiner
  connecting a declared full or explicitly restricted field space to H.

An alternative may replace one candidate before substantive work if the
replacement and reason are recorded within the same two-candidate budget.
At most one motivated extension may enter a selected contract; no survey
of fitted potentials, unbounded mechanism search or numerical screening.

**Required decision record.** For each considered candidate specify:

1. Evolved object (F, G, spinor amplitude, density or another explicitly
   defined field); space, norm/inner product and measure.
2. Hamiltonian/generator, dense domain/core, intended closure,
   self-adjointness or other needed generation property, lower-bound claim
   if used, and evidence still owed.
3. Time convention. For λ=λ₀−τ, use ∂τΨ=−KΨ and
   RK=(−∂²_k)R for a fixed readout. Name any alternate orientation and
   distinguish a canonical orbit from a bounded flow on a whole space.
4. Source, preparation/encoding and observation map with absolute
   normalization. State whether theta arithmetic enters only the initial
   condition or also an independently justified operator relation.
5. Exact real/complex, parameter, angular/radial and finite/growing-order
   scope. Respect the raw-G strip and the source's entire continuation.
6. What the existing baseline already establishes, what would be new,
   and a useful success test. If quantitative, declare the consumer,
   comparison norm, errors and same-information direct baseline.
7. Used premises and control screen from [the control cluster](../../../../../result-summaries/12_collision_controls_and_screening.md),
   including witness domains, source/domain failures and any audit still owed.
8. Evidence class, complete source/spectral/tail payment, limitations and
   precise failure/stop condition.

**Completion.** One concise decision in brainstorm/notes/, its actual
input-version/return record in research-progress/handoffs/, and a status
update in the sole register. It may select one fixed E01 proof contract
with method and success/failure test, or conclude with a named missing
premise/scoped stop. Repeating the known intertwining identities alone
