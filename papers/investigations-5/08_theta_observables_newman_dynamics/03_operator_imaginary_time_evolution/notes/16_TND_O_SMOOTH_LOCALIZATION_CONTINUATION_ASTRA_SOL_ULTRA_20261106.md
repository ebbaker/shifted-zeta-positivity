## 4. Mathematical priorities and freedom to change the approach

The objective is analytical progress on smooth localization and
height-dependent Newman dynamics, with a credible route to the relevant
infinite source, zero index, height or expansion/moment order. Develop the
argument systematically and expose its actual quantitative gain. Numerics
are supporting evidence, not the main work or a replacement for a limit.

Retain the canonical family and conventions

\[
H_0(z)=\frac18\xi\!\left(\frac12+\frac{iz}{2}\right),\qquad
\partial_\lambda H=-\partial_z^2H,\qquad
s=\lambda_*-\lambda.
\]

The height-window width \(h\), auxiliary-integral width \(b\), and physical
heat variance \(2s\) are different quantities. Mean zero density does not
by itself bound transverse displacement or eliminate interference. The
conditional estimate

\[
\lambda_*\le\tfrac12 e^{4A(T)}D(T)^2
\]

requires both full-path continuation to time zero and the displacement
and integrated-exterior-force hypotheses specified in Overview02 §6.
It is one possible consumer, not the theorem the program must force.

**Keep the Gaussian width law, smoothing kernel, observable and eventual
contradiction estimate fluid.** A Gaussian, bump, Poisson height kernel,
mesoscopic or adaptive profile, cluster comparison, direct heat estimate,
or another justified construction may be chosen. Neither
\(h\asymp1/\log T\), a separately bounded background, nor an
\(O((\log T)^{-2})\) collision envelope is mandatory. Recompute all costs
when changing the choice; preserve the useful lemmas and scoped failures.

Every activated proof contract must state the limit it can support and
the constant dependence needed there. Pay for full sums, tails, moving
parameters and any interchange of limits. A fixed-order estimate uniform
in unbounded height is useful; a long finite calculation without a
recurrence, majorant, remainder bound or uniform theorem is not the goal.
Compare genuinely new estimates with the existing positive-time benchmark
recorded in Overview02. Test a proposed mechanism against controls sharing
its actual premises. A new representation or an RH-equivalent criterion
alone is not a quantitative advance.
