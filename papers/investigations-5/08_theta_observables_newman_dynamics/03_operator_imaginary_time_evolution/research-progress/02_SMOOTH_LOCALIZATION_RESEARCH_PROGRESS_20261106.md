## Infinite-limit requirements for activation

Every activated contract names its exact limiting target and required
constant dependence. In particular:

- Complete theta-source and zero sums require actual tails/convergence;
  finite prefixes are not substituted for the canonical object.
- Height, frequency, window width and Newman time are treated in one
  stated regime whenever they vary together.
- Growing moment or derivative order requires explicit constant growth
  and a justified remainder or operator bound. Every-fixed-order estimates
  are not promoted to uniform order estimates.
- Finite analytical work must feed a recurrence, majorant, uniform theorem
  or decisive scoped obstruction; repeated finite checks are not the goal.
- A fixed-order theorem uniform in unbounded height is admissible. No task
  is required to take irrelevant limits merely to acquire an infinite label.

See [overview §8](../research-overview/02_SMOOTH_LOCALIZATION_RESEARCH_OVERVIEW_AND_PLAN_20261106.md#8-what-extending-to-infinite-order-or-index-means-here)
and [plan §§12–14](../research-overview/02_SMOOTH_LOCALIZATION_RESEARCH_OVERVIEW_AND_PLAN_20261106.md#12-what-a-fixed-e-contract-must-contain).
