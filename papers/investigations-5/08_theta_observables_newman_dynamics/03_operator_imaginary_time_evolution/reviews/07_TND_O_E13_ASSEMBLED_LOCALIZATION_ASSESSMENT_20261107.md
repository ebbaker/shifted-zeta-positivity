## 6. Note22: exact threshold lower bound

Accepted. The canonical threshold is finite, with upper bound `1/2`.
An initial nonreal zero persists on a small positive time neighborhood,
so the conditional hypothesis implies `Lambda>0`. A nonreal zero at
the threshold would persist to a time above it; therefore the threshold
slice is all real, including the endpoint. Applying Note19 at `R=1`
between zero and `Lambda` gives exactly

\[
 \Lambda\ge
 \frac{2d^2}{344A^2\|\chi\|_{(2)}\log^2(2+X)}.
\]

The zeta-coordinate numerator is `8|beta-1/2|^2`. This argument uses the
whole moment in the fixed window and requires no surviving label,
isolated tube or simplicity hypothesis. The constant is explicit in the
unevaluated absolute `A`; it is not a numerically evaluated bound.
The result is conditional on an off-line zero and is a lower bound
for the global threshold, with the exact stated height restriction.
No upper collision envelope, realness improvement or RH exclusion is
inferred.
