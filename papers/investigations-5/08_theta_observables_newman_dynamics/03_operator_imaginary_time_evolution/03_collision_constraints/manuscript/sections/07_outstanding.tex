\section{Outstanding tasks}
\label{sec:outstanding}

The outstanding question is whether the signed transfer changes a
quantitative conclusion for an actual pair of theta zeros on a finite
slab. The lower pilot lies inside an already certified interval and
its unsigned bound would suffice once confinement is paid. The
historical Lehmer example supplies time-zero data and a bound in the
opposite direction to the one needed for survival. Both acquisition
choices remain parked on their stated evidence \cite{OARecord}.

A useful next case requires three inputs before a numerical contract
can be fixed.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Specify the pair and its domain.}
Choose a certified actual simple-root pair at a named
\(\lambda_s>0\), a target \(\Delta\), and a box with initial midpoint
and gap enclosures. Screen the center curvature on the \(d=0\)
slice before investing in the full divided-difference floor.
\item \textbf{Define a quantitative comparison.}
Use the same pair, time slab, and complete source for the strongest
applicable direct sign, critical-value, root-jet, or contour bound.
Identify a specific unfilled lifetime, coverage, or force estimate.
Check whether unsigned confinement already closes the gap test.
Failure of the sufficient redundancy screen alone leaves the
actual comparison undecided.
\item \textbf{Pay the complete source and dynamics margins.}
Enclose source derivatives, finite evaluation errors, the uniform
factor floor, drift and curvature, and the signed force
\emph{upper} bound. Propagate every allowance through the two
confinement margins and the strict lower gap margin. An alternative
parameterization or estimator must provide the corresponding
conditioning and stopping argument. A numerical contract would
then have a fixed case, error and refinement limits, and a
specified return if any margin fails.
\end{enumerate}

The denominator floor and signed upper force are the central
unacquired canonical quantities. Small absolute theta tails do not
supply them. Source-derived lower contributions from identified
exterior zeros could bound the force from above if their product
and reality assumptions are paid. Direct divided differences leave
that global premise unnecessary, at the cost of certified local
source and factor data. Until one of these routes supports a
useful comparison on a specified box, the current results are a
conditional survival theorem and diagnostics for selecting an
application.
