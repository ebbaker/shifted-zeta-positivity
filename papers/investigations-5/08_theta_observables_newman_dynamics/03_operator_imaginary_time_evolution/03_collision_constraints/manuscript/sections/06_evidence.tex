\subsection{Interfaces with averaged observations}

The branch's setup assessment also compared local force with
statistical or Green observations \cite{OARecord}. In a finite cloud
with separated selected labels, let \(B_{ij}\) be the exterior
force on the segment joining labels \(i,j\), with no other zero
there. The endpoint identity for each exterior factor gives
\(B_{ij}=-\sum_{k\ne i,j}((z_i-z_k)(z_j-z_k))^{-1}\).
For an ordered factorial measure \(\alpha_3\), multiplying by a
test \(\psi\) and taking expectations gives
\[
 \mathbb E\sum_{i\ne j}\psi(z_i,z_j) B_{ij}
 =-\int\frac{\psi(a,b)}{(a-c)(b-c)}\,\dd\alpha_3(a,b,c),
\]
when the sum is integrable. A rule selecting adjacent or isolated
pairs may depend on the rest of the configuration. The average
alone leaves the pathwise force and that selection unpaid.

A Green observation instead sees a nonreal defect. At the double
event itself the depth is zero. Section~\ref{sec:geometry} gives a
local positive depth at \(\lambda_{\rm obs}=\lambda_*-s\) for
small \(s>0\). Using a time-zero observation sets \(s=\lambda_*\)
and needs a transport bound over that interval. In the inherited
positive Green representation, a defect of depth at least \(d_0\)
within range \(r_0\) of a center gives the lower signal
\(m(d_0,r_0)=d_0\sin(\pi/8)/\cosh(\pi r_0/8)\). If this occurs
on a fraction \(\vartheta\) of the centers, positivity and
integration give a first-moment signal \(\vartheta m(d_0,r_0)\)
and a second-moment signal \(\vartheta m(d_0,r_0)^2\).
A contradiction requires an independently paid upper estimate
below the corresponding signal. For a zero sum
\(J(X)=\sum_i g_X(z_i)\), the second moment includes both the
self term \(\sum_i g_X(z_i)^2\) and the distinct-label term
\(\sum_{i\ne j}g_X(z_i)g_X(z_j)\). These are alternative
conditional interfaces. The present analysis acquires no event
transport or signed Green bound.
