## 3. An unsigned bound makes the signed gap test unnecessary here

Let the accepted Note02 data hold on any fixed box: a positive factor
floor, \(|g|\le A\), \(|g_x|\le K\), with \(K\ge0\), initial
\(Q_-\le q(0)\le Q_+\), and the two strict confinement inequalities
\[
R_0+2A\Delta<R,\qquad Q_+e^{4K\Delta}<4D^2.
\tag{5}
\]
The first two hypotheses have not been acquired for (1). The following
is a conditional implication about what their acquisition would imply.

Since \(B\) is an average of \(g_x\), one already has \(B\le K\).
There is no need to calculate the signed model average \(b_P\) to use
that bound. Moreover
\[
E_K(\Delta)=\int_0^\Delta e^{4Ks}\,ds
\le\Delta e^{4K\Delta}<\frac{4D^2\Delta}{Q_+}.
\]
It follows that
\[
\eta_K:=Q_- -8E_K(\Delta)
>Q_- -\frac{32D^2\Delta}{Q_+}.
\tag{6}
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{\quad Q_-Q_+>32D^2\Delta\quad}
\tag{7}
\]
is a sufficient design screen for the gap condition to follow from
the unsigned data and confinement. The proof also covers \(K=0\)
using the integral definition. If a signed \(b_P+\kappa\) has been
acquired, the consumer can take the smaller of that bound and \(K\);
one is not required to use a worse signed upper allowance.

For (1), \(4D^2=225\) and
\[
\frac{32D^2\Delta}{Q_+}=\frac9{19000},\qquad
\eta_K>189-\frac9{19000}>188.9995.
\tag{8}
\]
The second confinement inequality alone would force this large
positive gap margin. The signed arithmetic average cannot change
pass/fail of the survival conclusion on this candidate once its other
premises have passed. It may sharpen a separate value enclosure, but
that would need a separately justified consumer or cost target.
Equation (8) is **not** a newly certified canonical \(\eta\): its
factor and confinement premises remain unacquired in the E01 model.

For reference, the two confinement conditions for (1) are exactly
\[
A<\frac{999}{10},\qquad
K<5000\log(45/38).
\tag{9}
\]
No estimate in this turn asserts that either threshold is achieved.

The screen (7) is sufficient, not necessary. A local heat control
\(h=(z-X)^2+2(\tau-T)\), with \(T=1/4\), exact model,
\(Q_-=Q_+=2,\ D=1,\ \Delta=3/16\), has \(A=K=0\).
It satisfies the upper-gap and gap inequalities, the latter with
\(\eta=1/2\), although \(Q_-Q_+=4<6=32D^2\Delta\).
Choose any compatible midpoint box around a positive \(X\).
Thus failure of (7) is not evidence of useful signed information,
nor evidence that the unsigned survival test fails.

## 4. A cost of the fixed uniform constant is already visible

One can quantify a conditioning loss without evaluating the proposed
source model. At the actual roots write \(\delta=b-a>0\) and let
\(U=h/((z-a)(z-b))\). The existing RT root-cell enclosures imply
the following deliberately loose inequalities throughout the slab:
\[
\begin{gathered}
13.77<\delta<13.78,\quad
0<-h_x(a)<8.65\cdot10^{-5},\\
0<h_x(b)<1.11\cdot10^{-6},\qquad
h_{xx}(a)>5.008\cdot10^{-5}.
\end{gathered}
\tag{10}
\]
Then
\[
U(a)=\frac{-h_x(a)}{\delta}<6.3\cdot10^{-6},\qquad
U(b)=\frac{h_x(b)}{\delta}<8.1\cdot10^{-8}.
\]
The factorization gives
\[
U_x(a)=\frac{2U(a)-h_{xx}(a)}{2\delta},
\qquad
|U_x(a)|>\frac{3.748\cdot10^{-5}}{27.56}
>1.35\cdot10^{-6}.
\tag{11}
\]
For **any** model and valid uniform constants in Note02 (7)–(8)
on a box containing this actual pair and its segment endpoints,
\[
M_1+e_1\ge |U_x(a)|,\qquad m-e_0\le U(b).
\]
Hence the particular uniform-ratio implementation necessarily pays
\[
A=\frac{M_1+e_1}{m-e_0}>\frac{50}{3},\qquad
K\ge A^2>\frac{2500}{9}.
\tag{12}
\]
This survives improvement of the source truncation error: it uses a
large derivative at one endpoint and a small denominator at the other.
The fixed sign must be \(\sigma=1\) for these roots if the floor is to
hold. A common scalar normalization does not change (12).

This lower bound is not an infeasibility proof: (12) is compatible
with the upper thresholds (9). It also does not lower-bound the
actual \(\sup|g|\), the actual force, or every possible estimator.
Pointwise relative bounds and different parameterizations are outside
this uniform-constant conclusion.

The already recorded actual-root velocities give the direct
same-source midpoint bound \(|c'|<0.668\) after time reversal.
By contrast, the E01 formula \(|c'|\le2A\) must have \(2A>100/3\)
for this pair. Thus its stated midpoint *allowance* is more than
49 times that existing direct allowance. This compares two proved
allowances using the inherited data. It measures neither actual
error nor runtime and establishes no universal method ranking.

## 5. Controls and the scope of the decision

The control screen is analytical; earlier interval certificates were
not replayed. The generic implication (5)–(7) is valid for colliding
control fields when their own data satisfy its hypotheses. At a matched
collision horizon its survival conclusion cannot hold, so at least one
premise must fail. For the exact quadratic at \(\Delta=T\),
\(Q_-=Q_+=8T\) and \(D^2>2T\) imply
\(Q_-Q_+=64T^2<32D^2T\); screen (7) fails explicitly.
An unmatched witness is not assigned a fabricated failed numerical bound.

For the canonical imports in (2), (4) and (10), the failed premises
from [controls12](../../../../../../result-summaries/12_collision_controls_and_screening.md)
remain:

| Control family | Why the canonical numerical inputs cannot be imported |
| --- | --- |
| Euler-factor | Different coefficients/completion and translated source; the canonical root and jet records do not evaluate that field |
| Weighted-square | Complete unit weights and full reflection fail |
| Reflected super-Gaussian | The integer-square theta expansion fails |
| Smooth shape, positive primitive and earlier counter-kernels | Exact theta arithmetic fails despite shared shape/endpoint properties |
| Finite-source and finite-prime orbit | Full source/reflection fails; high-gap witnesses do not select this fixed canonical pair |
| Fixed-event and finite-profile threshold | Exact theta coefficients/full profile and eventual simplicity are absent |
| RH-conditional endpoint | Ordinary Dirichlet-series arithmetic fails; escaping doubles are not witnesses in this box |
| Variable observation kernel | Literal cosine and the physical heat equation fail |
| Actual-profile measure relaxation | It is not a complete integer zero divisor of this field |

These differences do not themselves prove that a future derived signed
estimate rejects every control. No new theta-specific sign estimate is
proposed here. The same controls and the local quadratic also prevent
reading a regular factor, a negative force, or a finite conditional
survival test as a global exclusion.

## 6. B02 outcome

**Do not run a full E01-model acquisition on (1) for the proposed
collision-survival comparison.** Its coverage is already certified,
its signed gap gate is redundant once the other E01 premises pass,
and one of its fixed conditioning allowances is demonstrably weaker
than the existing direct allowance. No complete source model,
precision schedule or quadrature campaign is issued for this box.

This decision is restricted to the stated pair, starting slice,
geometry, consumer and uniform-constant implementation. The center
curvature sign and full factor floor are still undecided; rejection
is not being attributed to a proved sign failure. The investigation,
the accepted E01 theorem and other finite comparisons remain open.

A next conceptual cycle must find source-backed geometry where the
signed estimate changes an actual conclusion or a separately stated
bound. For a tight box with \(4D^2\) comparable to \(q(0)\), the
quadratic scale \(q(0)/8\) is a useful design guide. It is not a theorem
about a canonical collision time. Failing (7) is only the first
screen; an actual unsigned or direct comparator must still fail or
leave a specified quantitative gap. Inflating \(D\) just to defeat
the screen supplies no new information.
