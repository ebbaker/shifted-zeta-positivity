## Prioritize a finite gap bound and distinguish it from event jets

The two named candidates overlap. At a double event let
\(\beta=b/a\), \(\gamma=c/a-\beta^2\). [Parent Note11, §§1–2][jets]
and [Note07, §4][anatomy] give
\[
B_* = \frac{c}{6a}-\frac{b^2}{9a^2}
    =\frac{\gamma}{6}+\frac{\beta^2}{18}.
\]
Thus a new expression for these event jets can merely re-express the
same exterior curvature. Keep the two-candidate budget, but require each
candidate to identify the distinct estimate or consequence it offers.
Event-jet work can remain a separate candidate if it controls a critical
value over an interval or supplies genuinely stronger source information.
A bound only at the terminal event does not supply that interval control.

For the preferred gap comparison, choose a real pair at a starting slice
and reset imaginary time to zero there. Let \(q=(r_+-r_-)^2\), with a
certified initial lower bound \(q(0)\ge Q_-\). With a force upper bound
\(b_{\rm up}\), Note10's sufficient test is
\[
 B(t)\le b_{\rm up}\quad(0\le t\le\Delta),\qquad
 \eta_{\rm gap}:=Q_- -8E_{b_{\rm up}}(\Delta)>0,
\]
\[
 E_{b_{\rm up}}(\Delta)=\int_0^\Delta e^{4b_{\rm up}t}\,dt
 =\begin{cases}(e^{4b_{\rm up}\Delta}-1)/(4b_{\rm up}),&b_{\rm up}\ne0,\\
 \Delta,&b_{\rm up}=0.
 \end{cases}
\]
This is an inherited conditional comparison, not a newly obtained theta
estimate. The useful outputs would be the actual margin \(\eta_{\rm gap}\)
or the guaranteed lifetime \(\Delta\), compared with the strongest method
using the same source information. State the interval in Newman time too:
\([\lambda_{\rm start}-\Delta,\lambda_{\rm start}]\).

Acquire bounds on a domain valid up to a hypothetical first pair collision,
and prove that the isolation boundary remains nonzero there. Assuming the
pair already survives the whole interval would beg the question. Loss of
isolation or exit from the domain is a separate unresolved outcome. Initial
gap errors, boundary/source tails and time interpolation belong in the
same comparison.

The estimate need not be pointwise if the source more naturally controls
accumulation. Writing \(C(t)=\int_0^tB(u)\,du\), an independently obtained
envelope \(C(t)\le c(t)\) suffices when
\(Q_->8\int_0^\Delta e^{4c(t)}dt\). This is the same integrating-factor
consumer with a potentially less demanding input, not a third mechanism.
An endpoint bound on \(C(\Delta)\) alone does not control the integral.

For jet ratios, retain a paid denominator margin relative to a declared
field normalization, or a coupled estimate that handles its smallness.
Nondegeneracy \(a\ne0\) and upper bounds on source moments do not provide
a uniform lower bound on \(|a|\). Any imposed curvature or separation
floor restricts the theorem's event class and must appear in its statement.

The prior contour pilot's upper budget was **309.53 times** its same-source
direct budget [parent Note11, §4][jets]. Repeating that wide-gap comparison
has low value unless B01 identifies changed information, geometry or a
specific cost advantage. The number does not exclude contours near a
collision or under stronger boundary information.

## Fix the event and observation time before choosing a probe

Use a compact event class as a first milestone: isolated nondegenerate
double events in a stated time/height rectangle, with any required
conditioning floors explicit. Choose the rectangle because an improved
bound there would matter against the existing baseline; an arbitrary
already settled low-height box is only a calibration experiment.

Distinguish a local collision from a boundary of the global all-real
regime. A compact theorem about generic double events does not by itself
exclude every positive Newman threshold. That broader application needs
an implication to the selected finite event class, including multiplicity,
confinement and endpoint issues. A real double at \(\lambda=0\) is compatible
with RH; excluding it also addresses simplicity.

After a generic event, forward imaginary time corresponds to
\(\lambda_{\rm obs}=\lambda_*-s\). The emerging upper zero has depth
\(\sqrt{2s}+O(s^{3/2})\). Using a time-zero probe sets \(s=\lambda_*\),
which need not lie in the event's local Taylor neighborhood. Require an
explicit remainder, drift and isolation budget, or an independent
finite-time transport theorem. Neither analyticity nor the local germ
supplies that transport uniformly.

There is a useful distinction here: the **Green representation itself**
already holds for \(0\le\lambda\le1/2\) [parent Note25, §1][green]. The
specific prime/log acquisition and OC center-law moment interface now
available are at **\(\lambda=0\)** [OG B04][greenvalue], [OC Note11][oc11].
Obtaining a favorable bound at a nearby positive observation time is
therefore an alternative to transport, but that acquisition is still
unpaid. Do not misstate all Green theory as confined to time zero.

Real-zero tools keep their regime. The inspected [Rodgers–Tao v5,
Theorem 4.16 and Lemma 4.18](https://arxiv.org/html/1801.05914v5#S4)
state dynamics and gap identities on their all-real contradiction interval
\(\Lambda<t\le0\), with a principal-value convention. Their use for an
arbitrary complex cloud or positive-threshold endpoint needs further
justification. This recheck confirms the existing scope warning.

## Ask the other branches for the smallest sufficient quantity

For the force route, Note10's finite-cloud calculation is
\[
\mathbb E\sum_{i\ne j}\psi(z_i,z_j)B_{ij}
=-\int\frac{\psi(a,b)}{(a-c)(b-c)}\,d\alpha_3(a,b,c).
\]
It is an exact diagnostic with the stated separation/integrability
conditions. A pair-conditioned average needs its normalization, and
adjacency or isolation depends on the remaining configuration. Even a
third factorial marginal does not automatically encode that selection.
Nor does an averaged signed-force bound give the pathwise bound on the
chosen pair used above. A direct uniform exterior estimate can avoid
this hierarchy. These are scope requirements, not a theorem that every
pair method must reconstruct a full three-point function.

For the statistical comparator, let
\(M_2=|I|^{-1}\int_IJ_{\lambda_{\rm obs}}(X)^2dX\). If the event forces
depth at least \(d\) within range \(r\) on a subset of centers of relative
measure \(\theta\), the target is
\[
 U_2+\varepsilon_{\rm total}<\theta m(d,r)^2,
 \qquad m(d,r)=\frac{d\sin(\pi/8)}{\cosh(\pi r/8)},
\]
where \(M_2\le U_2+\varepsilon_{\rm total}\) is an independently acquired
upper bound. The visibility factor must hold uniformly over the proposed
event class; selecting a favorable center after locating unknown zeros
does not establish it. For space-time averaging, replace \(\theta\) by
the certified relative measure of the visibility set.

Request the contraction
\(\langle g_X^2,\nu\rangle+\langle g_X\otimes g_X,\alpha_2\rangle\).
It retains the one-point self term, distinct multiplicity labels and the
full pair contribution. A connected covariance alone is insufficient.
Keep smoothing bias, real-zero background, observation and source tails,
conditioning, time transfer and unfolding in the error budget. Near the
event the selected zero's square signal is of order \(s\); an unspecified
vanishing error gives no usable comparison. [OC Note14][oc14] and
[Note10, §§4–5][advisory] supply the relevant limitations.

Do not promote the second moment automatically. Compare it with a
same-time first-moment or pointwise Green bound. At the same visibility
floor, the first-moment test is \(U_1+\varepsilon_1<\theta m(d,r)\);
OG B04 also supplies a sharper exact finite-window first-moment signal.
Compare each acquired upper bound with its own signal and acquisition
cost. No universal dominance of one moment order is claimed.

OC can continue the full marginal program independently. OA needs an
estimate suited to its selected consequence, not completion of OC's
whole profile. OG retains its existing tasks; a feasibility screen for
acquisition does not establish a favorable value.

## Tighten the execution gate without demanding a new representation

Replace the plan's requirement for a “genuinely added arithmetic input”
with a **new source-dependent estimate, or a demonstrably sharper coupled
use of existing complete-source information**. The full source is already
fixed. A better bound need not introduce new data; it must identify what
information the proof uses that the failed estimate discarded. Merely
naming exact theta arithmetic still does not supply a signed inequality.

Before E01 is issued, B01 should provide all of the following in one
bounded request:

1. Event class, domain, observation time and the logical consequence.
2. The precise source functional and the method proposed to bound it.
3. The useful strict margin, with all acquisition and geometric errors.
4. The strongest equally informed direct comparator and the quantity to
   improve: lifetime, margin, coverage, or a separately labeled cost metric.
5. The control-premise audit, finite work limit and an informative failure
   outcome.

“Find an upper bound on B” or “acquire the mixed moments” is still an open
B problem. A stated missing-input contract can usefully close a comparison,
but it is not an execution-ready method. Conversely, a fixed-method
feasibility test can be a legitimate E task even if it returns an
unfavorable margin. Do not make guaranteed success a prerequisite for
that bounded test.

Retain the existing limit of two candidates and one focused clarification.
If neither supplies a method with an informative completion test, park
those candidates with their exact missing inputs. This does not exclude
other canonical estimates or statistical approaches.

## Classification and collision controls

The finite gap and fixed-depth/window Green comparisons are **conditional
finite-domain targets**, judged by their eventual numerical or symbolic
gain. Their identities are diagnostics. The unrestricted assertion
\(J_0(X)=0\) for every center is RH-equivalent and should be stated once,
not supported by sampled checks. This review acquires none of these
canonical bounds.

The following analytical premise screen uses [controls12][controls] and
the local control checks in parent Note11. Original interval certificates
were not rerun. A failed source identity is not claimed to be a failed
future numerical estimate.

| Control class | Consequence for the proposed work and premise that differs |
| --- | --- |
| Euler-factor | Positivity and heat evolution survive. Canonical coefficients/completion change; a proof must actually use their quantitative consequence. |
| Weighted-square | Square support and selected endpoints survive; full normalized reflection and complete unit weights fail. These controls have no all-real heat slice, so they are not automatically tests of an all-real-threshold hypothesis. |
| Reflected super-Gaussian | Reflection and the listed endpoints survive; the complete integer-square expansion fails. Parent Note11 explicitly has a collision with negative \(\gamma\), screening that sign alone. |
| Smooth shape and positive primitive | Positive shape and primitive information survive; exact theta arithmetic fails. Neither supplies the required signed estimate. |
| Finite-source and finite-prime | Selected finite measurements do not supply full reflection/source data. Keep the source-specific lack of an all-real slice and the high-gap, time-dependent witness quantifiers. |
| Fixed-event and finite-profile threshold | Exact finite profiles and moment bounds coexist with an uncontrolled deflated jet; complete theta coefficients/profile growth and eventual simplicity fail as specified. |
| RH-conditional normalized endpoint | Ordinary Dirichlet-series arithmetic fails. Escaping time-zero doubles do not refute a fixed compact statement; do not merge the separate polynomial control's hypotheses. |
| Raw-kernel and actual-profile relaxations | Respectively the full cosine/Newman equation and the integer complete-divisor constraints are missing; neither is a physical theta collision. |

For any actual isolated collision at time \(\Delta\), the exact gap law
gives \(q(0)=8\int_0^\Delta e^{4C(t)}dt\). Hence a valid upper envelope
and valid initial lower bound cannot pass the strict survival test on
that collision interval. Likewise positivity prevents a valid Green
upper bound from falling below a genuinely present defect's signal.
These checks confirm the direction of the conditional comparisons;
they do not identify a theta-specific gain. A control only needs to fail
a claimed exclusion on a domain containing its witness.

## Proposed wording and file changes

For the overview's opening and candidate discussion, the following would
make the intended first result explicit:

> The first cycle seeks a quantitative improvement for an isolated real
> pair on a declared finite time/height domain. It will compare direct
> theta/primitive jets and exterior-field estimates as acquisition methods,
> requiring a controlled initial gap, persistent isolation and a strict
> lifetime margin against the strongest equally informed direct method.
> Event-conditioned jets remain a separate candidate only if their bound
> yields a distinct interval consequence or stronger usable information.
> Green and marginal probes are comparators or justified alternatives;
> using them requires the same observation time, a paid visibility signal
> and a favorable complete upper bound. Each candidate must identify a
> source-dependent estimate and a concrete way to obtain it. Otherwise
> the cycle returns a scoped missing-input diagnosis.

requirements above; replace the unranked choice list with this proposed
priority. In the **plan**, add the five execution gates and clarify the
new-estimate requirement. In the **continuation**, require reading Note10
and this review before choosing the first event/domain. The existing
two-candidate limit, branch ownership and sole register should stay.
These scientific-document edits are proposed here, not applied.
