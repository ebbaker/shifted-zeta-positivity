## What this pass established and what it did not

The note makes two candidate signals explicit:

1. A nonnegative height-windowed pair statistic has an exact contribution
   2w^2 from one conjugate nonreal H_0 pair. The two zeta zeros share an
   ordinate. A microscopic window need not dilute this spike; its mass
   in an average over centers is only O(h/T). The coincidence signal
   does not increase as the pair's transverse separation grows.
2. A weighted analytic complex-difference statistic has a finite positive
   integral representation. An isolated pair contributes
   2w^2[1+W(d)cosh(a d)], where d is the final H_0 imaginary height.
   Its growth is potentially useful, but the total can contain interference.
   The rigorous lower comparison is (sqrt P-sqrt B)_+^2, with an explicit
   background-norm cost. No pointwise or moment arithmetic bound for
   this localized quantity has been established here.

Conditional two-root tube estimates transport the midpoint and y^2;
their constants require g=U'/U and g_z over the whole path. Near collision,
x=x_*-2ell s+O(s^2) and y^2=2s-4kappa s^2+O(s^3), for
s=lambda_*-lambda. Do not replace midpoint drift by critical-point drift.
The positive real-neighbor amplification inequality additionally assumes
a simple root of maximal imaginary part and a persistent neighbor count.

The localized transverse balance contains window-derivative, unequal-weight
interaction and exterior terms. A trajectory's sweep through heights at
different times is not a broad cluster at one final time. The exact
polynomial two-root control shows why that implication needs proof.

Maples–Rodgers' unconditional results are averaged local/mesoscopic
statements. Rodgers' sharper microscopic tail estimate assumes RH.
Polymath2019 Section 3 supplies actual complex-zero dynamics and a
spatial-barrier comparator; its wall and endpoint hypotheses remain due.
The earlier BGST asymptotic supplies no arbitrary localized version.
These are selected-passage checks, not an exhaustive literature audit.
