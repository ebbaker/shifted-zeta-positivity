The fixed family is H_0(z)=xi(1/2+iz/2)/8, partial_lambda H=-H_zz. Set s=lambda_*-lambda. We work in the H horizontal coordinate x; an ordinate window h corresponds to an H width R=2h. Physical heat variance is 2s. No auxiliary Gaussian width is used.

Let a in C_c^2(R) be real and nonnegative, f(x,y)=a(x)y^2, and
Q_a(s)=sum_j a(x_j(s))y_j(s)^2 over every zero, with multiplicity. The sum is locally finite, but its derivative sees every exterior zero. On simple intervals, write d=x_j-x_k, e=y_j-y_k and (x_t,y_t)=(x_k,y_k)+t(d,e). The proposed exact all-zero formula is

    Q_a' = 2 sum_{j<k}' K_a(z_j,z_k),
    K_a = [(f_y(z_j)-f_y(z_k)) e -(f_x(z_j)-f_x(z_k)) d]/(d^2+e^2)
        = integral_0^1 [2a(x_t)e^2-a''(x_t)y_t^2 d^2]/(d^2+e^2) dt.

The prime means the complete opposite-zero pairing specified by the full Hadamard dynamics, not arbitrary term rearrangement. The f_xy terms cancel before bounding. Although the first integral term is nonnegative, the curvature term is not. An absolute integral bound over all exterior chords is not automatically summable; the original endpoint expression and paired tail must be retained.

E08 is to prove or repair this statement, track the tail in a radius U tending to infinity uniformly on compact real time intervals and unbounded window centers via explicit parameter dependence, and test the sign on an exact real polynomial heat solution. Such a control shares the used heat dynamics and reflection where imposed; it need not share theta arithmetic. A negative result excludes monotonicity from that information class only.

## Classification, controls and baseline

Q_a=0 for every covering a at lambda=0 is RH, so it is not proposed as a new criterion. The calculus and a uniform bound are quantitative intermediate tools; the sign test is a scoped information-sufficiency question. Compared with M's weighted finite-cluster identity, the intended gain is exact cancellation of the mixed terms, removal of artificial small-gap denominators, and paid full-zero-index tails. It does not improve Polymath's real/simple positive-time benchmark exp(C/lambda).

The polynomial pair alone has Q'=4a when its center is stationary; its existence already shows positive production is compatible with collision. Adding real roots to one side tests horizontal weight flux. Smooth/reflected and positive-primitive controls share heat geometry; weighted-square controls need not have the global strip/count properties, so only local finite-cluster statements apply to them. Euler-factor controls preserve the generic paired-zero form when its convergence premises hold; none is claimed to meet an unproved canonical small-flux bound. Literal theta coefficients distinguish the canonical object but are not yet used to improve the sign.
