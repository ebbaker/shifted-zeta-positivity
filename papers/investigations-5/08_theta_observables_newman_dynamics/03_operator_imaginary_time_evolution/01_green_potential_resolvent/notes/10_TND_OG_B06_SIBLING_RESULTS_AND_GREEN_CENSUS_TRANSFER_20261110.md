The newer branches help OG in **finite acquisition and selection of
appropriate dynamical premises**. They do not yet supply an independent
all-height signed-log estimate or a collision-exclusion theorem.

There is a concrete finite reuse: the already certified OC census, with a
new Green tail estimate, implies
\[
 0\le J^{\rm G}_0(1500)
 <\frac{291}{65536}+2^{-157}<\frac1{200}.                 \tag{1}
\]
This is a consequence of stronger acquired zero-location information,
not new exclusion coverage or a signed-log computation. The proof uses
neither the value of the OC pair statistic nor its separation hypothesis.

problem.

Under avoid01, (1) is a **finite quantitative consequence of an inherited
certificate**. OC's smooth-statistic and OA's deflation statements are
acquisition/transfer theorems with paid hypotheses. No new RH-equivalent
criterion is proposed. A global vanishing reformulation is not a research

## 4. Focused transfer: a complete finite Green bound

### 4.1 Exact imported information

[OC Note22 §1][oc22] states that the complete census in
\[
 \mathcal R=(988,2012)+i(-3/2,3/2)
\]
has 388 occupied singleton cells. Every imaginary interval is exactly
\[
 [-3/32768,\ 3/131072].                                \tag{3}
\]
Counts include multiplicity. The accepted tree and geometry are recorded
in [OC exact record19][ocgeom]. [OC Review17][ocr17] assesses the original
outward-winding computation; [Review21][ocr21] explicitly inherits that
level when checking the later range execution. This note uses that
certification; it neither recomputes source balls nor replays the large
external contour trace.

Only completeness, the count and the upper endpoint in (3) are needed.
The horizontal gap, precise real coordinates and first moments are unused.
No imaginary interval is replaced by zero. At \(\lambda=0\) the canonical
strip \(|\Im z|\le1\) lies strictly within the vertical census boundaries.

The independent infinite-tail input is the explicit complete-source
estimate in [OC manuscript §17][oc17]:
\[
 \mathcal M<7,\quad H_0(0)>1/7290,\quad
 N_{\rm disk}(r)\le2r^2+40.
\]
It implies that the real unit slab starting at integer \(q\), restricted
to the canonical strip, contains at most
\[
 16(|q|+2)^2                                             \tag{4}
\]
labels. This loose bound avoids an unknown coarse counting constant.
The Gaussian projection's \(2^{-32}\) tail is not a Green tail and is
not used below.

### 4.2 Kernel majorants

Write \(a=\pi/8\), so \(3/8<a<2/5\). For \(0\le y\le1\), set
\(u=\sin(ay)\operatorname{sech}(at)\). Since \(0\le u\le\sin a<1\),
\[
 \mathcal G(t,y)=\operatorname{atanh}u
 \le\frac{u}{1-u^2}
 \le\frac{ay}{\cos^2a}\operatorname{sech}(at)
 <\frac y2\operatorname{sech}(at)\quad(y>0).             \tag{5}
\]
Indeed \(\cos^2a=1-\sin^2a>1-a^2>21/25\), hence
\(a/\cos^2a<10/21<1/2\). At \(y=0\) both sides vanish.
Consequently the two non-strict majorants
\[
 \mathcal G(t,y)\le y/2,\qquad
 \mathcal G(t,y)\le y e^{-a|t|}\le e^{-3|t|/8}            \tag{6}
\]
are valid throughout the canonical upper strip.

By the exact OG Green identity, \(J^{\rm G}_0(X)\) is the sum over
upper-half-plane labels only. Counting all 388 retained labels is a safe
overestimate, and (3), (6) give at \(X=1500\)
\[
 J_{\rm core}\le\frac{388}{2}\frac3{131072}
                 =\frac{291}{65536}.                  \tag{7}
\]
There is no extra conjugate-pair factor and no extra prime/pole term:
we are directly bounding the positive zero sum, not reassembling the
boundary-log formula.

Every omitted label has \(|x-1500|\ge512\). For each integer \(n\ge512\),
the two slabs at distance between \(n\) and \(n+1\) have, by (4), a
combined count at most \(32(1503+n)^2\). Endpoint overcounting is harmless.
Thus
\[
 J_{\rm tail}\le32\sum_{n=512}^{\infty}
                  (1503+n)^2e^{-3n/8}.                 \tag{8}
\]
Put \(n=512+j\). Then \(2015+j<2^{11}(1+j)\),
\(e^{-192}<2^{-192}\), and \(q=e^{-3/8}<4/5\).
The last inequality follows already from
\(e^{3/8}>1+3/8>5/4\). Therefore
\[
 \sum_{j\ge0}(1+j)^2q^j
 =\frac{1+q}{(1-q)^3}<225<2^8,
\]
and (8) is strictly less than
\[
 2^{5+22-192+8}=2^{-157}.                              \tag{9}
\]
Equations (7)–(9) prove (1). The small [exact record][exact] checks the
rational comparisons and input-file identities. It is bookkeeping for
this proof, not a fresh canonical evaluation or a winding certificate.

For comparison, the existing consumer with \(d=1/2,r=1\) has
\[
 m(1/2,1)=\frac{\sin(\pi/8)}{2\cosh(\pi/8)}
              >\frac1{16}>\frac1{200}.
\]
Here \(\sin(\pi/8)\ge1/4\) follows from the chord bound on
\([0,\pi/2]\), and \(\cosh(\pi/8)<2\).
The consumer is therefore met at this single center. But (3) already
confines every retained zero far more tightly and over a much larger
horizontal interval than that consumer excludes. No new exclusion
region follows. Neither a uniform-in-\(X\) conclusion nor arbitrarily
small depth thresholds follow from the fixed census.

## 5. What OA contributes to a continuation

For an actual isolated real pair under forward heat
\(h(\tau,z)=H_{\lambda_s-\tau}(z)\), OA uses
\[
 h=(z-a)(z-b)U,\quad g=U_z/U,\quad
 q=(b-a)^2,\quad c=(a+b)/2,
\]
\[
 q'=-8-4qB,\qquad c'=-g(a)-g(b),\qquad
 B=\frac{g(b)-g(a)}{b-a}.                              \tag{10}
\]
The useful contribution is a regular way of acquiring \(U,U_z,U_{zz}\):
shared-node second divided differences have derivative errors
\(\epsilon_2/2,\epsilon_3/6,\epsilon_4/12\), with no inverse-gap penalty.
At coalescence the deflated value is \(h_{zz}/2\).
See [OA deflation][oa2] and [source transfer][oa3].

This is worth retaining for a later **zero-aware acquisition** decision.
It is not plug-compatible with OG's value-error \(\delta\): derivative
errors through fourth order, genuine root/cluster information, and an
appropriate domain must be supplied. Nor does it by itself certify
logarithms on complex clusters.

OA's [survival theorem][oa4] requires a positive lower bound for the
deflated factor on the entire relevant parameter box and an **upper**
envelope \(B\le\beta\) throughout the time interval, together with
midpoint and upper-gap conditions. A terminal jet or a single Green value
does not supply these hypotheses. In particular, Green mass is zero for
real zeros, irrespective of multiplicity or separation.

The later [Lehmer direction assessment][oar6] retains a precise warning:
with real exterior zeros, a Lehmer upper bound on \(G_*\) gives
\(B\ge-G_*/2\), whereas survival needs an upper bound on \(B\).
The sign argument also depends on the reality of the exterior; it must
not be extended to arbitrary complex exterior zeros. The quartic control
has negative \(B\) and still collides: a favorable sign can delay the
collision without excluding it.

The low-box “redundant gap gate” result is likewise conditional on its
displayed sufficient inequality and domain; failure of that sufficient
condition does not prove that signed information is necessary.
The finite-pair pilot is parked in [OA Continuation11][oanext].
Neither this review nor the OC certificate supplies its required
positive-time actual pair/domain, a gain over matched direct tracking,
or a credible acquisition budget. Do not reopen it merely because (10)
has an appealing sign.

## 6. Collision controls: analytical premise screen

This review runs the [controls12][controls]/[avoid02][a02] screen
analytically. It is not a new numerical certification campaign. Generic
Green identities, smooth-statistic regularity and deflation may hold for
a colliding control; that is consistent with all results above.

| Control family | Exact premise unavailable for the theta application; consequence for the proposed reuse |
| --- | --- |
| Euler-factor controls, all indicated primes | The unchanged complete theta coefficients/conductor are absent. Shared positive-source and resolvent inequalities can still hold; they do not distinguish the control or supply its favorable Green value |
| Three-/four-square controls and finite theta-tail changes | Full normalized reflection of the complete theta square expansion is absent. Finite endpoints and positive source terms do not certify unchanged theta or all-real heat slices |
| Reflected super-Gaussian and smooth positive/primitive-compatible controls | The complete arithmetic square expansion is absent even when reflection, shape and primitive endpoint data are retained. Smooth collision regularity remains valid and does not exclude their events |
| Finite-prime orbit controls | Full source reflection is absent, although the prescribed finite prime identities and measurements hold. No objection to a paid infinite assembly follows |
| Atomic fixed-event and exact finite-profile controls, including continuous extensions | Unchanged theta coefficients, full profile growth and eventual simplicity at threshold are absent. Selected floors/moments do not pay the deflated-factor conditioning or the required force envelope |
| RH-conditional normalized endpoint controls | Ordinary Dirichlet-series arithmetic fails; do not incorrectly claim that their functional equation/completion fails. Real double zeros alone give no upper Green mass |
| Variable observation kernels | The full physical cosine readout/Newman equation fails; a finite cosine prefix is not that equation |
| Three-profile positive-measure relaxation | Integer divisor constraints and full physical source realizability are missing. Fractional slack measures are not a canonical zero configuration |
| Older collision kernels \(q,q_{T,a}\) | Their sources/transforms are not the literal theta/xi source. They cannot inherit its Euler anchor, prime baseline or source-error identity; their own generic acquisition is not a theta smallness estimate |
| SUSY smooth-density control | Replacing the discrete prime measure by \(e^r\,dr\) changes the arithmetic object despite retained archimedean data. The canonical prime baseline cannot be assigned to it |
| SUSY two-channel exclusion | This is a scoped exclusion for the specified norm-factorization architecture and support lengths, not a universal field counterexample. OG's signed logarithmic integral is outside that class |

For (1), the decisive extra input is the **complete certified thin
zero-location census**, not a property common to arbitrary positive
sources. The numerical enclosure (3), its complete count, and the
canonical count bound cannot be transferred to a control without
acquiring them for that control. A positive-depth zero in the consumer's
box would force \(J\ge m\), contradicting (1), so at least one of those
quantitative premises would fail there. A control may still satisfy a
valid finite bound on a different collision-free box. At an exactly real
collision Green mass vanishes: (1) is not a test of simplicity or survival.
These qualifications prevent the failed exact-source premise from being
mistaken for evidence that every derived inequality distinguishes theta.
The last three rows retain the source/class distinctions already checked
in [OG Note07 §5][og7] and routed by [positive-construction cluster04][positive].
