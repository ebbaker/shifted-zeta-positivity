**Primary source:** Pedro Caceres, *How long can an off-line zero survive
the de Bruijn-Newman heat flow? An explicit unconditional lower bound
for Lambda*, [Hexagon 2610.00207v1](https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00207v1),
12 October 2026, [13-page PDF](https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2610.00207v1).
This is an unreviewed related-work candidate, not an imported theorem.

The selected passages inspected were the abstract and Theorem 1.1,
the local-count qualification at the end of Lemma 3.2, the functional
and Proposition 5.2 in Section 5, and the collision-integration/order
argument in Section 6. The paper uses a sech weight times a compact
cutoff, upper-half-plane transverse powers, and growing order. It
claims a conditional-on-an-off-line-zero lower bound
\(\Lambda\ge\delta^2/(70\log\gamma\log\log\gamma)\).
The explicit local-count step states that its numerical sampling is
not an interval-arithmetic proof. No validation of that step or of
the paper's theorem was undertaken here.

This overlaps the present weighted-moment and collision-assembly
methods. It does not establish priority or equivalence of the results.
The present proof packets were developed without this paper and import
none of its estimates. A claim-level comparison remains a separate
task before making novelty claims. No third-party PDF, source archive,
or numerical output was downloaded into the repository.
