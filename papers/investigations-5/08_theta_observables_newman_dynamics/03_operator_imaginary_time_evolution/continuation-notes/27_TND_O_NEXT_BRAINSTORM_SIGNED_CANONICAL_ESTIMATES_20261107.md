\[
H_0(z)=\frac18\xi\!\left(\frac12+\frac{iz}{2}\right),\qquad
H_\lambda(z)=\frac12\int_{\mathbb R}e^{\lambda u^2}\Phi(u)e^{izu}\,du,
\qquad \partial_\lambda H=-\partial_z^2H.
\]
The source is the complete, even, normalized theta source. A reflected finite prefix is an integral approximation, not a replacement for its full reflection/endpoint identities. On \(0\le\lambda\le1/2\), canonical zeros \(z_j=x_j+iy_j\) satisfy \(|y_j|\le1\).

### A. Gaussian derivative defect: a paid collision signal

For normalized Gaussian variance \(v>0\),
\[
A_n(\lambda,X,v)=\int_{\mathbb R}w_v(x-X)
\big[(H_\lambda^{(n+1)}(x))^2-H_\lambda^{(n)}(x)H_\lambda^{(n+2)}(x)\big]dx.
\]
Note24 pays the exact quadratic complete-source formula, the factorial all-order majorant, both time directions of the order-series identity and explicit joint derivative-order, theta-index and radial errors, uniformly in real center. These are absolute acquisition estimates, not signed smallness.

At a real multiplicity-\(m\) event \((\lambda_*,x_*)\), let \(a_m=\partial_x^mH_{\lambda_*}(x_*)\ne0\), \(t=\lambda_*-\lambda>0\), and \(v=\kappa t\), \(0<\kappa<2\). Then
\[
A_{m-2}(\lambda_*-t,x_*,\kappa t)
=-\delta_\kappa |a_m|^2t+\mathcal E_m(t),\qquad
\delta_\kappa=1-\kappa/2.
\]
The explicit error \(E_\kappa\) in Note24(26) uses \(C=M_{m+1}(L)/2\), \(D=M_{m+2}(L)/2\) and tracks every finite \(m\). It pays the full Gaussian line, with no omitted analytic background. A sufficient new signed input would be
\[
A_{m-2}\ge-c|a_m|^2t,\qquad
0\le c<\delta_\kappa,\qquad
E_\kappa(t)<(\delta_\kappa-c)|a_m|^2t.
\]
No such input is proved. The ratios \(C/|a_m|\), \(D/|a_m|\) and their products cannot be suppressed in a moving-height or growing-multiplicity regime. The pure hierarchy uses \(v'=2\), whereas resolving windows have \(v'=-\kappa\). They cannot be identified. First-level positivity misses the cubic control; finitely many derivative checks do not close the hierarchy.

### B. Green potential: the preferred arithmetic interface

Let \(a=\pi/8\), \(K(x)=[16\cosh(ax)]^{-1}\), and
\[
G(d,y)=\operatorname{atanh}\frac{\sin(ay)}{\cosh(ad)}.
\]
Note25 proves the complete positive identity
\[
J_\lambda(X)=\sum_{y_j>0}G(X-x_j,y_j)
=K*\{\log|H_\lambda(\cdot)|+\log|H_\lambda(\cdot+8i)|\}(X)
-\log|H_\lambda(X+4i)|.
\]
Each boundary kernel has mass \(1/2\). Complete product limits, real-boundary multiple-zero logarithms, both exponential tails and absence of an extra harmonic term are paid. The statistic is scalar-invariant. A zero with \(y_j\ge d>0\), \(|x_j-X|\le r\) forces
\[
J_\lambda(X)\ge\frac{\sin(a)d}{\cosh(ar)}.
\]
The acquired upper allowance is only \(O(\log(3+|X|))\). Continuity through collisions does not imply the transverse moment's Lipschitz property: a real double event contributes at square-root scale.

At **time zero only**, Decision15 and E17 give
\[
J_0(X)=\mathcal L(X)-P(X)+G(X,1),
\]
\[
\mathcal L(X)=\int_{\mathbb R}K(x-X)\log|\zeta(1/2-ix/2)|\,dx,
\quad
P(X)=\sum_{n\ge2}\frac{\Lambda_{\rm vM}(n)}{\log n}
\frac{n^{-5/2}}{1+n^{-4}}\cos\!\left(\frac X2\log n\right).
\]
The symbol \(\Lambda_{\rm vM}\) is von Mangoldt, not the Newman threshold. The **plus** \(G(X,1)\) term pays zeta's pole at1. For **integer** \(N\ge2\), the complete prime tail is at most \(\frac23N^{-3/2}\), uniformly in all real \(X\). The Euler product is not assigned to \(\lambda\ne0\).

Positivity already implies the **lower** comparison \(\mathcal L\ge P-G\). The missing exclusion direction is instead a sufficiently accurate upper comparison, for example
\[
\mathcal L(X)<P(X)-G(X,1)+\frac{\sin(a)d}{\cosh(ar)}.
\]
A useful new estimate must have errors strictly inside this same defect margin. For varying \(X,d,r\), retain the height factor in Note25's boundary tail and the separate prime tail. An absolute approximation to \(H\) near real zeros is not a relative approximation to its logarithm.
