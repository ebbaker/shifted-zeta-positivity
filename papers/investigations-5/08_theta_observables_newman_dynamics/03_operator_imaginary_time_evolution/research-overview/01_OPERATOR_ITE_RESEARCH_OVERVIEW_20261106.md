## Scientific question and author clarification

Choose an evolved field Ψ, a function/Hilbert space, a Hamiltonian or
generator K, a domain and a fixed observation R such that

\[
\partial_\tau\Psi_\tau=-K\Psi_\tau,\qquad
\mathcal R\Psi_\tau=H_{\lambda_0-\tau}.
\]

For a time-independent observation this requires the correctly typed
intertwining relation

\[
\mathcal R K=(-\partial_k^2)\mathcal R
\]

on the declared common domain. A time-dependent observation needs its
additional derivative term explicitly. These equations specify the
representation problem; they are not a new collision criterion.

The author's meaning of Hamiltonian is imaginary-time evolution. A lack
of real-time unitarity is therefore not a failure of this objective.
Nonunitary flow need not have a non-self-adjoint Hamiltonian: a
self-adjoint lower-bounded K can generate heat evolution. For each proposed
operator, its actual realization, inner product, lower bound, time
direction and flow domain must nevertheless be established.

The motivation is a natural operator explanation of Newman evolution on
the source, scalar generating field or a justified encoding in the full
geometric model. A physical/geometric explanation is an eligible outcome
when identified as such; a signed estimating advantage is a separate claim.
Measurement and conditioning are optional later interpretations, not a
requirement or a substitute for selecting this generator.

## Fixed background and normalization

Keep the complete canonical source and its smooth reflection:

\[
F_\lambda(u)=e^{\lambda u^2}\Phi(u),\qquad
H_\lambda(z)=\int_0^\infty F_\lambda(u)\cos(zu)\,du,\qquad
H_0(z)=\xi((1+iz)/2)/8.

[recorded passage inspection](../../references/01_TND_E03_R2_SOURCE_USE_20261106.md); no main zero theorem is imported.

Let X multiply by u and set L=1−∂²_u in the angular-constant scalar channel,
with LGλ=8Fλ. The already established dictionary is

| Object | Increasing Newman parameter | Decreasing Newman parameter, λ=λ₀−τ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source Fλ | ∂λFλ=X²Fλ | ∂τF=−X²F |
| Real cosine transform Hλ(k) | ∂λHλ=−∂²_kHλ | ∂τH=∂²_kH |
| Scalar field Gλ | ∂λGλ=BGλ, B=L⁻¹X²L | ∂τG=−BG |

The identity −∂²_k cos(ku)=u²cos(ku) connects the first two rows.
Parent Note02 (38)–(40) also gives, on its common Schwartz class,

\[
BG=u^2G+L^{-1}(4uG'+2G),\qquad
\mathcal T G(z)=\frac{1+z^2}{16}\int_{\mathbb R}G(u)e^{izu}\,du,
\quad \mathcal T G_\lambda=H_\lambda.
\]

The raw G integral is initially paid only in |Im z|<1; the source transform
supplies the entire continuation. Its boundary cannot be handled by
multiplying a divergent integral by 1+z². B's algebraic conjugation alone
does not establish self-adjointness in ordinary L², a global semigroup
there, or equivalence of all proposed norms. Existence of every finite
power on the canonical family is not an order-uniform theorem.

The canonical source family exists for every finite real λ by its
double-exponential decay. Increasing λ gives an unbounded multiplier
e^(δX²) on unrestricted L² for δ>0. This is the inherited class-specific
limitation, not an exclusion of imaginary-time formulations on suitable
spaces. Initial research should name finite λ₀/τ/k or complex-strip
ranges. The count branch's physical slab and detector clock are not
automatically imposed on this branch.

## Relation to the geometric cylinder

The shared model is the complete radius-1/2 spherical cylinder, with

\[
\mathcal D_C=-i\Gamma\partial_v+2D,\qquad
L_0=\mathcal D_C^2=-\partial_v^2+4D^2.
\]

Retain both angular Dirac signs, all shells n≥1 and the full continuous
radial line whenever a claim concerns this full model. The spectrum and
maximal-domain comparison are inherited from parent Note02 §§1–3 and
shared Review01 §3. Camporesi–Higuchi v1, (3.40), (3.54),
(5.35)–(5.36), is the recorded primary angular input, through the
[parent reference guide](../../references/README.md).

Ordinary L₀ heat multiplies the radial Fourier amplitude in shell n by
e^(−τ(k²+4n²)); it does not differentiate that amplitude in k. The original
spinor model, the scalar G field and the source F are different objects.
Choosing an encoding or a new interaction must explain their relationship
rather than identify them by notation. A scalar-only result is permitted
with that scope; it must not be advertised as a full spinor realization.

The angular Green response recovers the integer-square theta sum, but the
scan β(u)=πe^(4u), source processing and spatial placement u=v remain
supplied in the inherited construction. Full reflection uses DLMF 20.7.32
at zero theta argument and lattice parameter iR², R>0, as recorded in
[the shared source-use receipt](../../references/01_TND_E03_R2_SOURCE_USE_20261106.md). A new intrinsic preparation
