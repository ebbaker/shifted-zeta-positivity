## What to retain

The author's follow-up prompted [a notation clarification][note] (§0):
the observation center X is distinct from a pair midpoint and from the
critical-point curve between two real roots. B10's time-position curve
is prescribed independently of those pair curves. The slow-motion example
is conditional, not a canonical velocity estimate. Studying
`J_lambda(m(lambda))` while following a pair is a legitimate separate
question; B10 does not automatically bound an average along that curve.

- B10 remains at `c=1`, block-constant `P_U=floor(L_U²)`,
  `U^(17/81+rho) <= ell <= U`, fixed `0<rho<=47/162`, and an unevaluated
  large-height onset. Its physical time is fixed inside each spatial window.
- The uniform regulator transfer implies
  `(J_(1/L_X)(X)-C/L_U)_+³ <= (27/128) R_(P_U)(X,1)`.
  Its interval integral is `O_rho(ell/L_U)`. This is an elementary
  corollary, not newly acquired arithmetic information.
- Fixed-height persistence must exceed a constant times `ell/L_U`,
  with that constant depending on the chosen height and B10's constants.
  A lower width much larger than `ell/L_U` would avoid comparing
  unevaluated constants. Bounded-width persistence does not suffice.
- One fixed-time zero of depth `y` has exact spatial Green mass `pi*y`.
  Its signal decays exponentially, and its cubic mass is comparable to
  `y³`. A zero surviving in heat time can still make only a narrow
  spatial peak along the moving observation curve.
- The explicit even quartic in the persistence note satisfies backward
  heat evolution, has an actual integer zero multiset and a finite
  all-real threshold, and retains an isolated positive peak along
  `X -> (1/L_X,X)` while satisfying far smaller Green averages.
  It lacks the canonical theta source. This screens only the generic
  heat/strip/Green-average class.
- Enlarging the strip spreads the signal but reduces its height; its
  first mass stays `pi*y`. No growing-strip version of B10 is available.

## Suggested next conceptual task

**Height and time dependent bootstrap, subsequent author proposal:**
[Note13](13_GNP_RS_HEIGHT_AND_TIME_DEPENDENT_STRIP_BOOTSTRAP_20261113.md)
distinguishes the allowed-zero envelope \(a_n(\lambda,X)\) from the

The classical uniform strip contraction preserves
\(\lambda+A^2/2\); a canonical gain beyond it remains unproved.
An exact even polynomial screens only pointwise substitution of a
variable width into that global law. For continuation, compare the
elementary inward extension \(m/(2M)\) with a complete Green or contour-count
implementation on the same height and time block. Pay the initial
observation buffer, all boundaries and all field/logarithmic errors before

**Author-proposed alternative, 13 November:** [Note12](12_GNP_RS_LOWER_TRANSVERSE_OFFSET_AND_LOG_OBSERVATION_20261113.md)
examines lowering the transverse observation offset b, keeping the strip
thickness eight initially. It gives explicit single-zero margins at
b=4, 2 and 5/4, a depth-scaled signal calculation, and the exact signed
correction needed when regularizing the center logarithm. A bounded next
comparison could rederive the complete costs at b=2 and b=5/4 before
entering the possible-zero strip or letting b shrink with a prescribed
depth. This is discussion and feasibility work, not a completed new
arithmetic estimate. Use zeta height T=|X|/2 and transverse offset b
consistently; the two are different coordinates.

First decide what extra canonical information could force **spatial mass**
from an assumed off-axis zero. A proof of ordinary continuity, a bounded
zero velocity or temporal survival will not supply the needed scale.
Two concrete alternatives deserve comparison:

1. Derive a constraint from the assumed zero on the **complete** arithmetic
   arm that forces a quantitative surrounding residual or many distinct
   nearby defects. Specify the actual interval and lower mass, and compare
   it with `ell/L_U` before extending the argument. Check integer cutoff,
   reflection, full-field errors, clock and all omitted coefficients.
2. Investigate a genuinely local residual estimate whose total allowance
   tends to zero. At the existing mean rate, an interval `ell=o(log U)`
   together with uniform bounded-scale persistence along the trajectory
   would exclude a fixed-height peak. This is far beyond merely improving
   the present positive power of U. Other improvements to the residual
   rate could change this comparison and are not excluded.

A useful bounded deliverable is one explicit lemma with its parameter
range and a mass comparison, or a scoped explanation of why the selected
lemma cannot follow from its premises. Do not promote the desired
amplification inequality itself to a result. No height scan can supply
uniform exclusion. A future proof must separately address shrinking
depths, the full c-band, bounded heights and time zero if those are claimed.

For any derivative route, retain
`dJ/dX = partial_X J - partial_lambda J/(X L_X²)` and account for
collision singularities. Use cluster symmetric coordinates when individual
root derivatives fail, but do not infer uniform bounds from a local Taylor
expansion with event-dependent constants. Moving the observer past a
nearly stationary zero is the basic kinematic issue even away from collisions.
