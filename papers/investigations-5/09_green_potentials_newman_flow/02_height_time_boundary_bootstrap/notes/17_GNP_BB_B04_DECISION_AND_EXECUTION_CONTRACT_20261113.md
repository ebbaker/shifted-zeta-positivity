## 1. Why this route is ready

[Note14][pairs] proves a strict finite-tail allowance using five fixed
classical exponent pairs for each actual logarithmic phase. Its target
\(y_*=567/1000\) lies below both the prior acquired width and the
particular A-chain allowance threshold:
\[
 \frac47-y_* =\frac{31}{7000},\qquad
 \frac{2927}{5152}-y_* =\frac{727}{644000}>0.
\]
The source class changes from separate powers using the A-chain alone
to a fixed wider A/B family; the canonical field, clock and absolute-head
comparison remain fixed. Thus E03's exact threshold is retained within
its original class and is not a lower bound for this wider class.

This is an estimate for the actual phase before taking each block's
absolute value. It is still a separate-block allowance. It acquires
neither the joint signed correlations used by the Green alternative
nor an optimal envelope over all classical exponent pairs. Only the
five displayed pairs enter E07. Note14's deeper exploratory searches
are outside its finite replay and supply no premise here. No additional
pair search or field scan is authorized by this decision.

The improvement is numerically modest and its smaller proof power
\(\kappa=1/200000\) makes the sufficient symbolic onset very large.
The value of this bounded successor is that it crosses the *scoped*
A-chain threshold using imported phase estimates and complete existing
providers. This decision does not assert a practical finite-height gain.

**Common time.** Reuse the proved positive-window provider of
[Note12 §2][e05], preserving its order of constants and evidence level.
Fix the published far-tail constant \(C_P\), then
\(C\ge\max(8,C_P)+1\), \(D=C+1\), the Gaussian window, enlarged
charts and provider constants, then the new comparison constants.
For \(1\le tL_x\le C\), \(0<t\le1/2\), \(y_*\le y\le9/8\), prove
\[
 |H_t/C_- -\zeta(s_t)|\le At+B_D(1+L_x)x^{-\kappa}.
\]
After all constants and height gates \(x_0\), choose finite \(L_*\) with
\[
 L_*\ge\max\{2,1/\kappa,4224A\},\quad 4\pi e^{L_*}\ge x_0,

\(At\le A/L_*\); no extra factor \(C\) is needed in this payment.
The decreasing tail factor for \(L_x\ge L_*\) pays the other half
of the relative-error allowance \(1/64\). Use the initial strip,

Polymath all-real theorem as E05 applies because
\(x\ge4\pi e^{C/t}>e^{C_P/t}\). Verify the closed join. The desired

This implication is the E07 **proof target**, not a theorem of B04.

\(X+\sqrt{1-y_*^2}\ge4\pi e^{1/T}\), and whatever intermediate-time
portion lies in the proved tail. The initial bounded-space exclusion
and the barrier portion with \(u<1/L_x\), including time zero, remain
unpaid. A direct final-time compact exclusion is an alternative missing
input; neither is supplied by compactness alone.

Note16's cutoff-only comparison \(T+8/49>1/5\) concerns **width 4/7**
and does not transfer to \(y_*\). Indeed
\(y_*^2/2=321489/2000000\) and \(y_*^2/2+1/27<1/5\).
However, the sufficient E07 recipe requires \(L_*\ge200000\), so its
guaranteed exterior cannot align with the finite initial verification
range audited in Note16. This is a limitation of the present sufficient
recipe, not a lower bound on the true onset or a general impossibility
of improving a Newman bound. No numerical campaign or tractable compact
gap follows from it.
