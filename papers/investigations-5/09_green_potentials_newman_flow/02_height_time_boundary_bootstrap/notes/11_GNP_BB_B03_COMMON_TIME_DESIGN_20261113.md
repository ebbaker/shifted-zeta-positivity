Read the [result index][results] and [avoid index][avoid], specifically
[cluster11][r11], [controls12][r12], [classification01][a01] and

a global strip statement and supplies no compact-region certificate.

## 1. The gap that has to be filled

E01–E03 use t=1/L_X with the time frozen on each local contour. Their
statements cannot simply be relabeled as fixed-time theorems. At a
fixed t, however, the same pointwise finite-arm calculation suggests
uniformity on

    1 <= t*log(x/(4pi)) <= C,

for each fixed C. Increasing c=tL_x strengthens both weighted-tail
power exponents: their c derivative is -v(1-v)/4<0. Low-head
heat removal costs A*t rather than A/L_X. Amplitude variation has
constants depending on C. These observations identify a new contract;
they do not extend the inherited provider's printed c<=8 domain.

## 2. A finite provider-window extension is the needed new input

The positive-time proof of HDA CS6 uses a bounded tilted Gaussian
center, originally at most 2+o(1) because tL<=8. For c<=C this
becomes C/4+o(1). Choose a new fixed Gaussian window after C and the
base-strip growth constants, enlarge the zero-time charts, and recheck
every exterior payment. The near exterior exponent is

    -v^2/t + |v|L/2 + c_b|v| + c_b v^2/x.

A fixed window proportional to C and c_b, and with its square large
relative to C times the polynomial growth exponent, can dominate this
by -v^2/(4t). The far-ray proof is still uniform for t<=1/2.
Local gamma Taylor and exact-to-elementary mode errors become O_C(1/x)
per mode; the full positive population remains O(E_o*x^(1/4)).
The finite correction and the initial remainder require their own
Gaussian payments as before. E05 must prove all of this, not replace
8 by C in a citation or edit the original HDA theorem.

This extension concerns positive times and j=0 only. It does not
request negative-time windows or differentiated field statements.

## 3. Join to the actual published outer theorem

On 13 November the primary [Polymath v2 Theorem 1.5(i)][poly] was
checked. It states, for 0<t<=1/2, that zeros with x>=exp(C_P/t)
are real, for a sufficiently large absolute C_P. Its normalization
H_0=xi((1+iz)/2)/8 agrees by the functional equation. Retain C_P
as an unevaluated but finite constant. Take C>=max(8,C_P)+1.
The new finite-c estimate and the published theorem then overlap:
the provider window supplies 1<=tL_x<=C, and tL_x>=C implies
x>=4pi*exp(C/t)>exp(C_P/t). There is no unpriced gap at their join.

For y*=4/7 and the finite six-pair route, the intended new bound is

    |H_t/C_minus - zeta(s_t)| <= A_C*t+B_C(1+L_x)x^(-kappa),

with kappa>0 fixed. Choose t0>0 after these constants and after all
finite onsets, so x>=4pi*exp(1/t), 0<t<=t0 pays the independent
reciprocal-zeta factor and a relative error below one. Combine
nonvanishing on y*<=y<=9/8 with the initial global width at time t.
Together with the published far tail this would give

    0<t<=t0, |x|>=4pi*exp(1/t), H_t(x+iy)=0 ==> |y|<4/7.

The cutoff is common to all points of the spatial tail at that t.
It escapes to infinity as t decreases. It cannot establish a
time-zero strip, exclude zeros in the missing compact interval,
or improve Lambda without those additional obligations. The wider
published all-real tail retains attribution; only the closer
transition range and its joining argument are the proposed gain.
