\section{Toward an economical all-region hierarchy}
\label{sec:frontier}

\subsection{A precise objective}
The eventual objective is a family of explicitly evaluable descriptions
of the same canonical field and physical observables, indexed by a
finite accuracy choice. Each member should carry its domain, positive
envelope, jets, residual, joining terms and evaluation budget. Increasing
the choice should produce a proved improvement with understandable
constants. The desired endpoint is a short formula for the error as
well as for the approximation.

This objective is a quantitative approximation programme. It is not
an RH-equivalent criterion, a new positivity implication, or a claim
to exclude collisions. Heat identities and finite recurrences apply
to much broader classes. The quantitative canonical inputs used here
are the exact theta source, its arithmetic completion, the gamma
factor and its paid sectorial estimates. Finite-product, cosine and
collision controls do not satisfy that complete canonical source
premise; their existence nevertheless prevents using the common
shape or heat equation alone as a zero-location argument.

\subsection{The next gains, in dependency order}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Name and compare the switch centers.}
Use the compact $P_4$ field theorem to compare the linear, full-saddle
and corrected-saddle centers under the unchanged regulator. State the
implicit equations, prove a lower bound for the time derivative on a
common interval, and compare displacement with the actual
$1/(X\log X)$ rounding width. The existing frozen request
\texttt{HDA-A03-AN01} owns this first step.
\item \textbf{Transfer a fixed positive order physically.}
Start with $P_2$ or $P_4$, not a growing order. Prove corrected phase,
collar and unit bounds, the weighted primitive estimates, direct time
and residual consumers, and the actual covariance mean product.
Report the resulting powers for each observable separately. A gain
in the field exponent with no physical gain is still useful evidence
about the transfer bottleneck, but is not completion of this task.
\item \textbf{Move the negative endpoint toward zero.}
Execute the frozen recurrence request \texttt{HDA-R05-AN08} on
$L^{-1}\le T\le2$, tracking every $\tau$ dependence and norm loss.
Only after that result is known should a strong through-zero bridge
be frozen. Preserve the existing weaker signed crossover meanwhile.
\item \textbf{Fill the remaining positive and compact regions.}
The existing small-positive request and the bounded-height compact core
have different inputs. A new uniform moving-time theorem must pay its
own arithmetic population, source pieces and residual. Compact-time
constants cannot be reused with an upper endpoint growing in height.
\item \textbf{Improve fixed-time arithmetic precision.}
At fixed positive time, gamma corrections eventually encounter the
retained arithmetic allowance $X^{-t\log2/4}$. To surpass that bound,
retain finitely many additional ordinary Dirichlet modes with their own
saddles and bound the full remaining population. This is a proposed
direction, not a theorem or a claim that the displayed exponent is sharp.
\item \textbf{Reduce conditioning and expose order dependence.}
Complete the weak-primitive filter consumer before claiming uniform
height cost. Separately derive usable $J$-dependent constants for the
positive hierarchy before selecting $J=J(t,X)$. Compare a proposed
improvement with an existing numerical exponent, allowance or cost;
additional formal coefficients alone are insufficient.
\item \textbf{Reassemble and audit.}
Replace components of Theorem~\ref{thm:global} only after their complete
consumer contracts are proved. Sum regional and joining budgets without
normalization changes. An independent audit of the inherited proof chain
and a complete implementation remain distinct milestones.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{What an improved theorem should report}
A result is ready for the Book manuscript when it states: the canonical
normalization and unchanged physical data; the exact order of choices;
the full buffered domain; the positive error envelope; every retained
arithmetic and contour payment; spatial and direct time errors; the
actual model residual; and the specific quantity improved relative to
the present theorem. If it is an evaluation theorem, it must additionally
state input representation, guards, provider costs and dependence on
height, time, order and requested bits.

The current synthesis already offers a useful division: the positive
and negative recurrences explain how to improve local models, while the
source construction explains how to maintain finite global coverage.
The outstanding work is to make their interfaces equally transparent.
That is the sense in which this is a programme toward a proof from
``The Book.''
