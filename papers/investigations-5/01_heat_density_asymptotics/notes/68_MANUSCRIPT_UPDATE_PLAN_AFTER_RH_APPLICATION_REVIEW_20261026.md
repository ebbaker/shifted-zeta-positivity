## 4. What to say about the applications

A short, clearly sourced discussion in the Book's frontier is enough.
Detailed application proofs remain in Investigation 5/02; B02 does not
create an applications manuscript or execute its next research request.

Use RT Note02's actual improvement. For one fixed contour Gamma of length
p and real interior interval J at distance at least b, assume an
independently established contour floor m, errors delta0<m and delta1,
and model derivative bound M1. The note obtains

\[
\kappa=\min\left\{
\frac{p}{\pi b^2}\frac{\delta_1+M_1\delta_0/m}{m-\delta_0},
\frac{2p}{\pi b^3}\left[-\log(1-\delta_0/m)\right]\right\}
\]

as an upper allowance for the derivative of the difference of the
exterior drifts. For ordered raw energy E_K, its exterior-term error
is at most `2 kappa E_K`, evaluated on the same true roots. Common
motion cancels; no internal minimum gap is needed for this exterior
comparison. At fixed normalized microscopic contour data, Note26 gives
kappa=O(mathcal L² epsilon_X), hence an O(epsilon_X) integrated relative
error coefficient on a time step O(mathcal L^-2).

These are conditional gains in the error-payment rule. The required
canonical contour floor, cluster count/location, initial energy and
model exterior payment have not been acquired. A contour floor controls
the exterior unit; it does not establish an interior first-jet floor or
individual-root simplicity. Rouché preserves total multiplicity, and the
projection compares model drift at true roots. Model-root dynamics would
add the actual heat residual and inverse-slope costs.

Keep the renormalized exterior allowance distinct from `2 kappa E_K`;
RT Note02 (12) gives the correct different sum. Raw-energy Gronwall
retains an RT-sized reference baseline and cannot replace the corrected
additive propagation. The source's dyadic boundary selection, reference
strip, shrinking index interval and integrated errors remain obligations.
Note02 §8 records local source-display/payment cautions; any use of them
must preserve their targeted audit status and PDF/HTML numbering.

The exact pair `z²-2s` and `z²-2s-epsilon` has zero exterior drift and a
uniform enclosing contour margin, but relative ordered-energy error
`epsilon/(2s+epsilon)` tends to one at collision. This scopes the
inference: excellent exterior control does not prove endpoint
nonconcentration. No RH, new Newman upper bound, simplicity theorem or
new zero-exclusion region follows from the reviewed bounds.

For present priorities, lower-margin acquisition and a matched complete
