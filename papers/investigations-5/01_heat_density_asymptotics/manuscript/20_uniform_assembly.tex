\section{Effective assembly on fixed time intervals}\label{sec:uniform-assembly}
We now turn the transfer identities into complete finite estimates and
then remove the shrinking-time restriction. The argument has three
steps: make the collar and primitive constants finite, follow one phase
gap, and control the regulator where its Gaussian coefficient vanishes.
This section consolidates Notes~47--49 \cite{n47,n48,n49}; the large
constant tables are recorded there, while the mechanisms and their
consumption are derived here.

\subsection{The common comparison contract}
Fix $\eta>0$, $\ell=h=Y=1$, source and convolution buffers through
$|\Im z|\le4$, and the analytic field buffer $|\Im z|\le5$.
For the actual field $F$, denote filtered total curvature by
$\mathcal C_F=K*(C_{{\rm reg},F}+T_{C,F})$ and put
$\mathcal B_F=K*\mathcal B_{\epsilon,F}$,
$\mathcal S_F=K*\mathcal S_{\epsilon,F}$. The finite list of scalar
comparisons, denoted below by $\mathcal E_{\rm tup}(F,H)$, consists of
\begin{equation}
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{quantity compared with its value for }H&\text{spatial orders}\\ \hline
 U,W&0\text{--}4\\
 R,\mathcal C,J,Q,\mathcal B&0\text{--}2\\
 U_t,W_t,R_t,\mathcal C_t,\mathcal S&0\text{--}2\\
 K*T_U\quad(\text{zero for }H)&0\text{--}4
\end{array}
\label{eq:ua-contract}
\end{equation}
Real and imaginary current components are counted separately. Current
order three is also bounded internally to pay source order two.
The comparison controls $\Delta Q$, both curvature derivatives in
\eqref{eq:sj-equations}, and fixed traces within these orders. It uses
the actual covariance of $F$. A scalar target $\theta$ means that
\emph{each} component has that allowance; a sum in an equation inherits
the sum of its component allowances. No second time derivative or
moving observation boundary is included.

\subsection{Finite collars and a complete fixed-gap budget}
Write $L_x=\log(2+x)$ on the positive exterior. The effective field
theorem, the exact model derivatives, and complex Stirling with its
sector remainder give, on $[95,97]$ beyond the stated binary onsets,
\begin{equation}
 \Theta_x\ge L_x/8,\quad |\Theta_z|\le0.51L_x,\quad
 |\Theta_{zz}|\le5/(4x),\quad
 |(\log\mathcal A)_z|\le2,
 \label{eq:ua-geometry}
\end{equation}
where $P=2\mathcal A\cos\Theta$. For a declared $U\ge2^{2050}+8$,
choose the first phase gap by
\begin{equation}
 n=\left\lceil\Theta(96,U)/\pi\right\rceil,\qquad
 \Theta(96,X)=n\pi,\qquad X\ge U.
 \label{eq:ua-first-gap}
\end{equation}
Monotonicity gives uniqueness and $0\le X-U\le8\pi/L_U<1$.
Take $\sigma=(64L_X)^{-1}$ and
$\tau=(4096L_X^2)^{-1}$. The real part of the phase stays within
$1/8$ of $n\pi$ on the enlarged gap for $|t-96|\le\tau$.
Thus $|P|\ge A/2$, and a comparison error at most $A/4$ leaves
the common segment floor $A/4$.

The finite collar argument is worth recording because a field estimate
alone does not bound a signed current near a zero. At a real model root
$c$, put $u=L_c(z-c)$ and $A_c=|S_t((1+ic)/2)|$. On $|u|\le2$,
integration of \eqref{eq:ua-geometry} and the sine quotient gives
$P/A_c=uB(u)$ with $1/16\le|B|\le4$. If the local relative error is
$e\ge10^6/c^2$, Rouch\'e's theorem first on $|u|=2$, then on
$|u|=32e$, pairs a unique simple real $H$ zero with $c$.
Dividing out each own zero and using the maximum principle gives
\begin{equation}
 H/A_c=(u-e_0)C(u),\qquad
 |e_0|\le32e,\quad 1/32\le|C|\le5,\quad |C-B|\le128e.
 \label{eq:ua-units}
\end{equation}
The slopes are at least $A_cL_c/16$ and $A_cL_c/32$.
For $h_F=F_{zz}/F$, subtract its pole at its own zero:
$h_F=v_F/(z-a_F)+g_F$. The same analytic disks give
\begin{equation}
 |v_P|\le5,\quad |v_H|\le8,\quad
 |v_H-v_P|\le2^{18}eL_c,\quad
 |g_H-g_P|\le2^{20}eL_c^2.
 \label{eq:ua-poles}
\end{equation}
For example $v_P=2(\log\mathcal A)_z+\Theta_{zz}/\Theta_z$ at a
model zero. Cauchy's formula on the larger disks bounds the removable
regular parts. Disks of radius $2/L_c$ are disjoint; each enlarged
support meets at most $4L_x$ collars. The complement has
$|P|/A\ge1/256$ and $|H|/A\ge1/512$.
The standard complex-analysis inputs are
\cite{DLMF110Coarse2026,DLMFCauchy1931,DLMFGammaOuter2026}; the finite
contours and constants are the EB1--EB4 table of Note~47.

To estimate a restricted exterior integral, enlarge each intersecting
collar before integrating the nonnegative primitive. In scaled variables,
translation of a regularized simple pole costs
$Ce(1+|\log e|)$: remove the disk $|u|\le2|e_0|$, then integrate
$C|e_0|/|u|^2+Ce/|u|$ radially on its complement. Interpolate the unit
by $B\exp(s\log(C/B))$ with the physical $q/A_c^2$ held fixed at
each point. Restore area $L_c^{-2}$, the pole factor $L_c$, and the
collar count. With $l=\log(2+x)$, the finite allowances are
\begin{align}
 e&=2^{44}(x-8)^{-2},&d&=2^{20}(x-8)^{-3},\notag\\
 \mathfrak s_O[1],\mathfrak w_O[1]&\le2^{26}e,&
 \mathfrak v_{m,O}[1]&\le2^{44}e(1+|\log e|+l^2),\notag\\
 \mathfrak z_{m,O}[1]&\le2^{22}d&& (m=0,1,2).
 \label{eq:ua-raw}
\end{align}
These are raw integral bounds for every restricted portion, not
whole-support signed estimates applied to a truncated test.

For small carrier-relative regulator, retain
$B_\Lambda,H_\Lambda,Z_\Lambda$ from \eqref{eq:sj-weighted}. Finite
versions of those bounds are
\begin{equation}
 \mathfrak w_O[a]\le2^{25}a^*B_\Lambda,\quad
 \mathfrak v_{m,O}[a]\le2^{37}a^*H_\Lambda,\quad
 \mathfrak z_{m,O}[a]\le2^{33}d a^*Z_\Lambda\quad(m=1,2).
 \label{eq:ua-weighted}
\end{equation}
They follow from the radial integrals of
$b^2/(|u|^2+b^2)$ and $b^2/(|u|(|u|^2+b^2))$, with $b=32\Lambda$.
On $[95,97]$, $q\le Q_+e^{-x^2/2000}$, $Q_+=16\eta^2+e^{220}$,
and each carrier is at least $e^{-x}$; hence one may use
$\Lambda\le\sqrt{Q_+}e^{-x^2/8000}$ beyond a finite
$\eta$-dependent onset. Every polynomial regulator factor remains
attached to its weighted primitive. Signed stress is reduced to simple
poles before taking magnitudes.

For completeness, the weak exterior heat allowance through order two is
\begin{align}
 D_{{\rm out},\alpha}\le\frac d{2\pi}\bigl\{
 &2^{22}(d_\alpha+2c_1k_{\alpha+x}+c_2k_\alpha)\notag\\
 {}+{}&2^{33}Z_\Lambda[2R_1(k_{\alpha+x}+c_1k_\alpha)
                  +(R_2+3R_1^2)k_\alpha]\bigr\}.
 \label{eq:ua-signed-heat}
\end{align}
This is the signed residual formula with the complete test partition
\eqref{eq:sj-partition}; it is not a bound for $\int|D_E|$.
The complementary shoulder includes the model residual $E_P$.

Finally, a fixed central regulator floor and the exterior integrable
pole estimates give a finite, source-independent $C_H$ with
$|\partial^\gamma U_H|\le C_H(1+\log(2+|x|))$, $1\le|\gamma|\le3$.
After first paying $k_\gamma\mathfrak s<1$, use
$M_\gamma=C_H(1+\log(2+R_c))+1$ on core and seam supports in
\eqref{eq:sj-covariance}. This order avoids a circular mean-product
estimate and avoids a growing-core floor in the exterior decay rate.
Substitution of the finite tables into
\eqref{eq:sj-tuple}--\eqref{eq:sj-source} yields, for each nonsource
column, the envelope
\begin{equation}
 C_\eta\{X^{-2}\log^6(2+X)+(1+X)^{16}e^{-X^2/32000}\}.
 \label{eq:ua-fixed-budget}
\end{equation}
Here $C_\eta$ is the explicit finite expression in Note~47 EB16--EB21.
Its source coefficients and binary onset pay arbitrary positive scalar
targets. The powers and onset are deliberately conservative. This
completes the finite fixed-gap budget, but its width still tends to zero.

\subsection{Follow the gap, keeping its width fixed}
The gap must be moved by its own phase equation; a root-speed bound
alone is insufficient. Fix its index and solve
\begin{equation}
 \Theta(t,X(t))=n\pi,\qquad X(t_0)=X_0.
 \label{eq:ua-moving-gap}
\end{equation}
On the real axis, the imaginary part of the exact formula
\eqref{eq:fi-time-cap} gives
\[
 |\Theta_t|\le\log(x)/4+3/x+388|\alpha|/x^2
              \le\tfrac12\log(2+x).
\]
Indeed $|\Im\alpha|<1$ and $|\Re\alpha|\le\log(x)/2+4/x$,
so $|\Im(\alpha^2/4)|\le\log(x)/4+2/x$. The other two exact
terms are paid by $|\alpha'|\le2/x$, $|\alpha''|\le8/x^2$ and
$|B|\ge1/2$. Thus
\begin{equation}
 X'=-\Theta_t/\Theta_x,\qquad |X'|\le8.
 \label{eq:ua-speed}
\end{equation}
Local inversion and this bounded displacement continue the same gap
through the whole compact interval. For $[95,97]$, take $t_0=96$,
$R_c=X_0+24$, $L=\log(2+R_c)$ and freeze $\sigma=(64L)^{-1}$.
Then $|X-X_0|\le8$ and one source on the union buffer suffices.
The real phase remains near $n\pi$; its imaginary part need not be
small because $|\cos\Theta|/\cosh(\Im\Theta)\ge|\cos\Re\Theta|$.

Put $D_0=P-G$ and
$\lambda(t,x)=b_*((|x|-X(t)+\sigma/2)/\sigma)$.
On either band let $v_b=\sgn(x)X'(t)$. Since $\sigma'=0$,
\begin{equation}
 \lambda_t=-v_b\lambda_x,\quad
 \lambda_{xt}=-v_b\lambda_{xx},\quad
 \lambda_{xxt}=-v_b\lambda_{xxx}.
 \label{eq:ua-cutoff-time}
\end{equation}
Direct differentiation of the actual field yields
\begin{align}
 E={}&\lambda E_P+\lambda_x(D_0)_z+\tfrac14\lambda_{xx}D_0
                                  +\lambda_tD_0,\notag\\
 E_z={}&\lambda(E_P)_z+\tfrac12\lambda_xE_P
       +\lambda_x(D_0)_{zz}+\tfrac34\lambda_{xx}(D_0)_z
       +\tfrac18\lambda_{xxx}D_0\notag\\
      &+\lambda_t(D_0)_z+\tfrac12\lambda_{xt}D_0,\notag\\
 B_t={}&\tfrac12\lambda_x(-(D_0)_{zz}+E_P)
                              +\tfrac12\lambda_{xt}D_0,\notag\\
 (B_z)_t={}&\tfrac14\lambda_{xx}(-(D_0)_{zz}+E_P)
       +\tfrac12\lambda_x(-(D_0)_{zzz}+(E_P)_z)\notag\\
       &+\tfrac14\lambda_{xxt}D_0+\tfrac12\lambda_{xt}(D_0)_z.
 \label{eq:ua-moving-defects}
\end{align}
These use $(D_0)_t=-(D_0)_{zz}+E_P$. They require no fourth source
jet, $X''$, or second time derivative. Keep component, spatial, and
moving contributions to $E$ separately until they have all been paid.
The exact nonholomorphic identities apply unchanged with these actual
$B,E$. Regional primitive bounds are summed at each time; they are not
differentiated. Likewise the moving exterior test partition is used in
an instantaneous spatial pairing, so its time derivative is not a term
of that pairing.

With the substitutions of Note~48 MW11--MW16, each nonsource column is
bounded by the finite expression
\begin{equation}
 B_{\rm mov}(X_0)=C_{\rm mov}
 \left\{\frac{L^6}{(X_0-24)^2}
 +(1+X_0+24)^{16}e^{-(X_0-24)^2/64000}\right\}.
 \label{eq:ua-moving-budget}
\end{equation}
Here $C_{\rm mov}=C_\eta^2$ with the finite $C_\eta$ above. Refine
the same source throughout this interval so its core column is at most
$\min(\theta/4,B_{\rm mov})$ and its band jets meet
\eqref{eq:sj-source-gap}. Each scalar error is then at most
$3B_{\rm mov}=O_\eta(\log^6X_0/X_0^2)$, uniformly on $[95,97]$.
Holding the source core reserve at a fixed quarter-target would not
prove this vanishing bound. The family has one fixed source for each
choice of $X_0,\theta$, not one source for all $X_0$.

\subsection{The two estimates that control the switch}
To extend to $J=[63,97]$, first extend the field theorem itself. In its
contour proof the upper-time constants remain those at $97$; the changed
lower-time reductions, for $\lambda_0=\log x\ge1000$, are
\[
 -63\lambda_0^2/100+(194/3)\lambda_0+6\le-\lambda_0^2/2,
 \quad63\lambda_0/200>3,
 \quad48e^{97/2-63\lambda_0/10}\le e^{-3\lambda_0}.
\]
The central, connector, middle, arithmetic and infinite-end estimates
therefore retain the same four jet constants and binary onsets.
This explicitly extends Theorem~\ref{thm:effective-outer}, rather than
using its $[95,97]$ statement outside its domain. Complex Stirling also
gives $e^{-x}\le|S_t((1+ix)/2)|\le e^{-x/4}$, with the lower bound
valid arm by arm on the full complex buffer.

Write the exact physical regulator as
\begin{equation}
 r=a(t)-\beta(t)x^2,\qquad
 a=2[(1-\chi)\ell_b+\chi\ell_+],\quad
 \ell_b=\log\epsilon_b,\quad \ell_+=\log(M_0/\kappa).
 \label{eq:ua-switch-r}
\end{equation}
In particular
$a'=2[(1-\chi)\ell_b'+\chi\ell_+'+\chi'(\ell_+-\ell_b)]$;
the amplitude mismatch is part of the time estimate. On $J$ the real
source saddle gives $36<\kappa<618$, $0<\kappa'<50$ and
$e^{-3}<M_0<e^{110}$. Put $A_\eta=512(1+|\log\eta|)$.
Then $|a|,|a'|\le A_\eta$, $0\le\beta\le1/36$ and
$|\beta'|\le1/8$.

\begin{lemma}[Switch flatness and arbitrary-regulator transfer]
\label{lem:ua-switch}
On $[63,97]$, with the value zero understood at $\beta=0$,
\begin{equation}
 |\beta'|\le4\beta\{1+\log^2(1/\beta)\}.
 \label{eq:ua-flatness}
\end{equation}
On a common segment floor $|F_s|\ge c_*A$ and with
$\delta_0=|F-H|/A$, the gap weight difference satisfies
\begin{equation}
 |w_F-w_H|\le
 \min\left(1,\frac{2(q/A^2)\delta_0}{c_*^3},
                    \frac{\delta_0}{2c_*}\right).
 \label{eq:ua-weight-branch}
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For $64<t\le97$, set $v=(t-64)/64$. The exact switch gives
$\chi'/\chi=(1-\chi)[v^{-2}+(1-v)^{-2}]/64$ and
$\log(1/\beta)\ge\log36+v^{-1}-64/31\ge v^{-1}$.
Together with $\kappa'/\kappa<50/36$ these prove
\eqref{eq:ua-flatness}. Flat matching proves it at and below $64$.
For the last branch in \eqref{eq:ua-weight-branch}, differentiate the
radial function $q/(|f|^2+q)$:
\[
 |\nabla_f w|=\frac{2q|f|}{(|f|^2+q)^2}
       =\frac{2w(1-w)}{|f|}\le\frac1{2|f|}.
\]
Integrate along the field segment. The small-$q$ branch follows by
instead using $|\nabla_fw|\le2q/|f|^3$.
\end{proof}

Split the \emph{estimates} on a support of large center height $u$ into
$\beta u\le16$ and $\beta u>16$. In the first case, monotonicity of
$s(1+\log^2(1/s))$ gives
\begin{equation}
 |r_t|\le A_\eta+2^{10}u\log^2(2+u),\quad
 |r_x|\le64,\quad |r_{tx}|\le2^{10}\log^2(2+u).
 \label{eq:ua-switch-weights}
\end{equation}
The raw bounds \eqref{eq:ua-raw} and the last branch of
\eqref{eq:ua-weight-branch} now give vanishing time errors.
In the second case the actual carrier-relative regulator obeys
\[
 \Lambda\le\sqrt{Q_+}
   e^{u+32-\beta(u-8)^2/2}\le\sqrt{Q_+}e^{-2u}
\]
beyond a finite onset. Use \eqref{eq:ua-weighted}, retaining each
polynomial regulator weight. No field is split at $\beta u=16$,
and no derivative of that proof-case boundary occurs.

The largest new band-time cost is visible directly in
\eqref{eq:sj-time-curvature}. Its finite ratio substitutions give
\begin{equation}
 \pi|(T_C)_t|\le2^{160}\mu L^2(L^2+|r_t|).
 \label{eq:ua-worst-row}
\end{equation}
The band area is at most $16\sigma=1/(4L)$. In the first regime
this costs $O_\eta(\mu uL^3)=O_\eta(L^3/X_0)$.
In the second regime each $r_t$ factor retains $w$ or $w(1-w)$,
and so is exponentially suppressed. A constant bound on $\beta'$
alone would give a nonvanishing $\mu X_0^2$ cost; flatness is essential.

\subsection{Finite construction and uniform theorem}
Choose the phase at $t_0=64$. On $J$, $|X-X_0|\le264$.
Set $R_c=X_0+300$, $a_X=X_0-300$, $L=\log(2+R_c)$ and again
freeze $\sigma=(64L)^{-1}$. Use relative band allowances
$\mu_o=2^{52}/a_X^2$, $\mu_s=a_X^{-2}$, and residual allowances
$d=d_1=2^{24}/a_X^3$. The new compact floor is
\begin{equation}
 e_c=E_{\rm sw}e^{-R_c^2/72},\qquad
 E_{\rm sw}=\min((2/5)\eta e^{-4},e^{-3}/618).
 \label{eq:ua-switch-floor}
\end{equation}
The earlier exponent $452$ was specific to $[95,97]$ and is not used
here. A fixed central floor at
$R_*=\lceil2^{2056}(1+Q_++A_\eta+|\log\eta|+\eta^{-2})\rceil$,
joined to the integrable exterior poles, supplies a finite constant
$C_{H,\rm sw}$ for the common logarithmic mean cap. Note~49 SW21
gives its explicit expression in the source moments.

Let $P_2$ be the source second-moment cap plus one and set
\begin{align}
 Q_\eta&=1+Q_++A_\eta+|\log\eta|+\eta^{-2},\notag\\
 C_{\rm sw}&=2^{8192}(1+C_{H,\rm sw}+Q_\eta+A_\eta+P_2)^{64},\notag\\
 B_{\rm sw}(X_0)&=C_{\rm sw}
       \{L^8/a_X+(1+R_c)^{20}e^{-a_X}\}.
 \label{eq:ua-switch-budget}
\end{align}
Each nonsource column is at most $B_{\rm sw}$: the first regime uses
\eqref{eq:ua-raw}, \eqref{eq:ua-switch-weights} and
\eqref{eq:ua-worst-row}; the second uses the exponential scale and
the weighted bounds. The remaining logarithms, finite Leibniz sums
and common mean products fit the displayed constant and powers.
On pure exterior supports retain the local height until taking its
decreasing supremum. There is no time-chart count multiplying the error.

For each core row let $D_r$ be its finite coefficient after factoring
$4\varepsilon/e_c^2$ from the positive table
\eqref{eq:sj-curvature-ledger}--\eqref{eq:sj-source}. Use the new
regulator coefficients of Note~49 SW9 and include the auxiliary
mean-error rows first. With $\rho_* =\min(\theta/4,B_{\rm sw})$, impose
\begin{equation}
 0<\varepsilon\le
 \min\left(1,e_c^2\min_{D_r>0}\frac{\rho_*}{4D_r},
                    a_X^{-2}e^{-X_0-320}\right).
 \label{eq:ua-source-tolerance}
\end{equation}
The clipped positive source constructor gives four absolute jet errors
at most $29\varepsilon/64$. Its proof uses only $e^{tu^2}\le e^{97u^2}$,
so it applies for every $t\le97$ on its transverse source buffer;
no lower-time restriction is needed for this absolute field statement.
The compact observable floor is used only on the stated finite domain.

\begin{theorem}[Switch-uniform all-height approximation]
\label{thm:uniform-switch}
For every $\eta>0$ and $0<\theta\le1$, a finite phase choice, frozen
width and one finite nonnegative source give an actual field
$F=G+\lambda(P-G)$ on $[63,97]$ such that every scalar component of
\eqref{eq:ua-contract} has error at most
\begin{equation}
 3B_{\rm sw}(X_0)\le3\theta/4
 \quad\text{for every }x\in\R,\ |y|\le1,\ t\in[63,97].
 \label{eq:ua-switch-theorem}
\end{equation}
The envelope is $O_\eta(\log^8X_0/X_0)$ under the simultaneous source
refinement \eqref{eq:ua-source-tolerance}. All component, spatial and
moving heat terms, compatibility terms and actual covariance are retained.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the phase inverse, geometry and exact moving derivatives above in
Theorem~\ref{thm:sj-transfer}. The preceding two-regime proof pays each
nonsource column by $B_{\rm sw}$; the source tolerance pays the core
column by $\rho_*\le B_{\rm sw}$. Auxiliary primitive-gradient bounds
justify the common mean cap before it is used in covariance coefficients.
One explicit onset is Note~49 SW25: choose $X_0$ as the first phase gap
beyond $2^m$, where
\begin{align*}
 m\ge\max\{&16384+\lceil\log_2(R_*+1024)\rceil
                 +\lceil8\log_2(2+C_{\rm sw}/\theta)\rceil,\\
             &\lceil16\sqrt{A_\eta+16}\rceil,
               \lceil\log_2[4e^{80}(1+A_\eta)+1024]\rceil\}.
\end{align*}
Then $a_X\ge2^{m-1}$, $L\le m+3$,
$C_{\rm sw}\le\theta2^{m/8}$,
$(m+3)^8\le2^{m/2}$ and $e^{-a_X}\le X_0^{-64}$.
These inequalities pay $B_{\rm sw}<\theta/4$ and all collar and
regulator gates. Sum the three columns. This is a finite sufficient
construction, without a claim of affordable evaluation or filtering.
\end{proof}

The full-saddle crossover center is the exact root of
$a(t)/2-\beta(t)u^2/2-\log|S_t((1+iu)/2)|=0$.
Along the seam its derivative additionally contains
$-[\beta X+\partial_x\log|S_t|]X'$.
It is distinct from the earlier linear-carrier center in
Section~\ref{sec:gamma-outer}. No displacement between these two
centers is asserted. The uniform estimate above treats every time
directly and needs no fitted or truncated inverse-logarithmic center.

The finite onset and source tolerance establish analytical existence.
The spare quarter-target is available for future arithmetic and continuous
filtering; it is not an executed certificate. The theorem does not yet
attach the positive infinite-time endpoint.
