\section{Temporal identities and the negative attachment}\label{sec:temporal-cover}
The spatial construction now covers a fixed interval across the physical
switch. To attach an endpoint, agreement of averaged observables is not
enough: the time cutoff acts on the fields themselves. Note~50
\cite{n50} supplies the exact temporal contract and a finite prescription
on all $t\le97$. These identities are then consumed by the effective
positive attachment in Section~\ref{sec:effective-positive}.

\subsection{Exact field and compatibility mismatches}
Let $F_i$ be smooth fields on a common full buffer,
$B_i=(F_i)_{\bar z}$, $E_i=(F_i)_t+(F_i)_{zz}$, and
$v(t)=b_*((t-a)/\tau)$. Put $D_0=F_2-F_1$ and $D_B=B_2-B_1$.
Then $\|v'\|_\infty\le c_b/\tau$, $c_b=2e^{4/3}$, and the
actual field $F=(1-v)F_1+vF_2$ satisfies
\begin{align}
 B&=(1-v)B_1+vB_2,&
 E&=(1-v)E_1+vE_2+v'D_0,\notag\\
 E_z&=(1-v)(E_1)_z+v(E_2)_z+v'(D_0)_z,\notag\\
 B_t&=(1-v)(B_1)_t+v(B_2)_t+v'D_B,\notag\\
 (B_z)_t&=(1-v)(B_1)_{zt}+v(B_2)_{zt}+v'(D_B)_z.
 \label{eq:tc-exact}
\end{align}
The direct $F_t,(F_z)_t$ have the corresponding mismatch terms.
No $v''$ is consumed by \eqref{eq:ua-contract}. On a zero-free
support put
\[
 d_0=|D_0|/|F|,\quad d_1=|(D_0)_z|/|F|,\quad
 d_B=|D_B|/|F|,\quad d_{Bz}=|(D_B)_z|/|F|.
\]
The added allowances for $(e_0,v)$, $(e_1,a_t^*)$, $b_t^*$ and
$h_t^*$ in \eqref{eq:sj-envelopes}--\eqref{eq:sj-time-curvature}
are respectively $|v'|d_0$, $|v'|d_1$, $|v'|d_B$ and $|v'|d_{Bz}$.
Observable comparison cannot supply these field mismatches: $F_2=-F_1$
has the same individual potential, density, current and covariance,
but its halfway blend vanishes. This is a control on the inference,
not a permitted modification of the canonical source.

\begin{proposition}[Signed temporal heat with nonholomorphic compatibility]
\label{prop:tc-variation}
Let $\delta_{D_0}$ denote variation $F\mapsto F+sD_0$ with $q$ fixed.
For $E_T=v'D_0$ and
$Z_{m,T}=w^mE_T\overline F/(|F|^2+q)$, every real compact spatial
test $\psi$ satisfies
\begin{align}
 4\pi\langle D_{E_T},\psi\rangle={}&
 2\Re\int Z_{0,T}\Delta\psi
 -4\Re\int Z_{1,T}\nabla r\cdot\nabla\psi\notag\\
 &-2\Re\int[Z_{1,T}\Delta r
              +(Z_{1,T}-2Z_{2,T})|\nabla r|^2]\psi\notag\\
 &-4\pi v'\int(\delta_{D_0}T_C)\psi.
 \label{eq:tc-signed-heat}
\end{align}
On the segment floor, the additional variation is bounded by
\begin{align}
 \pi|\delta_{D_0}T_C|\le{}&
 \vartheta(2b_0d_B+4b_0^2d_0)\notag\\
 &+2(d_{Bz}+3b_1d_0+d_1b_0+A_1d_B+6A_1b_0d_0).
 \label{eq:tc-variation-bound}
\end{align}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Vary $\Delta S=4\pi(\rho+C_{\rm reg}+T_C)$ at fixed $q$.
For the direction $E_T$, $\delta S=2\Re Z_{0,T}$,
$\delta w=-2\Re Z_{1,T}$, and
$\delta[w(1-w)]=-2\Re(Z_{1,T}-2Z_{2,T})$.
Moreover $\delta\rho=D_{E_T}$ by
\eqref{eq:sj-current-defects}. Vary the weak regulator curvature,
move the Laplacian onto $\psi$, and retain the variation of $T_C$;
this gives all signs in \eqref{eq:tc-signed-heat}.
For the bound, use
$\pi T_C=q|B|^2/D^2+2\Re(B_z u-F_zBu^2)$ with
$D=|F|^2+q$ and $u=\overline F/D$.
The product rule with $|u|\le1/|F|$ and
$|\delta u|\le3d_0/|F|$ yields
\eqref{eq:tc-variation-bound}.
\end{proof}
The last line of \eqref{eq:tc-signed-heat} vanishes in holomorphic
regions, but is necessary on a spatial joining band. The physical source
$\mathcal S=(1-2w)(r_t\rho+\nabla r\cdot\mathbf j)$ contains no
explicit $v'$. Its occurrence in $\rho_t$ cancels the same term in
$D_E$ before estimating the source balance. Direct time and heat-potential
rows still require the mismatch payment. Actual $Q_F$ is computed from
the blended field, never averaged from $Q_{F_1},Q_{F_2}$.

At zeros one can instead use a positive floor $\sqrt q\ge e$.
For absolute mismatches $D_0^*,D_1^*$ and $|F_z|\le M_1$,
\begin{equation}
 \pi|D_{E_T}|\le|v'|
       (2M_1D_1^*/e^2+4M_1^2D_0^*/e^3).
 \label{eq:tc-floor-heat}
\end{equation}
The rational formula for $T_C$ similarly remains smooth through multiple
zeros. These estimates are used where the physical regulator has a
global floor, not on a late-time unbounded height tail.

\subsection{A paid negative overlap}
Let $C=\Phi(0)\sqrt\pi/2$, $q_0=\eta C$, and write the exact
Gaussian heat field as
\begin{equation}
 N(t,z)=\frac C{\sqrt{-t}}e^{z^2/(4t)}\qquad(t<0).
 \label{eq:tc-negative-model}
\end{equation}
Choose $T\ge1$, $I_-=[-2T,-T]$ and
$v_-=b_*((t+2T)/T)$. Both $N$ and the fixed positive-source field
$G$ solve the heat equation. Their blend has $B=0$ and
$E_-=v_-'(G-N)$.

The complete-source Taylor bound of the negative endpoint gives, on
the full $|y|\le4$ buffer,
\[
 |\partial_z^j(H-N)|\le g_jT^{-(j+3)/2},\qquad
 g_j=\tfrac12 B_2^\Phi J_{j+2}(4),\quad
 J_m(4)=\int_0^\infty v^m e^{-v^2+4v}\,dv.
\]
These constants are finite by the explicit Gaussian recurrence used in
Section~\ref{sec:negative}; the global source bound is $B_2^\Phi$.
Choose the same $G$ to have four absolute jet errors
$\varepsilon\le T^{-5/2}$ for $-2T\le t\le97$ and every real height.
Then
\begin{align}
 |H-F_-|&\le(g_0+1)T^{-3/2},&
 |H_{zz}-(F_-)_{zz}|&\le(g_2+1)T^{-5/2},\notag\\
 |E_-|&\le c_b(g_0+1)T^{-5/2},&
 |(E_-)_z|&\le c_b(g_1+1)T^{-3}.
 \label{eq:tc-negative-jets}
\end{align}
Here $\epsilon_g\ge q_0/(2\sqrt T)$, $|r_t|\le1/T$, and all
spatial regulator derivatives vanish. Thus the pointwise primitives of
the compact transfer table have finite allowances
\begin{equation}
 s,w\le S/T,\quad v_m\le V_m/T^2,\quad z_m\le Z/T^2,
 \qquad s_t,w_t,c_t=O(T^{-2}),
 \label{eq:tc-negative-primitives}
\end{equation}
where $S=2(g_0+1)/q_0$, $Z=c_b(g_0+1)/q_0$ and
\[
 V_m=(g_2+1)/q_0+4(1+m/2)p_2(g_0+1)/q_0^2,
 \quad p_2=h_2+g_2+2,\quad h_j=\Phi(0)J_j(4)+g_j.
\]
The direct time coefficients are $2V_0+S+2Z$,
$S+2V_1+2Z$, and $S+2V_1+4V_2+6Z$ respectively.
Actual mean-gradient caps are $m_\gamma/\sqrt T$, with
$m_\gamma=2(h_1+1)\min_{i:\gamma_i>0}\|K_{\gamma-e_i}\|_1/q_0$.
They pay the actual mean-product subtraction in $Q$.

Define $K_-$ as the maximum of the resulting finite coefficients in
the compact transfer, source and heat-potential rows, including internal
current order three. It is an explicit finite sum/product rule in
$q_0,g_j,h_j,c_b$ and the kernel norms; Note~50 TJ10--TJ13 specifies
each substitution. Because every power is $T^{-p}$ with $p\ge1$,
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal E_{\rm tup}(F_-,H)\le K_-/T,\qquad
 T\ge\max(1,4K_-/\theta)
 \label{eq:tc-negative-budget}
\end{equation}
pays the overlap by $\theta/4$ per scalar. The same enlarged constants
pay the pure negative endpoint for $-t\ge2T$.
The weaker overlap rate does not replace the sharper pure-Gaussian
rates of Theorem~\ref{thm:negative-evolution}.

\subsection{One source from the negative overlap to the switch}
Use $G$ alone on $[-T,63]$. Its global absolute jet comparison and
the spatially uniform floor
\begin{equation}
 e_{\rm mid}=q_0/[1+\max(T^2,63^2)]^{1/4}>0
 \label{eq:tc-middle-floor}
\end{equation}
give a finite full-tuple coefficient $K_{\rm mid}(T)$ multiplying
$\varepsilon$. Impose
$\varepsilon\le\theta/[4(1+K_{\rm mid})]$.

To introduce the spatial saddle only on its proved branch, use on
$[63,64]$
\begin{equation}
 v_0=b_*(t-63),\qquad F_0=G+v_0\lambda(P-G).
 \label{eq:tc-preswitch-join}
\end{equation}
The flat product extends by zero into the inner region, where $P$ need
not be defined. At $63$ it is $G$ and at $64$ it is the moving spatial
field. Throughout this overlap $\beta=0$ and $\epsilon_g\ge q_0/9$.
The complex-buffer carrier bound gives
$A\le2e^{5\log(2+|x|)-|x|/4}\le e^{-|x|/8}$ at the exterior onset.
Differentiating the actual cutoff product and using its source error,
the required spatial and first-time jet mismatches are bounded by
\begin{equation}
 \delta_{
 \rm br}=2^{64}L^3(\varepsilon+e^{-a_X/8}),\qquad a_X=X_0-300.
 \label{eq:tc-bridge-jets}
\end{equation}
Terms initially carrying $L^4$ retain the field remainder $a_X^{-2}$;
$L/a_X^2\le1$ pays the extra logarithm. Source-error terms consume at
most three cutoff derivatives.

A finite coefficient converting these mismatches to the full tuple can
be specified without excluding a core zero. Freeze the moment caps
through three at time $97$, a common jet cap $M$, the floor $e=q_0/9$,
and $q\le Q=16\eta^2$. Expand the exact rational observables into
monomials $c q^a\prod_i u_i^{n_i}/D^b$, $N=\sum_i n_i$.
A coefficient for their field variation is
\begin{equation}
 |c|\max(1,Q^a)
 \{NM^{N-1}e^{-2b}+2bM^{N+1}e^{-2b-2}\},
 \label{eq:tc-rational-coefficient}
\end{equation}
with the first term omitted when $N=0$; for $\log D$ use $1/e$.
This follows by telescoping numerator factors and using
$|\delta D|\le2M\delta$. Sum over the finitely many monomials,
apply kernel norms through four, and include the actual mean-product
coefficients. Their maximum and $1$ define $K_{\rm br}$.
Then each scalar error is at most $K_{\rm br}\delta_{\rm br}$.
First enlarge $X_0$ so the exponential part is below $\theta/32$;
then require $\varepsilon\le\theta/(2^{69}K_{\rm br}L^3)$.
Note~50 TJ19 gives an explicit binary sufficient choice. These demands
are compatible with \eqref{eq:ua-source-tolerance} by refining the same
finite source. No inverse field or simple-core-root hypothesis is used.

\begin{theorem}[Finite prescription on the negative-to-central domain]
\label{thm:negative-central-cover}
For every fixed $\eta>0$ and $0<\theta\le1$, finite choices of
$T,X_0$, the cutoff width, and one nonnegative finite source give the
following smooth field prescription on all real heights and $t\le97$:
\begin{equation}
 F(t,z)=
 \begin{cases}
 N(t,z),&t\le-2T,\\
 (1-v_-)N+v_-G,&-2T\le t\le-T,\\
 G,&-T\le t\le63,\\
 G+v_0\lambda(P-G),&63\le t\le64,\\
 G+\lambda(P-G),&64\le t\le97.
 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:tc-domain-cover}
\end{equation}
Every scalar component of \eqref{eq:ua-contract} has error at most
$\theta/4$, uniformly for $x\in\R$, $|y|\le1$, $t\le97$.
At negative infinite time the prescription is exactly $N$ and retains
the complete vanishing errors of the negative endpoint theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose $T$ by \eqref{eq:tc-negative-budget}. Choose $X_0$ by
Theorem~\ref{thm:uniform-switch} at target $\theta/4$ and enlarge it
for \eqref{eq:tc-bridge-jets}. Choose one source satisfying the minimum
of the negative, middle, bridge and switch tolerances. All are strictly
positive finite demands and their source weights have upper time $97$.
The five estimates just proved pay the five rows of
\eqref{eq:tc-domain-cover}; their union is exactly
$(-\infty,97]\times\R_x$. Flat cutoffs give smooth matching. At any
time only its actual field is compared, so errors are not multiplied by
the number of rows. All defects in \eqref{eq:tc-exact} and
\eqref{eq:ua-moving-defects} are retained in the tuple.
\end{proof}
This is a mathematical formula with proved errors, not an entire exact
heat field. It is nonholomorphic on the spatial bands, and its algebraic
density has no asserted zero-count interpretation there. Parameters and
source may change with $\theta$. The theorem does not prove useful
computational cost or certify arithmetic and continuous averaging.

\subsection{The positive attachment contract}
For a spatial construction $F_c=G+\lambda(P-G)$, the positive temporal
blend must be taken at the field:
\begin{equation}
 F_+=(1-v_+)F_c+v_+P
   =G+[v_++(1-v_+)\lambda](P-G),\qquad
 v_+=b_*((t-T_+)/\Delta_t).
 \label{eq:tc-positive-join}
\end{equation}
The added holomorphic-core primitive is
\begin{equation}
 |v_+'|\int_{\Omega_p\cap\mathrm{core}}
 a\,w_F^m\frac{|(P-F_c)\overline F|}{|F|^2+q},\qquad m=0,1,2.
 \label{eq:tc-unpaid-primitive}
\end{equation}
This was the unpaid consumer in Note~50. Section~\ref{sec:effective-positive}
now pays it directly from a simple-root field floor, retaining $O(e)$
for the unweighted primitive and the physical weights in its consumers.
Observable agreement alone cannot supply the required field mismatch.
On spatial bands the last line of \eqref{eq:tc-signed-heat} remains.
After the finite late constants select $T_+$, the new compact table in
Section~\ref{sec:all-time} pays the entire intervening interval.
