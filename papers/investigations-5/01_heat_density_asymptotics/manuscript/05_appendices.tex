\section{Explicit weighted source allowances}\label{app:source}
Here are finite inputs for \eqref{eq:momentbounds}, extending the
complete-source argument in BW10 Note~20 \cite{bw20,n12}.
Put $d=1/4$, $c=1/(4e)$ and $h_0=\kappa^{1/12}$.
Require
\begin{equation}
 L\ge d,\quad \kappa\ge32,\quad t/\kappa\le c,\quad
 q_t(L-d)\ge q_t(0).
 \label{eq:sourcegates}
\end{equation}
These hold eventually. Define
\[
 Q_L=16e^{-3\pi e^{4(L-d)}},\quad
 d_L=\frac3{2\pi}e^{-4(L-d)}+\frac{Q_L}{1-Q_L},\quad
 e_c=e^{(4e/3)\kappa^{-1/4}}(1+d_L)-1.
\]
For any fixed nonnegative integer $p$ (here $p\le6$ suffices) and fixed $Y\ge0$, set $\lambda=Y/\sqrt\kappa$,
\begin{align}
 G_p(Y)&=\int_\R|w|^pe^{-w^2/2+\lambda|w|}\,dw,\notag\\
 D_p(Y)&=e_cG_p(Y)
 +\int_{|w|>h_0}|w|^pe^{\lambda|w|}
            (C_be^{-cw^2}+e^{-w^2/2})\,dw\notag\\
 &\hspace{15mm}
 +C_b(L\sqrt\kappa)^{p+1}e^{YL-c\kappa d^2}. \label{eq:Dp}
\end{align}
All integrals are Gaussian tails.
With the additional gate $Z_-=\sqrt{2\pi}-D_0(0)>0$, put
\begin{equation}
 A_p(Y)=\kappa^{-p/2}\frac{G_p(Y)+D_p(Y)}{Z_-},\quad
 e_\mu=\kappa^{-1/2}\frac{D_1(0)}{Z_-},\quad
 \widehat B_3(Y)=4e^{Ye_\mu}\{A_3(Y)+e_\mu^3A_0(Y)\}.
 \label{eq:allowances}
\end{equation}

To verify the bounds, extend by zero below $w=-L\sqrt\kappa$ the
rescaled source
\[
 B_t(w)=e^{q_t(L+w/\sqrt\kappa)-q_t(L)}
                         b(L+w/\sqrt\kappa).
\]
On $|w|\le h_0$ it differs from $e^{-w^2/2}$ by at most
$e_ce^{-w^2/2}$. Indeed, with $v=w/\sqrt\kappa$,
\[
 q_t(L+v)-q_t(L)
 =tv^2-\pi e^{4L}(e^{4v}-1-4v),
\]
and the cubic exponential remainder is at most
$(4e/3)\kappa^{-1/4}$ on this central region; the theta factor
differs from one by at most $d_L$.
For $v\ge-d$ the remaining near-left and right source is bounded
by $C_be^{-c\kappa v^2}$, using
$e^{4v}-1-4v\ge8e^{-1}v^2$ on $[-d,0]$ and
$\ge8v^2$ on $[0,\infty)$, together with \eqref{eq:sourcegates}.
On $0\le u\le L-d$, $q_t$ has no interior maximum:
$q_t''$ strictly decreases, so before the selected maximum at $L$,
$q_t'$ has at most a local-minimum zero.
The maximum on this interval lies at an endpoint; the last gate
in \eqref{eq:sourcegates} picks $L-d$.
The far-left source is thus bounded by $C_be^{-c\kappa d^2}$.
These estimates also cover the missing Gaussian tail of the half-line.

Multiplying by $|w|^pe^{\lambda|w|}$ and integrating gives
\[
 \int_\R |w|^pe^{\lambda|w|}
             |B_t(w)-e^{-w^2/2}|\,dw\le D_p(Y).
\]
Normalization is bounded below by $Z_-$.
The odd Gaussian mean is zero, which gives the smaller signed-mean
allowance $e_\mu$, rather than $A_1(0)$.
The variance is at most $\E(u-L)^2\le A_2(0)$.
Finally $|u-\mu|\le|u-L|+e_\mu$ and
$(a+b)^3\le4(a^3+b^3)$ give $\widehat B_3$.
This proves \eqref{eq:momentbounds}. For fixed $Y$,
$D_p(Y)=O(\kappa^{-1/4})$, yielding \eqref{eq:momentorders}.
No truncated theta source or measured variance is substituted here.

\section{Exact cosine-cell covariance}\label{app:cosine}
The elementary carrier also illustrates why averaging does not remove
the fluctuation term. For $H=M\cos(Lz)$ with fixed $L>0$,
set $\eta=\epsilon/M$, $Y=Ly$, $a=\sinh^2Y+\eta^2$ and
$d=\sqrt{a(a+1)}$. Averaging over a complete horizontal period gives
\begin{align}
 U_c&=2\log M+2\operatorname{arsinh}\sqrt a-2\log2,&
 (U_c)_y&=\frac{L\sinh(2Y)}d,\notag\\
 R_c&=\frac{L^2\eta^2}{2\pi}
 \frac{2a(a+1)+1-\eta^2(2a+1)}{d^3}. \label{eq:cosmean}
\end{align}
For the exact heat carrier $M'=L^2M$, its covariance is
\begin{equation}
 Q_c=4\pi R_c+(U_c)_y^2-4L^2+\frac{4L^2\eta^2}{d}.
 \label{eq:cosQ}
\end{equation}
These formulas follow by integrating
$\log(\cos^2(Lx)+\sinh^2(Ly)+\eta^2)$ and differentiating with
respect to $y$ and $\eta^2$ \cite{n05}.
In the distributional limit $\eta\to0$,
$R_c\to(L/\pi)\delta_0$ and $Q_c\to4L\delta_0$.
If a further fixed positive filter $G$ averages in $y$ or time,
the full covariance becomes
\begin{equation}
 Q_{\rm total}=GQ_c-
 \{G((U_c)_y^2)-((GU_c)_y)^2\}.
 \label{eq:secondcov}
\end{equation}
For transverse convolution by $k_h$, the limiting expression is
\[
 4Lk_h+4L^2\{(k_h*\sgn)^2-1\}.
\]
In particular this correction is not negligible merely because several
horizontal periods have been averaged. An arbitrary fitted pair
$M(t),L(t)$ does not satisfy the exact carrier heat equation;
\eqref{eq:cosQ} must not be used as an exact theta closure.

\section{Supporting numerical observations}\label{app:numerics}
The analytical arguments do not use numerical agreement as a premise.
Earlier local-closure, Gaussian, carrier and determinant checks are
recorded in Notes~04, 09 and 11--12 and their execution records
\cite{round1,round2,round3}. They include failed refinements and failed
finite source gates; passing samples do not certify an onset time.

For the explicit clock, 72 exact rational equalities in 24 phase/width
cases check \eqref{eq:finiteP}, \eqref{eq:finiteD} and continuity against
direct polynomial lattice sums, including kernel-edge crossings.
Ten floating theta zero-measure observations have maximum density
discrepancy $2.702\,10^{-8}$ in units of $L/\pi$ and derivative
discrepancy $3.708\,10^{-7}$ in units of $2L/(\pi\kappa)$.
Omitting phase reaches $0.003143$ in the latter units.
These are neither finite-regulator peak measurements nor interval
certificates; some discrepancies are near the observed refinement floor.
Note~19 gives the complete settings, refinements and conservative
allowance evaluations \cite{n19,explicitrecord}.

Nine small-argument checks of the finite-source primitives against
Gaussian quadrature give maximum floating discrepancy
$7.03\,10^{-16}$, including $t=0$ and $z=0$.
They check the primitive formulas, not a selected theta bridge or useful
transition overlap. Details are in Note~20 and its record
\cite{n20,primitiverecord}. No independent implementation or mathematical
review is claimed.

The finite-transition diagnostic fixes its contract before comparing
48, 96 and 192 source cells. At the trial time $t=64$, the 192-cell
zeroth-jet allowance falls from $3.79\,10^{58}$ to $5.64\,10^{-2}$
when source locality is retained. Interpolation dominates the new bound;
these are floating evaluations of analytical formulas. The trial still
fails the declared observable tolerances, and covariance filtering needs
further resolution. A raw error-function evaluation near $t=0$ and an
initial nodal-rounding allowance fail their checks; both failures are
retained. The local polynomial evaluator has a separate truncation bound.
Details and all arithmetic/filter limitations are in Note~23 and its
record \cite{n23,localrecord}. No uniform numerical certificate is claimed.

\section{Source crosswalk and control scope}\label{app:scope}
The exact framework is from Note~02; the small-width expansion and
pilot are from Note~04; fixed-region and cosine-cell formulas are from
Note~05; the generic regulator bridge is from Note~06.
Note~09 supplies the earlier finite-rectangle source-to-density and
weak-current comparison. Notes~11--12 supply the all-height negative
bound, global hierarchy, growing-height clock and determinant estimates.
Note~14 supplies the variable-regulator corrections, Note~16 the
differentiated carrier and weak transport bridge, Note~19 the score
refinement and exact phase formulas, and Note~20 the global protocol
and finite-source comparison. Note~23 sharpens its source and nonlinear
allowances and declares a first transition trial. The improved first term in
\eqref{eq:Vorder} follows by inserting Note~19's mean bound into the
earlier determinant proof.
Appendix~\ref{app:source} records explicitly the weighted extension
of BW10 Note~20 used in this draft.
The numerical appendix cites execution records separately from proofs.

The repository's collision register \cite{controls} imposes several
useful scope checks. Finite atomic collision controls fail the smooth
canonical-source hypotheses of the negative endpoint theorem.
Euler-factor and primitive perturbations change the unchanged theta
coefficients; weighted-square and reflected controls do not supply
the canonical source bounds used here.
The generic score identity applies to smooth sources with the stated
endpoint terms, and the finite-field stability estimates can also apply
to smooth colliding controls. Their canonical source constants cannot
be transferred unchanged. A fixed compact-source heat field has zero
heat residual but fails at positive infinite time by
Proposition~\ref{prop:fixedsource}; zero residual alone therefore does
not pay the source-approximation error.
Those failures explain why the canonical bounds are not automatically
available for the controls; the generic density, regularization and
determinant inequalities themselves may still apply to them.
A valid carrier lemma can hold in a collision-free box of a control.
At the positive three-atom double event used in Note~09, its carrier
gate fails ($a_*<0$).
Smooth perturbed sources can share leading endpoint asymptotics and
still collide earlier, so the endpoint limits are not collision
exclusions.

The stationary sine heat field has external zeros that cancel a
selected block's internal repulsion. Full finite Hermite heat
polynomials have a stationary pattern after $\sqrt t$ scaling but can
retain a multiple endpoint. They do not have the canonical smooth
source and logarithmic clock scale used here.
These controls rule out interpreting stationarity alone as endpoint
simplicity or as an unforced theta equilibrium.
The repository's evidence and scope policies \cite{scope} are therefore
part of the research record, not additional mathematical premises.
