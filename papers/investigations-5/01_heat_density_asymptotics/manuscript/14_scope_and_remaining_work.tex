Here $e_*=t^{3/2}/N_*^2$, $\mathfrak e_{\rm ev}=e_*\mathcal L^2$,
$d_*=t/N_*^3$, $N_*=1+X+t\mathcal L$ and
$\mathcal L=\log(2+t+X)$. The linear gamma model's sufficient restriction
$X\ge e^{4t}$ is not the paper's final spatial limitation. The full saddle
removes it and supplies static and direct evolution comparisons under
their separately stated quantifiers. Both models retain exact nonzero
heat residuals \eqref{eq:linear-heat-residual} and~\eqref{eq:full-saddle-residual}.
Static errors are not differentiated to obtain evolution; the full-saddle
model's observable equations include the additional residual corrections.
