\section{A common regulator and spatial averaging protocol}\label{sec:protocol}
All new endpoint and outer theorems use fixed $\ell,h>0$ and fixed
spatial convolution $K$. The general fixed space--time filter in
Section~\ref{sec:framework} remains a separate exact framework; the
new theorems do not automatically assert time-averaged evolution.

Define the compact kernel
\begin{equation}
 k(v)=\frac{315}{256}(1-v^2)^4\mathbf1_{\{|v|\le1\}},
 \qquad k_\ell(x)=\ell^{-1}k(x/\ell),\quad
 K(x,y)=k_\ell(x)k_h(y).
 \label{eq:kernel}
\end{equation}
It is nonnegative, normalized and $C^3$, with bounded weak derivatives through order four. 
For sufficiently large $t$, let $L$ denote the large solution of
\begin{equation}
 4\pi e^{4L}=2tL+9,\qquad \kappa=8tL-2t+36 .
 \label{eq:saddle}
\end{equation}
For the large saddle in \eqref{eq:saddle}, define
\begin{equation}
 M_0=2\pi^2e^{tL^2+9L-\pi e^{4L}}\sqrt{2\pi/\kappa},\qquad
 \epsilon_+(t,x)=M_0\kappa^{-1}e^{-x^2/(2\kappa)}.
 \label{eq:positiveRegulator}
\end{equation}

\subsection{One regulator for both endpoints}
Let $C=\Phi(0)\sqrt\pi/2$, fix $\eta>0$, and define
\[
 \epsilon_b(t)=\eta C(1+t^2)^{-1/4}.
\]
For $64<t<128$ put $v=(t-64)/64$ and
$\chi(t)=e^{-1/v}/(e^{-1/v}+e^{-1/(1-v)})$; extend $\chi$ by zero
for $t\le64$ and one for $t\ge128$. Set
\begin{equation}
 \epsilon_g(t,x)=
 \begin{cases}
 \epsilon_b(t),&t\le64,\\
 \exp\{(1-\chi(t))\log\epsilon_b(t)+\chi(t)\log\epsilon_+(t,x)\},
       &t>64.
 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:globalRegulator}
\end{equation}
This is positive and smooth at every finite $(t,x)$.
To check that the large saddle used in $\epsilon_+$ exists throughout
the switch, the maximum of $q_t'$ (with source exponent
$q_t$ as in \eqref{eq:q}) is
$(t/2)(\log(t/(8\pi))-1)+9$, attained at
$u_*=\log(t/(8\pi))/4$.
It is positive at $t=64$ and increasing thereafter; the larger zero
has $\kappa>0$. Smooth extension slightly below $64$ and the flatness
of $\chi$ justify the gluing. The switch points specify the regulator;
they are not onset times for Theorem~\ref{thm:explicit}.

At $t=-T$,
$\epsilon_b/ (\eta C_T)=(1+T^{-2})^{-1/4}=1+O(T^{-2})$.
Using \eqref{eq:Eminus} with this regulator and replacing $\eta$ by
$\eta(1+T^{-2})^{-1/4}$ in \eqref{eq:adaptiveNegative} preserves its
$O(T^{-1})$ scaled error. Indeed
$|\partial_{\log\eta}F_\eta|\le2F_\eta$, and $F_\eta$ is bounded on
each fixed scaled transverse strip, uniformly over real scaled heights.
For $t\ge128$, \eqref{eq:globalRegulator} equals
\eqref{eq:positiveRegulator} exactly.
On a compact time and spatial buffer it has a positive lower bound.
After the height dependence turns on, positivity alone does not supply
such a bound over all real heights.


It is useful to write
\begin{equation}
 \epsilon_g(t,x)=E_0(t)e^{-\beta(t)x^2/2},\qquad
 \beta(t)=\begin{cases}0,&t\le64,\\\chi(t)/\kappa(t),&t>64,\end{cases}
 \label{eq:regulator-beta}
\end{equation}
where $E_0=\epsilon_b$ for $t\le64$ and
$E_0=\epsilon_b^{1-\chi}(M_0/\kappa)^\chi$ for $t>64$.
The numbers $32,64,128$ have different roles: $32$ is an analytical time
floor in the outer proofs, and $64,128$ are chosen regulator switch
points. None is a certified asymptotic onset. For $t>64$ this smooth
regulator has no positive spatial floor on an unbounded height tail.

For a comparison field $F$ on the full test buffer, use the
same physical $q=\epsilon_g^2$, $r=\log q$, and write
\begin{align}
 S_F&=\log(|F|^2+q),& w_F&=q/(|F|^2+q),\notag\\
 U_F&=K*S_F,&W_F&=K*w_F,\notag\\
 \rho_F&=\frac{q|F_z|^2}{\pi(|F|^2+q)^2},&R_F&=K*\rho_F.
 \label{eq:common-observables}
\end{align}
When current is used, $j_F=qF_{zz}\overline{F_z}/[\pi(|F|^2+q)^2]$
and $J_F=K*j_F$; its complex value represents its real two-vector.
The actual $Q_F$ is defined by \eqref{eq:filtereddefs} with $F$ in place
of $H$ and this spatial kernel. Holomorphic identities are used only for holomorphic fields; the spatial
join requires the exact compatibility terms in
Section~\ref{sec:spatial-join}. Physical potential offsets are retained.
A carrier normalization inside a proof must carry the same change in
$q$; no harmonic correction or mass renormalization is implicit.

records their roles; the complete error contracts are collected in
Section~\ref{sec:remaining}.

Every row uses its stated full support buffer and fixed spatial filter.
The exact switch-center formula belongs to $P_{\rm lin}$; the implicit
full saddle is the distinct model $P_*$.

The notation key is as follows. The real source saddle $L$ differs from
$\mathcal L=\log(2+t+X)$, where $X=|x|$ in the exterior estimates.
The exact source mass $M=H(t,0)$ differs from its saddle approximation
$M_0$ in \eqref{eq:positiveRegulator} and the exact gamma factor
$\mathcal M$ in \eqref{eq:gamma-linear}.
The inner drift $\alpha=\mu'/\mu$ is local to
Section~\ref{sec:explicit}; in the gamma sections,
$\alpha=\mathcal M'/\mathcal M$. The lattice spacing $p=\pi/L$
differs from the complex saddle $\mathfrak p_t$ in
\eqref{eq:full-saddle-model}.
The regulator $q=\epsilon_g^2$ differs from the source exponent $q_t(u)$.
Finally $B_2^{\Phi}$ denotes the global source derivative bound, $B_m$
the negative-endpoint composition budgets, and $B_j(Y)$ the inner
centered positive moments; their local definitions remain in force.
