\title{Averaged densities for the theta heat flow:\\
explicit asymptotics and finite-time approximation}
\author{Drafted for Edward Baker}
\date{25 October 2026\\[3pt]\small Working draft; same-assistant review only}
\begin{document}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
We develop quantitative approximations to the unnormalized regularized
zero density of the canonical theta heat flow under one smooth time- and
height-dependent regulator. Potential forcing, spatial curvature,
current source and actual filtered covariance are retained. At negative
time a Gaussian field approximates the evolution uniformly over real
height, with covariance and covariance-Laplacian errors $O(T^{-3})$ and
$O(T^{-4})$. An explicit finite Bernoulli formula retains the positive
inner clock phase and gives density error $O(\kappa^{-1/2})$ and
current/time error $O(L\kappa^{-7/6})$ on buffered windows of size
$\kappa^{1/3}$. A noncompact full gamma-saddle field supplies an all-height
positive endpoint: static density and potential spatial jets have error
$O(t^{-1/2}\log^{-2}(2+t))$, while current, direct time terms, forcing,
signed source and actual covariance have absolute error $O(t^{-1/2})$.
Its nonzero residual remains in the observable equations. Static and
differentiated inner comparisons and a separate compact-time exterior
theorem are proved. A linear gamma model resolves the chosen regulator's
thin switch layer. The positive-terminal-value piecewise-linear cutoff
class is excluded from a fixed-time off-axis all-height physical-potential
contract. On compact full buffers, weak identities transfer field errors
of orders zero and two to the complete filtered system and give a local
time-joining estimate with its exact heat residual. Positive cubic sources
provide fourth-order interpolation remainders and finite Gaussian moments;
amplitude-aware signed-source bounds improve local transfer. A spatial join inside a model zero-free gap now gives a full-support,
all-height comparison on a short time slab, retaining every nonholomorphic
defect and actual covariance. Clipped cubic sources give constructive
finite jet accuracy; an explicit full-buffer outer field theorem supplies
constants at a very large finite onset. Fixed-width time coverage, useful
complete finite budgets and all-time analytic assembly remain open. The regulator's sharp infinite-height
crossover has distinct center and rounding scales; its correlation and
response interpretation remains open. No autonomous closure, accepted global assembly or earlier-time simplicity
conclusion is asserted. Numerical evidence
is supporting and diagnostic.
\end{abstract}
