## 1. Compact refinement of the complete hierarchy

Fix J>=0, fixed analytic buffers as in Note19, and a finite compact
I=[a,T] contained in[32,infinity). On right-hand charts take the real
center X large and positive, with z in the full fixed rectangles;
evenness gives the negative-height charts. Use Note19's P2J, positive
envelope A, branch p, B, and C2j exactly. All constants in this note
may depend on J,I and the fixed buffers. None is asserted uniform
when T grows with X or J grows.

On I the same branch equation yields, uniformly on the full buffers,
\[
p-w=O_T(\log X),\quad |p|=X/2+O_T(\log X),
\]
\[
a_s:=\Re(p-w)=\frac t4\log\frac{X}{4\pi}
                     +O_T(\log X/X).
\tag{RB1}
\]
Indeed Re(p-w)=t log(|p|/(2pi))/4+O(t/|p|), and t is now
bounded. This uses the actual saddle position, rather than the coarser
uniform bound a_s>=t logR/5.

The complete Dirichlet remainder estimate from Note19's contour proof
therefore improves on this compact domain to
\[
\frac{\text{arithmetic on }r\ge a_s/2}{|S|}
\le C_{\rm ar}e^{-a_s\log2+t(\log2)^2/2}
\le C_I X^{-t\log2/4}.
\tag{RB2}
\]
Every n>=2 remains included, since the original bound was
|zeta(q)-1|<=3·2^{-Re q}, Re q>=2. If Re w>=-Sigma and the
error in RB1 is at most one, one explicit sufficient compact prefactor is
\[
C_I=2[3\,2^\Sigma\sqrt{5/2}]
             (4\pi)^{T\log2/4}e^{T(\log2)^2/2}.
\tag{RB3}
\]
This intentionally loose finite choice pays both signs of the position
error on that shifted horizontal portion. Arithmetic on the remaining
segment and connectors is paid by the separate nonlocal ledger below,
not by this displayed prefactor. Its T dependence must not be suppressed
in a joint large-t limit.

The local fixed-order remainder is O_(J,I)(X^(-2J-3)). All actual
left/right noncentral segments, connectors, original middle and polynomial
moment tails retain their complete payments R^b0 exp(-ct log²R).
On I these are smaller than every specified fixed inverse height power
after increasing the height onset; no source term has disappeared.
Thus, including spatial Cauchy losses and the exact residual,
\[
|\partial_z^d(H-P_{2J})|
\le C_{J,I,d}\mathcal A(1+\log X)^d
       [X^{-(2J+3)}+X^{-t\log2/4}],\quad0\le d\le3,
\]
\[
|(H-P_{2J})_t|
\le C_{J,I}\mathcal A(1+\log X)^2
       [X^{-(2J+3)}+X^{-t\log2/4}],
\]
\[
|(\partial_t+\partial_{zz})P_{2J}|
                    \le C_{J,I}\mathcal A X^{-(2J+3)}.
\tag{RB4}
\]
These follow from the complete joint theorem and its exact time identity,
with RB2 replacing only its coarse arithmetic budget. Buffers are paid
before Cauchy; no error bound or moving contour endpoint is differentiated.

Whenever a log2/4>2J+3, arithmetic is smaller than the local allowance
uniformly on I. In particular 8log2>5, so P2 has pure X^-5 accuracy
on every fixed[32,T]. For P4 on[63,65], 63log2/4>7, giving
\[
\boxed{|\partial_z^d(H-P_4)|\le C_d\mathcal A
                     (1+\log X)^dX^{-7},\quad0\le d\le3,}
\]
\[
\boxed{|(H-P_4)_t|\le C\mathcal A(1+\log X)^2X^{-7},\qquad
|\operatorname{Res}(P_4)|\le C\mathcal A X^{-7}.}
\tag{RB5}
\]
This is a new compact-domain proof at t=64, not substitution of64
into an uninstantiated eventual all-height onset. At still higher J
keep the arithmetic term whenever its compact exponent does not pay
the requested local precision; gamma corrections alone do not grant
arbitrary fixed-time accuracy. The first eventual theorem and this
compact theorem have distinct quantifiers and constants.

## 2. Rechecked earlier elementary carrier on each fixed[32,T]

[Ansatz Note03][compact] originally proved its positive outer theorem
only on[32,33]. Here its argument is redone on a newly declared finite
[32,T], with new constants and onset. No out-of-domain citation is used.
For z near positive X let Lz=Log(z/(4pi)), continued from the positive
real axis, and define the holomorphic arms
\[
C_\pm(t,z)=\frac{\pi^{1/4}z^{7/4}}{32}
 \exp\left[-\frac{\pi z}{8}
 +\frac t{16}(L_z^2-\pi^2/4)
 \ \pm i\left\{\frac z4(L_z-1)+\frac{\pi tL_z}{16}
                               +\frac{7\pi}{8}\right\}\right],
\]
\[
b_\pm=L_z/2\pm i\pi/4,\quad
D_\pm=\frac{\pm i}{z}
       \{1/24-t/4-(7t/4)b_\pm-(t^2/8)b_\pm^2\},
\]
\[
F_1=\sum_\pm C_\pm(1+D_\pm),\qquad
E_o=|C_+|+|C_-|.
\tag{RB6}
\]
These preserve the exact normalization and unwrapped phase7pi/8
checked in Note03; the one-arm amplitude is half the cosine amplitude.
The strip envelope Eo includes transverse growth. The real-axis
amplitude alone would not pay the full buffered comparison.

Choose one constant V so Re(w+v)>=2 for v>=V and w=s,1-s
on the full larger buffers, and split the exact convolution at±V.
The two holomorphic gamma arms are
\[
J_V(t,w)=(\pi t)^{-1/2}\int_V^\infty e^{-v^2/t}M(w+v)dv.
\tag{RB7}
\]
Keep the original bounded middle and the complete ordinary Dirichlet
remainders on both rays. V is fixed in z and t, so the analytic
arm definitions have no differentiated moving endpoint.

On V<=v<=X^(1/3), sectorial Taylor estimates give
log M(w+v)=log M(w)+alpha v+alpha'v²/2+O(v³/X²),
and |alpha'|<=C/X. The cubic remainder and the square of the
quadratic exponential term, integrated against the tilted Gaussian,
are at most C_T(1+log⁴X)/X² relative to the nonzero linear gamma
carrier G_t(w)=M(w)exp(t alpha(w)²/4). All finite tilted absolute
moments have size C_(T,k)(1+log^kX); the absolute-to-complex Gaussian
loss exp(t(Im alpha)²/4) is bounded by a finite constant depending
on T and the strip. Requiring X^(1/3) to exceed a fixed T-dependent
multiple of1+logX pays the local Taylor and far-ray separation.

Expanding the exact quadratic Gaussian and its determinant gives
\[
\log J_V=\log M+\frac t4\alpha^2+\frac t4\alpha'
          +\frac{t^2}{8}\alpha^2\alpha'
          +O_T((1+\log^4X)/X^2).
\tag{RB8}
\]
The logarithm is taken only of a ratio to the nonzero G_t whose
ratio tends to one, never of H or a cosine. The omitted pieces are:

| Complete contribution | Normalized payment on[32,T] |
| --- | --- |
| Full ordinary arithmetic on both rays | C_T X^(-t log2/4) |
| Original bounded middle and missing lower linear-Gaussian tail | C_T exp(-2L_X²+C_T(1+L_X)), L_X=log(X/(4pi)) |
| Both true infinite gamma ends beyond X^(1/3) | C_T exp(-c_T X^(2/3)) |
| Local cubic/quadratic-square remainder | C_T(1+log⁴X)/X² |

For the middle, the real-part range is a fixed enlarged strip determined
by V and all buffers. Euler summation/reflection and bounded-real-part
Stirling give some finite polynomial X^d times exp(-piX/8); dividing
by G_t keeps exp(-tL_X²/16+O_T(L_X)), bounded above by the table's
expression since t>=32. The smaller-strip fourth-power bound is not
reused on this enlarged middle. For the far ray retain the height
exponential through
log(|M(w+v)|/|M(w)|)<=C v+(v/2)log(X+v), and then use Gaussian
domination with t<=T; a global source bound at the local endpoint
would not pay the exponentially small carrier. Arithmetic follows by
completing the tilted Gaussian with its log2 tilt, including every
n>=2; tails outside the Taylor interval are already in the far-ray row.
These checks establish the entire table on the new domain.

The one-arm logarithmic time remainder can also be paid directly. Set
Tlog=log M+t alpha²/4+t alpha'/4+t²alpha²alpha'/8. Approximate the
fixed gamma integrand by M(w)exp(alpha v)(1+alpha'v²/2), which is
independent of t before integration. Differentiate the original fixed
Gaussian kernel first; its multiplier v²/t²-1/(2t) raises the required
absolute moments by at most two powers. The normalized gamma error and
its derivative are therefore O_T((1+log⁴X)/X²) and
O_T((1+log⁶X)/X²), respectively. The same polynomial weight preserves
each middle/missing-Gaussian/far-tail payment. Dividing by G_t and then
taking the near-one logarithm gives
\[
|\partial_t(\log J_V-T_{\log})|
                  \le C_T(1+\log^6X)/X^2.
\tag{RB8a}
\]
This is kernel differentiation, not differentiation of RB8's O term.
No lower-endpoint Leibniz term appears since V is fixed, and no exact
heat equation for the truncated arm J_V is assumed. In fact, writing
K(t,v)=(pi t)^(-1/2)exp(-v²/t), direct integration by parts gives
\[
(\partial_t-\tfrac14\partial_{ww})J_V
 =\frac{K(t,V)}4[M'(w+V)+(2V/t)M(w+V)].
\tag{RB8b}
\]
This finite-endpoint forcing is superalgebraic relative to G_t on the
fixed compact domain, with the same middle/missing-Gaussian payment.
The kernel-derivative proof above neither drops it nor assumes it zero.

The zero-time sectorial expansions from Note03 CB4, followed by RB8,
give RB6 and hence
\[
|\partial_z^d(H-F_1)|\le C_{T,d}E_o
           \frac{1+\log^{4+d}X}{X^2},\quad0\le d\le3,
\qquad
|(H-F_1)_t|\le C_TE_o\frac{1+\log^6X}{X^2}.
\tag{RB9}
\]
Arithmetic is below X^-2 throughout[32,T] because8log2>2;
the other nonlocal rows are superalgebraic in height. Cauchy disks of
radius c/(1+logX) within the larger buffers pay every spatial jet.
Constants and onset depend on T; none is being reused for T growing
with height.

For completeness the exact residual has, with
Q0(b)=21/16+t/8+5tb/8+t²b²/16 and Lpm=log Cpm,
\[
R_\pm=\frac{\operatorname{Res}(C_\pm)}{C_\pm}
 =\frac{\pm i(1+7b_\pm+t b_\pm^2)}{4z}+\frac{Q_0(b_\pm)}{z^2},
\]
\[
\operatorname{Res}(F_1)=\sum_\pm C_\pm
 [(1+D_\pm)R_\pm+(D_\pm)_t
           +2(L_\pm)_z(D_\pm)_z+(D_\pm)_{zz}].
\tag{RB10}
\]
This is exact elementary differentiation, valid for the new interval
as well as the old one. The1/z terms cancel, giving
|Res(F1)|<=C_T Eo(1+log⁴X)/X². The exact heat identity applied
to H-F1 then proves the direct first-time bound RB9. No derivative
of the error allowance is taken.

## 3. Compatible envelopes, actual overlap and optional time splice

The compact implicit equation gives, by Taylor and determinant expansion,
\[
\log S_t(w)=\log M(w)+t\alpha(w)^2/4+t\alpha'(w)/4
             +t^2\alpha(w)^2\alpha'(w)/8
             +O_T((1+\log^4X)/X^2).
\tag{RB11}
\]
To see its uniformity, p-w=O_T(logX) and alpha'=O(1/X);
substitute the implicit displacement into log M(p)-(p-w)²/t and
expand log B. All segments remain in a fixed sector with |q|≈X.
For the matching time remainder, expand the exact implicit derivative
(log S)_t=alpha(p)²/4+alpha'(p)/(4B)
+t alpha(p)alpha''(p)/(8B²) about w. Subtracting partial_t Tlog
cancels the retained terms and leaves O_T((1+log⁶X)/X²). Together
with RB8a, this pays the direct-time versions of both one-arm log
comparisons; spatial derivatives of these holomorphic remainders follow
on the same paid Cauchy disks.
The same CB4 gamma-to-elementary expansion therefore yields, arm by arm,
S_t(w)/Cpm=1+O_T((1+log²X)/X). In particular
\[
\mathcal A\asymp E_o
\tag{RB12}
\]
on the whole complex buffer, including transverse exponential growth.
This is a compatibility bound, not a fitted amplitude normalization.

Both proved comparisons have the same exact H. On their genuine common
domain t in[32,T], X>=X_(J,T), the triangle inequality gives
\[
\begin{split}
|\partial_z^d(P_{2J}-F_1)|\le C_{J,T}E_o\bigg[&
 (1+\log X)^d\{X^{-(2J+3)}+X^{-t\log2/4}\}\\
 &+\frac{1+\log^{4+d}X}{X^2}\bigg],\qquad0\le d\le3.
\end{split}
\tag{RB13}
\]
The corresponding direct-time difference is bounded by the sum of RB4
and RB9, with the same comparable envelope. No termwise identification
of the two asymptotic organizations is required.

For a fixed C1 time cutoff0<=chi<=1 with chi=0 below and chi=1
above a declared compact common interval, and chi' supported inside
that interval, the holomorphic-in-z field
F_chi=chi P2J+(1-chi)F1 has the exact interface residual
\[
\operatorname{Res}(F_\chi)=\chi\operatorname{Res}(P_{2J})
 +(1-\chi)\operatorname{Res}(F_1)+\chi'(P_{2J}-F_1).
\tag{RB14}
\]
The last term is paid by RB13, not discarded. Field/jet and first-time
errors follow by the same bounds, including chi' in the latter.
For example on[63,65], J=2, the splice has field jets
C_chi Eo(1+log^(4+d)X)/X², first-time C_chi Eo(1+log⁶X)/X²
and residual C_chi Eo(1+log⁴X)/X². Its coarser rate reflects the
F1 chart. No spatial cutoff is introduced, so no loss of holomorphy
or unreported spatial interface defect occurs.

Choose a fixed finite T>max{65,t_(*,J)}. The compact chart genuinely
overlaps Note19's eventual all-height theorem on
\[
t_{*,J}\le t\le T,\qquad X\ge X_{J,T},
\tag{RB15}
\]
and the same compact chart reaches[63,65]. This is an existence overlap,
with finite but uninstantiated T-dependent height threshold; no numerical
onset or affordability statement follows. The remaining bounded-height
core is explicitly
\[
32\le t<t_{*,J},\qquad |x|<X_{J,T}.
\tag{RB16}
\]
Neither the compact theorem nor the optional time splice covers it.
The same implicit formula P2J is used on the two large-time/outer domains;
no cutoff is necessary merely to state their covered union. This is
not all-time/all-height assembly.
