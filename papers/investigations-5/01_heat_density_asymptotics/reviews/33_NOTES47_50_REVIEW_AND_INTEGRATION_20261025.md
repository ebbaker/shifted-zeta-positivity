## Analytical findings

1. **Finite budget is distinct from the field theorem.** Note 47 adds the
   collar slopes/units, pole residues, restricted raw integrals, weighted
   regulator terms and signed heat partition needed by Note 43. The proof
   enlarges every intersecting collar, including support-edge collars,
   before estimating positive primitives. It does not apply a signed
   whole-support theorem to a truncated integral. Its auxiliary primitive
   gradient rows justify common mean caps before covariance coefficients.
   The finite constants are deliberately enormous sufficient bounds.

2. **Gap motion is derived at the right quantity.** In Note 48, the
   imaginary part of the exact carrier time logarithmic derivative costs
   O(log x), whereas its magnitude could cost O(log^2 x). Dividing by
   Theta_x >= log(2+x)/8 bounds the speed. Root velocity is not substituted
   for gap velocity. Freezing width removes width-motion terms, and direct
   differentiation retains lambda_t D, lambda_xt D/2 and lambda_xxt D/4
   in the appropriate E, Ez, Bt and Bzt rows. No second time derivative is
   consumed by the declared tuple.

3. **The vanishing-error statement uses source refinement.** MW15/SW24
   make the core allowance no larger than the decreasing nonsource
   envelope. A fixed quarter-target would establish only a fixed error,
   not O(log^6 X0/X0^2) or O(log^8 X0/X0). The manuscript states that the
   source is fixed for each construction and may change with X0/accuracy.

4. **The switch is paid in physical units.** SW1 rechecks the three
   changed lower-time contour inequalities and retains upper-time 97
   constants. SW5 keeps the amplitude mismatch chi'(ell_+-ell_b).
   From the exact logit derivative, SW8 gives
   |beta'| <= 4 beta(1+log^2(1/beta)); this pays beta'x^2 by
   O(x log^2 x) when beta x <= 16. SW12 follows from
   2q|f|/(|f|^2+q)^2 <= 1/(2|f|), and so gives relative O(mu)
   weight transfer even when q/A_sum^2 is large. The small-q branch is
   separately retained. These are complementary estimates, not changes
   to the physical regulator or a field partition at beta x = 16.

5. **The largest compatibility-time term has a vanishing budget.**
   The pointwise mu L^2(L^2+|rt|) envelope, band area O(1/L), and
   flatness payment give O_eta(mu x L^3)=O_eta(L^3/x). In the other
   regime the actual w and w(1-w) factors retain exponential suppression.
   The manuscript exposes this derivation before the coarser log^8/x
   master envelope. The switch floor and fixed central mean cap are
   recomputed; the former [95,97] constants are not silently extended.

6. **The nonholomorphic temporal correction is essential and present.**
   Varying Delta S = 4pi(rho+C_reg+T_C) at fixed q gives TJ5's extra
   -4pi v' delta_Delta T_C term. The manuscript derives its signs from
   delta S, delta w and delta[w(1-w)], rather than importing the
   holomorphic formula globally. The physical source has no explicit v';
   that contribution cancels between direct density time and heat defect.
   Field-level mismatches cannot be inferred from tuple agreement, as
   the constant phase-reversal control shows.

7. **The negative and pre-switch attachments use valid floors.** On
   [-2T,-T], the Gaussian/source Taylor errors and width T give the finite
   K_neg/T budget. The weaker overlap time rate is not presented as the
   sharper pure-Gaussian endpoint rate. The same finite source serves the
   whole bounded slab because its absolute error majorant uses only
   t <= 97. On [63,64], beta = 0 and the rational-map coefficients have a
   global positive regulator floor; they remain valid through multiple
   events. The actual five-piece field has flat matching and domain union
   (-infinity,97] x R_x. No chart-count multiplier is needed.

8. **The positive gate is real and localized.** TJ25 needs mixed-field
   relative control with finite uniform collar/pole/mean constants.
   Eventual endpoint observable agreement cannot pay it. The proposed
   T_+ threshold is conditional until those constants are supplied
   independently of the chosen overlap/core/source. The interval from
   97 to that late overlap also needs effective compact constants. The
   manuscript states both obligations. Failure of a global regulator-floor
   majorant is not a counterexample to the relative route.

No source theorem was silently strengthened. The manuscript adds explicit
intermediate proofs and clearer organization, not a new all-time theorem.
The generic spatial/temporal identities can apply to colliding controls;
the canonical-source premise screen remains as in cluster 12 and Note 53.
