For Gauss rules, exact Legendre sign variations count roots in open
dyadic intervals, with a correct endpoint/midpoint-zero convention.
This replaces the exponential mesh by O(s h) exact count queries through
depth h. Integer recurrences, separation, derivative/weight conditioning,
positivity and rational affine mapping are all counted. No root oracle
or unit-cost rational arithmetic is hidden in the estimate.

For a fixed chart/calibration record, one complete query has an effective
sufficient bound

    Q=2+B+P+s_sigma+ceil(L)²,
    T_bit<=C(N+1)Q^40, M_bits<=C Q^20.

Here B is actual binary input length and s_sigma retains strict center
separation. All N modes are still processed; streaming avoids saving
them all. Since N is of order exp(L/2), this is not polynomial total
work in L. Large rational temporary operands, large phases and common

## Repairs, limits and next work

The chat preserves the following lessons and scoped repairs:

- Output-level roundoff does not pay accumulation in a long gamma sum.
  Exact/finer internal arithmetic repairs finite delivery; shifted
  Stirling then improves the term count.
- Arbitrarily tiny budgets or inflated majorants cannot support a fixed
  L-rate. Comparable dyadic budgets and controlled finite allocations
  keep the stated precision/cost qualifications honest.
- Scalar counts alone do not pay rational denominator growth or mode
  storage. Structured terms, dyadic accumulation and streaming matter.
- One successful height does not cover order jumps; continuous majorants
  and actual classical domains/onsets supply the all-height claim.
- A valid certificate for another input, a wrong primitive type, or a
  correctly reported capped computation must not be counted as delivery.
  Replay binds mathematical inputs and errors, rather than trusting PASS.

Remaining work includes pole-safe F_0/C_k software, scale/large-phase
providers, center and guard geometry, retained-integral data, the full
mode circuit and independent rounded channels, and complete evaluator
certification. The analytical manuscript still contains the earlier
conditional evaluator specification; Notes09--12 are not yet integrated
