## The mathematical target

Keep the full canonical source and normalization
`H_t=-H_zz`, `H(0,z)=xi((1+iz)/2)/8`. For physical statements retain
the prescribed `q=epsilon_g^2`, fixed spatial kernel K, unnormalized
density/current and actual covariance. A fixed transverse width is chosen
before an all-height limit; constants may depend on that width and its
analytic buffers. No RH or fitted-zero data enter the approximation.

The target has three linked levels:

1. **Field:** economical formulas for H, spatial jets through three,
   direct first-time error and the model's actual heat residual, normalized
   by a positive arm envelope or stated as absolute estimates.
2. **Physical behavior:** density, current, potential, covariance and the
   complete established tuple, including weak derivatives, regulator terms
   and all joining defects. Sharper field estimates need a proved consumer
   before their gain is assigned to these quantities.
3. **Effectivity:** finite source/coefficients, strict branch/domain guards,
   certified arithmetic and continuous filtering, and useful dependence on
   height, time, order and requested accuracy. A huge finite bound is a
   foundation to improve, not an affordable computation by definition.

A desirable future description would use regional finite models F(r,J),
positive allowances E(r,J), and overlap estimates to obtain one complete
error account. Its shape should separate local truncation, arithmetic,
nonlocal source/contour, joining, physical transfer and evaluation. A
proposed relation between successive E(r,J) must include constant growth.
This is the program objective; a uniform all-region hierarchy theorem is
not yet established.

## Priorities that produce measurable improvements

### Positive hierarchy and the physical switch

First execute existing [HDA-A03-AN01](../ansatz/research-progress/handoffs/HDA-A03-AN01.md):
name the linear, full-saddle and corrected centers and bound their
sensitivity/displacement under the same q. Compare the bound with the
rounding width `1/(X log X)`, not just the larger `1/log X` center shift.
The compact P4 input makes this a concrete next calculation.

Then freeze a bounded physical-transfer request for one fixed model,
preferably P4 on [63,65], with the exact list of density/current/time/
covariance outputs. Prove corrected phase and unit bounds, zero collars,
signed primitive estimates, actual means and residual consumers. A
successful return must give the improved exponent or logarithmic factor
for each output; if a consumer loses the field gain, identify that term.
Only afterward replace components of the global construction.

### Negative hierarchy and the approach to zero

Execute [HDA-R05-AN08](../recurrence/research-progress/handoffs/HDA-R05-AN08.md)
with its frozen target `L^-1<=T<=2`, the same p/K/heat window and
`exp(-L²/4)` combined delivered error. Pay the factor `exp(Y²/(4tau))`,
inverse-time powers, mode population, all tails and norm conversion.
The requested domain approaches zero but excludes it. Use the return to
choose the next overlap with the existing signed crossover; do not assume
that overlap preserves the stronger error automatically.

The remote negative Gaussian and fixed-family recurrence also need a
joint growing-upper-time bridge for a uniformly elegant negative picture.
A fixed-B constant cannot be reused at B=B(X). Freeze that domain only
after identifying which heat tail or curvature term determines the useful
scale. The absolute Gaussian remains the endpoint fallback meanwhile.

### Remaining positive regions and arithmetic refinement

Retain [HDA-A08-AN01](../ansatz/research-progress/handoffs/HDA-A08-AN01.md)
as the existing small-positive task. The interval between the shrinking
zero window and t=32, and the bounded-height compact core below each
eventual positive onset, require their own controls. The finite source
already covers absolute physical approximation there. A more economical
field hierarchy must pay its full arithmetic population and source ends.

At fixed positive time, arbitrary gamma order alone retains an arithmetic
allowance `X^(-t log2/4)`. A proposed improvement is to include the first
several ordinary Dirichlet modes with individual saddle corrections and
bound every omitted mode. The desired gain is a stated improved fixed-time
exponent and a controlled mode/order cost. No sharp lower bound or such
multimode theorem is currently claimed.

Positive growing order remains an optional refinement, not a prerequisite
for every bridge. Expose uniform J-dependent Bell, gamma and tail constants,
then compare admissible J(t,X) with retained arithmetic and contour errors.
An optimal-truncation slogan or a list of extra coefficients is insufficient.

### Conditioning, evaluation and global reassembly

Complete the parent Note61 weak-primitive filter consumer: collar guards,
log/pole tails, normalized providers, all spatial/moving/time commutators,
actual covariance means and the coordinate/time moduli needed by the
fourth weak kernel derivatives. The gain sought is removal or reduction
of the growing inverse-regulator dependence in a complete consumer.
Keep raw-density mass on punctured disks. Polynomial accuracy cost at a
fixed box is not already a polynomial-height theorem.

After new regional consumers close, reassemble in the existing noncircular
order of choices and compare the resulting total error and data size with
the present construction. Track selection cost and provider costs as well
as output precision. A full software implementation and the independent
inherited-chain audit are separate, still open obligations.

## Screening and evidence standards

This is quantitative approximation, not an RH criterion. The source
premises absent from the controls are specific: Euler-factor controls
change the canonical arithmetic coefficients; weighted-square controls
retain support/finite endpoints without the full normalized theta source;
reflected super-Gaussian and smooth positive-primitive controls lack the
canonical arithmetic square expansion; finite-prime perturbations preserve
only selected identities rather than all exact canonical coefficients.
Their heat identities and positivity survive, so neither property alone
can justify a new zero-location conclusion. No new control computation is
needed for this synthesis; any future criterion must repeat the relevant
premise screening against [Control12](../../../../result-summaries/12_collision_controls_and_screening.md).

Every promoted result must state its domain, norm, order of quantifiers,
complete payments, direct time/residual distinction and quantitative gain.
Numerics may test a named finite inequality with a declared stop rule;
sampling does not prove an unbounded theorem. Internal same-model or
cross-model LLM review does not equal specialist validation. Preserve scoped
obstructions, failed attempts and historical gates.
