## Mathematical target and conventions

Keep the full canonical source and normalization
\(H_t=-H_{zz}\), \(H(0,z)=\xi((1+iz)/2)/8\). Physical statements retain
the prescribed \(q=\epsilon_g^2\), fixed spatial kernel K, unnormalized
density/current, actual covariance and all defects. Fixed transverse
widths and analytic buffers are chosen before an all-height limit;
constants may depend on them. No RH or fitted-zero data enter the
approximation.

Write \(L=\log(X/(4\pi))\), \(\mathcal L=1+\log X\) and
\(\epsilon_X=\mathcal L^2X^{-3/4}\). Reserve \(\Lambda\) for the Newman
constant in application discussions. The program has three levels:

1. **Field:** finite formulas, spatial jets through three, direct first
   time comparison and actual heat residual, with absolute or positive
   envelope norms. A small residual is not itself a field error theorem.
2. **Physical transfer:** potential, density, current, covariance and the
   complete specified tuple, with weak derivatives, regulator terms and
   spatial/time joining defects. Each sharper field theorem needs its
   own complete consumer.
3. **Effectivity:** finite sources/coefficients, strict guards, certified
   evaluation and filtering, with useful dependence on height, time,
   order, resolution and the required margin. Fixed-data extra-accuracy
   cost alone does not supply that dependence.

A uniform all-region hierarchy would need regional models and allowances
with paid arithmetic, source/contour, overlap, joining, transfer and
evaluation terms, including growth of constants with order. The existing
finite all-time physical construction establishes existence. A sharper,
more uniform and less costly hierarchy remains the program objective.

## What positive coverage now means

Note26 proves a growing-shift initial remainder and pays the complete
finite population, direct-time moments and actual residual for the
existing frozen-N family. Its overlap with the signed window uses the
identical F_N, including RS completion. Consequently [0,1] is covered
without a new moving-cutoff splice. Notes25 and the earlier compact
positive theorem then give a high-height atlas on [0,T_*] for each
fixed finite T_*>=33, with T_*-dependent onsets.

This closes the old high-height positive field-domain gap at the stated
precision. The atlas does not construct a new F_N/F1 time join, make
global floor-valued chart selection differentiable, supply strong
negative exp(-L²/4) precision at zero, or give new small-positive
physical/core estimates. Those are distinct targets.

At fixed positive time the two-arm hierarchy retains the arithmetic
allowance X^(-t log2/4). Formally at t=c/L this allowance tends to
2^(-c/4), and at t=c/L² to one. This diagnoses the allowance, not the
true error or an extension of that theorem outside its domain. It
explains why retained arithmetic is a better starting input near zero
than adding gamma corrections alone.

The new conditional gain is concrete. On an isolated finite block,
contour projection extracts the whole exterior unit drift
\(D_H=2(U_H)_z/U_H\). For one fixed enclosing contour of length p,
an interior real interval at distance b, model floor m, errors delta0<m
and delta1, and model derivative cap M1, RT Note02 gives

\[
\|(D_H-D_F)'\|\le\min\left\{
\frac{p}{\pi b^2}\frac{\delta_1+M_1\delta_0/m}{m-\delta_0},
\frac{2p}{\pi b^3}[-\log(1-\delta_0/m)]\right\}=:\kappa.
\]

The ordered raw exterior-energy error on the **same true roots** is
at most 2 kappa E_K. Common motion cancels, replacing the inverse-cube
pair sum of separate absolute drift errors by an inverse-square sum.
With fixed normalized microscopic contour data, p,b of order
mathcal L^-1 and a time step of order mathcal L^-2, Note26 supplies
an O(epsilon_X) integrated relative error coefficient. This is an
improvement of a conditional payment rule; no canonical margin or
measured numerical advantage is acquired.

The missing data are independently justified contour lower bounds,
interior count/real location and initial energy, followed by the model
exterior term and its integrated boundary budget. The envelope is not a
lower bound. Rouché gives a count with multiplicity; the exterior
projection needs no internal minimum gap, but supplies no interior
simplicity or first-jet floor. The model need not solve heat for this
projection because its root dynamics are not used.
