## 2. Fourier and carrier payments

Use \(\widehat W(\xi)=\int e^{-i\xi\cdot X}W(X)\,dX\) and the Euclidean
norm of the two components. The positive common kernel gives |W|<=h and
int h=M, so |hat W|<=||W||1<=M without a component-count factor.
Parseval has factor (2 pi)^(-3). Conversion from the unitary convention
in [DLMF 1.14.1](https://dlmf.nist.gov/1.14.E1) and
[Parseval 1.14.8](https://dlmf.nist.gov/1.14.E8), checked 29 October 2026,
confirms the normalization; sign choice in the forward exponent does
not affect the norms. No external feedback theorem is imported.

Keeping the full low-frequency deficit gives
\[
 G\ge k^2E_0-\frac{M^2}{2\pi^2}
             \int_0^k(k^2-t^2)t^2dt
   =k^2E_0-\frac{M^2k^5}{15\pi^2}.
\]
The optimizer is k³=6 pi² E0/M². Hence
\[
 g\ge\frac35(6\pi^2E_0/M^2)^{2/3},\qquad
 \alpha\ge g/3\ge a_*:=\frac15(6\pi^2E_0/M^2)^{2/3}>0.
\]
This estimate needs no zero-mass hypothesis or positive physical margin,
and no optimal-Nash assertion is made.

For the carrier, [Note29][n29] (12) follows from
\[
 \|W\|_1\le a_3E_0^{1/2}L^{3/2}+R/L^2,\qquad
 a_3^2=32\pi/105.

Optimizing in L gives
\[

 \ge c_3\mu^{7/3}E_0^{-2/3},\qquad
 c_3=\frac37(15/(14\pi))^{2/3}.
\]
Thus r_*=c3 mu_*^(7/3) E0^(-2/3)>0 uses the supplied physical margin.
Positivity of h and E0 alone would not pay this floor.
Combining R'<=6M-2 alpha R with alpha>=a_* yields
\[

 (3M/a_*)(1-e^{-2a_*s})\le R_*:=\max\{R_0,3M/a_*\}.
\]
In particular chi=R*/r*>=1. With L=(log chi)/2 and c=3M/r*,
integration of (log R)'<=6M/r*-2 alpha on any lifespan interval gives
\[

 \int_t^{t+\ell}g\le3(L+c\ell).

All spatial integrals include the prepared inner ball.
