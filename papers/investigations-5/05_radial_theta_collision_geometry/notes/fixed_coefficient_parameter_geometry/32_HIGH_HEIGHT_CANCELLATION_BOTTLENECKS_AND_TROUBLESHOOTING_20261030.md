Explanatory synthesis after the author's manuscript request; no new theorem, acquisition or independent assessment. The main bottleneck is a mismatch of scales. The complete source and its derivative moments can be bounded rigorously, but the signed Fourier integral may be much smaller than the positive quantities used to bound its error. A fully paid absolute estimate can therefore be correct yet incapable of proving a positive physical margin. Increasing arithmetic precision only reduces rounding uncertainty; it does not remove a mathematically prescribed positive error allowance.

## What the recent positive results establish

The local construction now has a complete analytic dependency chain. The quadratic heat reference gives an exact first-jet combination, adaptive radii are explicit when its center discriminant is nonzero, and complete IBP pays complex derivatives, finite boundaries and exteriors. Taylor subtraction before source estimation improves a finite remainder. Retaining Gaussian slack produces a convergent allowance with log T_N=−β√N+O(log N); this is a statement about the payment, not the physical transform.

[R9](B03_R9_SIGNED_SOURCE_TRANSPORT_20261030.md), [accepted in E02 R9](../../reviews/fixed_coefficient_parameter_geometry/E02_R9_SIGNED_TRANSPORT_ASSESSMENT_20261030.md), supplies an actual finite rectangle crossing a simple zero: Q=[31/128,33/128]×[64,66]. H changes sign across the rectangle, while H_A stays uniformly negative. The first jet (H,H_A) consequently stays away from zero. This is stronger evidence of the method's local capability than the earlier scalar-positive pilots. However, the same source information already gives a direct derivative lower bound, so this does not show an auxiliary-flow advantage.

[R10](B03_R10_COMMON_FLOW_AND_FIXED_TRANSPORT_LIMIT_20261030.md), [accepted in E02 R10](../../reviews/fixed_coefficient_parameter_geometry/E02_R10_COMMON_FLOW_AND_TRANSPORT_ASSESSMENT_20261030.md), transports that known physical margin into one common c=1 flow with energy above 1e−35 at every auxiliary time on a small thickening of Q. This all-time conclusion keeps physical T and A in a finite region. It cannot be read backwards as a proof of the source margin it used.

## The main bottlenecks

**The error must respect cancellation, including mixed terms.** For a model J=M+R,

\[
 \|J\|^2=\|M\|^2+2\langle M,R\rangle+\|R\|^2.
\]

Replacing the mixed term by −2||M||||R|| can destroy a useful constructive contribution. A small-remainder comparison is one sufficient mechanism, not a necessary feature of the true field. The signed polynomial step in R9 succeeds partly because it forms P_1²−2P_0P_2 before range enclosure. The task is to find comparable structure when the number and arrangement of contributing phases change with height, while still paying every omitted source contribution.

**Tail convergence does not create a signed margin.** At a fixed center and radius, the improved payments converge to the same limiting quantity d_infinity=|D_0|−2b²r−F_infinity. Faster convergence is useful only if that quantity is independently positive. Moreover, fixed-degree moment payments are linear in |b| and |h_1|, whereas the discriminant is quadratic in small physical jets. At high frequencies the linear allowance can dominate. Adaptive degree/radius are not ruled out, but a global schedule and its source inputs are unproved. The discriminant is also only a sufficient local device: D_0 can vanish without J vanishing. A covering argument cannot assume the quadratic reference works at every point.

**A reference must have the right spectral scale.** [R11](B03_R11_FIXED_GAUSSIAN_SOURCE_RELATIVE_OBSTRUCTION_20261030.md) pays an explicit finite Gaussian source model and its complete error on [31/128,33/128]×[64,∞). The model has simple zeros and an independently positive model first jet through them. Nevertheless, the strict Hardy theorem implies that the nonzero complete theta transform cannot have a Gaussian spectral upper bound on an entire height tail. A fixed finite Gaussian model therefore cannot have uniformly bounded model-relative true error, nor physical-relative error at most q<1, on that tail. This is proof-author-checked, **not yet separately assessed**. It says more than failure of the chosen Cauchy–Schwarz payment, but says nothing against finite windows, adaptive models without a uniform Gaussian envelope, non-Gaussian references or infinite expansions.

**Preparation and positive carriers do not remove the sign problem.** The fixed flow preserves the physical vector mass; it cannot manufacture it. In the nonlinear feedback approach, the established global bounds and synchronization estimates already spend positive physical mass. Importing them into a proof of that first mass margin would be circular. The accepted prepared-tail result also shows that the proposed mixed-production density is positive sufficiently far out, even for the actual source at (1/4,2). Its integral may still have a favorable sign, but that requires spatial cancellation, including the moving conditional tail.

**Height and physical time have to be controlled together for the final target.** The finite rectangle near T=1/4 is not a cover of shrinking positive times or arbitrary frequencies. In the adjacent [S04 full-cutoff model](../../../../investigations-3/11_arithmetic_collision_rigidity/symmetries/04_time_height_scaling/notes/01_PHASE_RETAINING_FULL_CUTOFF_SCALING_20261018.md), the declared regime has 1≤κ=t log(x/(4π))≤2 and 0<t≤1/20. The natural arithmetic cutoff grows roughly like exp(κ/(2t)). Its full signed model and derivative have a paid replacement error, but the signed intermediate core remains unresolved. Fixed-mode approximations cannot simply discard the heat-dependent weights or phase shifts uniformly up to that cutoff. This is separate evidence from a different normalization/domain, not a transfer of R9 or R11 to that regime. Even a successful new local estimate would still need the time, height, multiplicity and endpoint coverage required by the physical reduction.

## Places where the author's troubleshooting would be valuable

1. **Identify a useful arithmetic grouping for a complete quadratic expression.** Can reflection or the complete Poisson relation couple the leading contribution and remainder so that their cross term is controlled together, allowing a large constructive remainder? The concrete obstacle is proving a quantitative inequality from actual coefficients and phases, not merely restating positivity of ||J||².
2. **Choose a reference with an independently justified amplitude and phase across simple zeros.** A non-Gaussian or adaptive family needs a scale derived from its explicit structure, not an assumed lower bound for H or J. It must approximate H and its actual frequency derivative consistently. Dividing by H creates a singularity at the simple zeros we want to cross; a regular two-component comparison or a proved change of local reference is preferable.
3. **Pay the integrated feedback cross term without spending the desired conclusion.** The exact expression is Q_beta(e)+∫C_beta. The prepared tail forces any argument to retain spatial cancellation. A useful new identity could couple these two terms or exhibit a separately proved gap in the carrier ceiling. A bound on each absolute term, or an error-decay theorem that already assumes positive physical mass, will not close the first estimate.
4. **Design a finite-to-asymptotic bridge with declared quantifiers.** Which domain is the next estimate meant to cover: a fixed positive-time strip at all heights, a shrinking-time regime, or a genuinely finite window? Fixing this early determines which model and remainder are relevant. Adjacent certified rectangles alone do not establish an infinite covering schedule or the T→0 endpoint.

The first question is the strongest conceptual place to help: find a relation that preserves the *complete* signed interaction, rather than insisting that every remainder be small. It is a direction for exploration, not a proposed theorem. The current next task remains E02 R11 assessment; B03 R12 is unstarted. No new numerical campaign is justified until a specific analytical consumer, source information, domain, full error payment and failure condition have been fixed.
