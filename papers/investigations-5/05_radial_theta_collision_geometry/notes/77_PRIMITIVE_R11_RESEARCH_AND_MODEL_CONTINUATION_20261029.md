The joint expression is now available in a useful exact form:
\[
 (c_j''-c_j)k_r+4r(c_j'+c_j)\partial_r k_r
                         +4r^2c_j\partial_r^2 k_r,
 \qquad r=e^{2u},
\]
where k_r is the spherical average of a translated unit Gaussian.
After adding both sphere terms and integrating against the complete
primitive, it reconstructs K1 F_j exactly. Propagation and pairing with
the true residual therefore preserve the full correlation.

primitive positivity.
