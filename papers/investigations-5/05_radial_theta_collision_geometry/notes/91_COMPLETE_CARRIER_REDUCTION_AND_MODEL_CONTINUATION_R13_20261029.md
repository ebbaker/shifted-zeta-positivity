The fixed drift is the gradient of
\[
 V(r)=\tfrac12r^2+\tfrac12\log(1+r^2).
\]

feedback equation have the classical Gibbs form
\[
 h_\lambda=M e^{-\lambda V}/Z(\lambda),\qquad
 Z(\lambda)=\int_{\mathbb R^3}e^{-\lambda V},\quad\lambda>0.
\]
Their scalar feedback ratio equals \(\lambda\). The quantity
\(Q(\lambda)=M^2Z(2\lambda)/Z(\lambda)^2\) is strictly increasing from
zero to infinity. Thus exactly one candidate has the limiting energy:
\[
 \frac{Z(2\lambda_*)}{Z(\lambda_*)^2}=\frac{E_0}{\mu^2},\qquad
 \pi(E_0/\mu^2)^{2/3}\le\lambda_*
