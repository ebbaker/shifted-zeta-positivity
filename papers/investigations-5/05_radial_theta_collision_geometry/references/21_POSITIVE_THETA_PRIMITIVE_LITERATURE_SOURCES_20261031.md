## CNV1986Turan

George Csordas, Timothy S. Norfolk, Richard S. Varga. *The Riemann Hypothesis and the Turan Inequalities*. Transactions of the AMS 296(2), 521–541. [Primary source](https://www.math.kent.edu/~varga/pub/paper_157.pdf).

Checked or discovered passages: Printed pp. 522–524: equations (1.8)–(1.12), Theorem 1.1, Proposition 2.1.

Selected primary pages inspected as rendered images because the PDF is scanned. The integrated kernel is K(t)=integral from t to infinity of Phi(sqrt(u)) du. It is not the manuscript's psi_theta. The proof treats moment indices at least two analytically and the first separately; the full 21-page proof was not audited.
