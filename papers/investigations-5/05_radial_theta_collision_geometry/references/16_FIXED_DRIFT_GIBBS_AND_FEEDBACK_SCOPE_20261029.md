Relevance to [Note90](../notes/90_CARRIER_FEEDBACK_DEFECT_AND_STATIONARY_CANDIDATE_R13_20261029.md):
the stationary profile \(M e^{-\lambda V}/Z(\lambda)\) for frozen
\(\lambda>0\) is the classical Gibbs form. Note90 derives the radial
zero-flux equation, its scalar feedback identity and the strictly
increasing mass-normalized L2 level directly. No stationary uniqueness
or nonlinear convergence theorem is imported from this article.

The fixed-potential free-energy structure does not automatically apply
to the carrier's state-dependent coefficient
\(\beta(h)=2\|\nabla h\|_2^2/\int d h^2\). R13 pays the difference between
the original coefficient and this ratio, but does not prove convergence
of either coefficient or of the profile. A rigorous attraction or
stability argument for that scalar feedback dynamics remains separate.
Do not interpret the article's convergence of a time-discrete scheme
as a long-time theorem for the present nonlinear feedback equation.
