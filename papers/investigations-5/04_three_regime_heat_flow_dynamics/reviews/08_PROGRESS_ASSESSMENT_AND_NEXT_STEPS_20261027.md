## The energy conclusion is decisive within its stated class

For a fixed isolated event of finite multiplicity m, Note10 obtains

\[
E(t_c+u)=\frac{\kappa_m}{u}-2\kappa_m\gamma+O(u),
\qquad \kappa_m=\frac{m(m-1)}{16},
\qquad \gamma=(\log U)_{zz}(t_c,a).
\]

The first root displacement is common to the cluster and cancels in the gaps. The coupled second displacement yields the gamma term. Reflection of the full scaled root set makes the energy even in the scale parameter, giving the stated remainder. These are the relevant structural checks; estimating the two signed source products separately would not explain this result.

At a hypothetical canonical all-real threshold,

\[
\gamma=-\sum_{\lambda\ne a}(a-\lambda)^{-2}\le -\frac{m}{4a^2}<0.
\]

Thus the exterior increases the bounded energy term. Its contribution to one annulus is

\[
I_r=\kappa_m\log4-6\kappa_m\gamma r^2+O(r^4).
\]

For dyadic r these corrections sum to a finite quantity. Better estimates of them cannot create a fixed saving on every annulus. This explains the zero strict margin in Note10 without attributing it to a crude absolute-value bound. The exclusion concerns the specified event-conditioned expansion and bounded analytic exterior information; exact theta event incompatibilities and additional global arithmetic estimates remain outside it.

[Note12][n12] strengthens the lower conclusion. With p=m(m-1)/2, centered variance V, root interaction f_i, and compressive exterior defect S, its identities give

\[
E=\frac{p^2}{2V}+\frac12\sum_i(f_i-py_i/V)^2,
\qquad V'=4p-2S\le4p.
\]

Since V(0)=0, E>=p/(8u)=kappa_m/u on the isolating interval. Under B_x<=-lambda, the stronger bound p lambda/[4(1-exp(-2 lambda u))] has the same leading coefficient. Its small-u constant is kappa_m lambda, consistent with the local curvature term when the exterior slope approaches 2 gamma. The published root equation and Hermite normalization agree with [Polymath Proposition 3.1][poly3].

This is worthwhile conditional geometry and a useful check on future estimates. It does not supply the arithmetic upper bound needed for contradiction. The [control screen][controls] explains why: polynomial controls saturate the leading coefficient; the positive atomic threshold realizes a nonzero compressive correction. The atomic example lacks theta's eventual threshold simplicity, but that extra premise has not supplied a quantitative local saving. Euler-factor controls have their own source arithmetic; weighted-square controls have no all-real time, so the global exterior-sign hypothesis must not be assigned to their collision slices. Each family's actual failed premise must be retained.

## The source improvement is useful but has a different role

[Note13][n13] removes an important input dependency: it acquires sufficiently small raw error instead of inserting exact vanishing moments into the approximant. The target |c| r^(m+N+1) still depends on the event amplitude, and energy transfer still requires wall, count, and conditioning gates. Its resolution-dependent finite models are frozen on each annulus; they are not automatically one holomorphic family in r.

[Note15][n15] and [Note16][n16] compare the same physical (H,H_x) target, retain both contour arms and the derivative of the prefactor, and complete reflection before truncation. Paying the shrinking analytic radius removes the apparent fixed-shift improvement in the leading Taylor-entry count. The moving contour gives a genuine improvement in the stated sufficient ledger on each fixed positive slab:

\[
KJq=O(X^{3/2})\quad\hbox{versus}\quad O(X^{3/2}\log X).
\]

This counts individual mode contributions before aggregation. Derivative generation, convolution, working precision, cancellation, and repeated-query amortization can change the computational comparison. Both expressions also grow as a power of numerical height X; they do not furnish inexpensive coverage merely because log X is a short input.

The [scalar replay reported in Review07][r07] fails the moving fixed-Q mode target at 2^16, 2^64 and 2^256, and passes at 2^512. These are budget evaluations, not computed theta jets or a practical crossover. They support the handoff's caution about onset. The constant zeta must be below b/16; a fixed-b asymptotic theorem cannot be substituted into a simultaneous small-b limit without further bounds.

For an eventual eventwise proof, constants bounded at b=0 are not necessary. Excluding every arbitrary fixed positive candidate would suffice. The present obstruction arises earlier: no excluding margin has yet been acquired even for a single new fully covered slab. The inherited exterior scale remains the one supplied by [Polymath Theorem 1.5][poly1].
