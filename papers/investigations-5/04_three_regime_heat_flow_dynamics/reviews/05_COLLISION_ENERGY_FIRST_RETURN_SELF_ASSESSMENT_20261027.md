## Findings and disposition

The return meets the first-package requirement to proceed beyond the source
interface: it supplies a direct shifted-source remainder and energy
transfer, evaluates the first coupled signed correction, tests the specified
transverse fallback, and completes the local error/limit audit. The central
arithmetic exclusion remains open. In particular, a correct event-conditioned
energy asymptotic cannot be relabeled an independently acquired upper estimate.

The quantitative changes are:

- Complete low-jet subtraction changes the frequency-tail allowance from
  a raw O(r^{-m}) normalized payment to O(r^{N+1}) at fixed exact moments.
  The moments and amplitude are an explicit acquisition debit.
- The complete source-to-energy transfer has finite constants on a
  derived scaled discriminant neighborhood, with no physical minimum gap.
- The inherited E=kappa/u+O(u^{-1/2}) is sharpened to
  E=kappa/u-2kappa gamma+O(u), including an explicit Cauchy remainder.
  Equivalently I_r=kappa log4-6kappa gamma r^2+O(r^4).
- Keeping the signed gamma exactly does not improve the coefficient.
  The all-real threshold sign gives a finite lower correction instead.
  The transverse check has the same failure with a complete bias payment.

This is a bounded approximation gain and a scoped failure of a tested
mechanism. It is not evidence for a new value of Lambda or for RH.

## Mathematical checks

1. **Source, domains and ownership.** The source remains
   exp(t_cv^2)Phi(v), with cos(av+rwv); no negative-time theorem is
   transported by a change of label. The fixed-order and moving-endpoint
   recurrence domains were read. Both fail to directly cover the required
   fixed-height r->0 use, so the direct integral was developed as required.
   No work on the other chats' Dobner or growing-window programs was assumed.

2. **Preparation through the collision.** The complete fixed-wall equation
   is imported from HDC Note12, including its unit forcing and complete
   H_zz/H quotient. The conditional wall and count are justified locally
   by analyticity, with a wall floor kept as data. This does not assert
   that the discriminant itself is positive at the collision. Its logarithm
   is used only on right-time simple-root slices.

3. **Factors and exterior sign.** With unordered pairs,
   sum S_i^2=2E and d(log D)/dt=8E+4B. Constant b produces a common
   translation and B=0. Stationary sine controls require B=-2E;
   deleting it falsely predicts root motion. The replay checks nonconstant
   exterior values on exact rational root configurations and rejects the
   equation with the exterior omitted.

4. **Scaled equation.** q_r=r^{-m}P and beta_r=r b give
   e_r=r^2E and B_scaled=r^2 B_physical. Thus the integrated exterior
   term has coefficient -1/2 in Note09 (6), and its bounded-unit allowance
   is O(r^2). The time derivative holds r,a,c fixed. The wall-radius,
   complete-source and derivative costs are explicit.

5. **Finite source hierarchy.** Expanding the combined kernel, rather
   than its factors separately, gives exactly h_n r^nP_n. Formula (8)
   retains the entire [0,V] remainder and complete low moments, and solves
   heat exactly. Cauchy on a complex r disk followed by scaled s,w buffers
   proves the stated factorial derivative constants. The remote theta
   bound follows from 2n^2 e^{4v}>=n^2+e^{4v}; the endpoint gate is
   preserved for v>=V>=1. No raw error is silently divided by a nonvanishing
   field. Approximate low moments add (11), and may destroy the scale bound.

6. **Energy transfer.** D is the usual monic discriminant; Q is the
   symmetric sum obtained by deleting one squared-gap factor. Hence Q/D
   is exactly the unordered energy. The boundary log in (14) is used only
   on its zero-free annular neighborhood, and integration by parts is
   legitimate there; an interior log with zero/pole singularities is not
   assumed. Newton's recursion proves the stated coefficient bound.
   Polynomial absolute sums on the coefficient box give (16). The
   discriminant floor follows from the explicit Hermite baseline and
   homotopy; it is not an assumed physical gap floor. No optimality or
   practical constant advantage over root disks is claimed.

7. **Coupled correction and analyticity.** The root perturbation has
   first term 2alpha s, common to every root, and second term
   (2beta-alpha^2)s w_i^0. Both coefficients were checked as polynomial
   remainder identities separately in alpha^2 and beta. Reflection under
   r->-r makes the symmetric energy analytic and even; this, not a
   generic O(r^3) remainder alone, justifies O(r^4). Cauchy on the paid
   complex-r disk supplies C_E. The discriminant and unit contributions
   were checked separately and then combined: either dropping the unit
   or its sign changes the result.

8. **Canonical sign is conditional and non-excluding.** The complete
   moment kernel (22) was preserved before its magnitude estimate. At an
   assumed all-real threshold the order-one product gives
   gamma=-sum_external(a-lambda)^{-2}. Evenness supplies the explicit
   contribution from -a. Neither conclusion contradicts the event;
   both hold for suitable controls. The sign improves a finite lower
   energy term, not the upper logarithmic coefficient. The source step
   distinguishing unchanged theta arithmetic has not been acquired.

9. **Transverse fallback.** On the pre-event slice the root drift is real
   and the imaginary part is even in r. This gives (27) with an explicit
   local Cauchy bound. The regulated potential uses the correct 1/(4pi)
   normalization and Lipschitz constant 1/eta. Integrating the logarithmic
   singularity on actual root disks gives eta^2 log(1/eta) bias with
   declared unit/far floors derived from Hermite gates. Taking eta=r with
   delta=O(r^3) makes all payments vanish. The physical regulator is
   |c|r^m eta. No time differentiation occurs; the terms required if it
   did occur are explicitly displayed in (30).

10. **Summation and quantifiers.** The annuli tile toward zero; their
    leading contributions divide by log4 when converted to a logarithmic
    coefficient. The gamma, analytic, source-tail and regulator payments
    are summable. Fixed persistent errors enlarge the coefficient; they
    cannot supply a saving. The completed tested budget has Delta_N=0.
    A larger window with other collisions is not silently assigned the
    single-cluster coefficient. Event parameters and N,V are fixed before
    r tends to zero. The exterior exp(C/b) height onset remains separate.
    Eventwise arguments may allow deteriorating constants at zero, but
    this return has no eventwise strict margin in the first place.

## Verification actually performed

m=2,...,12 and s=-1,1,3/2,4. It checks the Hermite recurrences and the
first two root corrections modulo P_m. An independently assembled quotient

not re-audited. No new external paper or third-party PDF was added.

lists, including the fact that finite-tail controls need not have any
all-real time. No control was rejected merely because its source is not
literally theta. This follows [avoid02][a02] and [avoid07][a07].
