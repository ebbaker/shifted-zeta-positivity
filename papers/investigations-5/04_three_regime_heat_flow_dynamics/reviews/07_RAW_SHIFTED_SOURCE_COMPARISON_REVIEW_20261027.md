## Findings

The comparison gives a complete analytical source-acquisition improvement,
with precisely scoped cost claims:

- Truncating after fullreflection preserves exponential errors; fixed raw
  modes and fixed raw frequency walls have actual algebraic endpoint errors.
- For fixed shifts, smaller Taylor order is offset by smaller analytic
  source disks in the optimized leading derivative ledger. The inherited
  retained-mode coefficient improvement remains, without a runtime claim.
- A moving strip gap zeta log^2 X/X, zeta<b/16, uses the positive-time
  carrier growth to give K=O(sqrt X/log X), Jq=O(X log X), and
  KJq=O(X^(3/2)) on each fixed positive slab. The matched raw sufficient
  ledger is O(X^(3/2)log X). Full frequency, quadrature and fixed-coefficient

The last is a logarithmic improvement in the declared source-data ledger.

evaluator, lower first-jet margin, or new Newman bound. The proof has
fixed b>0 and does not commute that limit with b down to zero.

## Checks and corrections

1. **Exact expression and seams.** The full even contour is shifted before
   either cutoff. Both arms and the derivative of exp(-sigma x) are present.
   The finite arithmetic and frequency-wall seams have signs D_0-D_V.
   Finite-mode Phi_K'(0)>0 follows from exact fullreflection and the negative
   omitted derivatives. The finite-wall first-jet signal survives phases
   at which its value component vanishes. These are fixed-cutoff statements,
   with no inferred necessary joint-growing complexity.

2. **Shared tail baseline.** The raw comparator uses the same sharp
   inherited exp(-pi M^2) mode bound at sigma=0, rather than the looser
   Note13 bound. The complete source constant, shifted mode-rate gates,
   physical derivative factors and both arm weights were checked.
   The frequency gate propagates beyond V>=1. In the moving construction
   the direct gate is used; the looser convenience onset is not imposed.

3. **Quadrature and normalization.** Source-disk radius delta consumes
   strip width. For finite retained modes delta=alpha-sigma is permitted;
   the entire finite source is bounded even at the damping boundary.
   The same t is retained in source and carrier before the supremum.
   Otherwise a separate B V^2 and -b log^2 X bound would conceal useful
   cancellation. Physical H_x is distinguished from the derivative of H/P.

4. **Precision and moving limits.** The fixed-shift precision threshold
   and tan(4g)=4(g+p) optimizer are correct for the specified ledger.
   The moving mode bound has beta_M>=4pi Q-9 and
   (1-r_M)^(-1)<=1+M/(2pi Q-4); no unknown denominator floor is assumed.
   With zeta<b/16 its normalized error decays as
   exp((zeta-b/16)log^2 X+O(log X)). All parameters are frozen on each box.

5. **Finite coefficients.** The general-theta allowance in Note15 (11)
   is conservative and valid. Fixed source coefficients preserve heat.
   The coefficient and final point-evaluation quarters are requirements,
   not executed computations. Only the lower endpoint of the permissible
   coefficient tolerance is safe for implementation; the replay records

6. **Scope and controls.** The approximation uses unchanged theta
   coefficients, but its success supplies no inequality that rejects a
   colliding control. Other even analytic sources can have their own
