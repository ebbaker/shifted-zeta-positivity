## Finding

The [result summaries](../../../../result-summaries/README.md) covered the
principal collision laws, controls and limitations, but omitted several
established positive results and much of the later geometric continuation.
The octonionic and manifold work deserved more than the previous blanket
“correct and sign-blind” caveat. Oriented associators and polarized Hodge
pairings retain signed information, and the sources prove actual integrated
positive estimates. Squared norms and intrinsic curvature can lose the sign;
none of the cited constructions supplies the missing global stationary
estimate by itself.

The audit follows the [patterns to avoid](../../../../avoid/README.md),
especially [status stronger than evidence](../../../../avoid/06_status_stronger_than_the_evidence.md),
[scoped exclusions](../../../../avoid/07_scoped_exclusions_read_as_universal.md),
and [lagging summaries](../../../../avoid/19_status_files_that_lag_the_findings.md).
Sources retain authority over these summaries.

not assume simple zeros at time zero. Its trace equivalence keeps the

control. Complete normalized coefficient laws can have genuine arithmetic
