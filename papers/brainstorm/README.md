# Brainstorming between investigation series

16 October 2026. Prepared for Edward Baker with substantial LLM assistance.
Model: **gpt-6-astra (Codex)**. Reasoning effort: **ultra**.

This folder holds brainstorming after a major series of investigations and
before the next research program is chosen. Each subfolder identifies the
series its discussion concerns. It preserves the alternatives, discussion and reasons
for narrowing them, while the source investigations retain their research
records.

- [Investigations 3](investigations-3/README.md): paths forward after the
  second investigation series; the initial Codex brainstorm and Claude's
  additional brainstorm are recorded, with discussion to narrow the options
  intended.

- [Investigations 4 brainstorm](../investigations-4/brainstorm/README.md) and [Investigation 12 brainstorm](../investigations-4/12_height_dependent_heat_contours/brainstorm/README.md) now live inside their research homes, following the author's 23 October 2026 affiliation correction. The [former location](investigations-4/README.md) retains a historical pointer.

Start with the repository's [result summaries](../../result-summaries/README.md)
and [patterns to avoid](../../avoid/README.md). Earlier brainstorming within
[investigations 2](../investigations-2/brainstorm/README.md) remains there.

Within a series, save brainstorm notes in `notes/`, assessments in
`reviews/` when needed, labelled support computations in `numerics/`, and
the relevant conversations in `chat-histories/`.
State the date, model and reasoning effort, and distinguish proposed ideas
from established results. A saved brainstorm does not open an investigation
or select a research program by itself.
