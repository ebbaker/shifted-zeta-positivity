## Sonin and positivity checks

The one-sided envelope at rate \(a=\delta/2<1/2\) gives the correct
zero exclusion. An independently positive split with adverse loss
\(\mathrm{poly}(r)e^{ar}\) yields such an upper envelope. With bounded
reference energy and \(E\leq\sqrt{A[F_r]\mathscr X(r)}\), the dual
sufficient rate is \(2a=\delta\), not \(\delta/2\).
Neither transfer assumes a rightmost zero.

The historical assessment's mean-only recommendation is superseded by
resonance; full positive ambient loss retains rate \(1/2\). Local
certificates remain bounded-window results. Source-selective overlap and
growing-head estimates are not incorrectly retired.

For \(E_b(z)=\xi(b-iz)\), a zero maps to
\(-\gamma+i(\beta-b)\), and \(E_b^\#(z)=\xi(b+iz)\).
The kernel's diagonal denominator is \(4\pi\Im z>0\). Strict diagonal
positivity excludes forbidden zeros; semidefiniteness may hide common
nonreal factors. Real boundary zeros are allowed by the target. At
\(b=1/2\), functional symmetry prevents strict dominance.
[Lagarias, Lemma 2.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0601653) supplies the
stated unconditional \(b\geq1\) and RH-conditional \(b>1/2\) baselines,
not the proposed independent inequality below one.

[Freitas, Theorem 1 and Lemma 3.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0507368)
matches the derivative normalization, reciprocal ratio and \(1/\tau\)
factor. One common \(\tau<2\) and all indices are essential. The displayed
Laguerre prime contribution follows by Leibniz differentiation of
\(\log\zeta(s)=\sum_{k\geq2}\Lambda(k)k^{-s}/\log k\), valid near each
fixed \(\tau>1\):

\[
\frac{1}{(n-1)!}\frac{d^n}{ds^n}
\{s^{n-1}\log\zeta(s)\}\bigg|_{s=\tau}
=\sum_{j=1}^n\binom nj\frac{\tau^{j-1}}{(j-1)!}
(\log\zeta)^{(j)}(\tau)
=-\sum_{k\geq2}\Lambda(k)k^{-\tau}L_{n-1}^{(1)}(\tau\log k).
\]

At \(n=1,2,3\) the kernels are respectively
\(1,\ 2-u,\ 3-3u+u^2/2\). This verifies the formula, not uniform
arithmetic domination as \(n\) grows.

## Approximation and source-range checks

For the prepared fractional-part function,
\[
\int_0^1\rho_\theta(x)x^{s-1}\,dx
=-\frac{\zeta(s)}s(\theta^s-\theta),\qquad0<\Re s<1.
\]
At a zero with \(\beta>1/p\), this functional is continuous on
\(L^p\), vanishes on the prepared span and takes value \(1/\rho\) on
the constant. Hence convergence excludes that open half-plane. The
constraint/span and general-\(p\) criterion agree with
[Delaunay–Fricain–Mosaki–Robert, introduction pp. 1–2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1101.1199).
No boundary evaluation or integer-only density theorem is assumed.

For a fixed finite \(P(1)=0\), \(F=(1-\zeta P)/s\) is holomorphic in
\(\Re s>b>1/2\). Elementary zeta partial summation with cutoff
comparable to \(|t|\) gives
\[
|\zeta(\sigma+it)|\ll_b(1+|t|)^{1-b}
\quad(b\leq\sigma\leq2,\ |t|\geq2).
\]
The polynomial is uniformly bounded there, so
\(|F(\sigma+it)|\ll_{b,P}|t|^{-b}\), whose square is integrable.
Pole cancellation controls the compact part, and absolute convergence
controls \(\sigma\geq2\), where
\(\int|\sigma+it|^{-2}\,dt=\pi/\sigma\).
Thus \(F\in H^2(\Re s>b)\). Constants depend on \(P\); this supplies
no convergence as its length grows. Continuous evaluation at a forbidden
zero has value \(1/\rho\), establishing the proposed sufficient target.

[Heath–Brown, Lemma 1](https://www.numdam.org/item/AST_1982__94__61_0.pdf)
supports the finite higher-identity framework; no unchecked exact
multilinear coefficient formula or applicable power-saving theorem is
asserted.

[Guth–Maynard v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.20552v2), introduction and
Proposition 12.1, supports the corrected source ranges. Direct substitution
of \(N\asymp X\) or \(X^{1/2}\), \(T\asymp X^{1/12}\log X\), still
fails the indicated improvements. This conclusion concerns that import,
not every future representation. Density permits an isolated zero.
