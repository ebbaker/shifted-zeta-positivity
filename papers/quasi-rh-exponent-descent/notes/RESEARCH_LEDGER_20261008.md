## Logical separation

1. Zeta-only quasi-RH is a bound on the real parts of zeta zeros. It supplies
   no Hecke or quadratic-family moment automatically.
2. A family strip plus the necessary reciprocal growth may imply smooth
   sums for that same character. Any such contour implication must retain
   its growth and profile hypotheses.
3. A new moment theorem can carry stronger information than the starting
   strip. If it proves an endpoint without the starting strip, that fact
   belongs in the conclusion rather than being described as a bootstrap
   from quasi-RH.
4. An exact arithmetic lift or a sharper conductor cutoff is progress on
   the reduction. Its residual signed estimate remains a distinct task.
5. Finite theta truncations and compact numerical checks require tails and
   a separate large-height argument before they can exclude all collisions.
   The third continuation supplies an effective large-height argument at
   every fixed positive time floor, but no compact numerical certificate
   or argument uniform as that floor tends to zero.
6. The localized integer quadratic moment implies a strip for every fixed
   primitive odd-conductor quadratic character. A proposed derivation from
   zeta-only quasi-RH must account for that stronger consequence.
7. The mixed cubic trace is a sufficient operator target, stronger than
   the original energy on its fixed column vector. Failure of an absolute
   operator bound is not failure of the original signed arithmetic target.
