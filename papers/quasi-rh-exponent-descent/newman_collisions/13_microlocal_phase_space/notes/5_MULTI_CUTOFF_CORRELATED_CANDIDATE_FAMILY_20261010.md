## 3. Active Schur acceptance, complete currents and closed coverage

Write the physical complete sum \(S_M=u+iv\),
\(S_M'=u'+iv'\). A genuine joint zero necessarily satisfies
\[
 |u|\le\eta/2,\qquad |u'|\le L\eta/2,
\]
\[
 \mathcal P=(2u/\eta)^2+2|u'|/(L\eta)\le1.
\tag{9}
\]
The second line is the first-jet Schur/Schwarz–Pick consequence of the
same fully paid holomorphic error disk, proved in Heat Note 18. For a
closed strip, let \(a_0,a_1\) be the distances from zero of the physical
intervals for \(u,u'\). Using upper bounds for both denominators gives
the rigorous strip lower bound
\[
 \underline{\mathcal P}
 = (2a_0/\eta_{\rm up})^2
       +2a_1/(L\eta)_{\rm up}\le\mathcal P.
\tag{10}
\]
All arithmetic in (10) is outward; the displayed source tests its lower
endpoint against one.

The adaptive algorithm accepts a strip when its paid value gap is
positive, or when **both** \(\underline{\mathcal P}>1\) and its
fully paid physical derivative gap are positive. Otherwise it bisects
the height interval. The first-jet Schur predicate is therefore used
directly for every strip retained by the value screen. The extra
derivative sign supports simple-zero counting; it also excludes a joint
zero by itself. Indeed, a positive derivative gap already makes the second
term of (10) exceed one, so the Schur check is redundant for exclusion in
these particular certificates. These certificates do not demonstrate an arithmetic
case where the curved Schur body alone succeeds while both component
tolerances fail.

The complete current is independently enclosed from the same physical
full sums:
\[
 \mathcal J_M=u'v-uv'=-\Im(S_M'\overline{S_M}),\qquad
 T_{\rm rect}=\frac\eta2(L|v|+|v'|).
\tag{11}
\]
This product includes all block, complement and cross interference;
the real observations retain the actual reflected carrier information.
Only two retained strips per new cutoff also have a positive lower bound
for \(|\mathcal J_M|-T_{\rm rect}\) at the chosen coarse subdivision.
The remaining current enclosures are inconclusive. Their active Schur
and paid derivative tests still certify them. A negative recorded
*lower gap* caused by an interval enclosure is not a negative actual
current, nor a genuine collision.
