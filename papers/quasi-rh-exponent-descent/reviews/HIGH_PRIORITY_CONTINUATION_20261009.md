## 2. Mixed arithmetic: a forced conductor domain and an exact remaining deficit

[Mixed Note 31](../mixed_character_families/notes/31_GATED_INVERSE_MASS_AND_POSITIVE_MOMENT_LIMIT_20261009.md)

\[
 \mathcal J=\sum_{u\in\mathcal C}|M_uQ_I(u)|^2
       1_{|S_u|^2\ge(c/C)U^{dm-\tau}H^{-b_M}},
 \qquad \tau=\lambda_1+e_M.
\]
The common character, original inverse, physical prime slots and all
deletion zeros stay inside this expression. No sharply selected family
is passed to a theorem requiring a smooth row ball.

The source's conductor-sensitive squared plain bound has exponent
\(d\min(m,\kappa-m)\), where \(\kappa=\log_UQ_\psi\).
This gives an actual necessary condition of the gate. If the total
witness, inverse, plain and height cost is at most \(\ell/4\), with
\(\ell=10^{-6}\), fixed constants are absorbed at the eventual threshold
and every gated row satisfies
\[
 \kappa>2m-\ell/(2d)\ge2m-1/720000.
\]
Thus the old low-conductor domain \(\kappa\le2m-1/1000\) is automatically
absent. This localization is conditional on a real loss allocation;
there is no evaluated detector manifest proving that allocation here.

Legal marked fourth moments, inverse-slot extension, and the existing bin
count yield explicit upper bounds for \(\mathcal J\). With original
whole-slot rounding and separate count-level costs at most \(\ell/4\),
two nonempty boundary strips of the buffered application wedge are already
paid. Their strict count reserves are \(37\ell/200\) and
\(137\ell/400\), before the final arbitrarily small power and threshold
costs. These are small conditional marginal improvements, not closure
of the maximal witness count.

The decisive new limitation concerns flat permitted amplitude profiles
\(g_i=dx\). At fixed \(r,m\), grant even the stronger fractional endpoint
capacities and zero losses. A one-level formal response table satisfies
**every** legal original-slot subset inequality of the named inverse,
plain-second and plain-fourth moments. Monotonicity in total subset width
proves this for all subsets; it is not an enumeration of a million-slot

\[
 \rho=\min\{R_*+\ell,A_I(r),P_{\rm plain}(m)\}.

On a central interval along the detector support boundary, of uniform
width greater than \(1/6000\), both marginal envelopes exceed

 \rho-R_{\rm new}=F_x\eta+\ell
 \ge\frac{220252413}{2012413000000}
 =0.000109446924\ldots,\qquad\eta=1/5000.

The inverse-slot shortfall cancels from this deficit. Finer rounding or
another Hölder interpolation of those same positive constraints does
not supply it.

This table is a limitation on the **named scalar marginal inputs at fixed
lengths**. It does not realize actual characters, physical coefficient
relations, a zero bin, or the whole source package at every length and

Those missing arithmetic relations are what the next proof must exploit.
The conductor localization is compatible with the table and does not

The next mixed checkpoint is therefore a genuine arithmetic bound for

## 3. Short families: growing logarithmic cancellation still fails this method test

[Short Note 28](../short_families/notes/28_GROWING_NORM_COMPENSATED_COFACTOR_BARRIER_20261009.md)
tests a case not covered by Note 27's fixed finite-span result. For a
squarefree cofactor \(P\) with \(J\) distinct prime ideal factors and
\(NP\le D^\beta\), \(\beta<1/20\), it combines the complete actual
blocks with coefficients
\[
 a_M=NM\prod_{p\mid P/M}(1-Np),\qquad M\mid P.
\]
The resulting cofactor coefficient is exactly \(\mu_K(b)Nb\): the
prime-pair Jacobian is compensated, and a growing mixed finite difference
kills the first \(J\) logarithmic moments. Equal-norm distinct prime
ideals are allowed. Its coefficient cost is explicitly
\(\sum|a_M|=\prod_{p\mid P}(2Np-1)\), and the block count is \(2^J\).

A uniform growing-order derivative and Taylor argument proves that the
continuum still has size \(D^{1-o(1)}\) under the whole cofactor norm
budget. The imported fixed-field prime ideal theorem transfers this to
the actual signed response in the explicit growing range
\[
 J=o\!\left(\frac{\sqrt{\log D}}{\log\log D}\right).
\]
The coprime coherent-row count then gives energy
\(D^{16/15-o(1)}\), incompatible with the target
\(D^{4/5+\varepsilon}\) for fixed \(\varepsilon<4/15\).

For larger \(J\), the absolute PNT error can exceed the continuum main
term; no actual-response lower bound is claimed there. Nor does a lower
bound for this constructed combination prove a lower bound for the full
response after arbitrary remaining-product cancellation. The new result
rules out this growing filter in its controlled range. It strengthens
the priority of the broader arithmetic mean and centered covariance in
Note 27, including compensation across different product shapes and a
separately paid complementary energy.
