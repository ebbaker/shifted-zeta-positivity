vector, complete disk and derivative errors, and fourth-jet threshold sign
were compared with [Heat Note 13](../newman_collisions/notes/13_RECENT_HEAT_RESULTS_AND_PATHS_FORWARD_20261009.md)

The finite vector is distinguished from the genuine scalar observation.
Fixed-time derivatives, fixed integer cutoffs, and the frozen center scale
are retained. No global real-root product for the locally normalized
function is inferred.

The exact Gaussian convolution, analytic transverse diffusion, product
oscillator lift, rotor theta trace, common-Gaussian composite heat factor,
normalized characteristic-function equation, SUSY ground-state/Ward
identities, Fock derivative transform, Wigner evolution, and polynomial
root dynamics received internal algebraic and sign checks. The elementary
radial inverse-marginal calculation and its nonlocal time evolution were
also checked. These checks concern the printed identities and conventions;
they do not verify a proposed arithmetic exclusion theorem.

The reviews corrected the comparison between actual-orbit and full-torus
estimates, defined the coherent derivative multipliers, distinguished
spectral fiber marginals from radial observation-space traces, made the
selfadjoint projection domain requirement explicit, and retained the
fifth/sixth jets introduced by time evolution. The multiplicity-three and
multiplicity-four necessary expressions have their own payment obligation.
The current local-to-average exponential gap and global coverage obligations
remain explicit.

Primary literature was consulted for the established mechanisms named in
the note. Citations support only their specified models or standard
identities. In particular, the localization, Nicolai-map, Parisi–Sourlas,
extension-operator, and Lee–Yang references do not supply the proposed
zeta dictionary. The three-bosonic/two-fermionic superspace model is an
explicit research proposal whose action, state, observable, and time
parameter still need construction.
