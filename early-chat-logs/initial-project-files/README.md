# Initial project files

13 October 2026. Prepared for Edward Baker using Claude Opus 5.5 (Claude
Cowork; configured identifier `claude-opus-5-5`, reasoning effort max), with
LLM assistance.

These are the project documents of the first three claude.ai projects of the
Riemann-hypothesis work. They are kept here because the sessions of that
period had no repository to save their outputs to. They come from the
claude.ai organization data export (`projects/<uuid>.json`); each document's
content is written unchanged.

| Folder | Project | Dates | Files kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| [riemann-hypothesis/](riemann-hypothesis/) | Riemann Hypothesis | 17–23 August 2026 | 44 of 45 |
| [rh2/](rh2/) | RH2 | 23–24 August 2026 | 61 of 77 |
| [rh3/](rh3/) | RH3 | 24 August – 14 September 2026 | 47 of 85 |

Files under `claude/` were written by Claude during the projects' sessions;
the others were uploaded to the projects. The conversations behind them are
in the Claude chat registries: chats 1–17 of the
[early Claude chat logs](../claude-cowork/README.md) (Riemann Hypothesis, RH2
and the first RH3 sessions), and the later RH3 sessions in the `claude-cowork`
registries of [misc](../../papers/misc/chat-histories/claude-cowork/README.md),
[shifted-zeta](../../papers/weil-positivity/shifted-zeta/chat-histories/claude-cowork/README.md)
and [susy-positivity](../../papers/weil-positivity/susy-positivity/chat-histories/claude-cowork/README.md).

For RH3, only the documents that are not reflected in the repository are kept:
the working notes, briefs, round reports, lab reports, handoffs and early
reviews. Its papers, verification files, later reviews and repository drafts
are already in the repository, verbatim or as later versions, so they are left
out (34 documents). A document counts as reflected when at least half of its
lines of 30 or more characters appear in tracked repository files; every RH3
document kept here has 6% or fewer of its lines there.

[manifest.json](manifest.json) lists every document of the three projects with
its creation time, size and SHA-256, and records where it is stored or why it
was left out. Besides the 34 reflected RH3 documents:

- 17 uploaded copies of files from an earlier project are kept once, at their
  first location (15 in RH2, 2 in RH3).
- The third-party papers `Dorey_et_al.pdf` (RH2, again in RH3) and
  `Perlmutter.pdf` (RH3) are left out; the repository keeps third-party papers
  out.
- `claude/outreach_and_protection.md` (Riemann Hypothesis) is marked “private —
  not for circulation” and is held back.

No file here is larger than 100 KB.
