  Lambda/y0^2 -> 1/2 regardless of background (the isolated-pair value),
  while |L_1|/y0^2 varies by orders of magnitude with it.  So Lambda is the
  CANONICAL quadratic, and the programme's discriminants are
  configuration-dependent multiples of it.
